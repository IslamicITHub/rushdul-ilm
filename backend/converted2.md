**General overview of the virtues of the month of Muharram and fasting in it**

| Topic | Key points (with sources) |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **Muharram as a sacred month** | • It is the first month of the Hijri year and one of the four *sacred* months (1st, 7th, 11th, 12th) – Qur’an 9:36; Bukhari 2958, Muslim 1679. <br>• Allah says “the number of months with Allah is twelve… of them four are sacred” – Qur’an 9:36. <br>• In sacred months, wrongdoing is more serious and righteous deeds are rewarded more – Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Tafsir Ibn Kathir. <br>• The month is called *al‑Muharram* (sacred) to emphasize its sanctity. <br>• The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “The best fast after Ramadan is in the month of Allah, al‑Muharram” – Muslim 1163. <br>• Fasting in this month brings a greater reward than in other months. <br>• The virtue of fasting in Muharram is due to Allah’s generosity and kindness to His slaves during it – Qawa‘id al‑Ahkam. <br>• The month is also a time to avoid sin, as “wrong not yourselves therein” (Qur’an 9:36). | **Sources**: IslamQA answers 204142, 21311, 69781, 10263. |
| **Fasting in Muharram** | • The Prophet (peace be upon him) encouraged fasting in the sacred months, including Muharram – Abu Dawud 2428 (though weak). <br>• The hadith of Abu Hurayrah (Muslim 1163) states that the best fast after Ramadan is in Muharram. <br>• The Prophet (peace be upon him) never fasted the entire month, so the hadith means “fast a great deal” rather than the whole month. <br>• Fasting in Muharram is a Sunnah and brings great reward. | **Sources**: IslamQA answers 204142, 21311. |
| **When to fast** | • The month of Muharram begins with the sighting of the new moon. <br>• If the new moon is not sighted, the month is considered 30 days; if it is sighted, it is 29 days. <br>• To be certain of fasting the correct days, one may fast two consecutive days (e.g., 9th‑10th or 10th‑11th) if the month length is uncertain. <br>• If one wishes to fast the *White Days* (13th‑15th) or other specific days, they should observe the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. | **Sources**: IslamQA answers 10263, 69781. |
