    Question ID: 36138Country: India

    Title: assalamaleikum dear mufti sahab,

    Question: I need some duas for (1) my sister's marriage, who had been divorced with all allegation, nothing is fact from her in-laws side for what they are seeking for divorce, Really she had a very bad phase that she had gone through, and now we are looking for matches which suits to her and can lead happy life. (2) My husband's relation with our family is not at all good, my parents' all efforts, money everything will always in vain, because whatever they do my husband will always verbally abuse them very badly, so I need some dua for good relation between my husband and my mother, brother?

    Answer ID: 36138Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 319/123/L=1433) (1) You and your family members should recite Ya Latif Ya Wadud. Also ask your mother to recite Ya Latif 1111 times after Isha salah with durood 11-11 times before and after. (2) You should keep on supplicating Almighty Allah after offering nafl salah for good relation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
