    Title: Is it allowed to go for Wudu or going to toilet while Azan is given?

    Question: Is it allowed to go for Wudu or going to toilet while Azan is given?

    Answer ID: 25479Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1483/1483/M=1431)

    If one is not in severe need, it is better not to go to the toilet out of respect of azan. In the same way before starting wudhu if azan was started, one should postpone the wudhu and answer the azan and then make the wudhu. However, in the time of need both the said acts are lawful. In case of no need too, it is not unlawful, but it is not preferred and better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
