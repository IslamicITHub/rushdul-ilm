    Title: Asalamualaikum Hazrath, 1. Can a wife person namaz behind her husband and husbans as Imam?. woman can perform namaz behind imam in masjid in any country. please clarify.

    Question: Asalamualaikum Hazrath, 1. Can a wife person namaz behind her husband and husbans as Imam?. woman can perform namaz behind imam in masjid in any country. please clarify.

    Answer ID: 21661Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 595/595/M=1431)

     

    (1) A wife can perform salah behind her husband provided she is standing behind him.

    (2) It is preferable for woman to perform salah in her home.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
