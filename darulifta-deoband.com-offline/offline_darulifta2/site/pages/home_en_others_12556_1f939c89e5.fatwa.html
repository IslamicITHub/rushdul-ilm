    Answer ID: 12556Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1101/850=H/1430)

     

    It is nice dream. It means that the marriage proposed and solemnized by parents shall have more blessings and barakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
