    Title: I need to ask "I am a converted Muslim and want to marry a Muslim girl, is this allowed and will Islamic society will give equal respect to us and our future generation

    Question: I need to ask "I am a converted Muslim and want to marry a Muslim girl, is this allowed and will Islamic society will give equal respect to us and our future generation"? I have accepted Islam wholeheartedly with my beliefs and faith in Islam.

    Answer ID: 36996Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 275/215/B=1433) Almighty Allah says in the holy Quran: إِنَّمَا الْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِخْوَةٌ All believers are but brothers. There is equality in Islamic society. You can marry the girl of any Muslim society whom you find suitable for yourself.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
