    Answer ID: 11209Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 333/333=M/1430)

     

    In the abovementioned case, it is better not to marry in that family, you should prefer religious-minded family.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
