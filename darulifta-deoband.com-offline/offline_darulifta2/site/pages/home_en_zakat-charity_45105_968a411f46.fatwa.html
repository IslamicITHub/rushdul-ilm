    Question ID: 45105Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Zakah and Loan

    Question: I have made an agreement with one of my friend to do business on conditions that me as investment partner and he as working partner on 40% & 60% basis. My friend was very poor and was not having enough income to meet his expenses, by the time he consumed all the money for his needs and has no money anymore to return back to me. He is in a bad situation now and will not be able to return my investment but promising he will return my investment once possible. Can I consider this investment amount as Zakah and withdraw my demand for money.

    Answer ID: 45105Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 735/682/N=1434) The money which you gave to your friend for doing business on muzaribah and he consumed all those money and now he does not have those money in his hand then in this case you cannot make intention of zakah for the same money and your zakah shall not be paid because in such case it is necessary that the same money is available with the poor person then the intention of zakah shall be valid. However, if you pay your zakah to him and get it back from him in return of your due amount then it is lawful. It is mentioned in Shami (3/308 published Maktaba Zakaria Deoband): قوله: "والمال قائم" لأنه لو رضي بذلك بعد ما استهلك الفقير المال لم تصح نيته اهـ It is mentioned in page no 190 and 191: وحيلة الجواز أن يعطي مديونه الفقير زكاته ثم يأخذها عن دينه اهـ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
