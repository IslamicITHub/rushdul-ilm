    Answer ID: 12226Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1016/793=H/1430)

     

    Both the dreams mean that you should divert your mind and heart from your uncle and his daughter. In this matter, none of the interpretations seems in your favour.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
