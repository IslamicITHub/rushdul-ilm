    Title: To whom can a will/wasiyatnama be done in Islam under Mohammedan law/Islam and to what extent

    Question: To whom can a will/wasiyatnama be done in Islam under Mohammedan law/Islam and to what extent? Kindly reply with Islamic references, in details.

    Answer ID: 37762Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 625/625/M=1433) One can make a will of some thing which he owns for a person who is not his heir; this will be out of one third property.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
