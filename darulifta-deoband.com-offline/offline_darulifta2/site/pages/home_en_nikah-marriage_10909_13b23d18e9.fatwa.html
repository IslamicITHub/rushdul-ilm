    Title: Assalamualikum Mufti Sb., Is it permissible to do Marriage younger brother if the Elder brother Marriage is not done due to elder brother is working in Gulf.

    Question: Assalamualikum Mufti Sb., Is it permissible to do Marriage younger brother if the Elder brother Marriage is not done due to elder brother is working in Gulf.

    Answer ID: 10909Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 224/223=M/1430)

     

    It is alright, but the elder brother also should not delay his marriage.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
