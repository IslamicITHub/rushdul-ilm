    Title: I was sitting in a Masjid and this girl Salma was sitting in front of me and as soon as she looked at me she was like shivered of fear

    Question: I was sitting in a Masjid and this girl Salma was sitting in front of me and as soon as she looked at me she was like shivered of fear, then I came out of Masjid and I see her brother talking to my brother and sister. I did not get a good feeling about her brother (probably as not so nice guy). (2) Where I see a very old building is getting collapsed and a few old cars were there on its roof and the whole building felt to ground. (3) Me and my eldest brother were trying to enter a house a dog came running out of it and tried to bite me, I tried to stop but it was just trying to bite me but not my brother". (4) I was in a house with lots of people and I was looking at a girl who was very beautiful and I liked her very much, but at the same time I see one more girl there very pious and felt like she is actually the girl I am looking in marriage? My brother is against this marriage and I think my family will collapse if I move further with this marriage. Please suggest me.

    Answer ID: 41367Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1436/L=533/TL=1433) If you are getting this indication in your istikharah that it is not better to marry there then you should preferably not marry there, . In order to get pious wife keep on reading the following dua extensively: رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
