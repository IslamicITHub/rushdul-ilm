    Answer ID: 7790Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1615/1533=D/1429)

     

    It is indication that you shall live longer than some of your relatives. Take interest in good deeds as much as you can. It is commendable to like Islamic atmosphere and want things to be on the Islamic line. You should thank Allah for the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
