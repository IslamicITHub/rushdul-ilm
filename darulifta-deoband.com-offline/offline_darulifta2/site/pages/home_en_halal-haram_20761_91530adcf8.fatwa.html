    Title: I would like to know about to buy and sell or having bussines of humain hair and feak(false hair) specially women's hair tell me hazarat mufti saheb it is halal or haram to do so?

    Question: assalamo alayqum hazarat mufti saheb I would like to know about to buy and sell or having bussines of humain hair and feak(false hair) specially women's hair tell me hazarat mufti saheb it is halal or haram to do so? if is haram so please tell me in details please.assalamo alayqum.

    Answer ID: 20761Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 373/373/M=1431)

     

    The trading of human hair is not right because it is against the respect of human parts. If there is no evil in dealing of the artificial hair, it is allowed in Islam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
