    Answer ID: 14632Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1314/1072=D/1430)

     

    Shoes are not necessarily interpreted as husband and wife. These are seemingly astray thoughts, you should neither pay any attention to it nor pursue to find out its interpretation, rather recite the dua:

    اللھم انی اعوذبک من الشیطان وشر ھذہ الرویا

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
