    Question ID: 35986Country: United Arab Emirates

    Title: I want to marry a girl, as soon as possible. My parents are delaying for searching a girl for me, and to make a decision for my life

    Question: I am Mubashir, an engineer, working in Dubai, UAE. I am 26 years old. I want to marry a girl, as soon as possible. My parents are delaying for searching a girl for me, and to make a decision for my life. So please tell how should I inform my parents that I want to marry soon, and also tell me a dua what I have to do, for my good partner and to marry soon. Hope you understand my question. Awaiting for your response.

    Answer ID: 35986Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 148/124/B=1433) You should convey your parents through someone that they should arrange for your marriage soon. If your parents make a delay, you can search your match by yourself and then with the advice of your parents you can marry. Recite Ya Latif (يا لطيف) extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
