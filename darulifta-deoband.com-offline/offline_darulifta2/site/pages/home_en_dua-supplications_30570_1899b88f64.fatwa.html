    Title: I am suffering from irregular periods from dday i got menses.am under treatment from d day one.am tired off it.ive got married from past 9monthhs.i want to concieve bt due to this prob til nw ive nt got any.....infact am suffering from pcos. if i eat tablet ill otherwise ill not.. i want baby asap n my health to be recovered as well. i do lots of zikr like 1.laillahah illa anta subhanaka inikuntu minzaulimeen 2.surahe maryam 3.surahe ibrahim. wht else shuld i do for my prob plz pray for me n tell some other zikr as well.il be much thankfull. awaiting for ur reply asap.

Am suffering from irregular periods from dday i got menses.am under treatment from d day one.am tired off it.ive got married from past 9monthhs.i want to concieve bt due to this prob til nw ive nt got any.....infact am suffering from pcos. if i eat tablet ill otherwise ill not.. i want baby asap n my health to be recovered as well. i do lots of zikr like 1.laillahah illa anta subhanaka inikuntu minzaulimeen 2.surahe maryam 3.surahe ibrahim. wht else shuld i do for my prob plz pray for me n tell some other zikr as well.il be much thankfull. awaiting for ur reply asap.

    Answer ID: 30570Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 265/245/B=1432)

    First, you should go through Unani treatment and get your menstruation period disorder cured. You should keep on reciting the following dua extensively:
    رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِي فَرْداً وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْوَارِثِينَ [الأنبياء : 89]
    Hadhrat Shaikh Zakariya (رحمة الله عليه) had suggested the following practice:
    Boil two eggs in the morning and after peeling them write the following ayah on one egg which the husband would eat:
    وَالسَّمَاء بَنَيْنَاهَا بِأَيْدٍ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ [الذاريات : 47]
    While, on the other egg write the following ayah which the wife would eat:
    وَالْأَرْضَ فَرَشْنَاهَا فَنِعْمَ الْمَاهِدُونَ [الذاريات : 48]
    This should be done for forty days without any gap, and during this period you should have intercourse time and again. It is hoped that you shall be conceived.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
