    Question: Assalaamu `Alaykum Maulana saheb, - Please provide me with an effective wazifa to cure my skinn problem on my private parts, which the docters can't do anything about. - Please provide me with an effective wazifa to get married asap. May Allah reward our `Ulama of Deoband. Wassalaam

    Answer ID: 15730Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1905/1529=H=1430)

     

    Recite the following verse 11 times after every salah with durood 11 times before and after, and blow on hands and pass on the hands over your body:

    وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

    Repeat the same action at night while going to bed, blow on water and drink it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
