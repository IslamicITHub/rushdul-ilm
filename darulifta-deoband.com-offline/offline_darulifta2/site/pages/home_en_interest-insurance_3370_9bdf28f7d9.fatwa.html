    Title: What is Islamic point of view about service in Commercial Banks?

    Question: What is Islamic point of view about service in Commercial Banks?

    Answer ID: 3370Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 307/262=B)

     

    If the bank is involved in interest transactions then it is not allowed to work with bank. It is narrated in hadith that the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) cursed one who eats interest, one who feeds interest, one who writes it and one who bears witness to it. They are equal in sin, he stated. (Mishkat: 244)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
