    Title: A dad had sex with his step daughter. What is Shariah ruling about it? Is his nikah with his wife still valid

    Question: A dad had sex with his step daughter. What is Shariah ruling about it? Is his nikah with his wife still valid?

    Answer ID: 36740Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 336/336/M=1433) If the above mentioned incident is correct then the mother of step daughter (adulteress) became haram for that person. Whether he divorces her or leaves her. وحرم أيضاً بالصهرية أصل مزنية الخ (درمختار)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
