    Title: i m married and my husband chat with other girls on internet is this correct after marriage talking with others .what shall i do plz tell mee it is jayaiz or not..plz help me in this matter.

    Question: assalamualikum, i m married and my husband chat with other girls on internet is this correct after marriage talking with others .what shall i do plz tell mee it is jayaiz or not..plz help me in this matter.

    Answer ID: 21955Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 822/645/B=1431)

     

    A married man should pass his life with chastity. In the presence of a good wife, paying attention towards other girls and chatting with them is against Islamic rules and unlawful. Make your husband understand through any respected person of your family.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
