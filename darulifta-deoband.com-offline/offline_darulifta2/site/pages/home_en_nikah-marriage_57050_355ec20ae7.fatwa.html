    Question ID: 57050Country: India

    Title: Halal

    Question: (1) If someone kisses (in lips & tongues) his wife's real younger brother (sala) with lust, will it invalidate his nikah with his wife? What if the same case is with wife's real sister? If nikah is not terminated then what is the kaffarah for this kind of act. (2) Kissing a young boy or a girl is forbidden in Islam. If someone done this then what is the kaffarah for this act.

    Answer ID: 57050Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 163/163/M=03/1436) (1) In both the cases, the nikah of husband and wife will not come to an end; the nikah shall remain intact. But he shall be sinful due to this unlawful act. It is necessary to do taubah and istighfar for the same. (2) It is not unlawful to kiss very small boy or girl out of love but it is not lawful out of lust. And in case they are adolescent it should be avoided.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
