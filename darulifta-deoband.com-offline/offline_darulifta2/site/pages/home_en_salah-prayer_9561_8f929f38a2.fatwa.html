    Title: Which is more significant, the rak'ahs of Sunnatul Muakkadah or the tasbeehs that are to be read after every Fardh Salaah?

    Question: Which is more significant, the rak'ahs of Sunnatul Muakkadah or the tasbeehs that are to be read after every Fardh Salaah?

    Answer ID: 9561Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2517/2071=H /1429)

     

    It is mustahab (desirable) to recite Tasbihat-e-Fatima after every obligatory salah, while performing Sunnah Muakkadah is nearer to essential. Obviously, sunnah is more important than mustahab. In obligatory salah after which there are Sunnat Muakkadah such as Zuhar, Maghrib, Isha, one should perform the sunnah just after the obligatory salah, and then should recite Tasbihat-e-Faitma. This is preferably better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
