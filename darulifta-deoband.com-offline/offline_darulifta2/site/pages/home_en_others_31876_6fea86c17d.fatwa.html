    Question ID: 31876Country: Pakistan

    Title: I was involved in a lot of haram activites. but lately i have reverted back to islam and have been repenting alot and have been praying regularly and have also started sitting with tableegh jamat and doing a lot of zikr.

    Question: I was involved in a lot of haram activites. but lately i have reverted back to islam and have been repenting alot and have been praying regularly and have also started sitting with tableegh jamat and doing a lot of zikr. i have also kept a beard, dont to plan to shave it again and planning to keep it fist long inshAllah. i dreamt that i was inside haram and had a glance at khana kaaba. i dont remember much from the dream, i cant recall what i felt in the dream but i do remember that i was actually over there standing infront of holy kaabah. can you please interpret the meaning of my dream? my second question is that my father, even though regularly prays, but is extremely against tableegh jamat. please tell me some useful zikr and duas which i should pray to Allah to change his heart because i really wanna go for tableegh and i want him to go as well.

    Answer ID: 31876Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 932/608/H=1432) (1) It is blessed and nice dream. In-sha-Allah, you shall be benefited by Tablighi Jama’ah if you follow all principles with sincerity. (2) You should take care of him and supplicate for him specially. Also, manage to convince him with the advice of local Tablighi Jamah elders with the intermediation of any suitable person, who shall make him understand with wisdom and foresight, it shall be useful for your father in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
