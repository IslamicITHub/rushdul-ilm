    Title: pls let me know soon as possible that my husbend had given me three talaaq by saying in an anger after an argument. He said one talaaq two talaaq three talaaq daughter of then my fathers name. Pls tell me is this valid I only have aweek left for my iddah to end.

    Question: Salaam pls let me know soon as possible that my husbend had given me three talaaq by saying in an anger after an argument. He said one talaaq two talaaq three talaaq daughter of then my fathers name. Pls tell me is this valid I only have aweek left for my iddah to end. Thank you

    Answer ID: 19690Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 455/341/D=1431)

     

    If the husband has given three talaqs and he admits it, or there are legal witnesses, then three irrevocable talaqs (mughallazah) will took place and their husband-wife relation came to an end completely. After passing iddah period you would be free to marry any man other than your husband. Your nikah with your first husband is not valid without a legal halalah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
