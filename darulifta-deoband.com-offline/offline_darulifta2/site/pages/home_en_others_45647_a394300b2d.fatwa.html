    Question ID: 45647Country: Pakistan

    Title: Dream

    Question: In recent past I had met a girl, she needed help from me in applying for job application process. In that period of interaction I saw a dream that: "I am with her, she is in pain, my mother minds this when she saw me with her, I told my mother that there is no such bad thing between us, she is really in trouble and needs help, my mother said that she is telling lie and no issue with her. I insist that there is no such thing, if you are in doubt then come with us we will take her to doctor etc. Saying this I go to home/room and preparing myself so that I can go with that girl. While I was preparing to go, my mother came to me and said happily that girl has got baby girl." Now after some days of this dream in reality I asked my mother for her, and said I want to marry her but my mother refused (I am Sayyid and the girl is Arien). Please note that the girl got married in her early age 18, but unfortunately that marriage could not continue and now she is divorced and there is no sign of baby from old marriage.

    Answer ID: 45647Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 882/685/H=1434) The meaning of your dream is that it is not good to go ahead with marriage. You are advised not to do your nikah without the permission and consent of your mother.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
