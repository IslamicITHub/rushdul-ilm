    Title: Can we pay zakat to our relative or any one who is not oeering Salah as I am paying jakat to one of relative who do not offer salah?if we have paid this before as I was not awire did we have to repay for zakat which I have paid earlier?

    Question: Can we pay zakat to our relative or any one who is not oeering Salah as I am paying jakat to one of relative who do not offer salah?if we have paid this before as I was not awire did we have to repay for zakat which I have paid earlier?

    Answer ID: 14764Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1129/921=L/1430)

     

    A person not performing salah regularly also can receive Zakah if he is poor deserving Zakah, the Zakah will be valid. If you have paid your Zakah to any deserving relative, your Zakah was paid off and there is no need to pay it again. It should be noted that Zakah can be given only to those relatives who are not one?s parents and offspring.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
