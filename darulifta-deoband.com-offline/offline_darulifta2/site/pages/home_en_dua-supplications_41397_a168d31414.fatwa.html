    Title: My first question is I receive the Quran ayat through SMS and I delete this SMS

    Question: My first question is I receive the Quran ayat through SMS and I delete this SMS. Can we delete the Quran ayat SMS? Please reply. My second question is that can I read the holy Quran ayat when I am napak?

    Answer ID: 41397Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1317/881/H=1433) If it is finished automatically it is better, however if you remove it then it is also allowable but if you keep it then it is also right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
