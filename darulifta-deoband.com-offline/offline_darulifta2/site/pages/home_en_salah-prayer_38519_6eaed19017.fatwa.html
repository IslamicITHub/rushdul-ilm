    Title: Please give me a ruling on the following

    Question: Please give me a ruling on the following: (1) Taravih salah is led by a Hafiz who does not follow the Hanafi Fiqh. As such he performs the witr salah with two rakahs, tasleem and then one rakah and tasleem. Is the witr salah of a Hanafi valid? (2) During the fardh salah one saf is full and there is only one person behind. Is his salah valid?

    Answer ID: 38519Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 873/B=155/TB=1433) (1) According to Hanafi Fiqh it is wajib to offer three rakahs witr salah with single salam. If any Imam leads witr salah with two salams in Ramadan then the salah of Hanafi shall not be valid. Hence a Hanafi should offer his salah separately. (2) Yes, the salah of that person shall be valid. Since people nowadays are not aware of the rulings, so if the person who comes after the first saf (row) is complete and he pulls someone from the saf then he does not come. Hence now the fatwa is that he should stand alone in the saf without pulling any one behind. His salah shall be valid without any karahah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
