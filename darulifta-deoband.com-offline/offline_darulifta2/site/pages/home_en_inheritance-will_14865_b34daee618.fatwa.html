    Question: "As it is haram to hold credit cards (As they are charging on transaction) is it OK to use other person credit card and in turn pay? As many times there is need to do on line transaction 1. Is it OK to use muslim brother credit card? 2.Is it Ok to use non muslim friend credit card for transaction?"

    Answer ID: 14865Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1304/1044=L/1430)

     

    If an heir withdraws from his right by taking something in return of his shares, it is permissible, and the remaining wealth will belong to the other heir.

    (2) By giving up right voluntarily, the share is not cancelled, rather one can claim the same later as well, as it is mentioned in al-Ashbah wa al-Nazayir. Yes, if the shares are distributed and an heir gives his shares to someone, it is allowable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
