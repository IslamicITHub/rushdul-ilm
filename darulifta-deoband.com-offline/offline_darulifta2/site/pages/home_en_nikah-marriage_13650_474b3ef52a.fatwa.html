    Question: AOA, my father have to marriges but unfortunatly First. wife was divorsed. Me from 2nd wife (imran). From First wife i have step sister, married. The daughter of step sister is mehram or non mehram for me????

    Answer ID: 13650Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 954/954=M/1430)

     

    The daughter of step sister is mahram for you:

    لقولہ تعالی: وبنات الأخ وبنات الأخت الآیۃ وفي الدر المختار: حرم علی المتزوج ذکراً کان أو أنثی نکاح أصلہ وفروعہ علا أو نزل وبنت أخیہ وأختہ الخ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
