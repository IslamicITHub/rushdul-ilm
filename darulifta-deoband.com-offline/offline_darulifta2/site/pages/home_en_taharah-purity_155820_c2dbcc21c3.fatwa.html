    Title: Ruling on Big Cat Saliva

    Question: I bought a lion cub. I came to know that lion is also from cat family. So, basically, it is a big cat. It is tamed and domesticated and plays with me and licks me and I cannot stop it as it is very playful. Is it's saliva pure as I only feed it halal raw meat? Can I pray and perform my religious duties without washing it's saliva from my garments and skin?

    Answer ID: 155820Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 157/123/N=2/1439)

     

    (1) Lion and its cub belong to the species of carnivore, rather the wild cat is also one of them and the saliva and the leftover food of beast is impure though their diet is not haram. Thus, the saliva of a cub is impure (Nijasat-e-Ghaliza, severe impurity). If it smears with your body or cloth then it shall turn impure, and in case, it spreads more than the depth of a palm (one rupee) then salah will not be valid without washing it. And if it is equal to the depth of the palm or less than it then salah will be valid with karahat. However, one should wash it and should not offer salah with impurity equal to one rupee or less than it. In case you want to do the tilawat of the Holy Quran or dhikr etc then too it is better to remove the impurity smeared with clothe or body first and then recite the Holy Quran.
