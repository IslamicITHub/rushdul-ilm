    Question: Salaamu aleikum, wanted to ask the meaning of the following dreams. I dreamt that it was the day of yaumul qiyamah and I was standing in a room, then my late grandmother came to me and greated me, then my great grandmother also came happy as well as my grandfather. They were happy about something, but didnt say anything. I then dreamt that I proceeded to a qabrstaan where my mother was buried, and she wasnot resurrected with the rest of us as a result of sacrifises that she had made for us in this dunya. Dream 2: My Aunt told me that she dreamt my fatherinlaw who is recently late crying and trying to tell her something. When he parted it was after our families had a huge fight, but he did tell us to make peace with each other before he went. Jazakallahu Khairan

    Answer ID: 21141Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 739/564/H=1431)

     

    (1) Ma-shaAllah, it is very nice dream. It is hoped that your grandmother, grand grandmother and mother would enter in paradise. It is the meaning.

    (2) This dream warns that in worldly affairs paying off each others right is necessary on all of you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
