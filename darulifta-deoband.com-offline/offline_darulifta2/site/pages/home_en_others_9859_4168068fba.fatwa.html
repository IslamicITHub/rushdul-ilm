    Title: Asalamu alaikum, I am eager to know what, seeing 2 of your family members go to hajj then come back can mean in a dream. Also, what does being scared that you will miss a prayer, or missing a prayer in dreams mean? Jazak Allah.

    Question: Asalamu alaikum, I am eager to know what, seeing 2 of your family members go to hajj then come back can mean in a dream. Also, what does being scared that you will miss a prayer, or missing a prayer in dreams mean? Jazak Allah.

    Answer ID: 9859Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2606/2102=H/1429)

     

    (1) If tilawah is done punctually in the house, there shall be great benefit to the family members in-shaAllah. This is the interpretation of the dream.

    (2) There is negligence towards zikr, salah, tilawah and fulfilling rights. May Allah protect us!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
