    Answer ID: 12089Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 600/600=M/1430)

     

    Recite ya Mughni (یا مغني) after Isha Salah 1100 times with durood 11 times before and after. Also, recite this dua after every salah:

    اللھم اکفنا بحلالک عن حرامک واغننا بفضلک عمن سواک

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
