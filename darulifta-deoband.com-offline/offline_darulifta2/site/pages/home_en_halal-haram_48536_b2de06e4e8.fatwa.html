    Title: Eating Haram

    Question: If a person mistakenly has eaten something haram, then if he repents and donates the equivalent amount money, will it be perfect, or else what he has to do?

    Answer ID: 48536Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1365/1365/M=11/1434) He should do taubah and istighfar extensively and should take care in future that such mistake is not repeated. It is better if he offers two rakahs of salah taubah and seeks forgiveness from Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
