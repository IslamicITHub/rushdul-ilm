    Answer ID: 22042Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 999/B=193/tb=1431)

    If it is admitted by the relatives in whose presence he has given talaq, then talaq took place though he has given fake talaq, as well as iddah period was over. It is wajib on husband to pay whole mahr (dower) if he spent sometime with her in privacy. So you have the right to demand your mahr.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
