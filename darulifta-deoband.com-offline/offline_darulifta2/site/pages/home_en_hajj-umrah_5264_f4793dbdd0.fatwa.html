    Title:

    My question is, if person won the lottery, than he want to offer me money to performe umrah, it is permissible for accept it.

    Question: salams, my question is, if person won the lottery, than he want to offer me money to performe umrah, it is permissible for accept it.

    Answer ID: 5264Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 257/257=M/1429)

     

    If that person is giving you the same money he got from lottery, then it is not allowed to accept it and go for Umrah; since the money is unlawful. But, if he gives you any some lawful money, then you should accept it. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
