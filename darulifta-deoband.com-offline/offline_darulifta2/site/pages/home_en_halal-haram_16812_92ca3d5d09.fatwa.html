    Question: I AM HAVING A HAIR FALL PROBLEM, FOR WHICH I AM USING A OIL (OIL SUGGESTED TO MY SISTER BY A HAIR SPECIALIST)I AM GETTING BENEFITED BY THAT... LATER I CAME TO KNOW THAT THAT OIL IS ALSO HAVING ALCOHOL... PLZ SUGGEST ME CAN I USE IT AND CAN I OFFER NAMAZ WHN IT IS PRESENT IN HAIR.. JAZAKALLAH

    Answer ID: 16812Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1611/1611/M=1430)

     

    The ruling for alcohol mixed in oil is that if it is made of halal ingredients like wheat, potato etc as is the usually the case with Indian alcohol, then it is allowed to apply it in hair, and Salah can be offered with it. And if it is made of unlawful ingredients then one should avoid it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
