    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1057/801/H=1431)

     

    You are advised to recite the following verse after each salah 11 times with durood 11-11 times before and after:

    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Repeat the same before going to the bed, and sleep on clean (tahir) bed according to the sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
