    Question ID: 27528Country: Pakistan

    Title: My wife is a gynaecologist. She tries to do this job keeping herself within boundries of Islam and with devotion to serve mankind. She wants to use her salary merged with mine. Is it lawfull for me to use her salary.

    Question: My wife is a gynaecologist. She tries to do this job keeping herself within boundries of Islam and with devotion to serve mankind. She wants to use her salary merged with mine. Is it lawfull for me to use her salary.

    Answer ID: 27528Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1559/1559/M=1431)


    When your wife will observe complete Shariah limits while in job and present her salary to you, the salary will be halal for you. Apparently, we do not see any reason being it unlawful. If you have any reason being it unlawful, write it clearly and submit the question again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
