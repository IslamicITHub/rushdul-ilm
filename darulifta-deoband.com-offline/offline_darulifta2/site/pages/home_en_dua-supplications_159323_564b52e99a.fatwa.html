    Title: Since past 3 years searching for bride. Many proposals are coming but still it is unsuccessful. Please suggest any dua for getting married soon.

    Question: Since past 3 years searching for bride. Many proposals are coming but still it is unsuccessful. Please suggest any dua for getting married soon.

    Answer ID: 159323Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 745/578/SD=6/1439)

     

    Read the below dua thrice after each salah and supplicate, in-shaAllah you shall get married soon:

     

    قُلْ إِنَّ الْفَضْلَ بِيَدِ اللّهِ يُؤْتِيهِ مَن يَشَاءُ وَاللّهُ وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ يَخْتَصُّ بِرَحْمَتِهِ مَن يَشَاءُ وَاللّهُ ذُو الْفَضْلِ الْعَظِيمِ .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
