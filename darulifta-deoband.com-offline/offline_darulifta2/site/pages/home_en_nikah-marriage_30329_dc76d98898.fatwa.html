    Title: Is it lawful to marry a women when she is passing through her period.

    Question: Is it lawful to marry a women when she is passing through her period.

    Answer ID: 30329Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1075/665/L=1432) It is not prohibited to do nikah in menstruating period, but it is haram to have intercourse in this state.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
