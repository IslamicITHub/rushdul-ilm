    Title: Books regarding salah

    Question: I would like to know some authentic book giving complete details about prayers in journey whether in bus or train and when to pray qasr.

    Answer ID: 46050Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 998/780/B=1434) Please read the following books: (1) Namaz Ki Kitab. (2) Kitabul Masail. (3) Ilmul Fiqh. (4) Bahishti Zewar. (5) Fatawa Darul Uloom, Salatul Musafir Chapter (6) Fatawa Mahmoodiya etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
