    Question: Asaalawalayqum! I and my husband have been staying away from our hometown for nearly 16 years. My husband is a government employee. We have been trying for a transfer to some nearby place to our hometown but it happens so that when there is full hope don?t know what happens and a final letter of agreement never comes. So, I request you to kindly tell of some dua that may help us. Also mention the time and no. of times it has to be read each day. We will eagerly wait for a reply. P.S ? kindly see to this, that the reply is given in English

    Answer ID: 28598Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 68/54/L=1432)

    Both of you (husband and wife) should keep on offering salatul hajah extensively and supplicate Almighty Allah. In-sha-Allah, soon your wishes would come true.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
