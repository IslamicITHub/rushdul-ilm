    Question ID: 29928Country: Pakistan

    Title: In, Muddarbah under Islamic banking, one party provides investment while the other gives technical expertise and management. Profit if any, is divided between both the parties on prefixed basis while loss, if any, is only borne by the investor. Is it not exploitation of the investor and does Islam allow this?

    Question: Assalaam o Alaikum, Question In, Muddarbah under Islamic banking, one party provides investment while the other gives technical expertise and management. Profit if any, is divided between both the parties on prefixed basis while loss, if any, is only borne by the investor. Is it not exploitation of the investor and does Islam allow this? JazaakAllah

    Answer ID: 29928Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 267/L/77/tl=1432)

    The ruling of mudharibah is that one person invests and the second one does labour and the profit is distributed as per the contract basis. In case of loss, first it is compensated from profit, then from capital amount. If the mudharibah is carried out accordingly, it is lawful according to shariah and there is no exploitation of the investor.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
