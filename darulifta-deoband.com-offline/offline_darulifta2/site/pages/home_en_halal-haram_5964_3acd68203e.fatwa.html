    Title: Is it permissible to Shredd and recycle Islamic litterature with other papers because theres no place where i can bury it because theres no soil in my back yard and i cant put it in river etc because the govermant does not allow?

    Question: Asalamualaikum Question- Is it permissible to Shredd and recycle Islamic litterature with other papers because theres no place where i can bury it because theres no soil in my back yard and i cant put it in river etc because the govermant does not allow? Wasalam

    Answer ID: 5964Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 681/642=L/1429)

     

    Yes, it is permissible to shred and recycle Islamic literature with other papers. If the ink is dried away in a machine then there is no need to be concerned about it, otherwise the ink should be preserved and put into running water. If the government does not allow putting papers in the rivers, one can burn it and put the ashes in the river.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
