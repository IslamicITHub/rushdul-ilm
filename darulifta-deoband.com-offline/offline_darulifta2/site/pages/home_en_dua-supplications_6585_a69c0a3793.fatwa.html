    Title: How to remove evil jinn in the light of quran and hadith?

    Question: How to remove evil jinn in the light of quran and hadith?

    Answer ID: 6585Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 693/638=D-1429)

     

    There is a dua in Behishti Zewar chapter 9 named Hirz Abu Dujana (رضی اللہ عنہ). You should write it and put it around the neck of the patient. The dua has been instructed by the Prophet (صلي الله عليه وسلم). Also, you should recite Surah Al-Falaq and Surah Al-Naas and blow it over the body. Besides, there is a booklet of Shiakhul Hadtih Maulana Muhammad Zakaria named Manzil which contains the verses of the Holy Quran and authentic duas which are very useful to remove ghoul, magic, Satanic effects and tested by the pious men.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
