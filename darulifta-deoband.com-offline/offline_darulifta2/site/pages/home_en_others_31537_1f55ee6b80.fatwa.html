    Title: dream

    Question: yesterday i was in a sound sleep .and i saw a dream that my grandmother has died.and i am weeping too much .so tell me please what is the reason behind this dream ,or if any misshapening come to be in my life,so tell me plz im very disturb,so before 6 year beck i saw the same dream that my uncle has died .and im weeping lot,after this dream my uncle has died after 3 month .so tell me plz.

    Answer ID: 31537Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 944/613/H=1432) Death means elevation in ranks i.e. the ranks of your grandmother shall be high in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
