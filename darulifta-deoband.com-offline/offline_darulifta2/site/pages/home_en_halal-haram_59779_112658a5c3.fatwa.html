    Title: Kitchen Construction

    Question: Can a kitchen be constructed in a place which was used as toilets for more than 10 years. Is it permissible? The toilets have been demolished and the place is cleaned well.

    Answer ID: 59779Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 516/511/SN=08/1436) Yes, it may be constructed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
