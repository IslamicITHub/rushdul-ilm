    Title: Khula & re-marriage with same person

    Question: kindly let me know , if anyone takes Khula can she re marry with the same person ? what are the conditions for that

    Answer ID: 33205Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1183/1183/M=1432) If a woman requests for Khula and the man accepts it and agrees for khula, one talaq bai'n takes place. She will have to do new nikah to live with him again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
