    Question ID: 58431Country: India

    Title: Credit Card Company Job is Halal or not?

    Question: I have been working for the credit card company from last few years. My work is in the Fraud Control Team (fraud investigation team). Many of my friends raise questions on my job. My job profile is to investigate the fraud transactions or other activities on a credit card. I do not deal with the interest on money, I simply confirm transactions or investigate other details on the credit cards. Please update me whether working for the credit card is fine or not?

    Answer ID: 58431Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 380/380/SD=07/1436) If you have not to deal with interest, rather you have only to examine fraud dealing or you have to carry out non-interest task concerning the credit card then your job is lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
