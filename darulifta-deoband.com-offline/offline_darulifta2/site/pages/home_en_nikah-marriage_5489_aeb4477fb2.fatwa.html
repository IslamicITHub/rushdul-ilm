    Answer ID: 5489Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 666/618=D/1429)

     

    If it is true that you have pronounced no divorce to you wife, then she will remain your wife as before; therefore her marriage with anyone else is unlawful as per the Shariah.

     

    Islam has allowed a man to marry four women provided the husband is capable of discharging rights to all of his wives with equality and justice.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
