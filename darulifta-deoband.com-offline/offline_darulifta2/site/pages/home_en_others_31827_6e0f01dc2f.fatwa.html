    Title: I saw a dream doing zina with my mother. im not married yet and have also been adopted by my parents.

    Question: I saw a dream doing zina with my mother. im not married yet and have also been adopted by my parents.

    Answer ID: 31827Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 727/602/B=1432) If the dreamer is a worldly man, he might be consuming unlawful and haram earning i.e. interest, so he should avoid it. But if he is a pious person, he shall achieve the status of abdiyat i.e. he shall have humility.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
