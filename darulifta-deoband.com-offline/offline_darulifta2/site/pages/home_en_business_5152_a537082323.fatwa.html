    Title: I am doing business of Poly Packaging and packaging machine supply. now i am received order from a Liquer company to supply Packaging machine and Polythine.i do not use my investment money and do not use profit amount. i want to use it's profit to pay Bank interest and other govt.offices & others to pay ramsom(Ghoos).it is legal or illegal? please Reply , then i proceed in this regard .

    Question: Assalm o alaikum , I am doing business of Poly Packaging and packaging machine supply. now i am received order from a Liquer company to supply Packaging machine and Polythine.i do not use my investment money and do not use profit amount. i want to use it's profit to pay Bank interest and other govt.offices & others to pay ramsom (Ghoos). it is legal or illegal? please Reply, then i proceed in this regard .

    Answer ID: 5152Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1181/883=H/1429)

     

    The income earned through supplying packing machine and polythene will not be labelled as haram (unlawful), it is allowable to spend the money in the ways mentioned above.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
