    Title: Shall a person have to had ghusl after giving bath to a dead body?

    Question: If any person gives ghusl to dead body of Muslim person, then should that person himself also need to take ghusl after giving holy bath to dead body?

    Answer ID: 61317Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 426/515/L=05/1437) It is not necessary for a person who is giving ghusl to dead body to take a bath after it. However it is mustahab for him to take bath. قال فى الدر المختار : وندب لمجنون أفاق .... أو غسل ميتاً (درمختار) وفى الشامى: قوله أو غسل ميتاً للخروج من الخلاف كما فى الفتح . (شامى: 1، 310-311)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
