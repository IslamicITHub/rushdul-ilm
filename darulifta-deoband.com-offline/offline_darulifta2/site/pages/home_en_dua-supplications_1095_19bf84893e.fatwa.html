    Title: I have a great hair fall, please give me any wazeefa for regrowth and control of hairs. My age is 19 years. Please, suggest me, i am unable to bear the loss.

    Question: I have a great hair fall, please give me any wazeefa for regrowth and control of hairs. My age is 19 years. Please, suggest me, i am unable to bear the loss.

    Answer ID: 1095Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 527/516=N)

     

    Get it treated by any expert doctor or hakim and keep on reciting this dua eleven times after every obligatory salah with durood three times before and after:

    رب انی مسّنی الضر و انت ارحم الراحمین

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
