    Title: Whether a adult muslim boy [above 15 years old ] can divorce his minor wife [below 15 years]?

    Question: Whether a adult muslim boy [above 15 years old ] can divorce his minor wife [below 15 years]?

    Answer ID: 23043Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1266/952/H=1431)

    If the boy (husband) is adult and gives talaq, it shall take place though the wife is not adult.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
