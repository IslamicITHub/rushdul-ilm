    Title: Wealth distribution

    Question: I have four brothers and one sister. Please tell me the wealth distribution in percentage. In 100% how much will be divided among ourselves.

    Answer ID: 49483Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 25/27/N=01/1435) If your father or mother died and they left only you and your four brothers and one sister as heir then all the property of the deceased father or mother shall be divided into 11 shares, after paying due rights proceeding inheritance. Out of which 2-2 shares shall go to each brother and your sister shall get 1 share. If you want to know something else then write the complete detail and submit your query again. The detail of Shariah division is as follows: Details: Sons: 5x02=10 Daughter: 1x01=01 Total: 11

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
