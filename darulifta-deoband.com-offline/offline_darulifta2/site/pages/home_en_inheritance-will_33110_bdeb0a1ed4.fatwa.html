    Question ID: 33110Country: India

    Title: My father died some 4 years back and has left some property.

    Question: My father died some 4 years back and has left some property. Please let us know how to share father's property? He is survived by my one stepmother, 2 brothers one stepsister and 2 stepbrothers. Please advise the sharing percentage.

    Answer ID: 33110Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1108/934/B=1432) If both of you and stepbrothers and sisters are from the same father, then all the property of deceased shall be divided into 72 shares as per the Shariah. Your mother shall get 9 shares, 14-14 shares will be given to each son and 7 shares to the daughter. The detail of distribution is as follows: Detail: Wife: 1x09 = 09 Sons: 4x14 = 56 Daughter: 1x07 = 07 Total: 73 Shares

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
