    Title: During intercourse can a husband & wife have sexual talk and can see each other private parts. Can we have sex in standing position.

    Question: During intercourse can a husband & wife have sexual talk and can see each other private parts. Can we have sex in standing position.

    Answer ID: 6977Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1782/1381=H/1429)

     

    It is allowable to talk about their sexual relation.

    Seeing is also allowable, but not better.

    Doing it standing is not haram or sin, but it is not better too.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
