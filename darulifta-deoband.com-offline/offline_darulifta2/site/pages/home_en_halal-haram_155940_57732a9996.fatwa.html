    Title: Can I see the videos of Islamic scholars like Tariq Jameel sb., Mufti Taqi Usmani sb. etc?

    Question: Can I see the videos of Islamic scholars like Tariq Jameel sb., Mufti Taqi Usmani sb. etc?

    Answer ID: 155940Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 156/104/SD=2/1439)

     

    According to Shariah, it is not lawful to watch videos that contain pictures.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
