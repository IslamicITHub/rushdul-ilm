    Question ID: 33064Country: India

    Title: I would like to ask you is there any verse regarding saving from nazar, because one of my aunt has asked us to recite the last two verses of Surah Al-Qalam seven times and blow it on our child.

    Question: (1) I would like to ask you is there any verse regarding saving from nazar, because one of my aunt has asked us to recite the last two verses of Surah Al-Qalam seven times and blow it on our child. (2) Taking off nazar using eggs, burning chillies and other sources is allowed or not? (3) Some people say reciting Nad-e-Ali will cure your child. Is this allowed? If any of the above ways is correct please tell me, if not then guide me with the correct method?

    Answer ID: 33064Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1531/932/H=1432) (1) Your aunt was alright. (2) How do you pull off the nazar (evil eye) with eggs and chillies? Write the complete detail. (3) You should never go through the practice of Nad-e-Ali. It is haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
