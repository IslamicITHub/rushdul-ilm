    Title: I am travelling for my job and going to stay 29 days , what way i should pray ? As traveler i have to shortan? or for some period (How many day)shorten and then pray as normal? or from start i have to pray as normal? Is there differnce in Shafi and Hanfi in above case and what is ruling in Shafi and what is in hanfi?

    Question: I am travelling for my job and going to stay 29 days , what way i should pray ? As traveler i have to shortan? or for some period (How many day)shorten and then pray as normal? or from start i have to pray as normal? Is there differnce in Shafi and Hanfi in above case and what is ruling in Shafi and what is in hanfi?

    Answer ID: 11278Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 470/470=M/1430)

     

    In the case mentioned above, you will offer full Salah when you reach your place of work, qasr (shortening salah) is not allowed, but if the place of work is at a distance of 77.25 km or more, then you perform salah with qasr during journey and when you will reach the place you will perform full salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
