    Title: Performing Umrah after returning from Tai’f

    Question: When I completed my hajj I went to Tai’f for ziyarat and then I came back but I didn’t perform umrah. please tell me is the dam fardh on me or not.

    Answer ID: 165457Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 71/310/SD=04/1440)

     

    It is not mandatory to perform umrah after hajj. Hence in the question mentioned above no dam is wajib on you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
