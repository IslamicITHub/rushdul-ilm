    Question: AOA> my question is that i and my husband read intercourse DUA befor we sleep at night and and then in midnight we do intercourse without reading that DUA.Is this tight way. i vl be thankful to you. Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 18758Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 77/77=B=1431)

     

    It is not sufficient to recite dua of intercourse before going to sleep; you should recite it when you make intention of having intercourse.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
