    Title: Dream

    Question: I want to ask about my dream which I saw sometimes. Later, I saw that I was in my village of my mother and there is very terrible environment, of family members whom are females relative members they react like suspiciously, I think there is something on her like jinn or else their eyes are black covered every family members read Qur'an and dua there is huge rainfall and I and another guy who is unknown for me take a glass of water and make dua in it hat glass and pushed on the house wall there is large smoke awake from the wall side and suddenly my eyes are open I am having fever that time. Please Hadhrat give the actual interpretation of this dream.

    Answer ID: 46200Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1041/1041/M=1434) Whatever you dreamt are misfortunes and troubles. Each one should evaluate his deeds and try to reform himself and follow the Quran and sunnah strictly. It is the remedy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
