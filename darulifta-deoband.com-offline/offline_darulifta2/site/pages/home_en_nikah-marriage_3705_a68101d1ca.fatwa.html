    Answer ID: 3705Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 463/419=D)

     

    (1)     It is not unlawful, but undoubtedly it is improper; one should adopt proper ways for pleasure.

    (2)     It is not good to have a habit of it.

    (3)     It is undesirable due to fear of entering backside. It is better to have proper ways of pleasure and foreplay.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
