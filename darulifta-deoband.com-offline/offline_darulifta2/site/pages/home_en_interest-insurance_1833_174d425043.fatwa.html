    Title:

    Is bank loan permisible in India for business purpose? if yes what are the criteria and if no why?

    Question: Is bank loan permisible in India for business purpose? if yes what are the criteria and if no why?

    Answer ID: 1833Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 683/679=N)

     

    It is not allowed; because this involves giving interest, and both giving and taking interest is haram:

    قال تعالی: و احل اللہ البیع و حرم الربوا (الآیۃ)

    و لعن رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم: اٰکل الربوا و مؤکلہ و کاتبہ و شاھدیہ و قال ھم سواء (مسلم و ترمذی)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
