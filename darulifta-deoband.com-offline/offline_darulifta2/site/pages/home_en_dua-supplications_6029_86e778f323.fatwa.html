    Title: salam , mery wife ko 2 month chal raha hai bo maa banee bale hai uske leya quran pak ki kiss surat ko parne chaye.

    Question: salam , mery wife ko 2 month chal raha hai bo maa banee bale hai uske leya quran pak ki kiss surat ko parne chaye.

    Answer ID: 6029Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 662/626=L -1429)

     

    She should often recite Surah Maryam and both of you should recite the below duas:

    ربنا آتنا في الدنيا حسنة وفي الآخرة حسنة وقنا عذاب النار

    ربنا هب لنا من ازواجنا و ذرياتنا قرة أعين و اجعلنا للمتقين إماما

    In-sha-Allah, Allah Almighty will bless you a pious child.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
