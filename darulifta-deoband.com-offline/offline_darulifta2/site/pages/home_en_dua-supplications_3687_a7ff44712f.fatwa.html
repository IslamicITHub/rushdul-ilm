    Title: Is it Sunnah to read after the Jumuah Salat 7 times Surah al Fatiha and also 7 times Surah Ikhlaas, Falaq and Naas? Is here a hadith about?

    Question: Is it Sunnah to read after the Jumuah Salat 7 times Surah al Fatiha and also 7 times Surah Ikhlaas, Falaq and Naas? Is here a hadith about?

    Answer ID: 3687Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 464/420=D)

     

    There are many traditions of the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) which signify the importance of reciting Surah Fatiha and Muawwazatain (سورۃ الفلق و سورۃ الناس) in different times like morning and evening, but we did not find any hadith which specify the number of recitation in 7 and after Jumuah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
