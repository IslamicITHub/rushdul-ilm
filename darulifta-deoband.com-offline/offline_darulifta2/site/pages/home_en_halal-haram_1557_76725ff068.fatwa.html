    Title: My wife shows desire in sucking parts of my body used for intercourse, is it permissible? Can I also do the same with her, specially in relation to her breasts?

    Question: My wife shows desire in sucking parts of my body used for intercourse, is it permissible? Can I also do the same with her, specially in relation to her breasts?

    Answer ID: 1557Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 521/518=M)

     

    The husband and wife can lick any part of each other?s body except the private parts (vagina, penis and anus). Likewise, one can suck the breast of his wife, but he should avoid it if he fears discharge of milk. In case the milk was discharged while sucking it is compulsory to spit; since it is haram to drink the milk of wife.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
