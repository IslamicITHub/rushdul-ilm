    Title: I am worried about my sister's marriage

    Question: I am worried about my sister's marriage. Please let me know some dua. Also please do dua for me, so that I perform my fardh with the blessing of Almighty Allah.

    Answer ID: 39080Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1092/915/B=1433) Keep on reciting Ya Latif Ya Latif (يا لطيف يا لطيف) extensively and ask your sister to read Ya Jame’ Ya Jame’ (يا جامع يا جامع) extensively. In-shaAllah, soon any good proposal shall come. As for salah, first make firm intention then we shall pray for you. Only by formal dua you shall not turn namazi.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
