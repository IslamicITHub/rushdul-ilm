    Question: I was going to marry my cousin without the consent of her parents after eid ul fitr but her mother did some black magic on her and she changed. me and my family is going through a tough time due to this but my grandmother[who is pious] has seen in her dream two times that we are having a party at home what does this mean?

    Answer ID: 9656Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2549/2085=B/1429)

     

    In-sha-Allah, you shall be married to any good girl and then you shall give party of marriage at your house.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
