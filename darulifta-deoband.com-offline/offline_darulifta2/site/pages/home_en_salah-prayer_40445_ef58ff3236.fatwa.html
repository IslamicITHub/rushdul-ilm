    Title: If a person is musafir at some place and then the time of a salah (say Zuhr for example) starts

    Question: If a person is musafir at some place and then the time of a salah (say Zuhr for example) starts. Now this person will be travelling to his place by such means (flight) that he will reach his place before the time for that salah ends. Then should that person offer qasr salah or should he reach his place and then offer the complete salah?

    Answer ID: 40445Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1157/744/H=1433) If he reached at his native place before the time of salah came to an end or such a place where he intends to stay fifteen days or more then it shall be wajib for him to offer complete salah. He should not do qasr.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
