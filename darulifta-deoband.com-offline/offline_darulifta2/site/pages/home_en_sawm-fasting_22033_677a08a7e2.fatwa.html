    Title: yesterday i kept the kaza roza of ramdhan month.but could not complete at around 9.30 in the morning i breaked it..what should i do for kafaara and what is the punishment for it.?????

    Question: yesterday i kept the kaza roza of ramdhan month.but could not complete at around 9.30 in the morning i breaked it..what should i do for kafaara and what is the punishment for it.?????

    Answer ID: 22033Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 775/533/L=1431)

     

    Breaking missed fast does not make kaffarah (expiation) wajib. It only becomes wajib by breaking the fast in Ramadhan.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
