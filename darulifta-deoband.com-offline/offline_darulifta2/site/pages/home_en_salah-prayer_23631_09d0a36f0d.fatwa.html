    Title: If I miss one rakat with the jamat and by mistake I do one salam with the imam and then relise that I have missed one rakat then what should I do?

    Question: Dear Sir, If I miss one rakat with the jamat and by mistake I do one salam with the imam and then relise that I have missed one rakat then what should I do ?

    Answer ID: 23631Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1072/804/L=1431)

    In this case you will complete your salah by doing sajda sahu. However, if you turned salam with the mim (م) of Imam's السلام or prior to it, in this case sajda sahu is not wajib. Usually muqtadis turn salam after the salam of Imam, hence we have mentioned the ruling of sajda sahu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
