    Title: I am a gril & my uncle had seen a dream of mine in which he sees that I had died and my body is not in kafan for burring but the same cloth which I had wore at death time,

    Question: I am a gril & my uncle had seen a dream of mine in which he sees that I had died and my body is not in kafan for burring but the same cloth which I had wore at death time, & around my janaza there are flowers & chadder on which arabic quotes are written but all can see my body through. they picked my body to go to grave yard but they took my body to a like ground, they kept my body down there and every body left from there telling my uncle you wait here we will back in 5 min. there is no light on the ground, every were their is dark but a light is felling on my janaza. my uncle think in his mind that why her body seems to be so fresh and no bad odors are coming though she died two days earlier. he got afraid and try to run but while he was escaping i catch her hand, any how he succeeded from escaping there. he gone home and told every body that she is alive, they brought my body back my home. and I got up with out bending my body. every body got afraid and began to run here & there.

    Answer ID: 32108Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 838/311/TL=1432) This is nice dream, ma-shaAllah. But, it indicates to laziness in you towards righteousness and good deeds which was pointed out by the darkness. You should try your best to carry out good deeds, ultimately it will bring good results in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
