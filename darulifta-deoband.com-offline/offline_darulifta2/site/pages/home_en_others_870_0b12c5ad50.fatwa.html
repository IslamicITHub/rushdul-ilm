    Question ID: 870Country: India

    Title: I have a doubt am working as a doctor. If i unintentionally prescribe a wrong medicine to one of my patient which might be a life threatening to him and if somthing wrong happens to him will it be accountable with Allah {s.w.a.t}

    Question: assalamualikum i have a doubt am working as a doctor. if i unintentionally prescribe a wrong medicine to one of my patient which might be a life threatening to him and if somthing wrong happens to him will it be accountable with Allah {s.w.a.t} thanking you.

    Answer ID: 870Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 319=319/L)

     

    If you tried to prescribe with complete caution and care and unintentionally committed any mistake and the patient was harmed by it, then you will not be accountable before Allah provided you are a registered physician and have been trained by any medical expert:

    قال علیہ السلام: ان اللہ تجاوز عن امتی الخطاٴ والنسیان وما استکرھوا علیہ (مشکوة: 2/584)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
