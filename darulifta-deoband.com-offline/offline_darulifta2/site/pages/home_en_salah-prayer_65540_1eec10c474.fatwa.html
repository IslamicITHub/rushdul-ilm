    Title: Delaying namaz is right or wrong

    Question: Delaying namaz for collecting fund for masjid is right or wrong?

    Answer ID: 65540Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 862/869/L=08/1437)

    It is not right to delay the salah regularly in the name of collecting fund for the mosque as it causes problem to the namazis. However, if there is delay sometimes which does not trouble the namazis then it is allowable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
