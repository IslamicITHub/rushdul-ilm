    Title: I WANT ASK A QUATION THAT IF A WOMAN WHO IS A MARRIED AND SHE TAKE A NIKAH OTHER MAN WITH OUT TALAK CAN THAT MARRIDGE IS RIGHT

    Question: regard assalamualleykum.I WANT ASK A QUATION THAT IF A WOMAN WHO IS A MARRIED AND SHE TAKE A NIKAH OTHER MAN WITH OUT TALAK CAN THAT MARRIDGE IS RIGHT.

    Answer ID: 25134Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1424/D=299/K=1431)

    It is not lawful for any married woman to marry a second man without taking talaq from her first husband. If she marries, this nikah shall be invalid and will not take place.
    لا يجوز للرجل أن يتوزج زوجة غيره و كذلك المعتدة . (هنديه : 1/280 كتاب النكاح القسم السادس المحرمات اللتى يتعلق بها حق الغير ط:زكريا ديوبند)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
