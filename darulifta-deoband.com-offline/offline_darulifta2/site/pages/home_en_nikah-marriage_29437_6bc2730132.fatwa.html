    Answer ID: 29437Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 234/234/M=1432)

    Qadiyanis are out of fold of Islam. It is unlawful to marry a Qadiyani. In the question mentioned above, if the girl is Qadiyani, your nikah will not be valid with her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
