    Title: I am hotel management graduate and iam working as proffesor in college of hotel managament teaching students subject of tourism managemnt and front office operation . is my earning halal.

    Question: Dear Scholer I am hotel management graduate and iam working as proffesor in college of hotel managament teaching students subject of tourism managemnt and front office operation . is my earning halal.

    Answer ID: 5839Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1040/912=B/1429)

     

    If you do not teach any un-Islamic subject then your work is right and your earnings are halal.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
