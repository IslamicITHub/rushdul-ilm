    Title: Please tell me some wajifa or Dua to cure my urinary problem , my urine come again and again .I am taking medicines also but thay are not so effective.Please tell me so that this problem can be cured by the will of Allah.

    Question: Please tell me some wajifa or Dua to cure my urinary problem , my urine come again and again .I am taking medicines also but thay are not so effective.Please tell me so that this problem can be cured by the will of Allah.

    Answer ID: 28683Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 89/89/M=1432)

    Probably, the problem of excessive urination is due to the weakness of bladder. You should consult any expert hakim or doctor. Recite surah al-Fatiha 7-7 times in the morning and evening, blow it on water and drink it. Also write the following ayah and tie it above novel:
    اللَّهَ يُمْسِكُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ أَن تَزُولَا وَلَئِن زَالَتَا إِنْ أَمْسَكَهُمَا مِنْ أَحَدٍ مِّن بَعْدِهِ إِنَّهُ كَانَ حَلِيماً غَفُوراً [فاطر : 41]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
