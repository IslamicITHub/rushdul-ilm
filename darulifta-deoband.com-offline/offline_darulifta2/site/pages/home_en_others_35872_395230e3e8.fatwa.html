    Title: My father died before 2 months. He was suffering from cancer and he was on death bed for about 3 months. After his death I often dream that he is walking and talking as he was doing before

    Question: My father died before 2 months. He was suffering from cancer and he was on death bed for about 3 months. After his death I often dream that he is walking and talking as he was doing before. Sometime I see he got cured and we become so surprise because we remember that he was dead.

    Answer ID: 35872Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 122/60/D=1433) The dream is nice. It indicates towards the fine condition of the deceased. You should do isal-e-sawab for him either by zikr, tilawat or sadaqah and charity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
