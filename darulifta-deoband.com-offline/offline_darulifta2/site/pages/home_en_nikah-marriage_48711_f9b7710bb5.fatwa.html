    Title: Halalah

    Question: Can divorced (three time divorced & divorced by court also) Muslim women again remarry with her first husband if yes so please explain in detail?

    Answer ID: 48711Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1589/1238/B=11/1434) After three talaqs it is not lawful to do nikah again without a Shariah halalah. The holy Quran states: فَإِن طَلَّقَهَا فَلاَ تَحِلُّ لَهُ مِن بَعْدُ حَتَّىَ تَنكِحَ زَوْجاً غَيْرَهُ [البقرة : 230]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
