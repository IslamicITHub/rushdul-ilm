    Question: Assalamualekum Janab, When Fajjar kaza is been offered, do we have to offer 2 rakaths of Sunat also before offering 2 rakaths of Fajjar kaza? Is dying of hair permissible? after dying of hair is gusal, and wazu completed? Is oral sex permitted in between husband and wife? Please reply in english, and also please give details.

    Answer ID: 9068Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1546/1290=L/1429)

     

    If Fajr qaza is made the same day before zawal then one should make qaza of sunnah with fardh, not later:

    ولا یقضیھا إلا بطریق التبعیۃ لفرضھا قبل الزوال لا بعدہ في الأصح (الدر المختار مع الشامی 2:512)

    (2) It is allowed to dye the hair with colour other than black provided the dye is not a substance preventing water to reach to the hair; otherwise the ghusl and wudhu will not be valid.

    (3) Licking private parts in not allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
