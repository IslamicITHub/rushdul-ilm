    Title: My wife had a ceasearing baby and he is now 6 months old. We're thinking to have the second baby after 2 years so for during this period of 2 years can I use condoms? 

    Question: My wife had a ceasearing baby and he is now 6 months old. We're thinking to have the second baby after 2 years so for during this period of 2 years can I use condoms?

    Answer ID: 24782Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1298/1298/M=1431)

    If the health of wife is weak or the health of the first child may be affected due to immediate pregnancy, then the use of condom is allowed temporarily. It is makrooh to use condom without any valid reason.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
