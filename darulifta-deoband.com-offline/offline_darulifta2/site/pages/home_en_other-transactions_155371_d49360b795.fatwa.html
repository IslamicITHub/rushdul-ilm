    Question ID: 155371Country: India

    Title: Credit received back in account filling petrol using debit card

    Question: When we fill petrol using debit card of rupees 500, 1000 cash back of 2-3 rupees comes in account after some days. Will this money amount received in account (in this form) considered as interest? (I do not have any credit card, I have only debit card).

    Answer ID: 155371Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 126/115/B=2/1439)

     

    This amount shall not be termed as interest rather this shall come under reward or hiba (gift); thus it is allowed to accept it and use it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
