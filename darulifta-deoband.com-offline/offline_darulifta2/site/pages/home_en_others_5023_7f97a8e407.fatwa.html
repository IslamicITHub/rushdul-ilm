    Question: Assalamu alaikum, Dear Muftis of Deoband, I hope you are well and happy. My mother had done istikhara for many days for my sister regarding marriage. 1. These are the dreams my mother had: 1.She is going inside a shop and selecting Comforter(huge thick blanket). She selecting one comforter, she says: ''This comforter is 100% allright but from this I have to select 1,2,3." A little background of the potential husband: He has two brothers, one elder to him and one younger to him. 2.She sees fire(about the height of man) burning. In a corner she sees an unknown girl standing. My mother's second brother that is my Uncle Nazeer puts off the fire! Then she is breastfeeding an infant and the infant refuses to drink. 3.She is breastfeeding an infant and only a drop goes inside the infant's mouth and it spits out and my mother's whole face gets covered with milk. 4. She is standing on some platform little above the ground, a lady brings fresh milk in a bottle. My mother says: "I cant get down, please go and keep it in refrigerator." 5. She saw some crows. She recognises one crow as a male. She then wonders" How was I able to differentiate between a male and femal crow? Before I was not able to recognise a male crow or a female crow". These are the dreams my mother had. Dear Mufti, my mother is confused as to what to do. Please tell me the taabeer of dreams. Jizzakallahu khair! Yahya

    Answer ID: 5023Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 999/192=D/1429)

     

    All the dreams indicate that the bridegroom your mother was doing istikhara about for your sister is suitable for the relation. Give a nod to it; it will be better, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
