    Title: We have a complex, which consists of 13 rooms, My question is 1.can we open a lodge in it,

    Question: We have a complex, which consists of 13 rooms, My question is 1.can we open a lodge in it, 2.Should the requirement like television should be provided to the customer.

    Answer ID: 31295Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 567/567/M=1432) If by lodge you mean only room on rent such as guest house etc, then there is no problem in opening a lodge. But it is not correct to provide TV facility to the customers.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
