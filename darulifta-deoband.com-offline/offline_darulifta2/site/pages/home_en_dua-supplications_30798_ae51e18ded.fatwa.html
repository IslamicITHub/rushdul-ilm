    Question ID: 30798Country: India

    Title: I was trying to cross a railway track with my parents to catch a train my parents crosses it successfully and when i was crossing it another train comming in opposite direction passes very close to me

    Question: I was trying to cross a railway track with my parents to catch a train my parents crosses it successfully and when i was crossing it another train comming in opposite direction passes very close to me infact it almost ran over me. Sir we had given money to some people for business but they are not returning the amount also some people cheated us for a huge amount and dissapeared so now we are facing alot of financial problems also i want to get married so please suggest me some dua and i request you also to make dua for us so that all our problems get sorted easily and peacefully.

    Answer ID: 30798Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 605/368/D=1432) It indicates towards the lack of vigilance and carefulness in religious and worldly affairs. In order to prosperity in livelihood, recite the following dua: اللهم اكفنى بحلالك عن حرامك واغننى بفضلك عمن سواك . For the suitable proposal, recite ya Lateef ya Wadud (يا لطيف يا ودود) 1100 times with durood 11-11 times before and after and then supplicate, in-sha-Allah, good results shall come out.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
