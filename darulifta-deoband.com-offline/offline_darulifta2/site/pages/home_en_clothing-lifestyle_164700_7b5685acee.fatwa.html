    Title: How to maintain the beared?

    Question: I am 19 year guy and alhamdulillah I have beard. And I never cut it. I leave it to grow as it is. But I have curly hairs on my beard. It seems some time awkward and ugly when I am going to some official places. What Shariah says about cutting extra hairs and curly hairs? I don't want to cut my beard even a single time in my life, in-shaAllah. But I think there will be Shariah way to overcome this problem because looks to be good is also the part of the Shariah.

    Answer ID: 164700Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1255/1152/M=11/1439)

     

    If your beard is curly then settle it by combing. And if the hair is more than one fist in length then it is allowable to cut the extra hair and it is wajib to keep beard one fist in length.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
