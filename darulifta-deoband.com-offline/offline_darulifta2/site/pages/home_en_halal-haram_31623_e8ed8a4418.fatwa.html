    Question ID: 31623Country: Pakistan

    Title: working abroad

    Question: My question is that i am study in spain and i work here i have awork permit of 20 hours /week i m work more from this so i ask that it is halal for me or not i will be very thankfull to u for that-

    Answer ID: 31623Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 773/267/TL=1432) Committing an illegal act that endangers one’s life and wealth is unlawful. However, the income earned outside the work permit will not be termed haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
