    Title: Nikah valid? Act of Kufr?

    Question: Someone’s wife told to her husband you missed the Isha prayer (sleeping), than the husband said by mistake “no problem”. It came out of his mouth unwittingly and ill-considered. He did taubah istighfar at the same moment. Was this an act of kufr? Is nikah still valid? Please reply as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 150672Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 683/550/D=8/1438)

    No problem, if it was uttered unintentionally. It is enough to make taubah from it. It is not a sentence of kufr. It also did not have any effect on the nikah, so worry not. It is recorded in one Hadith:

    من نام عن صلوة او نسيها فليصلها اذا ذكرها .

    If anyone of you forgets a prayer or sleeps and misses it, let him pray it when he remembers it.

     

    ليس التفريط فى النوم انما التفريط فى اليقظة .

    There is no sin in sleep, it is only when one is awake.

     

    It means that if a man misses salah when he is awaken intentionally then it is sin. But if by chance he slept and could not wake at the time of salah due to which his salah was missed then it is not sin rather it is forgiven provided he has not developed the habit of sleeping till late and has arranged alarm etc for waking up at the time of salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
