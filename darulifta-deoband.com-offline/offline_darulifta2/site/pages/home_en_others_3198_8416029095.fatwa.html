    Title: I had a dream of me reciting chapters of the quraan to my dad in my house, My dad first asked me whether I memorized surahs from the quraan and I replied 'yes'. Maulana Saab, was is the meaning of this dream.

    Question: Salem Alaikum I had a dream of me reciting chapters of the quraan to my dad in my house, My dad first asked me whether I memorized surahs from the quraan and I replied 'yes'. Maulana Saab, was is the meaning of this dream.

    Answer ID: 3198Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 210/205=B)

     

    It is expected that you shall be granted the honour of memorizing the Holy Quran.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
