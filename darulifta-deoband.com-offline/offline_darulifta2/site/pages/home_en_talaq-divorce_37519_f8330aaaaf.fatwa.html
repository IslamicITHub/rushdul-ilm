    Question ID: 37519Country: Pakistan

    Title: If a married person does not know how the divorce takes place and he used the word divorce for his wife without knowing its impact and wife does not know about these words

    Question: If a married person does not know how the divorce takes place and he used the word divorce for his wife without knowing its impact and wife does not know about these words. If the husband used these words two times, did talaq take place or not? If it took place then what types of talaq is this, clear words are used.

    Answer ID: 37519Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 619/424/D=1433) For talaq to take place, it is not necessary to know the impact of talaq. Talaq takes place if said in clear words. However it is necessary that it is attributed to wife verbally or virtually. What word did you say, quote the complete sentence and submit the question again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
