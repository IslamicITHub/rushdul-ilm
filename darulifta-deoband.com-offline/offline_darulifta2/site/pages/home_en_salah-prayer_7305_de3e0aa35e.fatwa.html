    Title: Assalaamualaykum Q)Because of this hadeeth will it be 1-makrooh e tanzeehi or 2- makrooh e tahreemi to fold trousers clothes etc and could you provide reference of the hadeeth.Jazakallah Rasool Allah Sallallahu Alaihe Wasslam said:" I was ordered that not to fold hair and cloth (In Namaz)" ted in Darr-e-Mukhtar with Shami Part one page 473. art one, Page 137)

    Question: Assalaamualaykum Q)Because of this hadeeth will it be 1-makrooh e tanzeehi or 2- makrooh e tahreemi to fold trousers clothes etc and could you provide reference of the hadeeth.Jazakallah Rasool Allah Sallallahu Alaihe Wasslam said:" I was ordered that not to fold hair and cloth (In Namaz)" ted in Darr-e-Mukhtar with Shami Part one page 473. art one, Page 137)

    Answer ID: 7305Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1240/1146=L/1429)

     

    It is makrooh-e-tahrimi to fold and play with clothes etc during salah:

    وکرہ لفہ ای رفعہ ? و عبثہ بثوبہ، وفی الشامی: وھی کراھۃ تحریم کما فی البحر (در مختار مع شامی 2:406)

    The abovementioned Hadith has been narrated by Bukhari:

    عن ابن عباس عن النبی صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم قال: امرنا ان نسجد علی سبعۃ اعظم و لا نکف شعراً و لا ثوباً (صحیح البخاری 1:112، باب السجود لعی سبعۃ اعظم، رقم الحدیث: 809-811)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
