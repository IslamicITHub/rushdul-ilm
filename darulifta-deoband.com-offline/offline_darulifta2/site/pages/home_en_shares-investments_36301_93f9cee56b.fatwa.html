    Title: Can I give my shop on rent to a person who wants to open a share market office in it? Please answer quickly.

    Question: Can I give my shop on rent to a person who wants to open a share market office in it? Please answer quickly.

    Answer ID: 36301Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 247/247/M=1433) You can give it on rent. Yes, if it is known that that person does the business of shares in unlawful way then it is better not to give him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
