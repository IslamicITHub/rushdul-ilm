    Title: Shariyat

    Question: How much gram silver ring has to be worn? Does Shariah give permission to wear silver ring?

    Answer ID: 63185Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 237/237/B=03/1437) Of course, it is lawful for males to wear silver ring provided it weighs less that 4.5 masha. If it is 4.5 masha or more than it then it is not lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
