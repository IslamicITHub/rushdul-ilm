    Title: I want to borrow money from a person working in my office for a period of six months. I will return the money with increased amount. Is it jayez.

    Question: Assalamu alaikum, I want to borrow money from a person working in my office for a period of six months. I will return the money with increased amount. Is it jayez.

    Answer ID: 29173Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 156/156/M=1432)

    It is lawful to pay more than the borrowed money provided there is no condition of extra amount and the creditor does not harbour a desire it to receive extra amount, rather it is good, and proved by hadith.
    "إن خياركم أحسنكم قضاء" و فى رواية عن جابر قال : كان لى على النبى صلى الله عليه وسلم دين ، فقضالي و زادنى (مشكوة)
    However, at the time of paying the debt one should say like this: this is your capital demand and this extra amount is from my side.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
