    Answer ID: 20115Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 451/L=137/tl=1431)

     

    As a result of fondling and oral sex, if you experienced no seminal discharge, no ghusl would be wajib on you. In this case, you need not to repeat those salah. And if you had seminal discharge and semen used to release from your private part then ghusl was wajib on you, in this condition your salah was not right, and hence now repeating of those salah is obligatory. If you do not remember, it is better to repeat all those salah. And whatever wrong relationship you established with a non-mahram boy, it is necessary for you to make tauba and do istighfar.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
