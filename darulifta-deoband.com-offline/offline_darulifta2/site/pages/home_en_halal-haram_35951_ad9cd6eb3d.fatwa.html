    Question ID: 35951Country: India

    Title: My question is that some hunters in our area bring pigeon and partridges (titar) to sell and they do zibah not in front of us

    Question: My question is that some hunters in our area bring pigeon and partridges (titar) to sell and they do zibah not in front of us, but they say that we have done zibah in halal way, but we do not know that whether they did it in halal way and sometime we think that may be the bird was dead and after they did zibah. Is it halal to eat this kind of meat? The hunters are Muslims.

    Answer ID: 35951Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 61/24/L=1433) It shall be lawful to eat those birds if those hunters are Muslims believing their words. Your doubt ‘that the bird might have been slaughtered after it was dead’ shall not be taken into consideration.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
