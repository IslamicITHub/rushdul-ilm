    Title: dream

    Question: I had a dream on Thursday night. I pray namaz and sleep in the state of wudhu. I am unmarried. I am very disturbed after this dream. I saw that I am doing shopping with my cousin sister and I am giving news to my friends that congratulations, yesterday I have given birth to twin baby son. And I am buying new clothes for this. I am very happy because of all this. Is there any meaning of this dream?

    Answer ID: 40975Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1274/846/H=1433) It means that there is fear of being caught in the long and useless desires of the world. The dream warns that you should decrease your desires and should get yourself busy in the thoughts of hereafter and making it beautiful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
