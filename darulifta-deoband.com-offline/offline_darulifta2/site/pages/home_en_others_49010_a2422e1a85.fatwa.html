    Title: dream of seeing rauza sharif

    Question: I had a dream in which I saw the rauza sharif (grave of our bloved nabi s.a.w) twice. I ask u for this dream interpretation.

    Answer ID: 49010Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1730/1317/H=12/1434) You will be given taufiq by Allah to do good deeds and avoid sins. It is nice dream. You should follow sunnah in each matter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
