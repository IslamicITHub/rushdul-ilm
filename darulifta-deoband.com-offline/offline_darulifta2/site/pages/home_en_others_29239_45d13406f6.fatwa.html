    Answer ID: 29239Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 234/D=11/k=1432)

    Your dream is blessed one. In-sha-Allah, Almighty Allah shall bless you happiness and respectability. You should bring firmness in your Iman and perseverance in good deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
