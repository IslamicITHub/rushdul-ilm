    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1729/269=H/1429)

     

    It is just superstition of magic (Jadu). You seem to suffer from stomachic and liver diseases, this affects the mind and heart and disturbs the Imagination Faculty. You should consult any expert physician and offer two rakah nafl (صلاۃ الحاجۃ) regularly and pray to Allah. You should keep your heart and eyes clean, study the book Bahishti Zewar, keep reciting half or one part of the Holy Quran as per your engagement. Why are Isha and Maghrib salah missed? You should perform Maghrib salah just after the sunset and one or half an hour later perform Isha salah.

     

    We do not have addresses of Ulama or Muslims living in Oslo and Norway. Hadthrat Maulana Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani Sahib, Darul Uloom Krachi Pakistan, has contacts in those countries, he keeps on visiting them, you should contact him and get your problems solved, in-sha-Allah, he will guide you well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
