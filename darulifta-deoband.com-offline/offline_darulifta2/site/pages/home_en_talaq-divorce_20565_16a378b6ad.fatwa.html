    Question ID: 20565Country: Bangladesh

    Title: if someone says his wife talaq 3 times at the same time out of anger and frustaration due to her daliverate mis-behaivior, will talaq be count as 3 or 1 or not at all? Both husband and wife want to continue the relationship and they have 2 children.

    Question: Assalamu Alaikum, if someone says his wife talaq 3 times at the same time out of anger and frustaration due to her daliverate mis-behaivior, will talaq be count as 3 or 1 or not at all? Both husband and wife want to continue the relationship and they have 2 children.

    Answer ID: 20565Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 446/376/B=1431)

     

    When he pronounced three talaqs, the three took place. In the age of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) ,a sahabi named Hadhrat Owaimir Ajlani (رضى الله تعالى عنه) gave his wife three talaqs in front of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم). The holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) implemented all the three ones, the words of hadith are as follows:

    فطلقها ثلاث تطليقات عند رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فأنفذه رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم و كل ما صنع عند رسول الله صلي الله عليه وسلم سنت . (ابو داؤد ج:1/ص:307)

    This hadith is sahih. After giving three talaqs it is not lawful to take wife back without halalah. The holy Quran says it clearly:

    فَإِن طَلَّقَهَا فَلاَ تَحِلُّ لَهُ مِن بَعْدُ حَتَّىَ تَنكِحَ زَوْجاً غَيْرَهُ . [البقرة : 230]

    Thereafter, if he divorces her, she shall no longer remain lawful for him unless she marries a man other than him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
