    Answer ID: 1395Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1274/991=H)

     

    Was this company established by the woman mentioned in the question or it was established by someone else? (b) What activities are carried out by the people of other religions? (c) Her husband works in the same company or somewhere else? (d) What do you mean by relationship? What is the purpose of asking the question? Please, explain the above points.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
