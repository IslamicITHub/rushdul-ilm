    Title:

    "After performing ghusl, is it necessary to change clothes if sperm did not pass through the underwear, or can I pray in the same clothes?"

    Question: At times sperm comes out of my private parts but does not comes out of my underwear so wearing of same clothes except that underwear and after performing gusul and going for namaz is allowed or do i have to change my clothes as well?

    Answer ID: 483Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 512/J)

     

    Yes, in this case you can pray in the clothes after having bath and taking off the underwear; since the clothes are clean.

     

    The above reply is in case of sperm, but if the fluid is other than sperm then the ruling may be different.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
