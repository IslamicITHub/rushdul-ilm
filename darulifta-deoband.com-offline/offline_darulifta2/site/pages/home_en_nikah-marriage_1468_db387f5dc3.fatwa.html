    Title: Nikah with aunti (chachi) is permissible?

    Question: Nikah with aunti (chachi) is permissible?

    Answer ID: 1468Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 848/848=J)

     

    Yes, you can marry your aunt after the death of uncle or after divorce provided she passes Iddah (prescribed period), as the Quran says: وأحل لکم ما وراء ذلکم (سورۃ النساء: 24)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
