    Title: ASAK, I got married on 9th Oct'2003 and the MAHR decided was INR 50000, I paid INR 35000 in Feb 2006. Balance how much i have to pay to my wife. It is INR 15000 or more than that.

    Question: ASAK, I got married on 9th Oct'2003 and the MAHR decided was INR 50000, I paid INR 35000 in Feb 2006. Balance how much i have to pay to my wife. It is INR 15000 or more than that.

    Answer ID: 19224Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 136/127/D=1431)

     

    In the abovementioned case, you will have to pay 15 thousand more as it is obvious from the detail mentioned above. If you have any doubt, submit the question again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
