    Title: Refund of money

    Question: I have asked some money from my friend for some days as I need it. He gave it to me. But now when I am returning total money to him, he do not takes return and says it do not require to return. What should I do?

    Answer ID: 61175Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1331/1430/L=12/1436) You are not asked to return the amount to your friend when he said that you do not need to return the amount to him. It is as if he has remitted the debt. The debt is remitted in case one calls it off. قال فى الهندية: هبة الدين فمن عليه الدين جائزة قياسا و استحسانا ....... هبة الدين فمن عليه الدين وابراؤه يتم من غير قبول من المديون (الهندية: 4/384)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
