    Question: Dear Muftisab, I have huge loans from the bank and have a lot of difficulty of paying the same. I am praying to allah to resolve me of my difficulties of finishing up the loans to the bank. I have a building in which there are 2 floors vacant and i have refused to let out the building to the banks because thier income is from interest.     Answer ID: 24638Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1326/L=397/tl=1431)

    You can rent your building to the bank; but it is not better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
