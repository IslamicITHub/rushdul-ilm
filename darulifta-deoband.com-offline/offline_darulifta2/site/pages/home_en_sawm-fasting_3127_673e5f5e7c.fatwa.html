    Title: If we are fasting and we do wudu, after we do kuli, you say in one of your fataawa to spit once and twice, I spit more than this, but after very little time, sometimes I swallow and I feel as if water went down my throat, yet I am not absolutely sure, since I spit, should I disregard this or should I make qaza for this fast?

    Question: If we are fasting and we do wudu, after we do kuli, you say in one of your fataawa to spit once and twice, I spit more than this, but after very little time, sometimes I swallow and I feel as if water went down my throat, yet I am not absolutely sure, since I spit, should I disregard this or should I make qaza for this fast?

    Answer ID: 3127Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 208/208=L)

     

    In the abovementioned case, you do not have to make up (re-perform) the missed sawm (fast):

    اذا بقی بعد المضمضۃ ماء فابتلعہ بالبزاق لم یفطر لتعذر الاحتراز (الشامی: 3/367، ط زکریا دیوبند)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
