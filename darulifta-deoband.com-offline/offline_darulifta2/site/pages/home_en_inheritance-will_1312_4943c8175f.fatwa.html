    Answer ID: 1312Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 552/542=N)

     

    Is not there any person alive from her family like father, mother, grandfather, grandmother, brother, sister, uncle, sons and daughters of brother and uncle? Provide, the detail of the relatives who are alive, then you will be given a reply, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
