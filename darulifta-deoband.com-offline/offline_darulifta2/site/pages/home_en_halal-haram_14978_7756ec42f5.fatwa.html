    Title: 1.Is blood donation allowed in islam? 2.Is donation of parts of body allowed?

    Question: 1.Is blood donation allowed in islam? 2.Is donation of parts of body allowed?

    Answer ID: 14978Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1166/957=L/1430)

     

    A person is not the owner of his body parts to act freely whatever he likes; therefore one cannot donate his blood and body part, but one can donate blood in order to save the life of his relatives.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
