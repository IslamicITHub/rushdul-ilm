    Answer ID: 16160Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1773/1433/D=1430)

     

    If a woman had spent time with her husband in privacy and then she was divorced, so she will have to pass iddah of three menses and if she is pregnant her iddah will come to an end only after delivery.

    (2) She is not allowed to talk to ghair mahram on phone unnecessarily, and in iddah she must be more careful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
