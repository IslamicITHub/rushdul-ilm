    Question: Aslwakm bohat din sey yeh bath samaj mey nahi arahi hai, kya ghar bikana, logo ko makanath dikhana,aur agar wo tayar hogaye lene ke liye. tho dono taraf sey paisey lena. bejney waley sey,kharedh ney waley sey.2% ya 3% key hisab sey. ex- aghar ghar 10lakh mey bika to us pey 2% lena. kya ye jayez hai.

    Answer ID: 7259Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1414/1330=d/1429)

     

    The wage should already be fixed, so if the man works for both parties then it is permissible for him to take wage from both the sides. The wage can also be fixed by percentage.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
