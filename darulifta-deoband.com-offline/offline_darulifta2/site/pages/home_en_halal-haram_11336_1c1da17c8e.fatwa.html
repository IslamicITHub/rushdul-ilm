    Question: Asalam u Alaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatahoo We wanted to know about whether a "jin" can be burnt or not? If yes then please tell us

    Answer ID: 11336Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 443/307=L/1430)

     

    If they are not getting away by other means then it is lawful to burn them down. It is better to write in the ta?weez that if you will not go, you will be burnt i.e. they are alerted before being burnt that if they do not go away they will be burnt. Then also, if it is not obeying, one is allowed to burn then down.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
