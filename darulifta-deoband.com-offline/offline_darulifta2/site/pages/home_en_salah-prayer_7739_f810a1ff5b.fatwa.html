    Question: I just want to ask to you about kaza namaza. If i could't perform fazr at right time, i go to masjid on johar time, when imaam sahab is about to perform johar farz namaz, should i perform fazr kaza namaz or join to imaam sahab to perform johar, i got confused about kaza namaz some person says that you should perform namaz with tarteeb, if you have completed johar farz namaz with imaam without perform fazr kaza namaz, then after performing johar namaz you should perform first fazr kaza namaz after fazr kaza you should perform johar farz again, because your johar is not complete without first fazr kaza namaz, you will get another reward which namaz you have performed with imaam, but you have not complete your johar. If i did a mistake please forgive me. But i just want to be clarified, i do't want to always get confused.

    Answer ID: 7739Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1225/1142=L/1429)

     

    One who does not have missed salah of six times from the time he became mature is called sahib-e-tartib. If he misses a salah, he should offer first the missed salah and then perform salah of the present time, it is not right for him to perform qaza salah after having offered the salah of the time. If one is not sahib-e-tartib then it is not compulsory for him to perform salah in order, he can perform whatever salah (missed or salah of time) whichever he wants. You can derive ruling for yourself according to this rule.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
