    Answer ID: 4212Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1365/1180=B/1429)

     

    It is very blessed for one to see the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) in dream, but his saying in dream will be considered neither hadith nor necessarily applicable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
