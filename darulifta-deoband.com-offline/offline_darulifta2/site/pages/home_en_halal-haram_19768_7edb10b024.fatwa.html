Our family is eagerly waiting for your response. Allah Kafiz.

    Answer ID: 19768Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 237/L-62/Tl=1431)

     

    Adopting the method of test tube baby is unnatural and unlawful.

    (2 & 3) This act is based on immodesty and shamelessness and should be stopped.

    (4) It is not allowed to help people in wrongdoings. So if you?re the husband of your sister forces her for this act, she is not asked to obey her husband.

    (5) The method of test tube baby is unnatural, also it involves many other un-Islamic acts e.g. masturbating for getting semen, getting the semen of woman, and opening her satar (private parts) before a person if one who injects it in the vagina is other than her husband. This (uncovering satr) before strange person cannot be allowed for getting benefit. And due to injecting the semen of husband into the uterus of non-mahram woman, increases its heinousness. Hence Muslims should avoid getting baby through this method. And one should be content with the decision of Almighty Allah. Islam does not absolutely oppose science and technology; it only opposes the systems that contradict Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
