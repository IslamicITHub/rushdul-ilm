When myself and Wife were on the verge of getting separated, I told my wife about my intentions of getting married to her sister.. Please suggest in this situation can I marry both the sisters? or Otherwise what to do?

    Answer ID: 19331Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 342/46/TH=1431)

     

    You can never marry your sister in law keeping your wife in nikah, it is invalid and haram. Also, it is major and haram to have intimate relations, openness and seclusion with her. You should immediately severe your relations with her and repent to Allah sincerely. Stay careful in future and keep on fulfilling the right of wife fully. You were deceived by the nafs and Satan that severance of relation with her and her valid Nikah will create troubles for her. The only way to protect for both of you is to pay attention to rectify yourselves.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
