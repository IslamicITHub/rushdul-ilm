    Title: I want to know that The service in bank is islamic or not?i have a call letter from state bank of india for the post of Assistant Manager (Electrical Engineering). In this post my job profile is totally related to electrical such as erection & commissioing of electrical items, building construcion of SBI, etc. Can i join this Company?

    Question: Dear Sir, I want to know that The service in bank is islamic or not?i have a call letter from state bank of india for the post of Assistant Manager (Electrical Engineering). In this post my job profile is totally related to electrical such as erection & commissioing of electrical items, building construcion of SBI, etc. Can i join this Company? Please reply soon Thanking You

    Answer ID: 24651Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1556/1225/B=1431)

    If your job is related to only electrical equipments and the building of bank and you are not involved in writing or checking the interest based calculations, then you are allowed to work in the bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
