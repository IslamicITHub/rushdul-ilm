    Title: Health problems

    Question: I have this problem since long whenever I get cured out of a sickness I get into other sickness it's been years now and this has led me into depression right now I've got gum disease and losing my hair also though I'm just 21 send me any wazifah which would help me out.

    Answer ID: 151311Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 918/838/B=8/1438)

     

    You should read بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم after each fardh salah 100 times. Read it for forty days, in-shaAllah, you won’t feel any depression.

     

    Note: For the problem of falling hair and gum you may contact any good and expert doctor or hakim.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
