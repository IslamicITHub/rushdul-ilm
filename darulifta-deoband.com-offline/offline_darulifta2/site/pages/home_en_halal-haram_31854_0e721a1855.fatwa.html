    Title: womens job as physician of male patients

    Question: Is it alright for a female muslim dentist lady to treat either muslims or non muslim male patients.Since a doctors role is to treat his patients.Kindly mention is it permissible in islam or not.

    Answer ID: 31854Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 939/939/M=1432) If there is compulsion as there are no men physicians available to treat the men patients or she only can treat such patients and no male physicians can do it, then she can treat men observing full purdah. It will not remain alright if she will violate the Shariah limits.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
