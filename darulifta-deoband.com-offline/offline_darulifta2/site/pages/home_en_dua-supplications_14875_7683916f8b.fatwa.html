    Question: as salaam muftisaheb my wife has deliverd malechild on 7 july 2009 so please give me dua to make him heathy & strongh & make dua to me &my family & my child also allah hafeej.

    Answer ID: 14875Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1180/977=L/1430)

     

    Contact any expert hakim or doctor.

    Write down the below dua and get it hanged around his neck, he shall be protected against most of the troubles and evils, in-shaAllah:

    بسم اللہ الذي لا یضر مع اسمہ شئ في الأرض ولا في السماء وھو السمیع العلیم

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
