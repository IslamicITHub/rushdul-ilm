    Title: I am from Rawalpindi, i need confirmation about Prize Bond ( In Pakistan ), Is Prize Bond halal or not, Please guide me about this (with explanation) Thanks!

    Question: I am from Rawalpindi, i need confirmation about Prize Bond ( In Pakistan ), Is Prize Bond halal or not, Please guide me about this (with explanation) Thanks!

    Answer ID: 12949Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 907/847=B/1430)

     

    Prize bond is a kind of gambling (qimar); therefore it is unlawful and haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
