    Question ID: 37517Country: Pakistan

    Title: By profession I deal with many laws, there is a principal of law that "whatever is not prohibited is permitted".

    Question: By profession I deal with many laws, there is a principal of law that "whatever is not prohibited is permitted". Is this principal applicable in Shariah laws as well?

    Answer ID: 37517Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 412/343/B=1433) In Shariah, a thing is permissible which is neither prohibited nor is a source of prohibition.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
