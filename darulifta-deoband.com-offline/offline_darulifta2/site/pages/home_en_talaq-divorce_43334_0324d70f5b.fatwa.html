    Title: Divorce Over Phone

    Question: I live in Delhi and got married with a girl from Bareilly under Muslim law. My wife was living in Bareilly with their parents since 14-November-2009 and in my 3.5 years of married life, she hardly spent 1.5 years with me. Even she did not inform me about my child birth. I wrote her letter to call her back but she did not return. As I was suffering a lot, and after waiting her for long 9 months, I had no other choice except divorce, so I divorced her over phone on 26-Augist-2010. Is my divorce valid? Can I go for marriage again?

    Answer ID: 43334Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 334/257/H=1434) As you admit to give talaq on phone, the talaq took place. But, what were the exact words of talaq you spoke, please write them to us.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
