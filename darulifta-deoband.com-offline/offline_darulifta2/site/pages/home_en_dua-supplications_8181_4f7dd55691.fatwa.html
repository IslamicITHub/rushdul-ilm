    Answer ID: 8181Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2001/1577=B/1429)

     

    We pray for you earnestly, may you get a proper match and the desire of you and your parents is fulfilled. Amen! Recite the below three duas hundred times each:

    یا جامع، یا لطیف، یا ودود

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
