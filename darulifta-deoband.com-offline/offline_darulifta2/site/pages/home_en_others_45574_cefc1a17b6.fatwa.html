    Title: Dream about Khatm-e-nabuwat

    Question: I had seen a dream long years back when I was not a practicing Muslim. Someone was saying Khatme-E-Nabuwat, Khatme-E-Nabuwat. After some days Alhamdulillah I changed to a practicing Muslim. Please explain the dream.

    Answer ID: 45574Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 787/620/H=1434) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. It means that Almighty Allah shall give you complete hidayah. Study the following books extensively: (1) Hayatul Muslimin. (2) Fazail Amal.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
