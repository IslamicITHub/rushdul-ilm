    Answer ID: 2160Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1470/1300=B)

     

    Until your father is alive you have no right in his property and possessions, he is the owner of all his belongings. The one third which he is giving to you is a gift from him; you have no right to complain against him. If he is giving the land with a condition not to give it on rent it is for your betterment, lest you should not loose it as the renters are mostly overridden by bad intentions.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
