    Title: I believe in Allah.He holds super power.sometimes bad things(i.e Allah cannot do it,shirok.... ),which I never believe, comes in mind unconsciously. it hurts me much mentally.now i feel anguished.what should I do to avoid such thinking? Please help me.

    Question: I believe in Allah.He holds super power.sometimes bad things(i.e Allah cannot do it,shirok.... ),which I never believe, comes in mind unconsciously. it hurts me much mentally.now i feel anguished.what should I do to avoid such thinking? Please help me.

    Answer ID: 20354Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 284/B=47/tb=1431)

     

    If some time unconsciously bad things come into your mind, recite istighfar immediately, and recite اعوذ بالله من الشيطن الرجيم  thrice and spit lightly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
