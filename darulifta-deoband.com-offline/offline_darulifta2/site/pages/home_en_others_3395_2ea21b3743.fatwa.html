    Question: Assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuh maulana, mai 11 saal ka tha jab may ne kwaab dekha ke mei apne class room mei khel raha hun jo 3rd manzil pe thi.mei upar se gir gaya jab ek baloon aya aur mujhe uda le gaya.mei ne dekha ke mai makkah(masjid-e-haraam)pahunch gaya aur udhar hi mai badaa bhi ho gaya aur mai masjid-e-haraam ka imam ban gaya.is ka matlab kya hai.ab mai 20saal ka hun.

    Answer ID: 3395Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 335/298=B)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah, the dream is blessed one. Keep hold of truth and honesty even if you face adverse situations, in-sha-Allah you shall gain tremendous success.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
