    Title: I have been offered a job in telemarketing where i have to deal with customers in reletion to open their account in ICICI PRUDENTIAL MUTUAL FUND. Plese guide me about this job.

    Question: I have been offered a job in telemarketing where i have to deal with customers in reletion to open their account in ICICI PRUDENTIAL MUTUAL FUND. Plese guide me about this job.

    Answer ID: 2300Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1011D/50K)

     

    If ICICI invests the funds of its customers in lawful businesses on the basis of loss and profit, you will be allowed to work for ICICI, but if it invests the funds in unlawful businesses like insurance companies, wine companies etc then it will be unlawful to work for them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
