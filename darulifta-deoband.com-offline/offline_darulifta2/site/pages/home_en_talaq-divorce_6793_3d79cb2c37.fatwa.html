    Answer ID: 6793Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 879/817=L/1429)

     

    In response to your wife?s saying: ?could you divorce me or are you going to divorce me", If you said ?yes? in your mind and did not pronounce it, you mentioned it at the time of asking a question, then there would be no Talaq on your wife.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
