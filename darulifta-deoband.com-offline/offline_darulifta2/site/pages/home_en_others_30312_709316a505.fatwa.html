    Title: Dream

    Question: Please could you tell me the meaning of this dream... In my dream i am breastfeeding a baby girl, the baby only drinks a little and is already full. can you tell me the meaning of this dream? as i sleep in wudu and i am unmarried

    Answer ID: 30312Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 660/396/D=1432) It indicates to divert especial attention towards the training and nourishment of children. It is mentioned in a hadith that a person who undertakes upbringing and nourishment of two daughters he shall be closer to me (the holy Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم) in the paradise.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
