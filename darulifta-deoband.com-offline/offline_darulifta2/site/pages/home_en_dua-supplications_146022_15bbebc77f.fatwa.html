    Question: My father is unable to speak, move due to brain damage caused by low sodium in blood. Kindly suggest duas and wazifahs to bring him back to normal health. Expecting our quick reply.

    Answer ID: 146022Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 145/120/B=2/1438)

    Your family members should read ‘Ya Salam, Ya Salam’ (يا سلامُ يا سلامُ) extensively. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
