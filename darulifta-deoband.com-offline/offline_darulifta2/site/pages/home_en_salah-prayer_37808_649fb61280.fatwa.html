    Title: When does qasar salah becomes wajib whether it is 38 miles or 40 miles or 48 miles there are difference of opinion

    Question: When does qasar salah becomes wajib whether it is 38 miles or 40 miles or 48 miles there are difference of opinion?

    Answer ID: 37808Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 638/638/M=1433) There is no opinion favouring 38 or 40 miles. Regarding the journey distance, there are four opinions in Shami etc: 63, miles, 54 miles, 48 miles, and 45 miles. The majority of the Fiqh scholars preferred 48 miles which at present is 77.25 kilometres. Hence if someone makes intention to set out on a journey of 77.25 km or more from his native place, then qasr is wajib on him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
