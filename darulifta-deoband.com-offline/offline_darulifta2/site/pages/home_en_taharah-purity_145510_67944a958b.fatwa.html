    Title: Urinary tract problem

    Question: A person has a condition in which a drop or few drops of urine keep coming out or moving forward in urethra after every few minutes. Does this problem break his wudhu? How should the person offer salah if this occurs again and again after making wudhu repeatedly? Does the person need to break his salah and perform fresh wudhu every time the problem occur?

    Answer ID: 145510Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 80/62/SD=2/1438)

    If the drops continue to such a great extent that the said person does not get a time that he could offer fardh salah after making wudhu and this situation remains during the whole time of salah. Then in such a case the said person shall be deemed excused as per the Shariah. So, for him, the ruling is that he will make wudhu for each salah and will offer as much fardh and nafl salah as he wishes with that wudhu, but as soon as the time of salah passes his wudhu shall break and new wudhu should be made to offer the next salah. There are some details in the rulings of excused person, hence it is better that the said person narrates his whole situation to some expert Alim or Mufti and follows the instructions given by him.

    وصاحب عذر من به سلسل بول ، إن استوعب عذره تمام وقت صلاة مفروضة بأن لا يجد في جميع وقتها زمنا يتوضأ ويصلي خاليا عن الحدث . (شامي 1/504 زكريا) (مستفاد:فتاوى محموديه ، جديد ڈابھیل 5/217، 216، 220، 221، 6/ 285، بهشتي زيور 1/54، احسن الفتاوى زكريا 2/74، 75).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
