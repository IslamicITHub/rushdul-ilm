    Answer ID: 19060Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 282/282/M=1431)

     

    In the case mentioned in the question, the best way for you is to abandon the intention of marrying the Shia girl. Going against one?s promise is permissible in this case; since there is a legal excuse from the Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
