    Title: I am facing fear while driving. Also lack of confidence in some matters related to job. Please suggest necessary dua

    Question: I am facing fear while driving. Also lack of confidence in some matters related to job. Please suggest necessary dua? I am also facing disorder in relations with my wife. My son is very lazy in studies. Kindly suggest necessary dua or wazifah.

    Answer ID: 35316Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2342/1328/H=1432) (1) Read durood extensively. (2) You and your wife should recite the following verse with the intention of dua after each salah 7 times with durood 7-7 times before and after. Also repeat the same act in the night before going to sleep: رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
