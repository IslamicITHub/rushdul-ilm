JazakAllahuKhir, My question is that I am living away form my home and for this I didn?t usually get a nutritious and balanced food and for the same I have developed certain deficiencies and My doctor has advised me to take Iron and Vitamin Supplements. These Capsules are however packaged in Gelatin Shells, which have animal origin. Please tell are these capsule hallal or haram. Can we take any medicines having gelatin shells ?

    Answer ID: 888Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 484=472/N)

     

    How the gelatine shell is made? And from which parts of which animal is it taken? Is it from Halal animals which are Islamically slaughtered or is it from Halal animals which are slaughtered un-Islamically? Or is it from Haram animals? Please, furnish the information with complete research, then only we can have a say.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
