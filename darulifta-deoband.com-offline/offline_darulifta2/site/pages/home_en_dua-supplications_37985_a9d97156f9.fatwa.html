    Question ID: 37985Country: India

    Title: Duwa for getting things easy and good health of mother and child during and after ceserian delivery

    Question: My wife is pregnant for third child. There were complications in 1st and 2nd pregnancy so I need some dua for getting things easy and good health of mother and child during and after delivery.

    Answer ID: 37985Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 663/567/B=1433) You and your wife should keep on reciting the below dua: اللھم لا سھل الا ما جعلتہ سھلا وانت تجعل الحزن سھلا اذا شئت All the troubles will go away inshaAllah!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
