    Title: i am clerk in government service if i put my savings as fixed deposit in bank, the intrest on fixed deposit is lawful, inshaallah give me reply.

    Question: i am clerk in government service if i put my savings as fixed deposit in bank, the intrest on fixed deposit is lawful, inshaallah give me reply.

    Answer ID: 11344Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 451/321=L/1430)

     

    The interest gained out of fixed deposit is also haram; if you invest your saving in any business the income will be lawful. Therefore, it is better to invest the amount in any fair business, instead of fixed deposit.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
