    Question: Assalam o Alaikum! Sir, I am a working lady.I am entering into 30s.I am still unmarried and waiting for a reasonable proposal.My parents particularly my father doesn?t take interest in arranging my marriage.They are not fear of my single marital status and increasing age.But I am much worried as in this society it is very difficult for a woman to live without a shelter and HUSBAND is a real shelter for a woman but my parents think as my job serves as a shelter and future protection for me.     Answer ID: 20839Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 531/L=176/tl=1431)

     

    Be regular in salah and supplicate Almighty Allah for better proposal. After Isha salah recite ya Lateef (يا لطيف) 1100 times with durood 11-11 times before and after.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
