    Question ID: 24955Country: FJ

    Title: What do the islamic scholars say about the following masala: is there any virtures of fasting on the day of 15th shabaan? is it based on any hadith? i wanted to know if there is any hadith separately regarding the fast on the day 15 shabaan, please with quotations?

    Question: assalamualaikum, what do the islamic scholars say about the following masala: is there any virtures of fasting on the day of 15th shabaan? is it based on any hadith? i wanted to know if there is any hadith separately regarding the fast on the day 15 shabaan, please with quotations?

    Answer ID: 24955Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1310/1033/D=1431)

    عن على رضى الله عنه عن النبى صلى الله عليه وسلم إذا كان ليلة النصف من شعبان فقوموا ليلها و صوموا نهارها فإن الله تعلى ينزل فيها لغروب الشمس إلى السماء الدنيا فيقول الحديث . رواه ابن ماجه (مشكوة 115)
    There are some more hadith on the same topic in which keeping fast on fifteenth of Shaban was emphasized; hence fasting on the day is mustahab.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
