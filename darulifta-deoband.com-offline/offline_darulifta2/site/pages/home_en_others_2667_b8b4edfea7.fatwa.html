    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 108/103=B)

     

    It is blessed dream. Allah willing, you will perform hajj. As far as missing salah is concerned it is an indication that you will be enabled to discharge the rights of people properly.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
