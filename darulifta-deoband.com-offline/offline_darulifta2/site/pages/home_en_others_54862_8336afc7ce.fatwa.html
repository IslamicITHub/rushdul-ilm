    Title: Dreams

    Question: In Ramadan I saw a woman in my dream. We were face to face. She did not speak to me. She was short and had beautiful eyes. People passing by were saying she is Hadhrat Maryam AS. What is the meaning? Also I saw Anwar Shah Kashmiri and my ustad in my dream. What does that mean? Please reply.

    Answer ID: 54862Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1424/1223/H=10/1435) (1) MashaAllah, both the dreams are nice. In-shaAllah you shall be honoured with piousness and godliness and your knowledge and deed shall increase. It is the interpretation of the dreams. You should follow sunnah in each matter. There is a heading in Bahishti Zewar: “Har Murid Balki Har Musalman Ko Rat Din Is Tarah Rahna Chahiye”, there are guidelines under this heading. Study all these instructions deeply for forty days without a gap.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
