    Answer ID: 1333Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1187/915=H)

     

    The consent of husband is compulsory for khula. It is suggested to reconcile and settle the matter with the mediation of some eminent persons. Madrasa Aminia Delhi and Jamia Khadimul Islam Bulandh Shahar Road, Hapur have Sharai Panchayat system. But, how much time it will take, it is difficult to predict. We can not decide any thing beforehand for the husband if he fails to abide by the order of the Sharai Panchayat. Please, go there yourself or write a letter to them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
