    Answer ID: 16080Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1488/1490/M=1430)

     

    One can touch breast of wife during fast and they can kiss each other at cheek as well, but if he fears ejaculation or ultimately intercourse, then it is makrooh, it should be avoided. Husband and wife can change dress before one another.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
