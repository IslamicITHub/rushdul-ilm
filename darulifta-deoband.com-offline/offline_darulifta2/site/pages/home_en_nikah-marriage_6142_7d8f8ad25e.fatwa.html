    Question ID: 6142Country: Pakistan

    Title: I am a convert sunni from dawoodi bohra (I have kept this as secret) but my wife is dawoodi bohri. Please tell me whether my marriage is ok or not! and what shall I do.

    Question: Dear Brother salam-u-alaykoom I am a convert sunni from dawoodi bohra (I have kept this as secret) but my wife is dawoodi bohri. Please tell me whether my marriage is ok or not! and what shall I do.

    Answer ID: 6142Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1256/1061=B-1429)

     

    The marriage to remain intact or to end depends on your wife whether she embraces Islam or not. You should contact reliable Ulama of Pakistan like Hadhrat Maulana Muhammad Taqi Usmani sahib; they will tell you the right thing along with the procedure.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
