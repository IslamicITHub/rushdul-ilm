    Title: Ayat Al Kursi after fardh salah

    Question: Is it OK to put right hand on head and recite: (1) Ayatal Kursi and also (2) YA QAYYOOM (eleven times) immediately after every fardh salah?

    Answer ID: 49555Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 53/46/H=01/1435) It is proved to recite some duas passing hands over head thus it is also allowable to recite Ayatal Kursi and Ya Qayyum. But we could not find the proof of reading Ayatal Kursi and Ya Qayyum 11 times by putting right hand over head.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
