    Question ID: 2695Country: India

    Title: I have a great respect towards them of doing such type of great effort to have a priceful path provided by allahsubhanahutalah so let it be my question is 1.is it compulsary to follow hanafi masalak im hanafi can,t i follow shafai rule. 2.i would like to know the meaning of my name "sarfaraz". 3.my friend is telling me that no need to follow any imam what should i say to him he is telling me that we are provided with quran and hadith give me the right suggestion. last but not the least ""may allah take your mission in very proudly manner and give you place in jannatul firdous and keep me in your dua's?

    Question: assalamualekum to all the ulamas of deoband have a great respect towards them of doing such type of great effort to have a priceful path provided by allahsubhanahutalah so let it be my question is 1.is it compulsary to follow hanafi masalak im hanafi can,t i follow shafai rule. 2.i would like to know the meaning of my name "sarfaraz". 3.my friend is telling me that no need to follow any imam what should i say to him he is telling me that we are provided with quran and hadith give me the right suggestion. last but not the least ""may allah take your mission in very proudly manner and give you place in jannatul firdous and keep me in your dua's"" jazakallah 

    Answer ID: 2695Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 98/95=B)

    For a Hanafi it is compulsory to follow Hanafi Maslak and for a Shafei it is compulsory to follow Shafei Maslak, it is not allowed for one to switch to follow the other maslak.

    (2) Sarfaraz means: person with high head

    (3) Nowadays, when irreligiousness, lustfulness and self-willing prevails, if people are let free to choose any maslak they will follow their own desires, therefore it is necessary to follow one maslak only. Yes, in view of any difficult situation the scholars of Islam can unanimously decide to follow any particular masla of other maslak.

    Taqleed is not done in the matters mentioned explicitly in the Quran or Hadith, but it is done only in issues that are derived from Quran and Hadith and about those which have different opinions of imams. In this case, we follow one opinion of an imam since we are not able to prefer one opinion on another due to not being mujtahid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
