    Question: Is it allowed for men to wear a silver bracelet? Kindly reply in detail.

    Answer ID: 3624Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 525/414=B)

     

    No, men can wear only silver rings weighing less than 1 misqal (=4.5 masha).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
