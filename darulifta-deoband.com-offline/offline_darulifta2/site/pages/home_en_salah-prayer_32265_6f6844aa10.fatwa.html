    Question ID: 32265Country: Pakistan

    Title: I would like to know that if some people are going to a wedding from karachi to haydrabad, it is a 2 hours drive, by car/bus, and maghrib salah time comes in during the journy.

    Question: I would like to know that if some people are going to a wedding from karachi to haydrabad, it is a 2 hours drive, by car/bus, and maghrib salah time comes in during the journy, so is it alright to read namaz in the car while the car is moving/being driven or is it farz on the car people to stop the car/bus to perform salah on the ground?

    Answer ID: 32265Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 796/666/B=1432) Usually offering salah in car or bus facing the qiblah is difficult; hence on should alight and offer salah on ground.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
