    Question ID: 31799Country: Pakistan

    Title: My husband drink almost after a day otherwise he is good with me .

    Question: My husband drink almost after a day otherwise he is good with me .he perform 5 hajj and lots of ummras.but now he is still in saudia with me but he drink he even did,nt pray.kindly give me (wazefa)that I read.(ta ky wo ya buri aadat choar dain aur Allha ki abadat main dil lagain)other way he help poors and lot of good things.plz plz tell me what I can do/

    Answer ID: 31799Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 694/582/B=1432) Recite ووَجَدَكَ ضَالّاً فَهَدَى forty times with full sincerity and blow it on water and give one mouthful of the water to your husband to drink daily in the morning and evening. It is hoped that this act would turn him towards righteousness and he shall do taubah from drinking.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
