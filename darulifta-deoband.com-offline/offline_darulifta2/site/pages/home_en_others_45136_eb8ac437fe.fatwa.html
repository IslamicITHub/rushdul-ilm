    Title: Dream

    Question: I had asked Allah for a thing in the salatul hajah and the same day I got this dream. I saw my nephew and niece running for chocolates and then my niece, she is around 5 years old got and gave me chocolates and I ate it. Then my nephew came and snatched away the chocolates from her and she started crying. I was trying to convince her and then I gave her my chocolate and then I woke up. I do not know whether she was convinced or not in the dream. Please let me know what this dream means.

    Answer ID: 45136Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 661/517/H=1434) It is nice dream. In-shaAllah, you shall attain in some of your objectives.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
