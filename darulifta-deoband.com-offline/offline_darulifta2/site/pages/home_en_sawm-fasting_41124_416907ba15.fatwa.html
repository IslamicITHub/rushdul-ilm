    Title: Can one apply balm on his forehead and oil on hair in the state of sawm

    Question: Can one apply balm on his forehead and oil on hair in the state of sawm?

    Answer ID: 41124Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1274/843/H=1433) It is alright. It neither breaks the fast nor make it makrooh.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
