    Title: As salam Walekum, i always see snakes in my dreams. Many times snakes follow me or bite or attack on me. what is meaning of this dream? Also 2 - 3 years ago, when i was sleeping (it was time between Asar & Magarib), & when i woke up, i could smell some perfumes smell(fragance), but when i turned to other side, smell was not there, again on changing side, i could smell fragance (on same place). Why this happened? please help me for the matter as soon as possible. Hoping for your help. Allah hafiz -asma

    Question: As salam Walekum, i always see snakes in my dreams. Many times snakes follow me or bite or attack on me. what is meaning of this dream? Also 2 - 3 years ago, when i was sleeping (it was time between Asar & Magarib), & when i woke up, i could smell some perfumes smell(fragance), but when i turned to other side, smell was not there, again on changing side, i could smell fragance (on same place). Why this happened? please help me for the matter as soon as possible. Hoping for your help. Allah hafiz

    Answer ID: 9517Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2504/207=H/1429)

     

    Perhaps you follow the wishes desires inspired by the Satan and nafs. These desires often come in your dream in the form of snakes biting and attacking you. In order to finish them, you should study Behishti Zewar and Fazail-e-Amal and read out them to others. You should also keep acting upon whatever you study in these books. In-sha-Allah, you will observe soon the profits gained.

     

    It is a common that sometimes one finds smell in one side and not in the other side. You should not fall in doubt by such kind of smell which you felt in one side. If you feel such a doubt, you should recite لاحول ولاقوة الا بالله العلى العظيم   and divert your attention from it. You should immediately engage yourself in other works such as to start studying Fazail-e-Amal, Behishti Zewar or doing the household chores.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
