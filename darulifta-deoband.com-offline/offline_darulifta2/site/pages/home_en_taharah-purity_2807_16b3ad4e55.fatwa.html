    Question ID: 2807Country: India

    Title: I woke up in the morning and offered fajar prayer in the mosque after doing ablution.Later I came to know that I had a nightfall.I want to know whether I have to repeat the prayer and is any sin in offering prayer witout gusul while not being aware of it.

    Question: I woke up in the morning and offered fajar prayer in the mosque after doing ablution.Later I came to know that I had a nightfall.I want to know whether I have to repeat the prayer and is any sin in offering prayer witout gusul while not being aware of it.

    Answer ID: 2807Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 87/87=L)

     

    Yes, you have to remake your Fajr salah; وجد فی ثوبہ منیاً او بولاً اعاد من آخر احتلام و بول و رعاف (الدر المختار مع الشامی 1/378), but you will not be sinful for praying the salah in ignorance with impurity since the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) said: 'My Ummah will not be answerable for the actions they commit in ignorance'.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
