    Question: What is the ruling about breastfeeding while pregnant? Please reply all aspects in detail?

    Answer ID: 9743Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2600/2081=H/1429)

     

    (1) It is lawful in itself. If it is harmful on medical grounds, then it is alright to wean as well.

    (2) You should specify the points in detail clearly which you want to enquire.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
