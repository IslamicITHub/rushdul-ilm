    Title: Plz tell me that Can a muslim works in a bank as an employee?

    Question: Assalam Alaikum, Plz tell me that Can a muslim works in a bank as an employee?

    Answer ID: 21425Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 544/544/M=1431)

     

    It is unlawful for a Muslim to do job in a bank where he has to record interest, calculate it and bear witness to it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
