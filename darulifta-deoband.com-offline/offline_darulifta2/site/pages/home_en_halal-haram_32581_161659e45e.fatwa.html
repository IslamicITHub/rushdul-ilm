    Title: Commission on Fruit Business

    Question: I am a fruit trader ,we get some mony as commission from buyer,beside this we also charg some mony call"JHOLI" Plese tell me is it 'Halal' to us.

    Answer ID: 32581Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 950/607/L=1432) The commission amount is halal for you. Why do you take money other than commission in the name of Jholi? Did you have any agreement earlier for the same or not? Make it clear and submit the question again, then in-sha-Allah, you shall be replied.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
