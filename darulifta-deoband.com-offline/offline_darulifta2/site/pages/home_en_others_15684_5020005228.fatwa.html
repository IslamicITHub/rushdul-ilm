    Answer ID: 15684Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1454/1381=B=1430)

     

    As per the Shariah, your husband is wrong to say so. Spilled milk can be washed without mixing water too.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
