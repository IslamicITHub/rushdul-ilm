    Question: I m a 28 yrs marid man acording to imam shafi (rah) when a husband having sex with his wifehe can sucks her milk?

    Answer ID: 11665Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 508/508=M/1430)

     

    It is haram and unlawful for husband to drink the milk of wife, but there is no wrong to take the breast in mouth and lick it, but if it is feared that milk will be discharged then it should be avoided.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
