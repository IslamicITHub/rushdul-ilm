    Title: My parents are fighting, and I am in depression. Can you inform me of a supplication to stop them fighitng? I sincerely need it.

    Question: My parents are fighting, and I am in depression. Can you inform me of a supplication to stop them fighitng? I sincerely need it.

    Answer ID: 15727Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1910/1534=H=1430)

     

    رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

    Recite the verse thrice after every salah with durood thrice before and after, and blow on water and preserve the water. Mix the same water with tea, milk, food etc which are used by the parents or you can give the same water to drink.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
