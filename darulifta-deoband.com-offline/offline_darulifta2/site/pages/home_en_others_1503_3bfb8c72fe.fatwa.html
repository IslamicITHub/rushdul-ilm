    Question: Assalam Alekum-- Please tell me Tabeer of dream; One day I saw a sea and I tried to jump over it. I am successful to jump over it and again came back on the same side by jumping. Other day I saw that a man is saying me Surah Baqar, Surah Baqar

    Answer ID: 1503Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1279/985=H)

     

    There is some laziness in following Islamic rulings. If you pay a bit attention, follow Shariah and forsake sins then you in-sha-Allah will soon feel improvements in you internal and external conditions.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
