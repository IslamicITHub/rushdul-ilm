    Title: I have read that if a husband is displeased by her wife her salaat is not accepted and the hur's curse her etc etc. but what happens when a wife is displeased with her husband because he gives no time to her. or she is not able to practise islam completely in her husbands home or she wants her husband to be islamic rather than materialistic, or she wants him to keep his trouser/izar/pants above his ankles and she is displeased with him because of reasons like this what does islam says regarding it.

    Question: i have read that if a husband is displeased by her wife her salaat is not accepted and the hur's curse her etc etc. but what happens when a wife is displeased with her husband because he gives no time to her. or she is not able to practise islam completely in her husbands home or she wants her husband to be islamic rather than materialistic, or she wants him to keep his trouser/izar/pants above his ankles and she is displeased with him because of reasons like this what does islam says regarding it.

    Answer ID: 29357Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 369/369/M=1432)

    We did not find any clarification about the displeasure of wife from husband being a curse. However, if the displeasure of wife is based on any appropriate reason and the husband is in fact guilty, yet the wife is patient and does not commit any shortcoming in fulfilling his rights rather she reminds him of his mistakes, then she shall get its reward and the husband shall be sinful due to not fulfilling the rights pertaining towards Allah and His slaves.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
