    Title: I want some dua as there are no things going right.

    Question: mufti saab i want some dua as there are no things going right,

    Answer ID: 24502Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1468/1143/B=1431)

     

    You should recite durood 100 times and لا حول ولا قوة الا بالله العلى العظيم 500 times daily for forty days along with supplicating Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
