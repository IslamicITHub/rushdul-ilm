    Title: sawm time

    Question: Where it is written that fasting time starting and end in which Hadith book, how about the six months day or night continuously fasting time.

    Answer ID: 59924Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1161/1118/L=09/1436) The starting and ending time of sawm is mentioned in the holy Quran: حَتَّى يَتَبَيَّنَ لَكُمُ الْخَيْطُ الأَبْيَضُ مِنَ الْخَيْطِ الأَسْوَدِ مِنَ الْفَجْرِ ثُمَّ أَتِمُّواْ الصِّيَامَ إِلَى الَّليْلِ [البقرة : 187] (2) People living in such areas shall follow the timetable of the nearest cities of their country.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
