    Title: Nafl fasting

    Question: If I break nafl fast by making love with my wife what I have to do to fulfil that nafl fast? Do I have to repeat the fast or do I have to keep more than one fast? Please guide us because it is very common here for youngster. Please make dua for us that Allah guide us to real Islamic world.

    Answer ID: 51662Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 535/535/M=05/1435) Fast shall break if one has intercourse in the state of fast. If it is done in nafl fast then there is no kaffarah, only the qadha of one fast is enough.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
