    Title: Aoa.i want to ask for how much time can husband live abroad from his wife??

    Question: Aoa.i want to ask for how much time can husband live abroad from his wife??

    Answer ID: 12330Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 667/667=M/1430)

     

    The husband can stay away for more than four months until his wife allows. If she is not ready, the husband will have to visit her within four months and fulfil her needs.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
