    Title: Is TV repairing as a profession Halal in Islam? Also how about repairing Computer Monitors?

    Question: Is TV repairing as a profession HALAL in Islam? Also how about repairing Computer Monitors?

    Answer ID: 545Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 544/J)

     

    The profession of TV repairing is not good. One should avoid it.

     

    As far as the matter of repairing computer monitors, it is allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
