    Title: Wether it is proved from any hadith that one can communicate through muraqba by sitting near the dead persons grave.2) wat should be the distnce between the feet while standing in namaz

    Question: Wether it is proved from any hadith that one can communicate through muraqba by sitting near the dead persons grave.2) wat should be the distnce between the feet while standing in namaz

    Answer ID: 8764Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2138/1741=B/1429)

     

    (1) Visiting the souls or having contact with them is related to kashf, I do not remember any Hadith having this meaning.

    (2) It is mentioned in Hadith, Hadhrat Abdullah bin Umar says: the Prophet (peace be upon him) used to keep his feet at a moderate space neither too close nor too apart. Therefore, the scholars of Fiqh say that the distance between both the feet should be at least of four fingers.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
