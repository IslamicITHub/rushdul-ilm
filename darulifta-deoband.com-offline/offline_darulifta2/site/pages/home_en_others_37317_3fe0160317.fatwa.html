    Title: I was in some place there were lots of graves and they were all in a big room and there were some people sitting. Suddenly, some idols came and started walking round

    Question: I was in some place there were lots of graves and they were all in a big room and there were some people sitting. Suddenly, some idols came and started walking round them and on graves then a person came he was looking like a Molvi type person. He stood there and started speaking, he was trying but voice was not coming out of his mouth. What happened next I stood up and went to the place where the Molvi sahib was standing and I recited surah Ikhlas and surah Fatiha loudly, then those idols ran away from that place or room and I felt peace. What I saw next there was a big grave of green colour and on its head it was written with white colour "Imam Abu Hanifa". I came out of that room and I saw there were lots of people walking around. I do not remember exactly but I think it was a mosque, and then I woke up. It was, I think, 10:00 in the morning.

    Answer ID: 37317Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 535/302/H=1433) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. The meaning is that you in particular and other Muslims in general shall receive benefit from the knowledge, taqwa and taharah of Imam Abu Hanifa (رحمة الله عليه) and shall get the taufiq to follow the sunnah. You shall be protected from those internal and external mischiefs which poison the atmosphere, in-shaAllah. If Fatawa Mahmoodiya is available, purchase it. It is a collection of the Fatawa of Hadhrat Maulana Mufti Mahmood Hasan Sahib Gangohi (رحمة الله عليه). Keep on consulting the local scholars if you are unable to understand at any point, and study it extensively. In-shaAllah you shall get a lot of benefit from it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
