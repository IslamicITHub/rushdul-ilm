    Title: Is commercial broking halal or haram

    Question: Is commercial broking halal or haram? For example: A person helps another person buy a shop worth 1 lakh in return expects 2000 rupees or 2% of the total.

    Answer ID: 35101Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    Is commercial broking halal or haram? For example: A person helps another person buy a shop worth 1 lakh in return expects 2000 rupees or 2% of the total.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
