    Question ID: 134Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: I have been working with bank for more then 14 years. Is the monet I earned OK?

    Question: I would like to ask a very important question related to my work. I have been working with bank for more then 14 years. I have been paid the salary for the time and services for the bank. Please advise if the money I earned is OK ?

    Answer ID: 134Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 529/H=337/H)

     

    According to Hadith, those are cursed who take interest, give it, write it, and who give witness to it. If you avoided all these things in your job then your earnings are lawful for you. And, when you are in Saudi Arabia we hope that your job is OK. But, we do not know what kind of banking system is prevalent there. However, if any question arises in your mind then better contact the local Ulama and Muftis regarding the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
