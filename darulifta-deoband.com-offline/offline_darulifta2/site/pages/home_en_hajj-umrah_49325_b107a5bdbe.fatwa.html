    Title: Hajj and Jamat

    Question: I stay in Mumbai in a rent house, in our masjid mashwera they have asked to go for 5 months jamaat (foreign), and I have not performed my hajj yet. The cost for going to foreign jamaat is around 2 lakhs rupees. What I have to do now? Should I have to perform my hajj with that money or go to foreign jamaat?

    Answer ID: 49325Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1935/510/TH=12/1434) If you have only 2 lakh rupees which suffice to go for hajj, then you should preferably perform hajj first. But if you have more money which may be sufficient for hajj as well as foreign Jamat, then you should do both the things.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
