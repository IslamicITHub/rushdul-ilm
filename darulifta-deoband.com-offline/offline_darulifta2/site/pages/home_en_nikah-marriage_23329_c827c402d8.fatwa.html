    Title: If a women or men do zina after their mariage will there nikah will be break? and what is the punishment according to islam for this shamefull activity please clarify it in each case

    Question: If a women or men do zina after their mariage will there nikah will be break? and what is the punishment according to islam for this shamefull activity please clarify it in each case

    Answer ID: 23329Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1014/752/L=1431)

    (1) It is very bad to commit adultery after marriage. However it does not invalidate the nikah.
    (2) If adultery is proved, the adulterer or adulteress shoulbe stoned to death as punishment. However in countries like India, these penal code are not in force, so such persons should do tauba and istighfar wholeheartedly, and should completely avoid such malicious deeds in future.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
