    Title: plz tell me that if some one is walking on a street and if some impure(like gutter water) falls on his leg then if he finds no water to clean 1)then should he perform abulation ? 2)or if he cleans that leg after reaching home( but in mean while if that gutter water dires up ) then if he cleans that part of his leg will it be ok?

    Question: as salamu alaikum plz tell me that if some one is walking on a street and if some impure(like gutter water) falls on his leg then if he finds no water to clean 1)then should he perform abulation ? 2)or if he cleans that leg after reaching home( but in mean while if that gutter water dires up ) then if he cleans that part of his leg will it be ok plz answer and dua ki guzarish

    Answer ID: 19640Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 233/185/L=1431)

     

    The wudu is not nullified by the gutter water, and it was not necessary to perform wudu immediately if you were without wudu.

    (2) The gutter water can be washed and cleaned even after it dries.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
