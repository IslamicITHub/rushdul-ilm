    Title: If in salat when we recite surah if three ayat are correct and if we make mistake in remaining ayat or 3 ayat in complete surah are correct and there is mistake in other ayats,is it require sajdah sahv (remedial prostration)?

    Question: If in salat when we recite surah if three ayat are correct and if we make mistake in remaining ayat or 3 ayat in complete surah are correct and there is mistake in other ayats,is it require sajdah sahv (remedial prostration)?

    Answer ID: 13928Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 955/760=L/1430)

     

    The sajdah sahv (remedial prostration) does not become wajib if one committed some mistake in qiraah (recitation of the Quran), but if the mistake was major causing change in the meaning of the verse, the salah will turn invalid, whether the major mistake occurred before reciting three verses or after.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
