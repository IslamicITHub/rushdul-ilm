    Question ID: 32267Country: Pakistan

    Title: I saw Hazrat Isa(AS) and Al-Mahdi in my dream, right by a sea coast which is the battlefield against Dajjal and his men.

    Question: I saw Hazrat Isa(AS) and Al-Mahdi in my dream, right by a sea coast which is the battlefield against Dajjal and his men. When i arrived i saw the face and figure of Isa(AS) and Al-Mahdi, who are standing firm, As in they have just killed the evil in that region and are now ready to move ahead to the final destination, with me, where Dajjal resides. Then as in they were waiting for me, I went right to the beautiful white horse of Hazrat Isa(AS) and climbed on it. Then with the feeling of riding with Isa(AS) we rode uphill to Dajjal's temple which is so close to our location that we can clearly see that huge mosque like temple. However while we are moving the ground below us starts to break and we almost fell down into the sea but then a feeling of help and satisfaction from ALLAH(SWT) came and the horse miraculously hung on to the corner of the land which did not break. Allowing us to breath with the joy that finally we have succeeded thanks to The Glorious ALLAH(SWT).

    Answer ID: 32267Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1089/679/H=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is nice and good dream. The meaning is that you shall be assigned important religious services provided you maintain certain contacts with the present Ahl Haq, Akabir Ummah and salf Salihin (رحمهم الله).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
