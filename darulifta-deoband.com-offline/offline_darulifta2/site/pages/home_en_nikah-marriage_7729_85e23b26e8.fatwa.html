    Title: My freind wants to ask that can he marry his Maternal aunt " Khala " if she is marryed to his uncle "Chacha".

    Question: My freind wants to ask that can he marry his Maternal aunt " Khala " if she is marryed to his uncle "Chacha".

    Answer ID: 7729Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1030/1030=M/1429)

     

    It is unlawful to marry to ones khala (maternal aunt) whether real or sister of mother from her father or mother. Please, clarify if the case is different.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
