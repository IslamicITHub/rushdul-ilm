    Answer ID: 30317Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 555/364/H=1432)

    Your concern is alright. In this case, there is only the fear of loss. Therefore, you should find out such a halal means in which there is no worry.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
