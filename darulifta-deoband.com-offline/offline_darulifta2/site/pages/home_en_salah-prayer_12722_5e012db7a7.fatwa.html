    Title: Assalamualaykumwarahmatullahiwa Barakatuh How one can complete his magrib salah when he join the magrib jamat in last khaida.

    Question: Assalamualaykumwarahmatullahiwa Barakatuh How one can complete his magrib salah when he join the magrib jamat in last khaida.

    Answer ID: 12722Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 763/763=M/1430)

     

    He will stand up after the imam turns his head for salam. Having recited sana, ta?awwuz and bismillah, he will recite Surah Fatihah with any other surah. Having completed one rak?ah with proper ruku and sajdah, he will stand up for second rak?ah. Just like first rak?ah, he will recite Surah Fatihah with any other surah and perform ruku and sajdah and sit for first qa?dah reciting Al-tahiyyaat. After Al-tahiyyaat, he will stand up for third rak?ah, recite Surah Fatihah and proceed to ruku and perform the salah in proper usual way.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
