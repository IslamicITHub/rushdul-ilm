    Question ID: 50145Country: LK

    Title: Nikah witness signed by a non-muslim?

    Question: In our country Sri Lanka a marriage registration has to be signed by: (1) the bridegroom (2) the guardian of the groom and (3) 2 witnesses. My question is can a non-Muslim sign as a witness; as this witness is called for in case of a divorce arise. We have a marriage registration law for Muslims different from non-Muslims and our Muslim divorce cases are handled by a Qazi court. Most of our local Ulama who know the above registration law say it is not correct for a non-Muslim to sign as a witness. I would like to know your ruling in the above matter as this has not happened in our town or country as far as I know, I am 56 years old.

    Answer ID: 50145Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 311/258/B=01/1435) It is necessary that two Muslim witnesses are present in the nikah of a Muslim. A non-Muslim cannot become the witness of a Muslim in nikah. So it shall not be valid if a non-Muslim signs as a witness in the nikah of a Muslim. Those who say it correct are wrong and what the local Ulama say is correct.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
