    Answer ID: 14907Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1503/1229=D/1430)

     

    First, you should contact any local aalim. And for understating Islam well, you should get and study the book ?Islam kya Hai? (by Maulana Manzoor Nomani) which is available in Hindi and English beside Urdu. You can express it verbally if it touches your heart; otherwise a person becomes Muslim by believing in the unity of Allah, prophethood of Hadhrat Muhammad and qiyamah and pronouncing the kalimah. After Islam, one is asked to follow other Islamic rulings such as salah, fasting etc and he is required to refrain from sins like infidelity, polytheism, wine, adultery, lying etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
