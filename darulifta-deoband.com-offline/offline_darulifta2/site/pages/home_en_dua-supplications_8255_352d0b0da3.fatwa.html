    Answer ID: 8255Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2147/387=TB/1429)

     

    Recite ya aziz (یا عزیز) after Isha in the state of wudhu facing the house of your in-laws 200 times for forty days. Also, recite durood thrice before and after. In-sha-Allah, their heart will melt down.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
