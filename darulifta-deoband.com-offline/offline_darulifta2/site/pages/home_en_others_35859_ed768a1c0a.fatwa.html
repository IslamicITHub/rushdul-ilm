    Title: I saw my late mother eating something in my dream, what does this mean? Kindly answer.

    Question: I saw my late mother eating something in my dream, what does this mean? Kindly answer.

    Answer ID: 35859Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 40/32/H=1433) Ma-sha-Allah, it is nice dream. It means that your mother was granted forgiveness and it is hoped that her status shall be raised in-sha-Allah. You should regularly do isal-e-sawab for her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
