    Title: ABOUT UMRAH

    Question: Can I do umrah before hajj? I am trying since ten years for hajj but I have no capacity for hajj now due to my economical condition.

    Answer ID: 55146Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1592/1324/B=10/1435) You may perform umrah in any month other than Shawwal, Zul Qadah and Zil Hijjah. It is sunnah to perform it. You may go for umrah whenever you wish.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
