    Question: Salaam, I want any dua or wazifa to change my husband's mind.My husband is converted muslim but now he want to stay with his parents who are non-muslim.I just want any dua or wazifa which help to change his mind to live with his parents and ready to buy seperate house for me.Please reply as soon as possible. Allah hafeez....

    Answer ID: 21663Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 594/594/M=1431)

     

    Recite surah Quraish (لایلف قریش) seven times with durood 11-11 times before and after and Ya Latifu (يالطيف) one hundred times with durood 3-3 times before and after in the morning and evening. Also supplicate Almighty Allah after each salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
