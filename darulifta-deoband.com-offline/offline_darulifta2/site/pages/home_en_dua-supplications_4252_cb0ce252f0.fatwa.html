    Title: I want to know the namaaz n duaa, on first night of wedding, bfore intercourse with wife first night.

    Question: I want to know the namaaz n duaa, on first night of wedding, bfore intercourse with wife first night.

    Answer ID: 4252Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 299/299=M/1429)

     

    On first night of wedding, you should recite this dua while holding hair of your wife's forehead:

    الهم اني اسأ لك من خيرها و خير ما جبلتها عليه و أعوذ بك من شرها و شر ما جبلتها عليه

    Afterwards, you should perform two rakah salah of thanks with husband standing forth and wife behind him. After the salah, you should do dua for goodness and blessings, mutual love and harmony. When you intend to have intercourse with your wife, you should recite this dua:

    اللهم جنبنا الشيطان و جنب الشيطان ما رزقتنا

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
