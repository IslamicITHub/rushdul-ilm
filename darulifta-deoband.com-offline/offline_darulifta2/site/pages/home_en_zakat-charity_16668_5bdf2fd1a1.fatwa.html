    Title: Assa la mu alaikum Q Is it permissible to give zakah to a person(who has no savings), who is in debt due to borrowing money from a bank to buy a house etc on Intrest (Mortgage)?

    Question: Assa la mu alaikum Q Is it permissible to give zakah to a person(who has no savings), who is in debt due to borrowing money from a bank to buy a house etc on Intrest (Mortgage)?

    Answer ID: 16668Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1667/1330/L=1430)

     

    If he has no cash amount and has some debts to be paid, he can receive Zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
