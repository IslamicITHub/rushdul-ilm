    Title: I consulted a doctor regarding this and he said that it is bladder malformation. Whenever I change my posture urine starts flowing down through my thighs and then to legs

    Question: I consulted a doctor regarding this and he said that it is bladder malformation. Whenever I change my posture urine starts flowing down through my thighs and then to legs. All the time my clothes will have the stains of urine. I use sanitary pads while I attend any occasion or on any important work. Excessive use of pads for long hours gets slight anal pain. I feel uncomfortable using pajamas, long dresses. I feel comfortable in shorts, short skirts that give exposure to air so as to keep myself dry. There is no male member in my family; no one stops me wearing such dresses. I wear short dresses only at home. I know it is against Islam to have exposure of my body parts, wearing short clothes. What should I do?.) I am having a lot of difficulty in doing my namaz. I perform wudhu, keep my namaz clothes separate, which I wear just before namaz. After completing I again change my clothes. Even in between namaz, while standing for ruku, doing sajdah, urine?

    Answer ID: 35467Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2028/B=388-TB=1432) If you suffer from the problem of urination in a way that you do not find time during the whole time of salah that you could offer salah in the state of taharah then as per the Shariah you are excused (mazoor). So, you would perform fresh wudhu after the time of salah starts and would offer salah; if the drop keeps on coming during salah then too you would complete your salah. Your salah shall be valid in spite of drops. But if the matter is not so, then you are not excused (mazoor) by Shariah. Your salah was not valid in case you were impure. Be clean again or change your clothes and perform wudhu and offer salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
