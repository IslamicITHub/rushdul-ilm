    Answer ID: 9335Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1647/290=TL/1429)

     

    It indicates that the condition of your father is nice. However, you should keep on doing isal-e-thawab for him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
