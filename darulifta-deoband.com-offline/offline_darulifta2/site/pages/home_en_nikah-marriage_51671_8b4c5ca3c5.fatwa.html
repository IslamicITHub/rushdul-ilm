    Title: Nikkah for my brother

    Question: We were looking for a bride for my younger brother. My mother's younger sister is having a daughter and both the parents decided to get my brother married to her (my mother's younger sister's daughter). They suggested that this marriage is allowed as per Islamic Hadith. Kindly suggest the clear explanation on the same.

    Answer ID: 51671Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 676/557/B=05/1435) It is valid to marry the daughter of one’s khala.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
