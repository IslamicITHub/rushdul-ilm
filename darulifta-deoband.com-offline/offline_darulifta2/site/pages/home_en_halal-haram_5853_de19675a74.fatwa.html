    Title: Is working as a advocate is jayaz in islam or not?

    Question: Is working as a advocate is jayaz in islam or not?

    Answer ID: 5853Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 409/409=M/1429)

     

    If an advocate follows up right cases and avoids telling lie then it is allowed for him to work as advocate.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
