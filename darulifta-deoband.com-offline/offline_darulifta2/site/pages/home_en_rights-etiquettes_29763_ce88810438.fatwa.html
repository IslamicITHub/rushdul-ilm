    Question ID: 29763Country: India

    Title: If wife is unwilling to meet her inlaws and asks her husband also to forego relatins with them what should husband do in such sityuations also towards whom is he more responsible towards parents or towards wife.please reply in light of shariah.Please reply at the earliest.

    Question: Dear Sir Assalamoalikum, if wife is unwilling to meet her inlaws and asks her husband also to forego relatins with them what should husband do in such sityuations also towards whom is he more responsible towards parents or towards wife.please reply in light of shariah.Please reply at the earliest.Thank you

    Answer ID: 29763Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 272/272/M=1432)

    One has rights towards one's wife as well as parents, but the rights of parents are more than the rights of wife. Husband is responsible to pay the rights of both. It is not right to behave someone in a way that the rights of other are infringed upon. The demand of wife to the husband to sever relations with his parents is wrong. The wife should provide her husband chance to serve his parents and she should not make him sinful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
