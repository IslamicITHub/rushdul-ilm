    Title: Dua for asthama

    Question: My father (age 60 years) is suffering from allergic asthma from past 20 years. Now a days it is getting worse and he has gone into depression and have become very weak. Please provide some dua for this.

    Answer ID: 159620Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 785/666/H=6/1439)

     

    You or any other family member or your father himself should read Surah Fatiha (full Surah) 41 times daily any time (it is better if it is read before Fajr salah) with durood 11-11 times before and after and blow on water and give that water to your father to drink. May Allah give your father perfect health. Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
