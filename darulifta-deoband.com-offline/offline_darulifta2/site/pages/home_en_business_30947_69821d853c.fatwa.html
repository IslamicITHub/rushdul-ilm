    Title: If my husband's earning is low and we are "unable" to give Zakah or pay rent regularly or give Sadaqah whole heartedly, can I operate a business from home and say to him that we will share the earnings. Is this right?

    Question: If my husband's earning is low and we are "unable" to give Zakah or pay rent regularly or give Sadaqah whole heartedly, can I operate a business from home and say to him that we will share the earnings. Is this right?

    Answer ID: 30947Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 476/365=L=1432) You can start a business at home. It is not prohibited for women to operate business if they maintain Shariah limits.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
