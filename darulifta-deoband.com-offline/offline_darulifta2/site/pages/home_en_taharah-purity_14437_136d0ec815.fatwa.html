    Title: Can a menstruating women recite ayats from the Quran which she has memorised and is it allowed for a menstruating women to apply heena on her hands?

    Question: Can a menstruating women recite ayats from the Quran which she has memorised and is it allowed for a menstruating women to apply heena on her hands?

    Answer ID: 14437Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1246/1012=D/1430)

     

    If the verse is containing a dua (prayer), she can recite it as prayer, but reciting other verses as tilawah is forbidden though she memorize it by heart.

    (2) She can apply henna.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
