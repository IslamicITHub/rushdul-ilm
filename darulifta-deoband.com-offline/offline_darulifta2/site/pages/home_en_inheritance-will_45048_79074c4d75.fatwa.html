    Title: How to distribute my jewellery

    Question: I have gold worth rupees 10 lakhs and I have 6 sons and 1 daughter. Now explain me how to distribute this within them. Please provide your answer with detailed information.

    Answer ID: 45048Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 665/665/M=1434) If you want to distribute that gold in your lifetime among all your six sons and one daughter then you should distribute its price into seven equal shares and give one share in the complete possession of each one. It is preferable to distribute equally among all male and female heirs during lifetime. لقوله عليه السلام: سوو ا بين أولادكم فى العطية .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
