    Question ID: 50626Country: HK

    Title: Women cutting hair after completing Umra

    Question: I would like to ask that after performing Umrah, if a woman's hair is not in one length, it is cut in two layers or more, then should the woman cut her hair off from the bottom of every layer or just hold all the hair together and cut some off from the bottom. Also what is the length of the hair that should be cut off?

    Answer ID: 50626Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 230/230/M=03/1435) It is wajib to cut hair equal to one third finger i.e. equal to one finger tip from one fourth of head. The woman should hold all her hair together to her forehead and cut equal to one finger tip from the end. المراد بالتقصير أن يأخذ الرجل والمرأة من رؤوس شعر ربع الرأس مقدار الأنملة ... (شامى)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
