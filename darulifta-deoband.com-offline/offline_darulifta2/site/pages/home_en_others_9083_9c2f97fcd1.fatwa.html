    Title: Mufti Shaheb, Somebody (male) has touched the private body parts of another person(male) intentionally. Is just making Tawba is enough for him, or he has to seek forgiveness from the second person? Jazakallah

    Question: Mufti Shaheb, Somebody (male) has touched the private body parts of another person(male) intentionally. Is just making Tawba is enough for him, or he has to seek forgiveness from the second person? Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 9083Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1157/1157=M/1429)

     

    It is sufficient to repent to Allah. If that person felt some resentment or he was hurt then one should apologized to him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
