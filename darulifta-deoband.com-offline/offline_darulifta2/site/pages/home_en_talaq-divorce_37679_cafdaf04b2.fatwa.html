    Title: My sister had gone through stomach surgery due to which the ayyam-e-haiz are extended than normal days

    Question: My sister had gone through stomach surgery due to which the ayyam-e-haiz are extended than normal days (no more regular periods are coming after operation). During this period, she has got talaq. Now the question here is, whether the iddah duration calculated will be 3 haiz (which would be 5-6 months in this case)? Or it can be calculated based on normal haiz days, i.e. approximately 3 months?

    Answer ID: 37679Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 779/779/M=1433) In the question mentioned above, the iddah of your divorced sister shall complete after three haiz (menstruation cycle) though it completes in five or six months.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
