    Question ID: 3259Country: Pakistan

    Title: My grandson was given gift, cash and gold since the time he was born in the last 10 years. They are kept in trust for him to enjoy when he atleast 18 years. Is zakat required to be paid on them.

    Question: My grandson was given gift, cash and gold since the time he was born in the last 10 years. They are kept in trust for him to enjoy when he atleast 18 years. Is zakat required to be paid on them. Thank u Mufti Sahab

    Answer ID: 3259Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 411/296=H)

     

    If the gifts were meant for your grandson and they are in his ownership, then Zakah is not obligatory on the cash and gold and sliver until he reaches the age of puberty.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
