    Title: I had a dream of a large python chasing me rapidly and I was running fast here and there after some time I went to a big stage and the snake got me and bit me

    Question: I had a dream of a large python chasing me rapidly and I was running fast here and there after some time I went to a big stage and the snake got me and bit me. I was lying in that stage shouting help, help, snake has bitten me but I was feeling normal. I did not feel any pain or unconsciousness. After some time a sound came nothing will happen until Allah (SWT) orders your soul to come out of your body and nothing will happen even if a large python has bitten you. So just stand up and go and after hearing that I stood up and went normally.

    Answer ID: 39406Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 667/651/N=1433) The meaning of your dream is that there are some big enemies of you amongst the human beings or Satan who want to harm you regarding religion or worldly affair. Apparently it will harm you but due to the protection from Allah you will not meet any danger in reality. Also the dream indicates that you should only trust in Allah saving yourself from the enemy. Also have firm belief that none can harm you as long as Allah does not want so.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
