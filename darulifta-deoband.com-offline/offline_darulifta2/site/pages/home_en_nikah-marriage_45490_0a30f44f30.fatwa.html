    Title: Calling Wife as Mother

    Question: Some people say that calling your wife amma, maa, in anger is prohibited. What does Shariah say about it? If someone is angry and tells his wife sorry ‘meri maa’ or any other word like this what should he do?

    Answer ID: 45490Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 871/688/B=1434) It is nonsense to call one’s wife mother or amma. However, if someone said so it shall not have any adverse effect on his nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
