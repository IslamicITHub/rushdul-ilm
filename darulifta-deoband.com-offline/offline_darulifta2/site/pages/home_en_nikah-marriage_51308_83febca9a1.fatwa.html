    Title: Nikah During Periods?

    Question: Is nikah valid if the dulhan (bride) is menstruating on the day of nikah? Is it necessary to be paak while doing Ijaab-o-Qabool in front of a wakil?

    Answer ID: 51308Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 450/450/M=04/1435) Nikah is valid even in the state of menstruation. Paaki (to be clean and pure) is not necessary for nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
