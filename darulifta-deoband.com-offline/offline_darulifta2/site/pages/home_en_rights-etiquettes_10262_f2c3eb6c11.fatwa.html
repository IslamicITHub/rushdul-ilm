    Answer ID: 10262Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 63/63=D/429)

     

    Get the below books from book deports of Deoband or Delhi and study them, in-shaAllah you shall get benefit of the world and the hereafter. Then, if any thing remains questionable write to us. The books are: (1) Adaab Al-Muasharat by Hadhrat Thanwi (2) Safayi-e-Muamalat by Hadhrat Thanwi (3) Uswah-e-Rasool Akram Sallallahu Alaihi Walsallam, by Hadhrat Dr Abdul Hayee.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
