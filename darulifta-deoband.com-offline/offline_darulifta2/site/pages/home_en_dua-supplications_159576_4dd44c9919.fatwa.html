    Title: Which dua or ayat should husband and wife or both recite during pregnancy?

    Question: Which dua or ayat should husband and wife or both recite during pregnancy?

    Answer ID: 159576Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 841/769/L=7/1439)

     

    During the pregnancy and in common conditions the parents should read this verse and they should recite it seven times after each salah. In-shaAllah, they will be blessed with child who shall be pious. The verse is as follows:

     

    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً.

     

    However, recitation of Surah Maryam is useful during pregnancy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
