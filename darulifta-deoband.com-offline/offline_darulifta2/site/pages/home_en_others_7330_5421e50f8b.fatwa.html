    Question ID: 7330Country: India

    Title: My sister was watching a dream that me and younger brother is eating meat of the dog what was that mean in the dream? Need your answer quickly because I am very disturb about this dream.

    Question: Aslam u Alikum. Hope you are doing good by grace of Allah. My sister was watching a dream that me and younger brother is eating meat of the dog what was that mean in the dream? Need your answer quickly because I am very disturb about this dream. Jazakalallah

    Answer ID: 7330Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1454/1369=D/1429)

     

    You and your younger brother shall gain financial and material benefits from your enemies. You should refrain from unlawful wealth and observe taqwa (piety).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
