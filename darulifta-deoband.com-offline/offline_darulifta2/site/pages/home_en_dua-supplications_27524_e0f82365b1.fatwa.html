    Answer ID: 27524Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2535/1575/H=1431)

    Recite the following verse 100 times daily any time and supplicate Almighty Allah and continue whatever you recite after salah.
    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
