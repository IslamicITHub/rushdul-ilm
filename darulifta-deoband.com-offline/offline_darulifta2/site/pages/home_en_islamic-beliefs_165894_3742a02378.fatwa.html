    Title: I would like to know if we can name our son Jibreel Mohammad?

    Question: We are having our second child any day now in-shaAllah. I would like to know if we can name our son Jibreel Mohammad? Thank you in advance.

    Answer ID: 165894Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 146/120/M=03/1440)

     

    Yes, you may give the name to your child. However, it is better to keep Mohammad first. Here are some other names you may choose if you want: Abdullah, Abdur Rahman, Hassan, Salman, Safwan, Huzaifah, Mohammad Saeed, Rasheed, Hammad, Sajjad etc. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
