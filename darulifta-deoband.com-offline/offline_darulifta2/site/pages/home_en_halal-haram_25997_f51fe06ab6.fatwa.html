    Title: I hav a property in aurangabad, and i want to give on rent some of my shop, to a bank.. is,a bank rent is right(jayaz) to take in islam shariya, please help me out of this... if not how to make possible.

    Question: assalam o alikum , i shaikh altaf ahmed, i hav a property in aurangabad, and i want to give on rent some of my shop, to a bank.. is,a bank rent is right(jayaz) to take in islam shariya, please help me out of this... if not how to make possible... please reply me soon... JAZAK ALLAH regards shaikh altaf maharashtra aurangabad INDIA 09890753271

    Answer ID: 25997Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2105/1430/H=1431)

    It is lawful, i.e. you can rent it, so is mentioned in Fatawa Khalilia. However, it is better to avoid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
