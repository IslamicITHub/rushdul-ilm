    Answer ID: 6951Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1748=H/1429)

     

    After every obligatory salah, before going to bed and after waking up, Recite Surah Quraish (No. 106) with durood eleven times before and after and blow on hands and pass on the hands over the body.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
