    Title: Zakat on Land Investment

    Question: I have made investment of some amount in partnership with my bro-in-law and uncle but the land is in the name of my uncle for past 10 years. I have invested X amount and now the value of land in 8X. Please advise will I be liable to pay zakah on this land if this is not registered under my name. If yes, please advise on what amount I will be liable to pay the zakah (on the purchased value or the current value).

    Answer ID: 150715Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 690/544/D=8/1438)

    By partnership if you intended to do business then whatsoever is bought with the joint money it shall be called business goods. Zakah is wajib on the business goods and cash in hand.

    What is the nature of the land mentioned in the question? Was it bought with joint money with the intention of business? If it is so, then each year zakah shall be wajib on its market price. Further, whatsoever amount you own you will have to pay its zakah.

    Note: Zakah is wajib on the owner, it is related to ownership. It does not have any relation with the name in paper. Though the name should be corrected i.e. the name should be of the person who is the real owner. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
