    Title: Umrah

    Question: My question is that after performing Umrah can I perform more Umrahs after doing niyat at Masjid Ayesha (Makkah).

    Answer ID: 57503Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 332/332/M=04/1436) If you want to perform second umrah after completing the first one then you may proceed for it while wearing Ihram from Masjid Ayesha (Tan’eem). One Ihram can suffice only for one umrah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
