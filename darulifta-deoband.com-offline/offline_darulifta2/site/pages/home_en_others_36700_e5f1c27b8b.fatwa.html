    Title: dream interpretation

    Question: My sister saw one woman of her colony who was a nurse during her lifetime, but now she is no more, my sister asked her about my brother's problem that my brother is not having any child. So she said that his wife is being under black magic when she was a child with age of 10-12, she cannot deliver a baby. What is the interpretation of this dream of my sister? My brother's wife name is Rukhsar.

    Answer ID: 36700Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 300/110/L=1433) Dream is not any definite thing that one has to relay upon. However, if you have doubt you may contact any pious Amil.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
