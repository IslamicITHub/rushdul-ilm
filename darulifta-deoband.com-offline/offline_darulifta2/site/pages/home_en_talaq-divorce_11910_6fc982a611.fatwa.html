    Answer ID: 11910Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 727/692=B/1430)

     

    Your wife was divorced with one Talaq.  After the delivery of the child her iddah was also over and she got separated (bayinah). The second Talaq given after the childbirth was in vain and ineffective. If you want to take her back as your wife, you can do so with new mahr (dower) and new nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
