    Title: My son is seven year old, for performing the namaj with jmaat at home. Can he become my muktadi and say takbeer as i will be do the imamat.

    Question: my son is seven year old, for performing the namaj with jmaat at home. Can he become my muktadi and say takbeer as i will be do the imamat.

    Answer ID: 28520Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 81/81/M=1432)

    If you missed the jama’ah of masjid or you are offering salah with jama’ah at home due to some excuse and there is none with you except your seven year old son, then he may become your muqtadi and can say takbir. However, it is better if you start salah by saying takbir yourself and place your son on your right side.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
