    Answer ID: 22883Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1214/923/H=1431)

    Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. It means that Almighty Allah blessed you with the wealth of Hajj maqbool (accepted by Allah) and it also means forgiveness of sins. May Almighty Allah bestow you its fruits and blessings the whole life!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
