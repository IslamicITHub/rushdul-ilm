    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1560/1474/B=1430)

     

    The best durood is the Durood Ibrahimi recited in salah. Whatever you have read at the website is correct. You should recite durood as much as possible with good intention. It is best solution for all kinds of trouble.
