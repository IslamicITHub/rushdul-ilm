    Title: Time for maghrib salah

    Question: I am a student sometimes when I go on bus the bus time is 6:30 pm but nowadays the time of sunset is 6:32 pm the bus stops at about 9:00pm. What should I do for Maghrib salah.

    Answer ID: 65351Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 586/586/SD=07/1437)

    It is not lawful to make qaza of Maghrib salah due to the abovementioned excuse. Hence in the above mentioned case, you must try to offer Maghrib salah in time anyhow. At least offer three rakahs of fardh, it shall take four or five minutes. Salah is such a tenet of Islam which has been strongly emphasized by the Holy Quran and Hadith and stern warning has been reported on missing it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
