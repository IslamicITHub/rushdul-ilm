    Question: i performed ummrah on 15-october-2010 (07 Dhul Qa`dah 1431)i stay in riyadh i never perform haj befour please let me know it is mandatory for me to perform haj this year also let me know what is the period of aiyaam of haj as i heard it start from 1st shawwal ?. secondly i have loan to pay

    Answer ID: 27933Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2111/B=1707/tb=1431)

    Hajj has become fardh on you. After paying the debts when you own money to an extent that you can perform hajj, then you should go for hajj and perform it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
