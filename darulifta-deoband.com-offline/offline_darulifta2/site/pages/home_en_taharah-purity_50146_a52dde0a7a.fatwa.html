    Question ID: 50146Country: India

    Title: HAIZH (Madhhab e Shafi)

    Question: I married before 2 years yet I have not blessed with child. My wife is suffering with irregular period in past two years but now period is continuing more than one month so she cannot perform her namaz and tilawat. She is hafiz-e-quran. So please give correct solution to perform her namaz and tilawat, make dua for us.

    Answer ID: 50146Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 300/241/B=01/1435) Now when the menses is coming timely from one month then she should not offer salah and do not recite the holy Quran during the menses. After it stops, she should offer salah and recite the holy Quran. The solution is to be in the state of purity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
