    Title: Whose farz is more imp for man its walidain or wife children more in case of same issue.

    Question: Whose farz is more imp for man its walidain or wife children more in case of same issue.what to do when father asked to give talaq?

    Answer ID: 33050Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1162/1162/M=1432) It is not known what do you mean by the same issue. However, if the situation of clash happens, one should try to keep both of them happy with wisdom and good policy. If it works, it is better; otherwise, one should obey his parents. There is an appendix attached to the 11th volume of Bahishti Zewar on this subject, you should study it for detail.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
