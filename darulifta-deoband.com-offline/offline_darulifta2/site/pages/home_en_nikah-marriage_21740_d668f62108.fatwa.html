    Title: I am married person from last 1.5 yrs and my marriage life also good. From last 3 months I fall in love with a divorced girl which have a 6 yrs child. By hearing her story, that her husband leaves her when her child is 7 months in her stommache. and now after 7 years he was ready to give her Talaq. In between they doesnt have any physical relationship. Now I wants to marry with her without informing my wife. I will go towards her once in a week and for that she also ready and also I will give the money, love and care. Now tell me Islam allow this.

    Question: Assalamuwaalikum, I am married person from last 1.5 yrs and my marriage life also good. From last 3 months I fall in love with a divorced girl which have a 6 yrs child. By hearing her story, that her husband leaves her when her child is 7 months in her stommache. and now after 7 years he was ready to give her Talaq. In between they doesnt have any physical relationship. Now I wants to marry with her without informing my wife. I will go towards her once in a week and for that she also ready and also I will give the money, love and care. Now tell me Islam allow this.

    Answer ID: 21740Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 969/731/H=1431)

     

    That poor (husband) has not given talaq so far. You not only understood his wife divorced before talaq but fell in her love. It is better for you to make firm and true tauba and amend yourself, and discard from your mind the fad of marrying that married woman. Determine to live with your wife in good manner, there is peace for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
