    Title: Does ones mother-in-law and father-in -law have the status of ones parents after nikah? Does a women have to look after them and obey them as she would to her own parents? What rights do they have upon her?

    Question: Does ones mother-in-law and father-in -law have the status of ones parents after nikah? Does a women have to look after them and obey them as she would to her own parents? What rights do they have upon her?

    Answer ID: 7291Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1116/970=L/1429)

     

    The parents in law also are like real parents; one should have good conduct with them as well like his own real parents.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
