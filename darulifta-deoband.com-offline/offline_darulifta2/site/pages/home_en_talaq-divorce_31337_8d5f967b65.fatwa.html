    Title: what is khula? how is it done? once it is done can the husband and wife get back ?

    Question: what is qula? how is it done? once it is done can the husband and wife get back ?

    Answer ID: 31337Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 519/397/L=1432) The incident of talaq or khula takes place in case of difference between spouses. In khula, wife withdraws from the right of mahr or compensates some amount to the husband and the husband takes her out of his nikah with the words of talaq or khula. By khula, talaq bai'n takes place. Hence, if the husband and wife want to live together after khula, they can do so after performing new nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
