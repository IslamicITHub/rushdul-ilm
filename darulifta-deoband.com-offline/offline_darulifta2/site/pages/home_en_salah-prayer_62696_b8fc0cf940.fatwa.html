    Title: Reading Sana after getting up

    Question: If you miss any of the rakahs of fardh salah being offered in jamah, are you supposed to read Sana after getting up after Imam's salaam?

    Answer ID: 62696Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 152/152/B=03/1437) Yes, Sana should be recited. It is mustahab and desirable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
