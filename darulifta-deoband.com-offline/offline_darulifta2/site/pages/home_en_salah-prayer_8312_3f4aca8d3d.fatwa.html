Even of you could pray one days Qaza Salahs every day which are only 20 rakahs (3 wajib witr), please do it. It only takes few mins to perform 20 rakahs according to the following instructions. 1) In Ruku and Sajdah instead of reciting "Subhaana Rabbiyal Azeem" and "Subhaana Rabbiyal A'la" three times, say it only once. Dua is not a must here. 4) In Witr, instead of full Dua-e-Qunoot just say "Rabbigh Fir Lee" one or three times.     Answer ID: 8312Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2273/1864=B/1429)

     

    If you are in much hurry, you can offer salah shortening it in this way; your salah will be valid, albeit it is better to perform salah calmly and seriously. You should recite Surah Fatiha in the last two rakah of obligatory salah and recite dua-e-qunoot in witr. Also recite complete durood and dua.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
