    Question ID: 32559Country: India

    Title: During Sa’ee (safa marwa), Is it necessary to climb upward at safa turning point of ground level corridor?

    Question: During Sa’ee (safa marwa), Is it necessary to climb upward at safa turning point of ground level corridor? Wheel chair person simply turns after one or two steps forward at safa turning point. Does safa hill start as soon as level ground starts to uplift ?

    Answer ID: 32559Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1185/744/H=1432) Sai' shall be alright in this state too. However, it is sunnah to go to such a height that the House of Allah is seen.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
