    Title: My sister (Yasmeen Taj) is very sensitive and gets hurt easily, she is not assertive and most of her in-laws and her husband take advantage of his.

    Question: My sister (Yasmeen Taj) is very sensitive and gets hurt easily, she is not assertive and most of her in-laws and her husband take advantage of his. Is their a dua to make her strong mentally and to make her able to speak her mind . People try to dominate her she find its very difficult to manage, she is very hurt by this and very unhappy . She is very introvert and that makes her suffer. Awaiting your quick response. Jazakallahu Khair.

    Answer ID: 31610Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 801/523/H=1432) Recite Surah Alam Nashrah (الم نشرح) 11 times after every salah with 11 times durood before and after, then blow it on your chest as well as on water to drink. Drinking cold water also is a temporary treatment to repel anger. Ask your sister to study Bahishti Zewar deeply specially the chapters dealing with in-laws in good manner. Also, ask her to ponder over her activities in loneliness and continue trying to remove her faults keeping patience against all pains and sufferings. The condition will change to better if she acts upon these instructions, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
