    Title: Assalamualaikum, Can a boy marry a girl who is richer than him , if the girl agrees for the marriage and keeps her mahr less as compared to mahr of other girls of her family?

    Question: Assalamualaikum, Can a boy marry a girl who is richer than him , if the girl agrees for the marriage and keeps her mahr less as compared to mahr of other girls of her family?

    Answer ID: 10213Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 107/79=H/1429)

     

    In the above-mentioned case, the Nikah is valid if there is no valid reason preventing Nikah and the Nikah is performed with the mediation of their guardians in proper way.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
