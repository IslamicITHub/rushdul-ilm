    Question ID: 23957Country: India

    Title: Please tell me the status of man made relations of brothers and sisters(muhbole bahen/Bhai) in Islam. Is there any Ayat in Quran-e-Kareem/Hadith saying that there is no such relation accepted in Islam?

    Question: Assalamualaikum ! Mufti Sahab , Please tell me the status of man made relations of brothers and sisters(muhbole bahen/Bhai) in Islam. Is there any Ayat in Quran-e-Kareem/Hadith saying that there is no such relation accepted in Islam?

    Answer ID: 23957Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1130/1130/M=1431)

    Almighty Allah says in Surah Al-Ahzab regarding one who is adopted (adopted brother or sister):
    وَمَا جَعَلَ أَدْعِيَاءكُمْ أَبْنَاءكُمْ ذَلِكُمْ قَوْلُكُم بِأَفْوَاهِكُمْ وَاللَّهُ يَقُولُ الْحَقَّ وَهُوَ يَهْدِي السَّبِيلَ [الأحزاب : 4]
    ادْعُوهُمْ لِآبَائِهِمْ هُوَ أَقْسَطُ عِندَ اللَّهِ فَإِن لَّمْ تَعْلَمُوا آبَاءهُمْ فَإِخْوَانُكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ وَمَوَالِيكُمْ [الأحزاب : 5]
    Allah has not made for any man two hearts in his chest cavity, nor did he make your wives whom you subjected to Zihar, your mothers, nor did he make your adopted sons your (real) sons. That is (merely) a word uttered by your mouths. And Allah says the truth and He shows the (rights) way.
    Call them by (the name of) their (real) fathers; it is more equitable in the sight of Allah. And if you do not know their fathers, then they are your brothers in faith and your friends.

    Islam does not stop to take care and help orphans, waif, stray and helpless children rather it teaches and emphasizes the same. However it prohibits to declare them as real son because it affects a part of Muslim Personnel Law. So by making someone adopted son, the rulings of real son will not be applicable on him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
