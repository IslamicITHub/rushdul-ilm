    Answer ID: 13115Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1276/1005=H/1430)

     

    There is no wrong in enforcing the Shariah law. The widow should be paid all her share in the property and wealth left by her husband. It is better to invite 2-4 influential and prudent persons to convince her to act upon the ruling of the Shariah. Taking the matter to court should be avoided to a possible extent.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
