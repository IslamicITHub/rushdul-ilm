    Answer ID: 5412Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1002/751=H)

     

    Seeing snakes in dreams means that plentiful mischief is pouring in the world. People think that they have eliminated mischief but in fact it did not come to an end, but will come up again in new faces. May Allah keep you and us safe from apparent and hidden mischief! Amen.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
