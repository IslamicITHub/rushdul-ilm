    Title: Is it compulsory to tell a person that the money being given to him is a Sadqah?

    Question: Is it compulsory to tell a person that the money being given to him is a Sadqah?

    Answer ID: 36396Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 121/73/H=1433) It is good to tell the person whom it is given what types money it is. If there is any special reason to conceal it then you ask it by writing again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
