    Answer ID: 11572Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 500/371=L/1430)

     

    You should marry the girl if the family members of both of you agree.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
