    Title: One of my friend insists that there was no Nimaz E Janazah of Rasoolullah peace be upon him. Please let me know what is exctly right as per fiquah.

    Question: One of my friend insists that there was no Nimaz E Janazah of Rasoolullah peace be upon him. Please let me know what is exctly right as per fiquah.

    Answer ID: 21326Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 492/492/m=1431)

     

    The janazah salah of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) was performed but no one led his janazah salah. Hadhrat Abdullah ibn Abbas narrates that on Tuesday when enshrouding of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) was done the janazah was placed on the edge of the grave. People entered in the room in group and performed the janazah salah individually without jama'ah. (Sunan Ibn Majah).

     

    It is narrated in Shamaa-il Tirmidhi that people asked Hadhrat Abu Bakr Siddique (رضى الله تعالى عنه): should janaazah salah be performed for Rasulullah?' He replied: 'Yes'. They said: 'And how?' He said: 'A group at a time should enter the room and perform the janazah salah individually without jamaa'ah, and return. In this manner all should perform the salah'. (Seeratul Mustafa Vol. 3)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
