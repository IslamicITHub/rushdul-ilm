    Title: I want to know about washing of legs in wazu, masa on socks made of cloth its ok or we have to wash our legs,if not ok i want to know the hadith (hear many people doing masa in wazu, itis correct to pray namaz behind them or to avoid

    Question: Assalam alykum i want to know about washing of legs in wazu, masa on socks made of cloth its ok or we have to wash our legs,if not ok i want to know the hadith (hear many people doing masa in wazu, itis correct to pray namaz behind them or to avoid

    Answer ID: 29267Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 190/190/M=1432)

    It is not lawful to do masah on the prevalent cotton and nylon socks because they do not fulfil the conditions that make the masah of socks valid. And those who offer salah after doing masah on cotton and nylon socks their wudhu and salah is invalid. It is not lawful to offer salah behind such persons. Masah is only valid on Khuffain (leather socks).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
