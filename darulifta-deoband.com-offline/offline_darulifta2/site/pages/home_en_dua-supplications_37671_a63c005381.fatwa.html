    Title: I have married from last 6 years, a child had expired in bally of my wife 2 years ago but now

    Question: I have married from last 6 years, a child had expired in bally of my wife 2 years ago but now, we have not any child, in medical check-up now I am suffering nil quantity of sperm. Please give me a dua for this that my diseases go out.

    Answer ID: 37671Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 628/379/=1433) Recite the following dua after each salah 11 times with durood 11-11 times before and after and do the same before going to bed in the night in the state of wudhu: رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
