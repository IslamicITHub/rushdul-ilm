    Question ID: 32558Country: India

    Title: My grandfather passed away a month ago. He had a joint account in bank with my grandmother.

    Question: My grandfather passed away a month ago. He had a joint account in bank with my grandmother. Now after his demise, My uncle is claiming for his share. Pls guide us on the following aspects- 1)Is my grandmother the sole owner of the money in the account? 2)Given the Will in which my grandfather mentioned that his wife will be the owner of the property and only after her will there be any distribution of property. What is the ruling considering the Will?

    Answer ID: 32558Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 898/749/B=1432) (1) What was grandfather and grandmother's ratio of combined account in the bank i.e. was it half and half or less or more? After knowing it we can have a say. Also who among the heirs of grandfather were alive at the time of his death, write their detail. (2) The will of grandfather for grandmother is null; it shall not be implemented because making will for heirs is unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
