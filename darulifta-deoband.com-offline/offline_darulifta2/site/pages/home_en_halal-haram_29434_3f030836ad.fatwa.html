    Title: I am a Bank Oficer at Shahjalal Islami Bank Ltd. My Question That If I Deposit Money in this bank under Mudaraba Deposit Account and Profit Given by this Bank on Mudaraba Saving Account But I Know that this Bank do not practice his business under islamic shariah in real of its operation, in this case Can I avail profit amount in this account

    Question: Assala Mu Alaikum I am a Bank Oficer at Shahjalal Islami Bank Ltd. My Question That If I Deposit Money in this bank under Mudaraba Deposit Account and Profit Given by this Bank on Mudaraba Saving Account But I Know that this Bank do not practice his business under islamic shariah in real of its operation, in this case Can I avail profit amount in this account

    Answer ID: 29434Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 246/246/M=1432)

    When you know definitely that the bank is involved in non-Shariah compliance businesses, it is not right to use the profits received from the bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
