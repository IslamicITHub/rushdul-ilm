    Title: Dream

    Question: Can you please interpret this dream for me? I dreamt of attending funeral of a buzurg (pious person). I was asked at what time the burial should be done. I answered as soon as possible. One buzurg in the funeral came and told me: Good you are fast on track which will benefit the nation.

    Answer ID: 40508Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1205/788/H=1433) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. The meaning is that you shall be given the taufiq of paying rights and following sunnah, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
