    Title: Dua to get proper match for marriage 

    Question: Assalam Alaikum, 1.my daughter is of a mariageable age,we are in the process of finding a suitable match for her since past 2 years .many propasals have also beem coming bt noting seems to get fixed.everything stops in the very initial stages .please suggest us any dua or what should we do.please give the best dua for marriage. thank you

    Answer ID: 18546Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 25/25L=1431)

     

    You should recite ya Latif ya Wadood 1111 times after Isha Salah with durood 11 times and 11 times after. In-sh-Allah, you will soon come across a perfect match.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
