    Title: Can we start Men hair saloon shop?

    Question: Can we start the men hair salon shop, because in this business we shave the beard and cut hair in different styles which is not allowed in Islam for Muslim brothers. So is this business allowed in Islam or not?

    Answer ID: 148169Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 498/516/M=5/1438)

    A Muslim should adopt pure and halal source of income. It is recorded in hadith that the part of the body which is nourished with haram wealth the hell fire is suitable for it. You may open hair salon shop but you should avoid cutting hair in unlawful style. It is unlawful and sin to cut or shave beard and its income is also unlawful. You may cut hair but it is makrooh to cut the hair in resemblance to the fashionable and transgressor people against sunnah in different styles. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
