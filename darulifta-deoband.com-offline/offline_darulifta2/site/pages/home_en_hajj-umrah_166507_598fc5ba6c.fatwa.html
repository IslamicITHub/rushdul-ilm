    Title: Performing Hajj with borrowed Money

    Question: I am intending to perform Hajj with my wife In-shaAllah, though I am earning good Alhamdulillah, I am not able to save a bulk amount due to various commitments. I am planning to borrow money from my brother to perform hajj and will return him money in installments In-shaAllah. Is it OK to do this?

    Answer ID: 166507Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 285/226/B=03/1440)

     

    If it is interest free debt then there is no harm in taking debt from your brother.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
