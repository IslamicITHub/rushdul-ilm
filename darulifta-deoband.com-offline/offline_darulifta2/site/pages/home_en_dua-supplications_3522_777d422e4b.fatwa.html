    Question: As Salamualaikum Please provide me any Zikar for to get rid of my family problems, any zakir to make my parents understand me any Zikar to make my wife understand me, i am in lot of tension because of my parents and my wife, i dont want to hurt my parents and wife feelings, according to my parents my sasural(in laws) have performed black Magic so that my life become hell, i love my wife very much. i dont want to loose her, so please recommend me any zikar.

    Answer ID: 3522Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 401/356=D)

     

    Recite this dua for wife and children: اللھم اصلح لی فی ذریتی

    And pray for parents: رب ارحمھما کما ربیانی صغیراً

    While going to bed, recite Surah Kafiroon, Surah Ikhlas, Surah Falaq, Surah Naas and Ayatul Kursi with durood before and after and blow on your body.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
