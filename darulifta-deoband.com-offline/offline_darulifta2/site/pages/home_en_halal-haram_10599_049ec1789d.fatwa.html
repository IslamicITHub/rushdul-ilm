    Title: Question: 9353 I would like to bring to your attention to FATWA 9353. It is said that one should avoid renting the land to the banks, for the rent will be paid with the interest money. My question is a person working in a company, which is operating its business through loan obtained from bank, is the salary taken by the person from the company halaal or haraam?

    Question: Question: 9353 I would like to bring to your attention to FATWA 9353. It is said that one should avoid renting the land to the banks, for the rent will be paid with the interest money. My question is a person working in a company, which is operating its business through loan obtained from bank, is the salary taken by the person from the company halaal or haraam?

    Answer ID: 10599Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 322/293=D/1430)

     

    If any employed person discharges lawful duties, his salary and income will be lawful and halal. The ruling is same even if the company includes some money taken as loan from bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
