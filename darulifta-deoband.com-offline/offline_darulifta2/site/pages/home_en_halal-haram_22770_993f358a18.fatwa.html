We use that money for poor and needful people. Is it ok to take that money. Even if she does not take that money they will chrge them same and not gives direct concession to patients.     Answer ID: 22770Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 921/723/D=1431)

    You should spend that amount on this patient or any other poor patient, do not use it for yourself.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
