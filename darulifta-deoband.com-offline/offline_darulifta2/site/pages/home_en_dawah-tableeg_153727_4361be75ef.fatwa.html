    Title: Did the holy Prophet Muhammad (SAW) send any caravan for Muslims rectifications (islah) while the time of Nubuwwat period.

    Question: Did the holy Prophet Muhammad (SAW) send any caravan for Muslims rectifications (islah) while the time of Nubuwwat period.

    Answer ID: 153727Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1337/1225/SN=12/1438)

     

    It is proved from the books of Hadith and Seerat that the Holy Prophet Muhammad (may peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) used to send delegates in order to teach the Holy Quran and necessary Shariah commandments to those who were new to Islam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
