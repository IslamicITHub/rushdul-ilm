    Title: Was the Prophet (saws) more beautiful than Yusuf (as)?

    Question: What is the evidence that Rasulullah (sallallahu alaihi wa sallam) is more beautiful than Yusuf (alaihi salam)?

    Answer ID: 107Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 451=272/B) It is narrated in a Hadith, the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) himself said: أنا ملیح و أخي یوسف صبیح I am Maleeh (white with redness) and Prophet Yusuf was Sabeeh (white). One who has a white complexion with slight redness is called beautiful and handsome, while being only white is not considered so charming and beautiful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
