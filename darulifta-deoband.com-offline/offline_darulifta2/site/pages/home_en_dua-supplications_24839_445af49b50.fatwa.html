    Question ID: 24839Country: India

    Title: I have got huge loans taken by me for business purpose. The business is in loss and the loan is mounting.The bank is after me to pay the loans and I am unable to pay the same. Please advise me duas so that I could pay the loans and be in peace.

    Question: Dear Muftisab, I have got huge loans taken by me for business purpose. The business is in loss and the loan is mounting.The bank is after me to pay the loans and I am unable to pay the same. Please advise me duas so that I could pay the loans and be in peace. Thanking You, Regards, Irfan

    Answer ID: 24839Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1352/1352/M=1431)

     

    You should put the matter of your loss before the bank and request them for appropriate time limit. Also, recite the following dua in the morning and evening daily:

    اللهم اكفنى بحلالك عن حرامك و اغننى بفضلك عمن سواك .

    Also keep on reciting durood extensively. May Almighty Allah free you from the burden of debt!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
