man?My parents have tried all possible solutions till now and nothing has worked.They have spoken to his elders also but to no avail.He & his sister also always accuse my character and says am characterless and cheap though Allah knows well how pure Iam.When i read Quran he always creates loud voice by TV or singing purposely to distract me.He also dsnt like me reading Quran for long time.Now the thing is the environment in our house is very bad.I am unable to tolerate his mean talk anymore and his mental torture as am alone here and he dsnt allow me to talk to anyone.Please advise me how I go about taking divorce.Also I want to ask if I can live separate from him for some months and go back to India so that in that time we both can think calmly?Please advise suitably.

    Answer ID: 7644Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2012/1651=B/1429)

     

    You should contact any influential man in America who can convince your husband. It will be better if you find any way out for reconciliation. If he does not compromise, so at last you can seek divorce from him. If he does not give divorce to you, you should try to get khula. In case he is not prepared to give khula too, then you should take this matter to any Darul Qaza of your area. The members of the Darul Qaza will hear both the parties and try to settle the matter, if they fail to do so, then the Nikah will come to an end if they nullify the marriage. Thereafter, you can marry any other man after passing three menses as iddah.

     

    You can come back India for some days after taking permission from your husband. You both might find some time to deliberate the matter calmly during this period.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
