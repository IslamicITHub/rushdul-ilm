    Answer ID: 1064Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 860/807=B)

     

    Khula takes place from both sides i.e. the wife says to husband: we can no longer live together; therefore give me divorce in return of mahr (dower). And, the husband gives divorce saying: I divorce you. It is necessary that it takes place in single meeting. If wife demands Khula and husband denies it then there is no divorce.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
