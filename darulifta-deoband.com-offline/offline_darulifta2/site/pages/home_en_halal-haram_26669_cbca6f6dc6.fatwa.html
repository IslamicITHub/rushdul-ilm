    Question ID: 26669Country: Pakistan

    Title: I have stopped listening to music alhamdulillah so i just wanted to know that sometime i sing song when sitting. is it ok to sing romantic or children song(nazam) without music and when no non-mehram is around. just for little fun?

    Question: AOA i have stopped listening to music alhamdulillah so i just wanted to know that sometime i sing song when sitting. is it ok to sing romantic or children song(nazam) without music and when no non-mehram is around. just for little fun?

    Answer ID: 26669Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2525/1568/H=1431)

    It is not allowed to sing the romantic and children songs for entertainment. You should intone the poems of Hadhrat Khawaja Azizul Hasan, Majzoob (رحمة الله عليه) specially the one named Muraqaba-e-Maut in privacy. In-sha-Allah, it shall fetch many benefits.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
