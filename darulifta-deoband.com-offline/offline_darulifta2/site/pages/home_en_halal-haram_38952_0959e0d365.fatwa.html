    Question ID: 38952Country: India

    Title: My name is Abdullah. I am currently staying in Dubai in search of job

    Question: My name is Abdullah. I am currently staying in Dubai in search of job. I converted to Islam 4 years back. 3 days ago I went to my uncle's home in Abu Dhabi. He gave me 500 Dirham. At that time one person came to his home in selling of credit card. From their discussion I heard that my uncle has taken loan from some bank and it will be closed within 3 months. But I did not hear about any bank interest on their speech. My uncle is a non-Muslim. He is working in an Oil and Gas company. My question is that can I use that money? If it is not allowed to use, can I use that money when I am not having any money?

    Answer ID: 38952Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1088/911/B=1433) Five hundred Dirham which your non-Muslim uncle gave you are purely halal and lawful without any doubt. It is not interest or haram money. You can use that money.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
