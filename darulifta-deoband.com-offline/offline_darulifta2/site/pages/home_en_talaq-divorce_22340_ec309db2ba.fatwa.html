    Title: if one divorce his wife three time befor vitness and after one year he again want to get marry with his x-wife so is it possible according to sharah or not?

    Question: if one divorce his wife three time befor vitness and after one year he again want to get marry with his x-wife so is it possible according to sharah or not?

    Answer ID: 22340Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 761/587/D=1431)

    It is unlawful to marry a woman whom one has divorced thrice, neither within iddah period nor after it, until legal halalah does not take place. The method of halalah is that a woman divorced with three talaqs will marry any other man after completing her iddah period and that man after having intercourse divorces her or dies, only then after completing her iddah period it will be lawful and possible for this woman to marry her first husband.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
