    Answer ID: 13341Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 904/843=B/1430)

     

    According to Hanafi Fiqh, it is makrooh to hold second jama?ah in any part of such mosque. People should offer their salah individually.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
