    Title: I have a Mango plantation 40 km from my place, I am working in a company and my father states with me and is too old to look after the plantation. This being the case is it permissible for me to give it on lease for 3-5years?

    Question: AS SALAMUALAIKUM!!! I have a Mango plantation 40 km from my place, I am working in a company and my father states with me and is too old to look after the plantation. This being the case is it permissible for me to give it on lease for 3-5years?

    Answer ID: 24443Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1212/1212/M=1431)

    What will be the way of giving the mango plantation on lease for three or five years? The lawful condition of giving the land of plantation on lease may be like this that first the trees are sold to the contractor, and if the trees have already bore fruits, give the garden on sharecropping and then give the land of garden to the same person on rent and make your share of the garden fruits halal for the contractor.
    فلو كان للموجر اى رب الأرض فالحيلة أن يبيع الزرع منه بثمن معلوم و يتقابضا ثم يوجره الأرض كما في الخلاصة عن الأصل و كذا لو ساقاه عليه قبل الاجاره اهـ (شامى).
    For detail, see Ahsanul Fatawa vol. 7, Kitabul Ijarah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
