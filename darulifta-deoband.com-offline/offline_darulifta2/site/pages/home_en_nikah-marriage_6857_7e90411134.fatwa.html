    Question: Ass-salam-Alaikum Janab, ek muslim ladki ek christian ladke se court marriage kerti hai, aur un dono se aulad bhi paida hoti hai,uske 2 saal baad ladka bhi islam me dakhil ho jata hai, to ab iske sudhaar ke liya aur tauba ke liye keya kiya jaye. yesa mere ek dost ke saath hua hai. meharbani ker ke jald se jald bataye jis se gunaho se tauba ker sake.

    Answer ID: 6857Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 938/858=L/1429)

     

    The girl should repent and seek forgiveness from what she has committed, for she had unlawfully been with that Christian boy for so long. Whenever she had intercourse with him it was adultery and the children born to them were illegitimate. Now when the boy has accepted Islam, if the guardians of the girl want to get her married to him, then they can do this but the two must observe purdah (veil), they should live separately and avoid seeing each other until they marry.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
