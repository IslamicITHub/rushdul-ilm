    Question: In continuation with my Q # 20145, about your querry. I am staying in Saudia along with my family.I used to visit India on vacation for approx 1 month every year along with my family. 1, Staying at Aligarh (my sasural). Case 1: less than 15 days. Case 2: more than 15 days. 2,Staying Bahraich(my native). Case 1: Less than 15 days. Case 2: more than 15 days. My baligh son born in Aligarh. Considering above conditions please reply where (Aligarh/Bahraich) my wife and son pray Qsr.

    Answer ID: 20832Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 533/399/D=1431)

     

    (1) When you, your wife and your adult children intend to stay more than fifteen days whether at Bahraich or Aligarh, all of you have to offer full salah.

    (2) You intend to stay less than fifteen days at Bahraich whereas you have not cancelled the nativity of Bahraich completely, you would offer complete salah. In the same way your wife and adult children in case of staying for less than fifteen days will perform full salah. And when you, your wife and children will intend to stay in Aligarh for less than fifteen days, all of you will do qasr.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
