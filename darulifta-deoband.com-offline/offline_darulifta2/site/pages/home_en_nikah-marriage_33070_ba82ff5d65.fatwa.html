    Title: (1) What Kinds of girl should a Muslim marry?

    Question: (1) What Kinds of girl should a Muslim marry? (2) How nikah is accepted by Allah? (3) Why walimah is important for Muslim?

    Answer ID: 33070Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1209/1209/M=1432) (1) It is mentioned in hadith: تزوجوا الودود الولود . فاظفر بذات الدين . It means that one should marry religious girl that loves too much to her husband and begets more children (it can be assessed by the women of her family) and is obedient to her husband. (2) Nikah should be done in line with the way of Prophet and with the intention of practicing sunnah. (3) Walimah is sunnah. The holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) said: أولم ولو بشاة . The practice of sunnah is very important for a Muslim.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
