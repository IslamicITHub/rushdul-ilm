    Title: My wife owns the house we live in. She wants to will the house to me. We have two sons but she wants me to inherit the entire property after her death. Can she make a will and do so?

    Question: My wife owns the house we live in. She wants to will the house to me. We have two sons but she wants me to inherit the entire property after her death. Can she make a will and do so?

    Answer ID: 10526Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 194/178=B/1429)

     

    The will of wife for husband is invalid; since husband is one of her heirs. It is advisable to wife that she should gift the house to the two sons and give it in their possession.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
