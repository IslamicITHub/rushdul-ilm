    Title: My name is Faiyaz Pathan. Is my name Faiyaz right or wrong

    Question: My name is Faiyaz Pathan. Is my name Faiyaz right or wrong?

    Answer ID: 36718Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 338/338/M=1433) Faiyaz name is correct. It means: generous, beneficent.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
