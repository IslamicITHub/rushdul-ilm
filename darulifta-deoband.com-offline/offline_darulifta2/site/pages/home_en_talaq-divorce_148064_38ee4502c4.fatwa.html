    Title: Divorce related problems

    Question: I want to know that in anger if husband repeats talaq 3 at a time, then divorce happens or not? Later if husband regrets and said may be I have given in anger and also said that even I don't remember that I repeated talaq word 3 times. Then in above condition talaq happens or not?

    Answer ID: 148064Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 505/485/M=4/1438)

    It is bida’h and unlawful to give three talaqs at the same time. Someone gave three talaqs during the lifetime of the Holy Prophet Muhammad (may peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), so he became angry but enforced all of them. Thus though it is against the sunnah to give three talaqs at a time but if the husband does such act that he gives three talaqs together then all the three talaqs shall occur and the wife shall become mughallazah bainah and thus would be completely haram for the husband. Talaq also takes place if given in the state of anger. Usually, one gives talaq in the state of anger. It is useless to repent after giving talaq and come with the excuse of anger. If talaq is proved by the confession of husband or by Shariah testimony then it cannot be denied.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
