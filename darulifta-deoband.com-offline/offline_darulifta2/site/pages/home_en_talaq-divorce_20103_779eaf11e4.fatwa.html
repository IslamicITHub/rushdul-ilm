    Title: Salam,My husband gave me three divorces in email. I was pregnant at that time but my husband was not aware of that fact. So did that talaq happened?

    Question: Salam,My husband gave me three divorces in email. I was pregnant at that time but my husband was not aware of that fact. So did that talaq happened?

    Answer ID: 20103Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 448/355/H=1431)

     

    All the three talaqs took place and you became haram for your husband whether your husband was aware of the pregnancy or not, in both the situations the ruling is same. Talaq given by letter or email takes place as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
