    Title: On the 26th day of Ramadan, at around 3:45 am I had a dream that I am getting in my house and I see a skinless python is lying dead and beside it my fiancée is standing

    Question: On the 26th day of Ramadan, at around 3:45 am I had a dream that I am getting in my house and I see a skinless python is lying dead and beside it my fiancée is standing. I thought that he had killed that python but later in fraction of seconds the python is invisible and there lies my fiancée unconscious. I request you please guide me regarding this night of mine.

    Answer ID: 35962Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 262/148/H=1433) The meaning of your dream is that both of you lagged behind in good deeds and tended towards bad practice. You should do tauba and istighfar and reform yourself.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
