    Question ID: 38619Country: Pakistan

    Title: Please interpret the following dream for me. Rasulullah (SAW) is returning after being exiled from Madinah

    Question: Please interpret the following dream for me. Rasulullah (SAW) is returning after being exiled from Madinah. I reach the boundary of Makkah and announce that he Rasulullah (SAW) is returning. Then I reach the Haram and announce the same again. Finally, I reach at kaba's door and open a package that he (SAW) gave me. It is a copy of the holy Quran. Then it is time for Maghrib prayer, so I stand by the open door of the kabah and start the azan in a loud voice. The reverberation from inside the kabah overwhelms me, and I proceed to read the azan in a low voice. This azan is heard in all of Makkah.

    Answer ID: 38619Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 867/531/H=1433) Ma-shaAllah, it is very nice dream. The meaning is that if you are attached to Ulama and any perfect Shaikh as well as Tablighi Jamah as per the rules and work with sincerity then you shall be assigned religious service in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
