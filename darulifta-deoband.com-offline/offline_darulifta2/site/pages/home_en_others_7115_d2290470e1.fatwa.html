Q2-How many Sahabah were there some say there were more then 124000,what is the exact number with refrence? Q3-Did Prophet alayhis salaam know the names of all the Sahabah?

    Answer ID: 7115Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1556/1333=B/1429)

     

    There is no wrong if it said in a state of extreme love towards the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم).      

    (2) According to most of the historians, the number is 124000.

    (3) We do not know this.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
