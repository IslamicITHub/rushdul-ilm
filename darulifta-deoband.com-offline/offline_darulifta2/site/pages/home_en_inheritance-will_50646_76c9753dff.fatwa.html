    Title: inheritence

    Question: A woman dies leaving behind three sons and one daughter [3 sons & 1 daughter]. How her property shall be divided? A person dies leaving behind his wife, three sons and one daughter [1 wife, 3 sons, 1 daughter]. How shall his property be divided?

    Answer ID: 50646Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 435/B-88/TB=03/1435) In case the questions are true, all the property shall be divided into 7 shares after paying due rights proceeding inheritance. Out of the total each son shall get 2-2 shares and the daughter shall get 1 share. The division of the property is as follows: Details: Sons: 3x2=6 Daughter: 1x1=1 Total: 7

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
