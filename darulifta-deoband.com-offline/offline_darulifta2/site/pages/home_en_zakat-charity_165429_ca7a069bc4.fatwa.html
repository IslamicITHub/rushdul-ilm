    Title: Loan Amount

    Question: One of my Office colleague - Non Muslim (Hindu) asked for a loan of Rs. 20k and informed that he will return it in 1 month. It's close to 2 years now and he is yet to pay the amount in spite of numerous follow-ups. He always assures to give after sometime. He is having very huge loan amount at present owing to medical treatments of his family members. I have also lost hope of receiving money. Can I consider this amount in Zakat and inform him about it, while giving out Zakat amount for present year.

    Answer ID: 165429Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 58/56/M=01/1440)

     

    Firstly, he is not a Muslim and zakah cannot be given to Non-Muslim. Secondly, you have given him the amount as a loan and thus you cannot make zakah intention for the same. So, it is not lawful for you to deduct it as the zakah of current year.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
