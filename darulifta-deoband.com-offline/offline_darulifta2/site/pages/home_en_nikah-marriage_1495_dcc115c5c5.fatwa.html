    Answer ID: 1495Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 927/866=B)

     

    A Muslim can not marry a Shia girl. According to the beliefs of Isna Ashari Shias, as written in their books, they are out of folk of Islam; therefore Shia and Sunni can not marry each other. It is surprising how you performed the nikah and how your parents consented for the same.

     

    As far as the credibility of certificate is concerned, you should contact the embassy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
