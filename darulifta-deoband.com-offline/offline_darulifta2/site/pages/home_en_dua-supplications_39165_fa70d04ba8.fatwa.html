    Title: I am facing a lot of back pain and also neck pain running towards my fingers which is known as SLIP DISC

    Question: I am facing a lot of back pain and also neck pain running towards my fingers which is known as SLIP DISC. kindly give me some dua so that I regain a good health and strong body because of this my life is getting very badly affected.

    Answer ID: 39165Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1227/1021/B=1433) Recite the following dua seven times turning towards Allah wholeheartedly and sincerely and with the belief that you shall get cured with this dua, and blow on your body: أسأل اللہ العظیم رب العرش العظیم أن یشفینی

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
