    Title: kaya saiyed ko zakat de sakte hain Mufti mehdi hasan (R.A) & mufti Saeed ka kya khayal hai

    Question: kaya saiyed ko zakat de sakte hain Mufti mehdi hasan (R.A) & mufti Saeed ka kya khayal hai

    Answer ID: 3464Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 231/231=L)

     

    It is unlawful to give zakah to a syed (member of the Prophet?s family صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم): ولا الی بنی ھاشم (الدر المختار مع الشامی: 3/299)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
