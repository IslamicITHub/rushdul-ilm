    Title: I am studying MBBS in my family my father is also a BAMS doctor but he could not pray 5 times namaz because of his practice

    Question: I am studying MBBS in my family my father is also a BAMS doctor but he could not pray 5 times namaz because of his practice. And when I follow Islamic rules purely he gets angry on me. Always I could not obey him so he said always you are disobeying me. Please tell me what can I do?

    Answer ID: 35562Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2051/D=457-File D=1432) If one's father orders against the fardh and wajib commands of the Almighty Allah or asks to commit any haram and sinful act then it is not lawful to obey father in this case. It is fardh to obey Almighty Allah. لا طاعة لمخلوق فى معصية الخالق . But you should behave him politely and in good manner. Do not disrespect him. Never show harsh attitude or use tough words. If you find time to say your words then speak him directly with respect or with the mediation of your mother. Also keep on supplicating for him. رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيراً

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
