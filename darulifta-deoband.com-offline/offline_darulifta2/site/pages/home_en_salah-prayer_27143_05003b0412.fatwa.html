    Question ID: 27143Country: Pakistan

    Title: I wanted to know that are we allowed to perform nafl salah in a moving car where the direction of qibla cannot be determined?

    Question: Assalamoalaykum, I wanted to know that are we allowed to perform nafl salah in a moving car where the direction of qibla cannot be determined? JazakAllah khair

    Answer ID: 27143Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2101/B=1698/tb=1431)

    Facing the Qiblah direction is one of the conditions of salah. If it is missing, salah will not be right. However, nafl salah can be performed in moving car.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
