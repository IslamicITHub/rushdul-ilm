    Title: About the book Nasqsh-e-Sulaimani

    Question: (1) What is Naqsh-e-Sulaimani (AS)? (2) And what are the numbers which are used in Tawiz? Is it halal or haram?

    Answer ID: 156770Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 267/217/SD=3/1439)

     

    (1) Naqsh-e-Sulaimani is a book of Tawiz. Hakim al-Ummah Hadhrat Maulana Ashraf Ali Thanavi (may Allah have mercy upon him) has forbidden to read it. (Bahishti Zewar, vol. 10, p. 614)

    (2) It is lawful to write numbers in the tawiz whose meaning is known as it is an understood. (Ahsanul Fatawa, vol. 8, p. 257, Mutafarriqat al-Hazar al-Ibahat, pub. Zakariah, Deoband)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
