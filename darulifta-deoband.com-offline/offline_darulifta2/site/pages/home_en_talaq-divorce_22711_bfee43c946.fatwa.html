    Answer ID: 22711Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1172/896/H=1431)

    (1) The nikah is still valid.
    (2) The woman should file her case in the local sharai punchayat. If they cancel the nikah after due investigations as per Islamic Sharia, then she can do the second nikah after completing her iddah period. The details of sharai punchayat or Jama'atul Muslimin is mentioned in Al-Hilah al-Najiza Lil Halilah al-Ajiza.
    (3 & 4) Same as the answer no 2.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
