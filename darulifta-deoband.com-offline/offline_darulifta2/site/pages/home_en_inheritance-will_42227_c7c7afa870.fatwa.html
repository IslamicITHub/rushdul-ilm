    Title: Division of money of uncle

    Question: I am Zubair from Farrukhabad. My uncle died this year, he was unmarried, and he was in railways. How his money will be divided between his two brothers and two sisters?

    Answer ID: 42227Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2135/1698/B=1433) If there is no other heir except of two brothers and two sisters then the money of the deceased brother shall be divided into six shares. Out of the six shares, 2-2 shares shall go to each brother while 1-1 share shall go to each sister. The division shall be as per the Quranic verse: للذكر مثل الانثيين The detail of division is as follows: Details: Brothers: 2x2= 4 Sisters: 2x1= 2 Total: 6 shares

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
