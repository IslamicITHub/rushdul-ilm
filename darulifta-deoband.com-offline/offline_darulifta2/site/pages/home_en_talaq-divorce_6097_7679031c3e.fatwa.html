    Answer ID: 6097Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1669/1289=H/1429)

     

    You should settle the financial dealings with your wife, give her one Talaq Baain and pay her full mahr. You had better look for any match for yourself and get married.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
