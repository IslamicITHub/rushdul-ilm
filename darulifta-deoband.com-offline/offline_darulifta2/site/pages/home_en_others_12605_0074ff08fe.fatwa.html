    Answer ID: 12605Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1150/888=H/1430)

     

    The nafs and Satan are trying to overcome, whereas getting stuck in the net of the world, following the carnal desires and pleasing oneself by the whispering of the Satan is like death from him. The dream serves as warning that one should refrain from such bad thoughts.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
