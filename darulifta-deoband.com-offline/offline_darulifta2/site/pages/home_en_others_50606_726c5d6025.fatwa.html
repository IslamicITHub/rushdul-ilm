    Title: The Prophets name in my dream.

    Question: Few weeks back I had a dream I did not remember exactly but it happened like this. "I was in masjid with jamat and one guy was reciting Fazail-E-Amal book and we were listening to him. He asked me the questions (I do not remember what the questions are) but I answered him couple of times the Four Prophets name. They are: 1. Mohammed Nabi (PBUH). 2. Ibrahim (PBUH). 3. Musa (PBUH) (this one I don’t remember exactly). 4. Sulaiman (PBUH). He asked me again and I repeated this to him again. The Next Dream: I was in maktab madrasa and it is over. Me and other students came along with Ustad. We saw a bike and went near to it and chatting something. One dog was coming near us and suddenly it bites my front private parts in front of everyone. The Next dream: I don’t remember the scenario but a dog bites my private parts as in the earlier dream. Can you please tell me the meanings of these dreams?

    Answer ID: 50606Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 422/369/H=03/1435) (1) In-shaAllah, you shall get benefit from Fazail Amal. It is nice dream, ma-shaAllah. Also study the book extensively and read it out for others with sincerity especially the second part such as Fazail Sadaqat. (2) Someone shall behave painfully and if you forgive him then you shall not get any harm, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
