    Title: sunnah prayer

    Question: Is it mandatory to offer sunnah salah at home or if it is not, then is it acceptable in Islam to offer sunnah salah at home?

    Answer ID: 40796Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1301/1301/M=1433) It is not necessary and wajib to offer sunnah salah (which are offered before or after the fardh salah) at home, however it is better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
