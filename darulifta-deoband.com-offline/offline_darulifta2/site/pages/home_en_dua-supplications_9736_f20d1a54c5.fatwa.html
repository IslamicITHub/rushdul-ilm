    Question: I have recently been married, Alhamdulillah. My husband and I and my mother-in-law wishes for me to have twin sons soon InshaAllah. I am not yet pregnant, but we pray that Allah grant us twin boys InshaAllah when I do become pregnant.I would like to know if there are any special duas/wazifas I can do for having twin boy babies soon InshaAllah. I kindly seek your advice.

    Answer ID: 9736Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2598/2083=H/1429)

     

    Recite the below Dua 100 times after every obligatory salah:

    ھب لنا من ازواجنا و ذریاتنا قرۃ اعین واجعلنا للمتقین اماما (الفرقان 74)

    Leave the thought of twin etc and prefer what is granted by Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
