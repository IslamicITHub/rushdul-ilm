    Question ID: 46348Country: India

    Title: Dawah Tabligh

    Question: Is it fardh for a person to go to tabligh jamah? If a person is doing his fardh regularly following sunnah and giving dawah to his friends then what is the necessary to go to jamah. I am interested in going to jamah, but my parents asking these questions. Please reply briefly.

    Answer ID: 46348Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1086/848/B=1434) A person who regularly offers fardh, wajib and sunnah acts and invites his friends towards religion he is pious and shall go to Jannah, in-shaAllah. It is not fardh or wajib to go into jamah; rather it is a mustahab practice. If someone does not go to jamah he shall not be accountable for the same. However, by going in jamah one shall get the experience to work among the people, one develops self-denial. One develops the habit to offer salah regularly and to wake for tahajjud. Also one learns many things while attending the meeting of talim etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
