    Title: I am student how can i increase my memory and concentration in studies.i my sself forgetting my subject even exam time facing this more?

    Question: assalamu alaikum im student how can i increase my memory and concentration in studies.i my sself forgetting my subject even exam time facing this more?

    Answer ID: 24860Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1758/1286/H=1431)

    Offer salah regularly and shun sins. After each salah, recite the following verse thrice with durood 3-3 times before and after:
    قَالَ رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي . وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي . وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي . يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي . (سوره طه ركوع 2)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
