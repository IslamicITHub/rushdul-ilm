    Question ID: 51583Country: Bangladesh

    Title: To go 3/1 Chilla in Tablig Jamat is mendotory for me?

    Question: To go 3/1 chilla in tabligh jamat for rectification of myself and (/or) other Muslim or non-Muslim (men and women) is mandatory (fardh/mustahab) for me? Since, I am an Engineer.

    Answer ID: 51583Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 987/791/B=06/1435) In order to reform oneself or others one should go to jamah for one or three chilla. However, it is not fardh or wajib like other obligatory acts.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
