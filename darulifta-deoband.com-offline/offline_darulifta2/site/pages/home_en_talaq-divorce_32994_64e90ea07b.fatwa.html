    Question ID: 32994Country: Pakistan

    Title: triplw talaq

    Question: I want to asked a question. one day i ordered my wife not to done that work in future if u could do this then triple talaq will be vailed on you from my side.she could done that work after few months .now my question is that triple talaq or one talaq is vailed.at that time i m in normal condition not in anger. i am confused. pleaze reply to me i am very thankful to you.plz also tell me the concept of saudi darul ifta about triple talaq.

    Answer ID: 32994Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1076/667/L=1432) In the question mentioned above, if your wife committed the act on which you conditioned the talaq, then all the three talaqs took place on your wife and she became mughallazah bainah (irrevocable divorced) and haram for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
