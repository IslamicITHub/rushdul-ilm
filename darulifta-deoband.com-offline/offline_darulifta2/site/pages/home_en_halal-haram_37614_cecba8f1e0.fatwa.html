    Title: Is it right for Muslim woman to run a beauty parlour

    Question: Is it right for Muslim woman to run a beauty parlour?

    Answer ID: 37614Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 44/51/N=1433) The work in the name of adornment that is performed in a beauty parlour all or most of them are unlawful as per the Shariah. And it is unlawful to commit an unlawful job by oneself or get it done by someone else under one's patronage by employment and take compensation for it. Hence it is not allowable for a Muslim lady to run a beauty parlour.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
