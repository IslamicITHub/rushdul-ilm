    Question ID: 4619Country: India

    Title: My question is that recently one of our newphew died in car accident. And the mother of the newphew saw him in her dream without any clothes on his body and another relative saw in dream that one of his eye is missing. Kindly please explain me the meaning of this two dream. Thanks a lot.

    Question: Dear Mufti Sahab, Aaslam Alaikum My question is that recently one of our newphew died in car accident. And the mother of the newphew saw him in her dream without any clothes on his body and another relative saw in dream that one of his eye is missing. Kindly please explain me the meaning of this two dream. Thanks a lot.

    Answer ID: 4619Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 915/889=D/1429)

     

    You should do isal-e-sawab exclusively for him through kalimah and tilawah. All the family members should recite something and deliver its reward to him. Likewise, they can do it by charity and feeding the needy. The family members should leave sinful acts and adhere to good deeds, salah, zakah etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
