    Title: Assalamualaykum, Janab Mere 2 Saval he. Saval-1-Khana khate vakt Bich me Pani pina Sunnat he? Saval-2-Khana khate vakt ya Aakhir me Daya(right) Hath(hand) kharab hota he, to us Vakt Baye(left) Hath se Pani pi sakte he?

    Question: Assalamualaykum, Janab Mere 2 Saval he. Saval-1-Khana khate vakt Bich me Pani pina Sunnat he? Saval-2-Khana khate vakt ya Aakhir me Daya(right) Hath(hand) kharab hota he, to us Vakt Baye(left) Hath se Pani pi sakte he?

    Answer ID: 12058Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 867/647=H/1430)

     

    (1) We have not studied anywhere that it is Sunnah.

    (2) In this condition also, it is unlawful to drink water with left hand, rather one should clean right hand as much necessary and use it for drinking water.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
