    Title: I have lost a few items that belong to me and can not find them, i think they are somewere in my house but after looking for them thorougly i still have no luck. can you help me finding them by giving a dua or other ways for looking for the lost items.

    Question: salam, I have lost a few items that belong to me and can not find them, i think they are somewere in my house but after looking for them thorougly i still have no luck. can you help me finding them by giving a dua or other ways for looking for the lost items. jazakallah

    Answer ID: 6402Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 752/701=L/1429)

     

    Recite انا للہ وانا الیہ راجعون you shall get your lost thing, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
