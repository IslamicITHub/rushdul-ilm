    Answer ID: 3161Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 207/197=B)

     

    She is going to face any difficulty, but she will be safe.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
