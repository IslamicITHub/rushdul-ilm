    Title: In todays world we see that there are various ayat e quran,hadees,and naat are available in internet which is being downloaded and circulated.

    Question: In todays world we see that there are various ayat e quran,hadees,and naat are available in internet which is being downloaded and circulated. Is all these circulation is jayez in context of shariah.

    Answer ID: 31759Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1006/565/D=1432) It is allowable to download and propagate it with the intention of tabligh and preaching while maintaining its respect. But it is makrooh to do it for amusement and to adopt a way that causes disrespect.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
