    Question: AOA,Sir i am working in a multinational Bank in UAE.Now my question is that i am working in a Loan Dept.And v have islamic and non islamic loans as well but there is hardly and difference i can find in both the loean(Islamic & Conventional)expect the word intrest and Profit.And its mandatory for us to sell both the products equaly.So working for that dept is ok for me as i m very uncomfortable please help me.

    Answer ID: 10155Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 111/99 B/1429)

     

    In loan department, it is difficult to avoid interest-based transactions; therefore you should look for other lawful work.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
