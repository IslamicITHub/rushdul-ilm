    Title: Aslam o alaikum i wanted to confirm tht is thr any hadith regarding a student tht if student does nt avoids sins during his study period thn he wil b punished in 3 ways,one is tht he wil die in his study peroid before completing it. Or anythng similar to it like this

    Question: Aslam o alaikum i wanted to confirm tht is thr any hadith regarding a student tht if student does nt avoids sins during his study period thn he wil b punished in 3 ways,one is tht he wil die in his study peroid before completing it. Or anythng similar to it like this

    Answer ID: 8380Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2224/1716=H/1429)

     

    Yes, some scholars have narrated this Hadith that such a person who does not avoid sins as student and does not adopt piety he will face any of three things: (a) either he will die at a young age (b) or Allah will let him in any unpopulated place (c) or he shall be in service of any ruler. (Talimul Mutallim: 115)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
