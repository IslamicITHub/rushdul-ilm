    Question ID: 30227Country: India

    Title: Is it necessary to be in Wudhu whilst slaughtering an animal?Can some one who is Maazur(urinary problem)slaughter animals?Can he lead the prayers if there is no one else who can lead the prayers properly?

    Question: Assalamualaikum, Is it necessary to be in Wudhu whilst slaughtering an animal?Can some one who is Maazur(urinary problem)slaughter animals?Can he lead the prayers if there is no one else who can lead the prayers properly? Wassalam.

    Answer ID: 30227Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 465/256/D=1432)

    (1) It is not necessary.
    (2) Yes, he can do it.
    (3) He cannot become Imam if he is excused as per Shariah.
    قال فى المراقى فلا يصح اقتداء غيره اى اذا توضا مع العذر . ص: 288
    Yes, if the Imam and muqtadi all are excused as per the Shariah, then he can do imamah. (ibid) The definition of Shariah excused is written in Bahishti Zewar, it should be studied thoroughly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
