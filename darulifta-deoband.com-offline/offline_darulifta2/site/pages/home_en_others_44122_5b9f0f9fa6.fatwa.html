    Title: Person seemingly having reverted to Islam in dream

    Question: I have a sorrow in my life, which is that my little sister has left Islam. First she started replying to our salams which "hi" and then a while later I found out why. I regularly make dua that she should come back to Islam. On a couple of occasions I have recently seen her in dreams. On one occasion she replied to my salam with "wa aleikum asalaam" and on the second occasion I was surprised to see that she had decided to wear hijab. In both cases I had the feeling in the dream that she had come back to Islam. Do these dreams carry any meaning? Also please advise me in how to make dua for her to come back to Islam and also I request your personal duas for her to come back to Islam.

    Answer ID: 44122Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 361/360/N=1434) (1) Your dream indicates that your sister shall return to Islam. May Allah materialize this dream and bestow her true sense of Islam. (2) With the same intention, you should offer two rak’ah salah and supplicate Allah for her with the depth of your heart. You should also keep convincing her with politeness and wisdom. We also pray for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
