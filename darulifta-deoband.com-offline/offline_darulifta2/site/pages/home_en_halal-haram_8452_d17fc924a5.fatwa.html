    Title: I have completed hotel management & joined a five star hotel as a chef my job is of making pizzas some of the pizzas have pork(pig)as toppings hich i have to cut & cook is this job permissible pleasee reply at the earliest allah hafiz.

    Question: I have completed hotel management & joined a five star hotel as a chef my job is of making pizzas some of the pizzas have pork(pig)as toppings hich i have to cut & cook is this job permissible pleasee reply at the earliest allah hafiz.

    Answer ID: 8452Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2007/1593=H/1429)

     

    It is unlawful to slaughter pig and cook its pork. Also, this job is not allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
