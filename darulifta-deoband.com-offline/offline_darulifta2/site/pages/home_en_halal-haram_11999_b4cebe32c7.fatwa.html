    Answer ID: 11999Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 613/443=L/1430)

     

    If the health of woman is weak, she is unable to bear pregnancy or she fears unbearable pain in case of conception, or any expert religious Muslim physician prescribes it or she fears about the health of her already born child, then in such conditions she can use any contraceptive temporarily. If there are no such difficulties, using contraceptives temporarily also is against the wish of Shariah and a cause of Allah?s displeasure. So, in view of the excuses mentioned above, using contraceptives is makrooh (undesirable).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
