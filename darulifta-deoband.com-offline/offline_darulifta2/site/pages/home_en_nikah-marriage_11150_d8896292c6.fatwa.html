    Title: Assalamualaikum 1- A Muslim person was having a affair with a Non Muslim English woman, she became pregnant and had a child from him, the English woman has left him 2 years ago, she also took the child with her, the question is that does the child belong to him because he had the child without Nikah(getting Married)? 2-The Muslim person has repented from the sin of adultery,and cut ties with the Non Muslim English woman and the child after she left him but the question keeps coming in his mind that if the child who is staying with his Non Muslim mother also becomes a Kafir will he get punished in the here after for the wrong the child does?

    Question: Assalamualaikum 1- A Muslim person was having a affair with a Non Muslim English woman, she became pregnant and had a child from him, the English woman has left him 2 years ago, she also took the child with her, the question is that does the child belong to him because he had the child without Nikah(getting Married)? 2-The Muslim person has repented from the sin of adultery,and cut ties with the Non Muslim English woman and the child after she left him but the question keeps coming in his mind that if the child who is staying with his Non Muslim mother also becomes a Kafir will he get punished in the here after for the wrong the child does?

    Answer ID: 11150Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 292/88=TL/1430)

     

    (1) The lineage of the child will not be established with him.

    (2) If the person has repented to Allah sincerely and he feels regretful then he will not be responsible for the sins commited by the child:

    ولاتزر وازرۃ وزر أخری (الآیۃ)

    But, if it is possible for him, he should try to covert the child to Islam to protect him from the humility of the hereafter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
