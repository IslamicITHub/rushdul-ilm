    Answer ID: 15042Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1290/266=TL/1430)

     

    The abovementioned job is lawful and its income is also halal. There is no wrong in developing software only, but those who use it in unlawful ways will be sinful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
