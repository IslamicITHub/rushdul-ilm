    Title: If a lady does not follow her "iddat" after divorce. how is it?

    Question: If a lady does not follow her "iddat" after divorce. how is it?

    Answer ID: 5879Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 557/L=38/TL/1429)

     

    By not following ?iddah? if you mean that she does not observe the restrictions imposed by the Shariah (like going out of home, applying makeup etc) then she will be doing a great sin. However, if you mean that she gets married to any person during iddah then this act of her is unlawful and haram, their physical relation will be regarded adultery; such woman is asked first to complete her iddah and then get herself married.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
