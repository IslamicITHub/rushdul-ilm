    Title: I WILL MARRIED LAST 5 YEARS, BUT I HAVE NO KIDS. SO PLEASE GIVE ME SOME ADVICE & SEND ME SOME DUAS FOR IT. I BELIEVE ONLY IN ALLAH.

    Question: SALLAM.W I WILL MARRIED LAST 5 YEARS, BUT I HAVE NO KIDS. SO PLEASE GIVE ME SOME ADVICE & SEND ME SOME DUAS FOR IT. I BELIEVE ONLY IN ALLAH. KHUDA HAFIZ

    Answer ID: 25928Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2142/H=740/th=1431)

    Both of you (husband and wife) should recite the following verse after each salah and before going to bed 11 times with durood 11-11 times before and after in the state of wudhu:
    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
