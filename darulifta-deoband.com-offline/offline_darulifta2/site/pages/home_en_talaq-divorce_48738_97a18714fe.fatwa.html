    Question ID: 48738Country: Pakistan

    Title: Divorce on condition of staying at mother place

    Question: I tell my wife if you stay at you mother home at night 1 talaq will happen, however you can visit her in day time and come back. We are staying in Saudi Arabia planning to visit Pakistan. But if I change my mind and allow her to stay at her mother place, will one talaq take place?

    Answer ID: 48738Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1408/1408/M=11/1434) If you have not said the said conditional words regarding any specific night rather you said it unconditionally then whenever the wife will stay at her mother’s home in the night then as per the condition one talaq shall take place whether she stays there with your permission or without it. The ruling shall be similar in both the cases. Now you cannot terminate this condition by changing your intention. Talaq shall definitely take place when the condition is met. However, you will have the right to take your wife back within iddah period.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
