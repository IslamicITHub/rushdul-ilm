    Question ID: 36966Country: India

    Title: I am getting married next month on 4 Feb 2012, my intention is to follow the Sunnah way but my mother-in-law during the day of nikah wants to invite all the known people

    Question: I am getting married next month on 4 Feb 2012, my intention is to follow the Sunnah way but my mother-in-law during the day of nikah wants to invite all the known people, if she does this then there will be big party in the bride/girl's house, I wanted to avoid it. Please let me if it is permissible to have a party/feast in the girl's house? Also please let me know who are people whose presence is compulsory during nikah?

    Answer ID: 36966Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 433/D=76/File-D=1433) Get the book "Islami Shadi" from the market and follow the instructions of the book in setting your programme. It is mentioned in hadith that the most blessed nikah is one which carries less expenses. It is better if more people attend the ceremony of nikah, that is why it is sunnah to hold nikah in the mosque. اعلنوا هذا النكاح واجعلوه في المساجد Whereas gathering more people in the name of barat or invitations from girl side to many people creates causes many evils and problems. You should avoid it. Just two or three people should go with the boy and do nikah in mosque and get the bride home. And the next day (after rukhsati up to three days) the boy should arrange walimah whichshould have no pomp and show. It is enough if you avoid rituals and unfair things.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
