    Question: salam-alayakum My husband who is working the distribution work.sohe ahs to go any where in town.so he is peerforming namaj behind any imam and any masjid. sunni people r saying that dont perform namj in jamati masjid and behind jamati imam.go in sunni masjid. He cant go specially sunni masjid. so what answer v can give them so they will stop ti say like thgis . jazakallah

    Answer ID: 23681Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1309/1043/B=1431)

    When the salah is right in the mosque of jamati and behind jamati Imam, it is obstinacy of sunni people to stop it and of create dispute and division in Ummah which is abhorred in Shariah. It is mentioned in hadith:
    صلوا خلف كل بر و فاجر ، أو كما قال عليه الصلاة والسلام

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
