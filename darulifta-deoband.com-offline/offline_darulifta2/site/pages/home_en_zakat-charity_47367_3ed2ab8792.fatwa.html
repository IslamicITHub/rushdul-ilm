    Title: zakat

    Question: If a Sayyid hides himself and accepts zakah, is the zakah paid or I have to pay it again similarly if a gair mustahiq shows himself as mustahiq and accepts zakah is it paid or I have to repay it.

    Answer ID: 47367Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1154/883/D=10/1434) (1) If you have given him after you considered him eligible for zakah then your zakah was paid and the person shall be sinful who has deceived. But if you were already doubtful whether he was eligible for zakah or not or you were not sure about him then in such case it is not lawful to give zakah. Zakah shall not be paid till you are sure that the person is eligible for zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
