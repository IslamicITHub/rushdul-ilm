    Answer ID: 8818Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1425/1189=L/1429)

     

    Make her understand with wisdom and politeness. Do not go away from her for a long time. Give her books for study like Tuhfa-e-Dulhan, Bahishti Zewar, Talimuddin etc so that she is aware of your rights and behave you well. Negligence from religion and lacking knowledge of rights pertaining spouse causes dispute and quarrel between husband and wife. Strive to get her obedient and religious.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
