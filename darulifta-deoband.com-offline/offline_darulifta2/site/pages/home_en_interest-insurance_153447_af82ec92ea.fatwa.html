    Title: Usage of interest money (riba) from savings bank account

    Question: I have read many fatwas regarding interest money generated from savings bank account. And even I know it is prohibited in Islam and haram. But after reading your fatwas I came to know that this money can be used to pay our taxes to Govt. My Question is 1. How much amount of interest money can be used (like 10000 or more or is there any limit)? 2. What type of charity can be done (Is that charity to believers or non believers)? 3. Where else interest money can be used?

    Answer ID: 153447Posted on: Jul 29, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1500/1482/B=12/1438) 

    (1) Any amount of interest, whether it is ten thousand or more, may be paid towards income tax; thus, the haram money was returned to the place from where it originated.

    (2) Those who are extremely poor, destitute, distressed, indebted, or victims of calamity, whether Muslim or non-Muslim, may be given interest money without the intention of reward. One must not give it with the intention of charity, but rather with the sole intent of purging unlawful wealth from one’s possession.

    (3) Interest money may also be used to pay any non-Islamic tax that has been imposed upon you. However, it can not be used for constructing roads, public lavatories, or drainage.

    Note: The ruling is correct on the condition that the interest in question was received from a government-owned bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
