    Title: Is it permissible to view animate pictures on Internet computer?

    Question: Assalaamualaykum Q) Is it permissible to view animate pictures on Internet computer?

    Answer ID: 7322Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 852/852=M/1429)

     

    It is unlawful to see the pictures intentionally.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
