    Title: I was with my daughter, making tawaaf of the ka'bah and was able to see the Hajra-e-aswad several times.

    Question: I was with my daughter, making tawaaf of the ka'bah and was able to see the Hajra-e-aswad several times. I even was reciting the Hadeeth in the dream that the stone came from Jannah and it was white and became black due to the sins of the people. My husband and my son were not with us.The mataaf was empty-no people at all. I was telling my friend that we must perform 'Umrah but she told me we'll do it the next day. Then, I saw myself inside the ka'bah and I performed two Rakaats of Nafl salaah in it. When it was time to come out, I called my friend, but my voice came out a bit loud and it was unintentional. In the dream itself, I realised it was not right to speak loud in the masjid.

    Answer ID: 31730Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 991/D=147/K=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is blessed dream. In-sha-Allah, you shall be blessed to visit Haram Sharif. You should do good deeds and try to fulfil the rights of Allah and His slaves, and avoid sins specially from going about unveiled in public and from the affairs of immodesty. Read durood and zikr extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
