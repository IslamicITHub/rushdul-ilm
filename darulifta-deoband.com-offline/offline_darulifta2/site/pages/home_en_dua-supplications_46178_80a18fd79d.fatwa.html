    Title: Dua for fulfillment of every desire

    Question: Is there a dua or a way how Allah (swt) fulfils every need or desire?

    Answer ID: 46178Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1212/1003/H=10/1434) You should hold your iman and good deeds firmly and save yourself from each sin and fulfil the rights of Allah and of slaves. Save your tongue and heart from every makrooh act and observe taqwah and taharah, in-shaAllah your iman shall be intact till your death. You will be successful in the hereafter and you will get entry in Jannah and there your each and every wish shall be fulfilled.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
