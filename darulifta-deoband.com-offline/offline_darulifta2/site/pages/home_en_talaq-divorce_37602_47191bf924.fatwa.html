    Title: I have a question regarding ruju after khula/talaq in relation to my friend's marr

    Question: I have a question regarding ruju after khula/talaq in relation to my friend's marriage. Actually boy was not having any intention to give her talaq thinking that it is a big sin but girl's family members asked for talaq thinking that boy is not suitable/fit for his wife. In talaqnama some text was written which has meaning that due to dissimilar nature of both and despite convincing each other finally it is not possible to live together for both. They have returned all things of each other and boy has given talaq to the girl. And after talaq they can marry wherever they want and not having any pending claims for further action. After 5-6 months (iddah passed) of talaq, they knew that all this was due to attack of sifli ilm and started to morn for what they have done in past. Weather it is possible to get back to married life again after suitable treatment of that sifli ilm. Whether it was khula/single talaq or 3 talaqs have taken place. Please explain in details.

    Answer ID: 37602Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 453/381/B=1433) In the question mentioned above, it seems that the boy has divorced with his intention and choice in his full sense and understanding. After divorce the boy authorized her to marry where she wishes and in future they do not have any pending claims for further action. Hence, talaq took place without any doubt. If he gave one talaq then one took place and if he gave three then three talaqs took place. After 5 or 6 months it shall not be taken into consideration to say that this talaq was due to sifli amal. Whoever told that it is a sifli amal he is not prescient; his words are not definite and final. Hence it is not lawful to follow him as per the Shariah. It is not a khula, rather it is a talaq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
