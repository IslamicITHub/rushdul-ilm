    Title: I am paying heavy deposit to the owner of a house refundable after three years without any interest agaist which he is giving me his property (house/shop) which I want to rent to third person. After 3 years or as per contract when owner return my deposit, I will release his property. In this case owner is getting deposit for said period which is interest free. I am getting passasion of property for which I am getting rent from third party. This is called "Sub Leasing". Kindly let me know what is Shariyah's ruling about Sub Lease.

    Question: I am paying heavy deposit to the owner of a house refundable after three years without any interest agaist which he is giving me his property (house/shop) which I want to rent to third person. After 3 years or as per contract when owner return my deposit, I will release his property. In this case owner is getting deposit for said period which is interest free. I am getting passasion of property for which I am getting rent from third party. This is called "Sub Leasing". Kindly let me know what is Shariyah's ruling about Sub Lease.

    Answer ID: 20454Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 476/394/B=1431)

     

    If you are taking that shop or home on rent, in this case you cannot give it to others on rent. You are not authorized for it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
