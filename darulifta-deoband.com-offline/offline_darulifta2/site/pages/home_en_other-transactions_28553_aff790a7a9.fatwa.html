    Question ID: 28553Country: India

    Title: Once I had broken a flask in chemistry laboratory. I was asked to give Rs. 50 but I didnot pay. Now when I remembered the event I gave Rs.50. Please guide me what shall I do still I feel guilty.

    Question: Assalaamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullaahi wa Barakatuhu! Respected pious scholar,once I had broken a flask in chemistry laboratory. I was asked to give Rs. 50 but I didnot pay. Now when I remembered the event I gave Rs.50. Please guide me what shall I do still I feel guilty.

    Answer ID: 28553Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 66/52/L=1432)

    If you have paid that amount though at a later time, then too you paid what was due on you. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
