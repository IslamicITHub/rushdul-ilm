    Title: Dua for job

    Question: Asslamu Alekum Mufti -e - Ikram Shahab , My Name is Suhail Rashid, I am staying in Bangalore location I lose my job last 5 month . i am very worried, daily last 4 month alhumduliallah i m reading suree Waqia after magrib namaz. second thing when ever i go for interview always i did not clear any interview Can you please tell me any wazifa so that i can get a job also clear the interview Please pray for me i m very worried last 5 months. Regards, Suhail Rashid 9740719620

    Answer ID: 155300Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 91/59/SN=2/1439)

     

    Read the following dua extensively:

    اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ .

    Also read Surah Fatiha with Bismillah 41 times between the Fajr and Fajr sunnah daily with durood 11-11 times before and after. Also attend the interview with full preparation. In-shaAllah, you shall be successful and the door of provision shall open for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
