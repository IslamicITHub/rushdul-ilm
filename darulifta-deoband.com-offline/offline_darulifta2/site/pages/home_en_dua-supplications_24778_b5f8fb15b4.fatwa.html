    Title: I am searching for a job but i am not getting and i also i have commited Gunah-e-kabira(zina). what is the dua to apologise(maafi) kaise mangna. thats y i am not getting job or else.

    Question: I am searching for a job but i am not getting and i also i have commited Gunah-e-kabira(zina). what is the dua to apologise(maafi) kaise mangna. thats y i am not getting job or else.

    Answer ID: 24778Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1299/1299/M=1431)

    Offer two rakat salat al-taubah, and ask for forgiveness from Allah earnestly and make firm intention not to repeat the sin in future. Offer five times salah regularly and supplicate Almighty Allah after each salah for any lawful job. Recite Ayatul Kursi and Surah Fatiha 3-3 times in the evening and morning along with durood 100 times.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
