    Title: pls tell me a tasbi for safe-gaurding health , wealth and family from "balaiyat".

    Question: pls tell me a tasbi for safe-gaurding health , wealth and family from "balaiyat".

    Answer ID: 11730Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 497/497=M/1430)

     

    Offer the five time salah regularly and make charity in order to avoid troubles and difficulties. Also, keep on praying:

    اللھم انی اعوذبک من شر عبادک، حسبنا اللہ و نعم الوکیل

    For prosperity and barakah, recite ya Mu?izz (یا معز) ya Wahhab (یا وھاب) 1100 times with durood seven times before and after. Use hygienic food for good health and pray to Allah as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
