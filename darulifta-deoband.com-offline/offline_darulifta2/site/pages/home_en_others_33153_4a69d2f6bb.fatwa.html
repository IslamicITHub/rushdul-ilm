    Title: According to our Islam is their any belief to select the direction of entrance door while making home? If yes,

    Question: According to our Islam is their any belief to select the direction of entrance door while making home? If yes, then which direction is good and which is not good for the family and why? Please give me the reply in detail.

    Answer ID: 33153Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1187/988/B=1432) There is no Shariah ruling regarding it. You can select any direction for your entrance door you wish. However, you should note that the door should be fixed in a direction where there is no unveiling.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
