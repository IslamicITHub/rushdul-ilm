    Title: swallowing a drop of milk or semen?

    Question: Due to excitement during sohbat what will happen if one swallows a drop of milk or semen?

    Answer ID: 32277Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 906/573/L=1432) It is haram to swallow the drop of milk or semen while having intercourse. If someone happens to do so, he should do taubah and istighfar. However, the nikah will not be affected due to it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
