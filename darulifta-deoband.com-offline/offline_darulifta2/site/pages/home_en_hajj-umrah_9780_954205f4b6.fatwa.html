    Question: Dear Mufti Saheb, Assalamu alaikum, [1] Can Saee of Tawaf-e-Ziyarat be performed on 5th or 6th of Zil Haj before going to MIna on the 8th of Zil Haj? Is the state of Ihram compulsory to perform the Saee of Tawaf-e-Ziyarat ? [2] If one has to leave back to his home country on the 13th of Zil Haj, can Tawaf-e-Wida be performed any time after Tawaf-e-Ziyarat example on the 12th of Zil Haj after Fajr Namaz before the Ramee of 12th Zil Haj? Jazakallah khair,

    Answer ID: 9780Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 29/29=L/1429)

     

    (1) Yes, one can perform the saee of tawaf-e-ziyarah before going to Mina on 5th or 6th Zul Hijjah, but it is conditioned that it is preceded by ihram and nafl tawaf; since every saee should be offered after tawaf. In this tawaf it is sunnah for men to do iztiba of ihram sheet (take out the right hand till arm and leave it uncovered) and ramal (stride confidently) in tawaf.

    (2) The saee performed after wuqood Arfa along with tawaf-e-ziyarah is not conditioned with ihram, but the saee of hajj which is performed before wuqoof is conditioned with ihram.

    (3) Tawaf-e-Widaa (farewell tawaf) can be performed after tawaf-e-ziyarah in the days of Qurbani, though the rami is yet remaining. But, it is better to perform tawaf-e-widaa after performing all rituals of hajj just before leaving Makkah; since the Messenger of Allah (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) said: While departure from Makkah the last work should be tawaf. So, in performing tawaf-e-widaa before rami, the rami will occur in the last not the tawaf.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
