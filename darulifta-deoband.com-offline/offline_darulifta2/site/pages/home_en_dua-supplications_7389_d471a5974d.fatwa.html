    Title: A child has bad habbits like telling lies etc is there a dua to blow on the child so that the child refrains from bad habbits?

    Question: Assalamu Alaikum A child has bad habbits like telling lies etc is there a dua to blow on the child so that the child refrains from bad habbits? Jazakallahukhairan Wassalam

    Answer ID: 7389Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1627/1395=B/1429)

     

    ووجدک ضالا فھدی

    Recite the above dua thrice, blow on water and give it to him for drinking. It is necessary to recite it with complete sincerity and strength of Eiman.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
