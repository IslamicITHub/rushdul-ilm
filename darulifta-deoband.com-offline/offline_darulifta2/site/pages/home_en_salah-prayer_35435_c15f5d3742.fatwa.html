    Question ID: 35435Country: India

    Title: QASAR SALAAH

    Question: What is the ruling on Qasr Salah with respect to the following: (1) Minimum distance of travel to a destination from place of residence when Qasr becomes applicable? (2) Minimum and Maximum days of stay at that destination for which Qasr is applicable? (3) Fardh is 2 Rakah for every Salah as we know. Do we have to fulfil all the respective Rakahs for Sunnah prayers also for every Salah?

    Answer ID: 35435Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1842/1296/L=1432) (1) Qasr (of Salah) shall be wajib after setting off for a jourmney of 77.25 or more. (2) If one intends to stay for less than 15 days, qasr shall be wajib, while in case of intending to stay for more than 15 days one will have to offer full Salah. (3) Yes, there is no qasr in Sunnah Salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
