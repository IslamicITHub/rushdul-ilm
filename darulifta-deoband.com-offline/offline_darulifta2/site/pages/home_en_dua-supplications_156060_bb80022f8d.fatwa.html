    Title: Due for exam

    Question: Next month I have my exam. I study well and also write well but somehow I cannot pass my exam. kindly suggest me a dua to pass in my exam and also to increase my concentration suggest me dua.

    Answer ID: 156060Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 253/199/B=2/1439)

     

    Before sitting in the exam you should read Ya Aziz (يَا عَزِيزُ) twelve thousand times though you read it in many days and many stages. In-shaAllah, you shall pass.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
