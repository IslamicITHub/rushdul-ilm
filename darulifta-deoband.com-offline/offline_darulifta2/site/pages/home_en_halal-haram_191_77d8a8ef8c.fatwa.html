    Title:

    What is the ruling of having azan or quran recitation as your mobile/cell ringtone?

    Question: What is the ruling of having azan or quran recitation as your mobile/cell ringtone?

    Answer ID: 191Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 189/M=185/M)

    It is not good to have Azan or Quran recitation as ringtone on one?s mobile. It lessens the importance of Zikr-ul-Allah, therefore it should be avoided.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
