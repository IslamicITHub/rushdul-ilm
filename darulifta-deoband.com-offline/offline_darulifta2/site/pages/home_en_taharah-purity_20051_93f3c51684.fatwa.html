    Answer ID: 20051Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 376/376/M=1431)

     

    After urination, first clean the drop of private part with tissue paper or clod and then wash it with water, this is the best way. However, istinja can be done with water only. Left hand should be used for istinja. The warning of grave punishment is meant for those not saving themselves from the splash of urine.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
