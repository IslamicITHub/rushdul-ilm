    Title: Dua/wazifah for cure of heart problems

    Question: My mother's heart valves are not working properly. Operation was recommended by the doctor but we delayed it. Now my mother has many problems like illness, pain in body, tiredness and the doctors are refusing to do the operation since my mother is not fit. Can you please advise us all the dua and wazifah we should do.

    Answer ID: 151675Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 997/960/SN=9/1438)

     

    Ask your mother to read the following dua extensively:

    رَبِّ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ الضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ .

    Your family members should also read the following dua near your mother i.e. one member should recite it and the rest ones should say amen:

    أسأل الله العظيم رب العرش العظيم أن يشفيك .

    In-shaAllah, she shall be recovered soon.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
