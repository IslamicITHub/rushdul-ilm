    Title: I am away from my wife means I am working in gulf. My question is can I talk related to sex on phone. Please guide me in this matter.

    Question: AssalamualaikumRBT, Respected mufti sahab I am away from my wife means I am working in gulf. My question is can I talk related to sex on phone. Please guide me in this matter. Allah hafiz.

    Answer ID: 21063Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 426/426/M=1431)

     

    There is no problem if you talk on phone in secrecy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
