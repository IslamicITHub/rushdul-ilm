    Title: Iis the book written by sheik ul hadis Zakariya [r.a] sahi [correct]? People say there many lies [jhoot] in that. Up to what extent is it right? Please, respond.

    Question: Iis the book written by sheik ul hadis Zakariya [r.a] sahi [correct]? People say there many lies [jhoot] in that. Up to what extent is it right? Please, respond.

    Answer ID: 1546Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 907/907=J)

     

    The ahadith mentioned in the book Fazail Aamaal written by Hazrat Shaikhul Hadith Maulana Zakariay (Rahimahullah) are neither mauzoo (fake) nor lies. All the ahadith mentioned therein are credible in Fazail chapter as per the rule of muhadditheen (scholars of Hadith). Therefore, those who blame it they are wrong and tell lie.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
