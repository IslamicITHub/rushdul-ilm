    Title: Use of hair colors

    Question: I want to know that can I use Garnire, Streax etc for coloring the hair? Why I not use colors? Which product is halal and which is haram? Why it is haram? Which mehdi I use for coloring? Is black mehdi haram? Why?

    Answer ID: 59515Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1243/1219/L=10/1436) (1-6) It is allowed to dye the hair other than the black dye provided after applying the dye it does not leave layer like paint on the hair. However, you may use other colour of henna instead of black. It is sunnah to use red henna. Black henna is unlawful as Hadith warns those who apply it. It is recorded in Hadith: “There will be people in latter ages who will dye their hair like craws of pigeons, they will not smell even the fragrance of the Paradise.”

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
