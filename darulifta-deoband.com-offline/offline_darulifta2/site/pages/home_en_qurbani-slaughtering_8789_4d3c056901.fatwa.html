    Title: What is a significant of Haqeeqah (For new born baby)?

    Question: What is a significant of Haqeeqah (For new born baby)?

    Answer ID: 8789Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1083/1083=M/1429)

     

    It is sunnah to observe aqiqah of newborn baby. The Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) performed the aqiqah of his grandchildren (Hadhrat Hasan and Hadhrat Hussain). By aqiqah, the troubles go away from the baby and it is protected against problems.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
