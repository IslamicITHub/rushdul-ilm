    Title: Use of Brush Made of Pig Hair

    Question: Me and my brother having the hardware shop respected to paint and polish items we are selling the (PAINTING BRUSH) so of the people says that this is haram because this is made by the hair of (khinzir) but the hair is more 7 to 8 inches long hair that hair belongs to that animals or not. We do not give people wrong or correct information. Please we want the clarification and the brush made in Sherkote, Bijnor, Utter Pradesh, India. NOTE: If your answer will be reply in Urdu it is more useful for me.

    Answer ID: 50580Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 409/365/H=03/1435) Pig is impure by nature. Its hair is also impure. It is not lawful to sell or buy the brush made of its hair. So it is mentioned in Fatawa Mahmoodiya etc regarding such brushes. Note: If you are sure that the said brush is made of pig hair then the ruling shall be same as mentioned above. (Z)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
