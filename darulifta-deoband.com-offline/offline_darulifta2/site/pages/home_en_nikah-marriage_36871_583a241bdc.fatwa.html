    Question ID: 36871Country: India

    Title: We have agreed to pay INR 51,000/- as Maher to bride on the day of marriage

    Question: We have agreed to pay INR 51,000/- as Maher to bride on the day of marriage (nikah). My question is that can I give the gold of same amount to bride instead of the hard cash as mahr in replacement or I have to give only money? Whether Money can be replaced by gold for cash Maher or not?

    Answer ID: 36871Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 435/388/D=1433) You can give jewellery in mahr but you should inform her. But, if money was fixed in mahr and later you changed your intention and decided to give jewellery in mahr then too you should tell her clearly that you are giving her these jewelleries in return of money. If the wife accepts it then it is right. The main thing is that whatever is fixed in mahr it should be paid, whether it is cash or in form of gold, silver, kind, vehicle, animal etc. In case one is giving something else other than what was fixed then it is necessary to inform in clear words and it is also necessary that the woman accepts it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
