    Title: Child stammering issue and Urine passing in sleep

    Question: My child age is 5 years. He is unable to speak properly. He is having the stammering problem due to that reason he cannot speak up properly in school and in front of others. And also he is passing urine in the bed while sleeping. Kindly suggest how to overcome from these issues.

    Answer ID: 57785Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 442/448/L=04/1436) Recite the following verse 21 times and blow on a biscuit and give it to the child to eat. قَالَ رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي. If he passes urine in the bed then consult any hakim. Also recite four Quls while going to bed and blow on the whole body of the boy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
