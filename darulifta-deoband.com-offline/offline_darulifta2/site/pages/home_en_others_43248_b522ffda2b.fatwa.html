    Question ID: 43248Country: India

    Title: Istikhara

    Question: I had doubt about a guy whether will get married to him or not. So did istikharah namaz I got a dream in two parts where in first part I got a dream where I tear a white paper into pieces and put it down, and second part of my dream was I saw some dead bodies on them white cloth. So I am scared about this could you please tell me about this. I want to know is this dream positive result or negative result?

    Answer ID: 43248Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 135/135/M=1434) Your dream is negative. It is better to leave the selection of the groom to your parents and your family members.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
