    Title: What is the exact weight of misqal?

    Question: What is the exact weight of misqal?

    Answer ID: 148113Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 450/401/H=5/1438)

    According to the book Qawaid al-Fiqh (p. 464) and other books, a mithqal is equal to 4.5 masha. As per the present calculation, one mithqal is equal to 4 gram and 374 milligram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
