    Title: reading of books, newspaper (english) is allowed in washroom or not

    Question: assalamoalaikum if you please tell me that reading of books, newspaper (english) is allowed in washroom or not.

    Answer ID: 19472Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 193/193/M=1431)

     

    Bathroom is not a place to read books and newspapers whether they are in English, they should not be studied therein.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
