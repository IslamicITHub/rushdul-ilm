    Title: Dream

    Question: I dreamt that I am on a beach in western India. I meet a young boy. He seemed around 15 years old. He said to me that he was a Sahaba and his name was Imran and he was attacked and killed by two sharks. So I said that it must have been very painful. He said a little bit but in the hereafter he has been relieved of all pain and sufferings and he has been given a good life. Please interpret it for me and I apologize for using your valuable time.

    Answer ID: 46255Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 972/756/H=1434) It means that the nafs and satan want to dominate you. Strive to follow sunnah rather follow all the commandments of Shariah completely. Also have close contact with righteous people.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
