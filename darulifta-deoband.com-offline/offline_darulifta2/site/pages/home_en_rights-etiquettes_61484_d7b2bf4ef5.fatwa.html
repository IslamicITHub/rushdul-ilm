    Title: Looking in the mirror makruh

    Question: Is it makrooh to look in the mirror if there is no need for it?

    Answer ID: 61484Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 709/709/SD=11/1436) It is wastage of time to look into the mirror without a need. Hadith stops from wasting the time. It also says that the person who saves time is good Muslim.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
