    Answer ID: 5745Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1578/1197=H/1429)

     

    The meaning of your dream is that you should never try to marry that person. You must not rely upon him though he makes definite promises of abandoning Shiaism and converting to Sunni.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
