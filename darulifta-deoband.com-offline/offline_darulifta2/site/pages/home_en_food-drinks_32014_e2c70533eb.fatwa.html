    Question ID: 32014Country: India

    Title: Hotel meat and boliled chicken

    Question: Assalamalyeikum Mufti saheb.Is this valid to eat Nonveg items in the Muslim hotels as they say they do Halal. But how can we know if they are doing proper Halal or Not. Also eating Chicken which has been halal with use of hot water.It is helpful if you give some idea on the way of proper halal.

    Answer ID: 32014Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 745/619/B=1432) (1) If the owner of Muslim hotel claims that the meat is halal, his words shall be acceptable. But if you have any doubt due to any reason you can avoid eating it. The ruling of haram shall be applicable if you observe yourself being slaughtered on non-Shariah method. (2) Usually they put chicken in hot water after slaughtering for awhile until the skin softens and gets off easily. It is not right to eat such a chicken that is put in hot water so long that its filth is cooked and its bad smell is absorbed in the meat, thus all the meat gets impure and unclean. Eating such chicken is not right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
