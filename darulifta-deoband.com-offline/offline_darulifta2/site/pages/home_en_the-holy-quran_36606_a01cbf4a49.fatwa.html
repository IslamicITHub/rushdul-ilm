    Title: I am reverted Muslim I am little bit confused please help me we Muslim believe that there are 5 pillars of Islam

    Question: I am reverted Muslim I am little bit confused please help me we Muslim believe that there are 5 pillars of Islam: (1) Oneness of God, (2) Salah, (3) Roza, (4) Hajj, (5) Zakah, where does the holy Quran stands in this 5 pillars of Islam

    Answer ID: 36606Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 404/404/M=1433) The holy Quran is the origin and root of the five pillars of Islam. You need not worry about its status.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
