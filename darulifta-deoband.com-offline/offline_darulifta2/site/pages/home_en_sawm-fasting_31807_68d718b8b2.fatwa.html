    Title: Diabeties and Fast

    Question: What is the orders of shariah about fasting to Blood sugar patient

    Answer ID: 31807Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 684/574/B=1432) If he feels unbearable pain in keeping the fast, he is allowed to leave the fast. If he is able to make up the missed fasts he should do so, otherwise he should pay fidya (expiation) for the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
