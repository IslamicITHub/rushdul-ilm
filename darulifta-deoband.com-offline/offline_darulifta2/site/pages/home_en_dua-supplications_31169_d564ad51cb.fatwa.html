    Title: My sister has often some bad dream about herself or the other family members

    Question: My sister has often some bad dream about herself or the other family members, usually predicting some future unhappy events. Most often the happening turns out to be nearly the same. She wants to have some advice in this regard.

    Answer ID: 31169Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 513/391/L=1432) Ask your sister to recite Ayatul Kursi and four Quls before going to bed and blow on her hands and pass her hands over her whole body. Also, ask her to recite tasbih Fatimah before sleeping. She should also recite the following dua five hundred times in one meeting or several meetings with wudhu: حَسْبُنَا اللّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ [آل عمران : 173] In-sha-Allah, gradually the chain of bad dreams shall come to an end and she would get rid of unpleasant incidents.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
