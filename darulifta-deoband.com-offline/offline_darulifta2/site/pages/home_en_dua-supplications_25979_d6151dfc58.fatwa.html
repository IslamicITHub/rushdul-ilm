    Title: I am searching for the job from past 1yr. Still iam not getting any possitive result .. Please give a dua to read everyday to read ayath to keep virdh . ( Procedure when & how many times to read )

    Question: Aslm... DUA FOR RISQ :- Moulana Iam searching for the job from past 1yr. Still iam not getting any possitive result .. Please give a dua to read everyday to read ayath to keep virdh . ( Procedure when & how many times to read ) Thanks Abdullah

    Answer ID: 25979Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2107/H=624/th=1431)

    Recite the following ayat after each salah 11 times with durood 11-11 times and supplicate:
    رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ [البقرة : 201]
    Repeat the same practice in the state of wudhu before going to clean bed, this practice should be followed until you develop firm belief in raza bil qaza (to content oneself with what is destined by Allah).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
