    Title: I have heard that we can offer Chasht at the time of Ishraq? Is it true? Do some deobandi scholar allow this?

    Question: I have heard that we can offer Chasht at the time of Ishraq? Is it true? Do some deobandi scholar allow this?

    Answer ID: 899Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 731=684/B)

     

    The time of Chasht Salah also starts 20 minutes after the sunrise. The Ishraq Salah time also starts at the same point. So, if you performed Chasht Salah at Ishraq time then it is right, Shariah allows it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
