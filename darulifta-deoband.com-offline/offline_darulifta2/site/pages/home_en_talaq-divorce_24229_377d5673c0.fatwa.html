    Answer ID: 24229Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1165/1165/M=1431)

    A woman (wife) should not demand for talaq with no valid reason. It is sin. It is mentioned in hadith that such woman will not get the fragrance of Jannah. Follow the right and appropriate measures whatsoever may be to maintain nikah. If your wife has any reasonable complaint that is in line with Shariah, remove the same and get it solved by mutual understanding.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
