    Title: Praying for birth of son

    Question: Is it lawful in Islam to pray to Allah for birth of a son? If yes, is there any dua for it.

    Answer ID: 50677Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 448/358/B=03/1435) Yes, it is lawful to supplicate Allah for the birth of a child. The supplication of Hadhrat Sayyidina Zakariya (على نبينا وعليه الصلاة والسلام) is mentioned in the holy Quran for the same purpose. رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِي فَرْداً وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْوَارِثِينَ [الأنبياء : 89] If the husband and wife recite this blessed dua with durood before and after extensively on daily basis, then they shall have a son, in-shaAllah. May Allah bless you a pious son, Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
