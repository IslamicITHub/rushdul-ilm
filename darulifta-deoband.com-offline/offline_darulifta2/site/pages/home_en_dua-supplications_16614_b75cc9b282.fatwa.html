    Answer ID: 16614Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1674/1333/L=1430)

     

    Perform two rak?ah as Salatut Taubah, repent to Allah sincerely with firm intention not to repeat the sin again, in-sha-Allah your sins will be purged away.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
