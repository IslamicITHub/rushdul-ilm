    Title: Is there any cure for getting rid of having an acne type disease on one's back?

    Question: Assalamu Alaykum, Is there any cure for getting rid of having an acne type disease on one's back?

    Answer ID: 5591Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 184/184=M)

     

    Allah has created medicine for every disease. Yet, these medicines depend on Allah?s order to cure a person. The servants have been asked to look for ways of treatment. There are medicines for the abovementioned diseases as well. For treatment, you should consult a physician or a hakim.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
