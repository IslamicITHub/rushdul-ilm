    Title: Salah procedure during travel

    Question: When I am travelling in a flight, I could not able to offer the fardh salah in the standing position. Can I offer salah in the sitting position? If I offer the salah in sitting position whether I have to repeat the salah again or not?

    Answer ID: 160733Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 799/913/H=8/1439)

     

    If your plane lands at a time that you may offer salah easily on ground then you should offer salah after you get down from the plane. However, if you fear that your salah will be missed then you should offer it in the plane. If you feel too weak to stand, and offered Salah facing the direction of Qiblah and sitting on floor (away from seat) then even your fardh salah will be valid and you will need not to reoffer it. In case you offered your salah sitting while you were able to offer salah standing then your fardh salah will not be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
