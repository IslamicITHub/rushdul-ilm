    Question ID: 35336Country: India

    Title: My father and my chacha (my father's brother) are not talking to each other from a long time due to some misunderstanding

    Question: My father and my chacha (my father's brother) are not talking to each other from a long time due to some misunderstanding. If we talk about something to make them together with my father or chacha, they will not listen. I think this is due to the other relatives who are creating problems between them. They are not talking to each other from past 12 years. Can you tell me what we can do for this?

    Answer ID: 35336Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2043/B=415/TB=1432) Their anger and discontinuance of talking with each other is a major sin. Allah shall not accept their fardh and nafl salah, dua and any prayer due to it. If they died without reconciliation it may cause them to go to hell. This is mentioned in hadith. Instead of trying yourself, you should seek the intermediation of some influential people in this matter whom they would accept. You should also pray for their reconciliation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
