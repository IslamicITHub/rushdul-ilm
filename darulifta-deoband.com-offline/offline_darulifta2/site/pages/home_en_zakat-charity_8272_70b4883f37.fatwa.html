    Question: assalam alaykum, mera sawal zakat se hai. mal ki zakat har sal nilana hai ya sirf ek sal keliya?yani agar were paas 10 tola sona hai us ki zakat mai ne adaker di thi pichle sal kay fir se is sal us per zakat lagoo hogi...? agar lagoohai tu guzarish hai quran aur hadit ka hawala djiye. is ke liya chand loog bzid hain ke ek maal kiliya her sal zakat lagoo nahi hooti. khas ker ahle hadit jamat ye sabit ker diya hai ke quran aur hadit mai is ki koi daleeel maujood nahi hai ke ek maal ke liya har salzakat ada kijaye sir ek bar dena kafi hai. un ka kahana hai ki quran aur hadit mai kahan likha haike usi mal kiliya har sal zakat ada karo jo tumhare paas saloon se rakah hoa jai . umeed ke aap mere sawal ko samajh gaye. jazak allah

    Answer ID: 8272Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1775/1671=D/1429)

     

    (1) Yes, you will have to give zakah again when the second year is over.

    (2) It is mentioned in the Hadith: لازكاة في مال حتي يحول عليه الحول (اخرجه الترمذي)

    The zakah is ordained in growing wealth; growth takes place round a year, so zakah is obligatory when one year is over. And when the second year begins after one year, there is new growth in it which will come under the ruling of new wealth. If there is no real growth but only the growing ability, then that much will suffice, such as gold or silver apparently have no real growth but have the growing ability in case they are invested in business. Therefore, there will be new zakah every new year, such as the time of salah comes daily then new salah is performed, it is not enough to offer salah once in life. It is narrated in the Hadith that the Prophet (صلي الله عليه وسلم) had taken zakah of tow years in advance from Hadhrat Abbas: استسلف من العباس زكاة سنتين (اخرجه البيهقي في السنن الكبري)

    You should have written what and how the sect Ahl-e-Hadith proves it from the Holy Quran and Hadith.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
