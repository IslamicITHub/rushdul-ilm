    Title: I would like to ask about iguiarmi sharif, 11 of rabiul akhir. Because some people celebrate it like a holly day, so , what is the point of view of shariah abou it, and why people celebrate it.

    Question: I would like to ask about iguiarmi sharif, 11 of rabiul akhir. Because some people celebrate it like a holly day, so , what is the point of view of shariah abou it, and why people celebrate it.

    Answer ID: 5167Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1162/971 =H/1429)

     

    (1)  It is a baseless in the eyes of the Shariah and Islamic teachings. Neither the prophet Muhammad (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) and his companions celebrated Gyarahvin Sharif (the eleventh day of Rabiu Awwal) nor they ordered to celebrate it and encouraged it in any way. It is neither proved theoretically and practically by the successors of the sahaba.

    (2)  Many rituals prevail and people stick to these rituals in a way as if the religion has ordered them to do so whereas it is not so. This custom (celebrating Gyarahvin Sharif) is one of them. May Allah bestow the followers of Islam good understanding, Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
