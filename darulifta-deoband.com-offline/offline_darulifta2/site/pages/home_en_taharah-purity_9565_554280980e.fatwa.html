    Question: Salam,it is gud to sleep in wuzu.i do my wuzu and go to sleep .i read the three quls and then ayat kursi and when i lay down to sleep i feel to fart (pass the aiir).do i have to do my wozo again if i fart or the first wozo was enough.i will get the reward all night or not?

    Answer ID: 9565Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2518/2081=B/1429)

     

    There is no need to remake the wudhu. In both the cases, whether the wudhu remains or breaks off, you shall get the reward of all night.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
