    Question: Assalamo Alaikom Dear Mufti Sahab, I'm working for a company supplying fuels to military invaders in my country, I've stolen money but regret now. can I purify my sins by giving it back or I can even give it as a charity? جزاکم الله خیرا

    Answer ID: 16996Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2084/1678/H=1430)

     

    Stealing is unlawful. If you can return the stolen goods with ease you should return it to the company. Otherwise give it to the poor and needy ones, and make true tauba.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
