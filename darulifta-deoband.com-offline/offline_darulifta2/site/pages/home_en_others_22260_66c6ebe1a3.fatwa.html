I saw a snake which is eating himself from his tail, at last only this head remians with both the eyes coming out. 2. As my father stays abroad, I saw in dream that i have recived a call saying that my dad is died. 3. I Saw i am taking out water from a well.

    Answer ID: 22260Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 737/577/D=1431)

    (1) In-sha-Allah, your enemies will perish and suffer a reverse.
    (2) In-sha-Allah, your father will live long.
    (3) In-sha-Allah, there will be growth and prosperity in your wealth, so keep on spending in good deeds and doing kindness towards your relatives.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
