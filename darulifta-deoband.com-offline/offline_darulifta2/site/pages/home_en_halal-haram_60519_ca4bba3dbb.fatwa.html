    Title: Using images of living beings in website designing

    Question: I am a website and stationary designer. Can I use images of living beings (people or animals etc) in my design if the images are provided to me by my clients?

    Answer ID: 60519Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 631/631/SD=10/1436) It is severe sin in the Shariah to photograph the pictures of living beings or get it photographed by someone else. Using the picture of living being in the design is unlawful and haram. Hence you should not use the picture of living beings in your design. In case a customer wants to add any living picture in his design, you should reject it politely.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
