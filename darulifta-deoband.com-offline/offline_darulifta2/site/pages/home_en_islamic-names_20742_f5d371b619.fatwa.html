    Question: I wanted to know the proper name of "AAHAD" , whether it could be called by saying "ABDUL AAHAD" OR "MOHAMMAD AAHAD" or only "AAHAD". Please guide me.

    Answer ID: 20742Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    Fatwa: 368/368/M=1431

    Abdul Ahad should be the name.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
