    Question: What is the haukam of rafa-yadain in islam?because according to the hadith of sahih bukhari it is said that nabi pak did that act?and many of the sahabah and taba tabaeen also did ... and some said he sometimes did and sometimes did not?secondly,is there any difference in women and men namaz(salat)

    Answer ID: 5924Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 674/632=L/1429)

     

    According to Hanafi Fiqh, raf?-e-yadain is not sunnah except when starting salah. As far as raf?-e-yadain from the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) is concerned, it was in the beginning of Islam.

    (2) Allama Shami has mentioned 26 differences between the salah of a man and a woman. You should refer to it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
