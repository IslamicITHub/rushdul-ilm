    Title: Need reference for hadith quoted by Imam

    Question: When I visited the masjid some Hadith were being quoted by the imam. The first Hadith said that Prophet Mohammed (SAWS) gave his pachna blood to one sahabah named Zuber (RA) to dispose at a safe place but he drank the blood and came back to Prophet Muhammad (SAWS) and mentioned that he has drunk the blood and the Prophet Muhammad (SAWS) said the sahabahs that whoevers blood is mixed with my blood then fire of hell is haram on him. Kindly suggest me the source of this Hadith from any authentic book.

    Answer ID: 147225Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 399/364/H=4/1438)

    The imam spoke correctly.

    ذكر الدار قطنى وغيره أعطى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ابن زبير وهو غلام دم محاجمه ليدفنه فشربه فقال له النبى صلى الله عليه وسلم من خالطه دمه دمى لم تمسه النار ويل لك من الناس وويل للناس منك – فأورده في النجم الوهاج والقاضي عياض في الشفاء (التاريخ الخميس ، ج 1 ص 354، مطبوعه مكتبه دار صادق بيروت)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
