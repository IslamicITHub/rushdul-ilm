    Title: Internet service provider

    Question: I am an internet service provider giving internet service in home, shops and offices etc. I want to know that this business is halal for me or not. Please give me this answer very soon.

    Answer ID: 44591Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 559/440/D=1434) Internet is used in both lawful and unlawful ways. Hence the person who provides the service shall not be sinful rather the person who uses it in wrong way shall be sinful. However, if the service provider considers about someone that he shall only use it in unlawful way then he should avoid providing it to him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
