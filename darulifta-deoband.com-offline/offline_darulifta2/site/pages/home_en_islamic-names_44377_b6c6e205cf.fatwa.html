    Title: Islamic Names Muhsin ul Islam

    Question: Could we name our expected boy Mahsheen-ul-Islam! How is the name and its meaning? We will appreciate your earliest reply.

    Answer ID: 44377Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 502/502/M=1434) You can keep the name ‘Muhsinul Islam’ for your son. Muhsin means one who does good and kindness with someone.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
