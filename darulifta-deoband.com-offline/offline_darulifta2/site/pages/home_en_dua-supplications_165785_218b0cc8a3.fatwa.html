    Title: Dua which I can recite so that nobody can ever insult me or put blame on me.

    Question: Dua which I can recite so that nobody can ever insult me or put blame on me.

    Answer ID: 165785Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 136/109/M=03/1440)

     

    Read the below verse extensively:

     

    وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَاء وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَاء بِيَدِكَ الْخَيْرُ إِنَّكَ عَلَىَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

     

    Similarly, read the below dua 3-3 times and mu’awazatain (Surah Falaq and Surah Naas) in the morning and evening:

    لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله العظيم

    بسم الله الذي لا يضر مع اسمه شئ فى الأرض ولا فى السماء وهو السميع العليم .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
