    Title: Islamic views about marriage and sharing bed

    Question: I am getting married in June and later Ramadan starts, I would like to know can we share bed during Ramadan or when we observe fast also or what is Islamic view/restrictions about it. Also adding on the question can we have oral sex?

    Answer ID: 44955Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 751/454/L=1434) It is lawful to sleep with wife and have sex during the night of Ramadan. Likewise it is also lawful to sleep with her during day time and have foreplay with her. However, if by foreplay one fears ejaculation or falling in intercourse then it is necessary to avoid foreplay. Oral sex is makrooh and bestiality. [M]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
