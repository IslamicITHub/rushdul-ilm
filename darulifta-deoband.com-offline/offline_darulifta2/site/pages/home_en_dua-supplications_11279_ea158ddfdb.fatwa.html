    Answer ID: 11279Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 654/457=H/1430)

     

    Recite Surah Alam Nashrah and Surah Quraish thrice after every obligatory salah with durood thrice before and after and pray to Allah. Repeat the same action at night when you go to sleep with wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
