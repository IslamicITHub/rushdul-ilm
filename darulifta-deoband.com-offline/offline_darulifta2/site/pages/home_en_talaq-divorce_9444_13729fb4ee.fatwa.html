We came to know after 8 days that she is pregnant, is we are not husband and wife now ? will my coming baby is not valid ? please help me.

    Question: I said 3 times talaq in anger during fight to my wife, it was not my intention to give her talaq, I love her lot, we have one kid and we both don?t want be separated, we want to spend life together. We came to know after 8 days that she is pregnant, is we are not husband and wife now ? will my coming baby is not valid ? please help me.

    Answer ID: 9444Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2473/2059=B/1429)

     

    Divorce takes place in a state of anger as well as pregnancy. When you gave divorce thrice in clear words, then now it will not be counted what intention you had, because divorce takes place without intention too if pronounced in clear cut words. Now she is no longer your wife, both of you should live separately.  If you want her back you should have halala sharaee. The born baby shall be yours and will be legitimate.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
