    Title: I would like to know if zakah is eligible for a family whose saving are less than 613 grams of silver, but they live in a rented house whose advance is Rs. 50,000. Does the house advance count as their savings?

    Question: I would like to know if zakah is eligible for a family whose saving are less than 613 grams of silver, but they live in a rented house whose advance is Rs. 50,000. Does the house advance count as their savings?

    Answer ID: 152727Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1101/1029/B=10/1438)

     

    612 gram and 360 milligram silver is the original nisab. If someone has silver in this quantity then he is rich as per the Shariah and it is wajib on him to pay the fortieth part as zakah. The advanced amount of rented house which is deposited shall be counted as his saving and when he receives it the zakah of previous years shall be wajib on it. If someone has less than 612 gram and 360 milligram silver then he is not sahib-e-nisab, such person is eligible to receive zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
