    Answer ID: 27501Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1993/1612/B=1431)

    You should always pay a visit to your mother to see and enquire about her health and carry along with you some gifts and other necessities of life. Give a cool hearing to her bitter words and do not retort her. Keep patience and do not lose hopes. All the same, do not send your wife to her. Yours this sufferance shall earn you reward.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
