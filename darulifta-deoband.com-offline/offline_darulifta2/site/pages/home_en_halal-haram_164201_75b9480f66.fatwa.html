    Question ID: 164201Country: India

    Title: On behalf of distributing food for swaab for late person

    Question: If any body wants to take sawab for his late father and for this he/she cooked food and invite relative people as well as madaris poor students and jamati. So is it right to feed that people that can arrange their lunch themselves? Or the food should be distributed to most deserved people i. e. poor children. Please elaborate clearly all the facts.

    Answer ID: 164201Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1216/921/SN=11/1439)

     

    The food which is fed with the intention of isal-e-sawab should be fed to the poor and needy ones (whether they are relative or madrasa students). It should not be fed to the rich and sahib-e-nisab people. Thus that person should act accordingly. It should be noted that it is not lawful to arrange feast after the death of someone with fixed day, date (for example, third, fourth or fortieth day), rather it is bida’h. If one wants to feed then one should feed poor and needy ones any day without fixing day and date or give food to the poor. Rather it is better that they are given money instead of food.

     

    وفى البزازية: ويكره اتخاذ الطعام في اليوم الأول والثالث وبعد الأسبوع الخ (در مختار مع الشامى 3/148، ط: زكريا)"

     

    Also see Fatawa Darul Uloom Deoband (18/479 and afterwards).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
