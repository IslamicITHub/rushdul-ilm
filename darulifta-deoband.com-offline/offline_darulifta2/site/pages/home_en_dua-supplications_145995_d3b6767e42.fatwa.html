    Title: For the past two years I have been facing repeated lung related problems (infections etc). I am also asthmatic. May you please give me some wazifah to help relieve these problems.

    Question: For the past two years I have been facing repeated lung related problems (infections etc). I am also asthmatic. May you please give me some wazifah to help relieve these problems.

    Answer ID: 145995Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 143/108/H=2/1438)

    Recite surah Alam Nashrah (complete surah) after each salah three times with durood 3-3 times before and after, and blow on water and drink it. Do the same in the night before going to bed in the state of wudhu. May Allah give you complete recovery soon! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
