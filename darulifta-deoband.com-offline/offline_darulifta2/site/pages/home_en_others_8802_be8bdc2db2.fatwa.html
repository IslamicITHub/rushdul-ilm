    Answer ID: 8802Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2182/1693=H/1429)

     

    It is good dream. The death does not mean demise but it is glad tiding for your grandmother that she will be getting the divine favour of stepping from lower grade to higher ones.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
