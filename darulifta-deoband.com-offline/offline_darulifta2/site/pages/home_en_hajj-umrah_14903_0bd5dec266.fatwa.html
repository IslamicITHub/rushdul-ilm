    Title: As salamaliakum brother, i would like to perform Hajj along with my mother. but i have Bank Loan. So could u tell me that is it Permissible to perform Hajj or not. Thanking You!

    Question: As salamaliakum brother, i would like to perform Hajj along with my mother. but i have Bank Loan. So could u tell me that is it Permissible to perform Hajj or not. Thanking You!

    Answer ID: 14903Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1447/1179=d/1430)

     

    It is better to pay off the debt first and then perform hajj. If the resources of repaying the debt are at hand and the hajj may not create any problem in repayment, so you can perform the hajj and the hajj will be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
