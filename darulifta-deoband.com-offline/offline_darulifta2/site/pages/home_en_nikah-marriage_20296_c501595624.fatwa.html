    Title: can a muslim get marrige with a kafir for get her love?

    Question: can a muslim get marrige with a kafir for get her love?

    Answer ID: 20296Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 360/254/L=1431)

     

    Nikah of a Muslim with non-Muslim is not lawful. Almighty Allah says in the holy Quran:

    و لا تنكحوا المشركات حتى يومن الآية

    Wed not idolatresses till they believe. [1:221]

    However, if she accepts the truth of Islam and accepts Islam wholeheartedly, then a Muslim can marry her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
