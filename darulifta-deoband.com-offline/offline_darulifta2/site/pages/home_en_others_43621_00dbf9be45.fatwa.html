    Title: having intercourse

    Question: I am a 28 year old married man; I often get a dream where I am having sex with my younger sister. We are 1 brother and 2 sisters, but I have only dreamt such thing with my younger sister only and never with the youngest one. I have seen this dream before as well probably more than 10 times I get it almost in every 4-5 months. Kindly help me with the interpretation of this dream.

    Answer ID: 43621Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 305/197/L=1434) It is a satanic dream. Recite Ayatal Kursi and four Quls before going to bed and blow on your hands and pass your hands over your whole body. In-shaAllah, you will not have such dreams.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
