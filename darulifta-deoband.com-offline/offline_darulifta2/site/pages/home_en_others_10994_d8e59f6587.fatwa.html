    Answer ID: 10994Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 335/322=D/1430)

     

    It indicates to following Shariah, especially to the importance of salah. Whatever the restrictions and hindrances may be, but you should regularly do good deeds and do not be careless to it. Be mindful of it whether on-station or out-station, though people try to deviate by different tricks or the nafs and Satan create laziness. Do not pay attention to it and discard it considering them as satanic whispering and pay your attention to doing good deeds. It is nice dream, ma-shaAllah. May Allah keep you firm and steadfast!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
