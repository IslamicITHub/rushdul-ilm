    Title: Can girls be the witness in or during Nikaah? (Kya Khawateen bhi Nikaah mein gawaah banai jaa sakti hain)?

    Question: Can girls be the witness in or during Nikaah? (Kya Khawateen bhi Nikaah mein gawaah banai jaa sakti hain?)

    Answer ID: 24097Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1201/912/L=1431)

     

    If there are two women along with one man then they can become witness in nikah. Only women though many cannot become witness.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
