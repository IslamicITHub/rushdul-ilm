    Title: Is it required for a woman to take ghusl if she has wet dreams

    Question: I have question regarding ghusl. (1) Is it required for a woman to take ghusl if she has wet dreams? And I am ashamed to ask this 2nd question, does woman require having ghusl after orgasm due to masturbation? Please try to answer question as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 37048Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 272/212/B=1433) (1) Yes, it is wajib on a woman to have ghusl if she has wet dream. (2) If semen is discharged after masturbation then ghusl is wajib.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
