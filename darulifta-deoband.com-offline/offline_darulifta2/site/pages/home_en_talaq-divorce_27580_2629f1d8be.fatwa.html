    Question: In normal environment,my wife was taking with me to go her father house.I argued for not going and that she went to baranda.I was lying in bed and my wife returned to room and saying Talak three times in my front.I thundered completely and asked him for doing the same.She replied that I said the same jokingly.Still we are living together and intend to stay in future with the help of Allah.What is the position of our marriage status.

    Answer ID: 27580Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1822/1318/L=1431)

     

    In Islam, husband is authorized to give talaq, not wife. Hence, if your wife pronounced the words of talaq, no talaq will take place. Your nikah is still intact.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
