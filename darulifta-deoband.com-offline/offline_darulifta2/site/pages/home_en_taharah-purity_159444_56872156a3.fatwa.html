    Title: If blood or pus dries up on one's skin, will that prevent water from reaching the skin during wudhu or ghusl?

    Question: If blood or pus dries up on one's skin, will that prevent water from reaching the skin during wudhu or ghusl?

    Answer ID: 159444Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 669/626/M=6/1439)

     

    If blood or pus smears the body and dries then it should be cleaned during wudhu and ghusl because both these things are impure and they could be removed by rubbing gently.

     

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
