    Title: Dream

    Question: I got a dream which I want you to interpret. I saw in a dream that an Alim was explaining me about Hadhrat Abu Bakr (RA) and Hadhrat Umar (RA)? I do not remember anything else? And that Alim is known to me. I will be very much thankful if you interpret my dream.

    Answer ID: 43760Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 375/L=165/TL=1434) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. It shall benefit you in this world as well as in hereafter, in-shaAllah. However, it is necessary to know the Shariah rulings related to business and follow the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
