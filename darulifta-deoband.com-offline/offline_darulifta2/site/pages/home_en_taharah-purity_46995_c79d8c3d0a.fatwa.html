    Question ID: 46995Country: Pakistan

    Title: Can we touch or recite quran when madhi come out and whudu is obligatory?

    Question: After the sexual arousal when madhi comes out and it breaks our wudhu, can we touch the holy Quran in that condition without making wudhu or can we recite the holy Quran or any dua without touching the Quran or any other book. Kindly answer it.

    Answer ID: 46995Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1200/787/L=10/1434) Wudhu is necessary in order to touch the holy Quran. It is not lawful to touch the holy Quran if the wudhu broke. However, one may recite the holy Quran or read any dua without touching the holy Quran. Wudhu is not necessary to recite the holy Quran or dua.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
