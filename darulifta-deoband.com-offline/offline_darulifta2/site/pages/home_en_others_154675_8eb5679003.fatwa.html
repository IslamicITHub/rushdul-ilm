    Title: Dream Interpretation

    Question: I am requesting for your guidance please. I saw my deceased father who passed away in 2009, asked me for money. I would be grateful if you could please give me it's interpretation.

    Answer ID: 154675Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1435/1403/N=1/1439)

     

    Asking for money can be interpreted as to request for isal-e-sawab. So you should do sadaqah and charity extensively on behalf of your deceased father and also read the holy Quran etc more and more and convey its sawab to him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
