    Question: Is it permissible to have a shower or should one rather have a bucket bath? Also, is it permissible to stand and bathe or should one rather sit and bathe?

    Answer ID: 7642Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 968/968=M/1429)

     

    One can have bath as per his easiness, it is allowed. However, it is better to bath sitting with a bucket.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
