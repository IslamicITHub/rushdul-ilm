    Title: Currently i am in bit trouble,I am getting married after six months and the date for the marraige is fixed by my parents.The parents of the girl r not much deendar nor my parents.I have heard that on the day of marraige in the hall there r chances that they r making single stage for sitting me and my wife.Also i have heard that there r some chances of photography thr.My father had told them for avoiding photograhy and request for two stage for sitting.I am involed in Jammat work and pray 5 time salaat.So please provide some information on this.Also Please give information on how to stop fighting between my father and my brother since they fight for some misunderstanding again and again.So please give some special dua to avoid this fight to heppen at my home.I am always doing dua for them from allah to give love affection between them.Now i have started making dua for about my maaraige to allah to make good and blessfull of allah and help to avoid this marraige day situation.

    Question: Assalaamu-Alei-Kum brothers, Currently i am in bit trouble,I am getting married after six months and the date for the marraige is fixed by my parents.The parents of the girl r not much deendar nor my parents.I have heard that on the day of marraige in the hall there r chances that they r making single stage for sitting me and my wife.Also i have heard that there r some chances of photography thr.My father had told them for avoiding photograhy and request for two stage for sitting.I am involed in Jammat work and pray 5 time salaat.So please provide some information on this.Also Please give information on how to stop fighting between my father and my brother since they fight for some misunderstanding again and again.So please give some special dua to avoid this fight to heppen at my home.I am always doing dua for them from allah to give love affection between them.Now i have started making dua for about my maaraige to allah to make good and blessfull of allah and help to avoid this marraige day situation. Zazaak-Allah

    Answer ID: 6770Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1289/1211=D/1429)

     

    You should punctually recite this dua:

    اللهم اصلح لنا شاننا كلہ، اللهم اصلح لى في ذريتي، اللهم انى اسألك الهدى والتقی والعفاف والغنى

    And you must strictly stop photographing. Also, it is not allowed to share a stage before nikah. It is also not Islamically lawful if non-Mahram women or men have a reach there. Therefore, there should be a place for sitting where only women can come to the girl and no non-Mahram men should approach there. Likewise, only men should go to the boy, no non-Mahram women should be there. If it is possible for you to persuade your brother, you should tell him something about status and respect of father so that he must respect the father in dealing the matter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
