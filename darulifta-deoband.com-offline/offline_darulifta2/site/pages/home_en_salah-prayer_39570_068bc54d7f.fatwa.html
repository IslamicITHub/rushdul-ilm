    Question ID: 39570Country: Pakistan

    Title: When I am praying salah I usually face a problem. I usually get sudden thoughts delivered to make me laugh

    Question: When I am praying salah I usually face a problem. I usually get sudden thoughts delivered to make me laugh however I do not laugh but when I try to stop from laughing I feel like the sound level of my tilawat or breath rose due to the procedure of abstaining from laughing. Usually I do not really hear it out myself, for example the sound of breathing out or while reciting in the salah and the sound level rising, usually to an unnoticeable extent. The question is that does salah really get affected by this? It is not regarding the ordinary sound of laughing in the salah and I think that no one can know that I am laughing in the salah.

    Answer ID: 39570Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1500/1172/B=1433) In silent salah while reciting surah if voice is heard due to laugh then salah shall be null and if the next person hears it then wudhu shall break as well. You will have to offer salah again after making new wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
