    Title: There is one of our colleagues getting interest by giving loan to the people. We asked him about this that it is wrong but he said that these people are doing business by getting loan, anyhow sometimes he has to drop us from working place to our home. Is it right to go with him on his car.

    Question: There is one of our colleagues getting interest by giving loan to the people. We asked him about this that it is wrong but he said that these people are doing business by getting loan, anyhow sometimes he has to drop us from working place to our home. Is it right to go with him on his car.

    Answer ID: 16012Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1463/1455=M=1430)

     

    It is unlawful and haram to receive interest on loan. If his car was purchased from interest and haram money, you should avoid accompanying him in the car to possible extent.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
