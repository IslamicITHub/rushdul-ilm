    Title: (1) While travelling if my namaz gets qaza then after reaching my place wherever I am going so after reaching there. Would I will offer full qaza namaz or it will be considered as qasr namaz? (2)And what will be its niyat?

    Question: (1) While travelling if my namaz gets qaza then after reaching my place wherever I am going so after reaching there. Would I will offer full qaza namaz or it will be considered as qasr namaz? (2)And what will be its niyat?

    Answer ID: 160689Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 938/663/SN=8/1439)

     

    (1) Whatsoever salah of four rakah you have missed during your Shariah journey, you will perform them as qaza with qasr, not full salah.

    (2) It is enough to say: “I am making the intention to offer so and so salah missed during my journey”.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
