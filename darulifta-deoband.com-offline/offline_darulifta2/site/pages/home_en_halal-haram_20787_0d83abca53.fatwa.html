    Title: The one who studied degree by educational loan (interest). Now that person joined job. Is that salary halal or haram?

    Question: Assalaamu Alaikkum mufti sb.. The one who studied degree by educational loan (interest). Now that person joined job. Is that salary halal or haram?

    Answer ID: 20787Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 553/353/L=1431)

     

    Salary would be halal, it cannot be termed haram provided the job is of lawful nature.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
