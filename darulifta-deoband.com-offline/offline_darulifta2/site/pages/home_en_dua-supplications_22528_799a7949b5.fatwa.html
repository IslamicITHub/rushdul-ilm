    Answer ID: 22528Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 950/698/L=1431)

    Recite istighfar 100 times in the morning and evening regularly. Also, recite the following dua 100 times any time:

    لا أله الا أنت سبحانك أنى كنت من الظمين

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
