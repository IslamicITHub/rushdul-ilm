    Answer ID: 30511Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 319/290/B=1432)

    Islam has given the rights of talaq to male i.e. husband. A wife cannot divorce her husband. You should file your case in any Darul Qaza. If they revoke your nikah after investigating all the matters in case there is no way for reconciliation as per the Shariah, then you can marry someone else after passing your iddah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
