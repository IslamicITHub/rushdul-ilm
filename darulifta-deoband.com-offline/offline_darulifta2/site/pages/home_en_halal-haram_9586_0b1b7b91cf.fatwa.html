    Question: Galay mein Taweez dalna kaisa hai ....Taweez pehnnay ka tariqa kya hai yaanay iismein dhaga ya maum ki dibbi ho ya kis tarah ho...........Taweez ya dhaga hath mein bandhnay ki sharyi haisiyat kya hai.Jawab irshad farmayein.

    Answer ID: 9586Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2553/2109=B/1429)

     

    One can hang taweez around his neck or right arm provided it contains words from the Quran and Hadith, it is free from words of shirk and one has a belief that the effect is only from Allah not from the taweez. Taweez can be soaked in wax, sewn in cloth and tied with thread around neck or right arm. It is allowable for children and unlettered people to tie taweez, but the learned ones should recite the duas and verses.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
