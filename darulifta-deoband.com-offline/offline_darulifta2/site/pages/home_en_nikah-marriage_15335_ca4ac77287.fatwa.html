    Title: i m a married woman i want to know that 1. i m about to become a mother in this condition can i do suhbat with my husband?

    Question: i m a married woman i want to know that 1. i m about to become a mother in this condition can i do suhbat with my husband?

    Answer ID: 15335Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1283/1026=L/1430)

     

    Having intercourse with wife is lawful until it hurts her or it is harmful for the pregnancy. If there is no such problem, you may have intercourse with your husband. However, basically having intercourse in pregnancy is not prohibited.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
