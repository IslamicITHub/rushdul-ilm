    Answer ID: 17104Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2073/1670/H=1430)

     

    The dream warns you that the desire for world have transgressed to some extent beyond limit. You should try to correct it, i.e. you should adopt good deeds and avoid evil deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
