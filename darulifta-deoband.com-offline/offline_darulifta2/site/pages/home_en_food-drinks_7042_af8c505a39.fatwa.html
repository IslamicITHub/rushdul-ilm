    Title: What about killing the animals in Islamic sharia for non vegetarian food please clearify this qustion?

    Question: What about killing the animals in Islamic sharia for non vegetarian food please clearify this qustion?

    Answer ID: 7042Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 807/807=M/1429)

     

    If the lawfully eatable animals were slaughtered as per the method prescribed by Shariah then it is lawful to eat it. It is allowed to slaughter or hunt the lawful animals for food according to Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
