    Title: I am studying in sweden and while studying working part time in Mc donalds restaurant.Its a cleaning job where we have to clean all the utensils,these utensils contain meat,beef (sometimes pork also).so my question is whether it is permissible to wash those utensils wearing a gloves. Also whether this work is permissible or not and the money obtain through this work is halal or haram.

    Question: as salam alaikum, I am studying in sweden and while studying working part time in Mc donalds restaurant.Its a cleaning job where we have to clean all the utensils,these utensils contain meat,beef (sometimes pork also).so my question is whether it is permissible to wash those utensils wearing a gloves. Also whether this work is permissible or not and the money obtain through this work is halal or haram.

    Answer ID: 4291Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 599/559=L/1429)

     

    The job is allowable and the earning is also lawful. But, this job is not good, look for any other job and leave it when you find any other suitable job.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
