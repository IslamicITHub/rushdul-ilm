    Title: I have divorced a girl for no fault of hers a small fight led to talaq as i was upset cause she teased me by taking some other guys name,as now we both r married again to different people i always feel guilty of what i have done pls help me.

    Question: I have divorced a girl for no fault of hers a small fight led to talaq as i was upset cause she teased me by taking some other guys name,as now we both r married again to different people i always feel guilty of what i have done pls help me.

    Answer ID: 1955Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 630/625=M)

     

    If the girl was faultless and you hurt her by giving her talaq, so now you should ask her forgiveness and keep on repenting to Allah as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
