    Title: I listen the bayaans of Moulana Tariq Jameel while listning i will have ask myself that inshallah i will follow right path after some time as i get into my people i will forget and starts the life as usual will u plz give me azkaar or some thing else that i shall follow the right path. thanking you.

    Question: As salam alaikum, Respected ulama of deoband, I listen the bayaans of Moulana Tariq Jameel while listning i will have ask myself that inshallah i will follow right path after some time as i get into my people i will forget and starts the life as usual will u plz give me azkaar or some thing else that i shall follow the right path. thanking you.

    Answer ID: 7616Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1785/267=B/1429)

     

    First of all, you should get determined to follow the Straight Path and seek forgiveness from Allah for your past life. Get company of any pious and religious aalim and pray to Allah reciting the below dua sincerely with present of mind:  

    اللھم حبب الینا الایمان و زینہ فی قلوبنا کرہ الینا الکفر والفسوق والعصیان واجعلنا من الراشدین

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
