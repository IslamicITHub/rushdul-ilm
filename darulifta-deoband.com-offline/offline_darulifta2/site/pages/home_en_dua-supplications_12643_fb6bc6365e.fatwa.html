    Title: as salaam muftisaheb please give me dua or zikhar to become famous and belovedd in family;reliates and friends and protect from enymies

    Question: as salaam muftisaheb please give me dua or zikhar to become famous and belovedd in family;reliates and friends and protect from enymies

    Answer ID: 12643Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 731/547=L/1430)

     

    Proceed to do good deeds and try to follow the sunnah. In-sha-Allah, you will be loved by family members, relatives and friends. In order to protect yourself from enemies, recite the below mentioned verse 11 times after every salah:

    ربنا لا تجعلنا فتنۃ للقوم الضالین ونجنا برحمتک من القوم الکافرین

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
