    Question: Asalaamu Alakum, I work in a software company here in India - Some days back, I saw in my dream my manager telling me to resign from the job(Lay off) after this dream I am perplexed as to what is this dream about- 1) Is it the indication I Should resgin from job 2) Is this dream Satan's whisper to cause distress 3) I am completely confused and I am praying Allah (Rabbul izzath) to guide me to the right path. Pls help in the interpretation of this dream. Jazakallah. Abdullah

    Answer ID: 8947Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2016/1866=D/1429)

     

    You must not resign if there is seemingly no problem with you in the employment. If your job in the software company is within lawful limits then you should continue it. The dream is satanic; do not pay any attention to it. Also, keep on praying to Allah to make the employment a source of blessings and good fortune for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
