    Question: My question is i want love marrige it is right or wrong she is muslim but she is khan i am sheikh my parents object it because the difference of cast what can i do?

    Answer ID: 2534Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1732/1528=B)

     

    As per the experience, love marriages do not last long, after some time the love is replaced by differences. And, in case of parents? disapproval the marriage is bereft of blessings of parents. You should stay obedient to your kind and loving parents, and marry according to their wishes without interference. There is none well-wisher of children more than their parents, whatever they will decide it will be better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
