    Title: What if someone misses years of prayers, including during Ramadan fasts, does he have to make all prayers and all Ramadan fasts?

    Question: What if someone misses years of prayers, including during Ramadan fasts, does he have to make all prayers and all Ramadan fasts?

    Answer ID: 157976Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 379/330/SD=4/1439)

     

    Yes, it is mandatory to make qaza of all the fardh or wajib (witr) prayers and all the fardh fasts which you missed after calculating them; otherwise it shall remain due on you. It is not enough to make taubah and pay fidyah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
