    Title: Hair Cutting Salon

    Question: Is it permissible for Muslim to open hair cutting salon for men? Please reply in the light of hadith.

    Answer ID: 45736Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 988/658/L=1434) A Muslim may open salon shop. However, it is haram as per the Shariah to shave one’s own or other’s beard. Hence it shall be haram for barber to shave the beard as well as the income derived from it shall not be halal. It is also not free from being makrooh to do fashionable or non Shariah haircut.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
