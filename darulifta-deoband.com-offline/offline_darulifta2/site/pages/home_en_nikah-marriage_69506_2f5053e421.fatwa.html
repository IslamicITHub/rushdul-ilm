    Title: Marriage in the month of Muharram

    Question: Is this permissible to marry in month of Muharram please explain with Hadith and Quranic verses?

    Answer ID: 69506Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1082/1067/M=12/1437)

    It is lawful to marry in the month of Muharram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
