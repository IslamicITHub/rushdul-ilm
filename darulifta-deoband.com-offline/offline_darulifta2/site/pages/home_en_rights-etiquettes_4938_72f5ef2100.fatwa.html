    Question ID: 4938Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Allah stated in Quran in surah AL-Ahqaf verse#15"AND WE HAVE ENJOINED MAN TO BE DUTIFUL AND KIND TO HIS PARENTS."does this imply only on man not woman,I want to know the duties of woman towards her parents.

    Question: Asslam-u-Alaikum, Allah stated in Quran in surah AL-Ahqaf verse#15"AND WE HAVE ENJOINED MAN TO BE DUTIFUL AND KIND TO HIS PARENTS."does this imply only on man not woman,I want to know the duties of woman towards her parents. Jazakallahu-khairan.

    Answer ID: 4938Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1049/981=D -1429)

     

    The word insan includes both men and women. Like men, the women are also asked to fulfil the rights pertaining parents such serving them financially, physically, obeying them and avoiding to hurt them. Some daughters are negligent of these things and they keep themselves away and put the burden of this responsibility upon their sister in laws (brother?s wives).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
