    Title: I would need to know if I can send a poor man/woman on Alms (Zakat money) to hajj. for example if my teacher or friend is a poor person and he loves to go to Hajj but he does not have any money to go there, can I submit Alms (Zakat money) to send him/or her to Hajj?

    Question: Asalamulikum Warahmatullah. Dear Mufti Sahib, I would need to know if I can send a poor man/woman on Alms (Zakat money) to hajj. for example if my teacher or friend is a poor person and he loves to go to Hajj but he does not have any money to go there, can I submit Alms (Zakat money) to send him/or her to Hajj? Wasalam Ahmadullah Ahmadi from kandahar Afghanistan

    Answer ID: 27852Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2116/B=1715/tb=1431)

    They can go to perform hajj with the zakah money if you give it in their possession and make them its owner.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
