    Title: Watching Nabi kareem pbuh in dream but without a beard

    Question: I saw a large gathering of men they were celebrating something and carrying someone on their shoulders. I knew in the dream that the person they were carrying on their shoulders he was the holy Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) but he had no beard. I saw the face very clearly.

    Answer ID: 41477Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1737/1343/B=1433) If you are not having beard then you should grow it. Shaving beard causes pain to the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
