    Title: In our city, after rains the government is giving Rs.2000 to each family as a relief. But I am not affected by rain and there is no damage for my property. Am I elegible to receive the amount of Rs.2000. Please clarify.

    Question: In our city, after rains the government is giving Rs.2000 to each family as a relief. But I am not affected by rain and there is no damage for my property. Am I elegible to receive the amount of Rs.2000. Please clarify.

    Answer ID: 10033Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 63/36=H/1429)

     

    You can receive the amount if as per the rules and conditions set by the government you are eligible for that, otherwise not.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
