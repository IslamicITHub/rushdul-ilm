Can i read dua during ruku(I mean to say after subhana rabial azeem 3 times can i say "subuhun - qudoosun- rabul malaa-ika ti-varrooh") same in sajda (Subhana rabial aala and then "subuhun - qudoosun- rabul malaa-ika ti-varrooh" ? and same in total salah like (after ruku rabana lakl hamd- then " hamdan kaseeran tayiban mubarakan fi"?

    Answer ID: 26527Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1511/1511/M=1431)

    These azkar are related to nawafil, i.e. one can read them in nawafil.
    و ما ورد حمل على النفل منفردا ً كما مر (در مختار)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
