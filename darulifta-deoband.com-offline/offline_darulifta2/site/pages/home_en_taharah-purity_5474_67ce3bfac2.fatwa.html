    Question: I live in uk. my question is since i have started namaz.when im offering any namaz i always feel that my wozo is breaken. some time i do 2 wozo twice. it only happens in namaz. what shall i do?

    Answer ID: 5474Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 976/724=H)

     

    Wuzu will not break only by such feeling. After completing salah, get busy doing your work and do not pay attention to the feeling. By doing so, your problem will be solved within a few days, In-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
