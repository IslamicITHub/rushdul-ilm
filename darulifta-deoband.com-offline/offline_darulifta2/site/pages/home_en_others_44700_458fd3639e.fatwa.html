    Title: Dream

    Question: (1) I have a dream that I am in the terrace of my grandma house (it has been sold), sudden water came fully like flood, I climbed the top of the house and started namaz (niyat bandh kar khade gaye). (2) One night I saw flood came like qayamat all over the world, some person and I got small boat single and was eating pomegranate.

    Answer ID: 44700Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 611/482/D=1434) The meaning is obvious. At present the flood of sins and immodesty has been entered in the houses, so in this time the safety is possible only with the boat of religion and punctuality in salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
