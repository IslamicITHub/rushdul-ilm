    Title: Touching hadith books

    Question: Is wudhu required for touching hadith, fiqh and Islamic books?

    Answer ID: 59104Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 902/926/L=07/1436) Wudhu is not required for touching Fiqh and Islamic books. However it is better to touch in the state of wudhu. Wudhu is mandatory to touch the holy Quran only. ويكره أيضاً للمحدث ونحوه مس تفسير القرآن وكتب الفقه وكذا كتب السنن الى قوله والأصح أنه لا يكره عند أبى حنيفة رحمه الله (كبرى: 52)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
