    Title: A hospital is providing excellent treatment to poor Muslim patients completely free. Can I donate money to this hospital from my zakah?

    Question: A hospital is providing excellent treatment to poor Muslim patients completely free. Can I donate money to this hospital from my zakah?

    Answer ID: 151250Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1047/984/M=8/1438)

    In order to pay the zakah tamlik is a condition i.e. the amount of zakah is given to some eligible person and he is made its owner. You may give your zakah in the possession of some poor Muslim patient then he has the option whether he uses it in his treatment or uses it for his other needs. It is not correct to give zakah money directly to the hospital. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
