    Title: I WANT TO KNOW THAT IT IS LEGAL TO DO LIFE INSURANCE & TO BECOME LIFE INSURANCE AGENT IN THE EYES OF SHARIYAT.

    Question: ASSALAMUALIKUM I WANT TO KNOW THAT IT IS LEGAL TO DO LIFE INSURANCE & TO BECOME LIFE INSURANCE AGENT IN THE EYES OF SHARIYAT.

    Answer ID: 6874Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 710/710=M/1429)

     

    Life insurance consists of interest and gambling which have been declared haram by the Quran. Therefore, it is unlawful to be agent of life insurance.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
