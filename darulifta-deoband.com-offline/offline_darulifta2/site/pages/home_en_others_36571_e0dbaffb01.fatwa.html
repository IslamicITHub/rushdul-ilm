    Question ID: 36571Country: India

    Title: I used to see that I went inside earth (zameen) and there I saw few people unknown

    Question: I used to see that I went inside earth (zameen) and there I saw few people unknown, then I came out from very narrow way to above earth (zameen) in a huge very huge mosque I have never seen such huge mosque. Please tell me the meaning of my dream.

    Answer ID: 36571Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 311/311/M=1433) The dream indicates that there shall come ease after hardship, in-sha-Allah. The condition is that you follow sunnah and good deeds with patience and endurance.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
