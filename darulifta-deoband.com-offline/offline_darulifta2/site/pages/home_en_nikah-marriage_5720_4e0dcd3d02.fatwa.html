    Title: My husband want to drink my breast milk. i m his wife. plz repy me that can i give her my milk? he is so insisting but i m not agree with him.

    Question: My husband want to drink my breast milk. i m his wife. plz repy me that can i give her my milk? he is so insisting but i m not agree with him.

    Answer ID: 5720Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 323/323=M/1429)

     

    It is haram for husband to drink the milk of her wife and obeying husband in unlawful matters is not lawful: لا طاعۃ لمخلوق فی معصیۃ الخالق۔ Therefore, you must not let your husband to drink your milk. If he does so forcefully he will be sinful. Tell him this ruling and show him this fatwa.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
