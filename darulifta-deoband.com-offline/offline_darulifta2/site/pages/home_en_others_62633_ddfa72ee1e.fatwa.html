    Title: My name is sameer and my late grandma came in my dream in morning.

    Question: My grandma who passed away month ago came in my dream. Actually the time of dream was when I slept after Fajr prayer. She was happy in my dream and was trying to feed me something. Somehow I agree but didn't swallowed it. Please tell me what does this means.

    Answer ID: 62633Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 284/282/H=03/1437) Ma-shaAllah it is a nice dream. The meaning is that your grandmother is getting benefit from your good deeds and isal-e-sawab. But there is lack of khushu and khuzu in good deeds as well as lack of sincerity from your end. Try to remove it and pay more attention in sending the rewards to your grandmother.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
