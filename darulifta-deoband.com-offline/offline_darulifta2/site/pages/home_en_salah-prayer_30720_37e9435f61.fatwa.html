    Title: Is It Permissible Offering Namaz Wearing Shoes Can We Do This?

    Question: Assalamalaikum Is It Permissible Offering Namaz Wearing Shoes Can We Do This Pls Reply

    Answer ID: 30720Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 602/395/H=1432)

    If the shoes are entirely clean and you offer salah at your home alone, it is allowable provided the shoes do not have shape that causes problem or inconvenience.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
