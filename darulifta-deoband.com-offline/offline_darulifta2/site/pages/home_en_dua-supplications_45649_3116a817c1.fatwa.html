    Title: Dua for my examination

    Question: My examination is there on 8th of June of B.Tech in Jamia Millia Islamia. Please suggest me a dua by reading that I get pass in this particular examination. Please it is very important exam for me.

    Answer ID: 45649Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 814/637/H=1434) Read the following dua after each salah and before going to bed in the state of wudhu three times with durood 3-3 times before and after and blow on your chest and blow it on your hands and pass your hands over your body: رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ. May you succeed in the said exam and in all your good deeds and save you from evils! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
