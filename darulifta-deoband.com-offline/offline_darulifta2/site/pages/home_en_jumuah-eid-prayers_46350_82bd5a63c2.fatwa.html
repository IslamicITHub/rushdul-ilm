    Question ID: 46350Country: India

    Title: Jumuah Khutba and Speech

    Question: in Hyderabad one Mufti working as an Imam and Khatib of one Masjid, on every Friday he used to give speech (URDU BAYAN) where he works as an Imam (1st Masjid) from 12:40 to 1:10pm, then again he will give same speech in 2nd masjid 1:15pm to 1:45pm, then again he will give same speech in 3rd Masjid 1:50pm to 2:15 pm. So my question is shall we continue to do speech in all Masjids is it permissible according to Shariah (all 3 masjids are in a range of 2 kilometers) (in 3rd Masjid he offers Arabic khutbah and Jumuah salah.

    Answer ID: 46350Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1282/877/L=11/1434) It is makrooh tahrimi to go out of mosque after azan. Hence the practice of the said imam to address in three mosques and in last lead the salah with Khutbah is not correct. He should only address in the mosque where he leads the salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
