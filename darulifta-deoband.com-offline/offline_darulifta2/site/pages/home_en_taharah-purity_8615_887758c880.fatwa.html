    Title: I have attached bathroom, there is no partition between commode and bath shower. Can i perform Wuzu and Ghusal there?

    Question: I have attached bathroom, there is no partition between commode and bath shower. Can i perform Wuzu and Ghusal there?

    Answer ID: 8615Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1346/1140=L/1429)

     

    Yes, you can do wudhu and ghusl provided the water is not splashed after mixing with urine drops and the wudhu water does not flow through the lavatory.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
