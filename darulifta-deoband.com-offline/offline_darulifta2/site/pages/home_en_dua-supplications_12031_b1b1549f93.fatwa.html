    Title: Is there a dua to read so one can get married really soon?

    Question: Is there a dua to read so one can get married really soon?

    Answer ID: 12031Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 524/476=B/1430)

     

    One who wants to get married should recite ya Jami?u (ياجامع) 1111 times any time, while his or her parents should recite  ya Lateef (یا لطیف) 1111 times after the Isha Salah. They shall receive good proposals soon, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
