    Answer ID: 14894Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1186/981=L/1430)

     

    In the abovementioned case, it is better that the boy is politely persuaded to give divorce or give khula? in return of money in a way that his wife will withdraw the mahr and then he will give the talaq in return of the mahr. If he is not ready to give talaq or khula?, the case should be taken to any Shariah Panchayat or Darul Qaza where the nikah will be cancelled in due procedure after enquiry and in case of denial from husband.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
