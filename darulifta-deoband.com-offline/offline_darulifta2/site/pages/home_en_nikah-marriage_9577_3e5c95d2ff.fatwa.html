    Title: Is Waleema permissible only after a man's first nikah or he can have a waleema each time that he divorces his previous wife and remarries again.

    Question: Is Waleema permissible only after a man's first nikah or he can have a waleema each time that he divorces his previous wife and remarries again.

    Answer ID: 9577Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2514/2068=B/1429)

     

    Walimah is not related specially with ones first Nikah, rather it is sunnah to give walimah feast each time one marries.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
