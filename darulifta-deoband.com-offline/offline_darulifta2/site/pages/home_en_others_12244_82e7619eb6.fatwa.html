    Question: Assalamu alaikum i have seen a dream in which i was traveling on the sea everyone was talking about the ship which is biggest in the world then suddenly i have seen a fish came out of the water which is bigger then the ship and it passed before me and i woke up Q2: i came after isha prayer from mosque and i slept, in the dream i saw a janaza of the person which is burring into the kabar i felt it was in saudi and i was standing there some one told me in my ears that he is going to jannat i felt those are not humans i understood that was my father kindly interpret the above two dreams thank you

    Answer ID: 12244Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 817/682=D/1430)

     

    Both the dreams are nice. Seeing fish in dream indicates to gain worldly honour and benefit. The second dream indicates that your father was granted forgiveness. You should strive hard to follow the Shariah and avoid sins, and perform isal-e-thawab (delivering the reward to dead) through tilawah etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
