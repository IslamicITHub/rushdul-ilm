    Title: I came back from Gujarat to Mumbai at 4:40 am. While Subhe-Saadiq starts at 4:55 am. I made Wudu and want to perform Isha Qaza because in prepearation of Namaz times of Sehr ended. So in this case should I perform Isha half (2) or full (4) Rakats? Like this if any one reaches his destination when the sun is to set, should he perform full or do Qasr?

    Question: I came back from Gujarat to Mumbai at 4:40 am. While Subhe-Saadiq starts at 4:55 am. I made Wudu and want to perform Isha Qaza because in prepearation of Namaz times of Sehr ended. So in this case should I perform Isha half (2) or full (4) Rakats? Like this if any one reaches his destination when the sun is to set, should he perform full or do Qasr?

    Answer ID: 2056Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1874/1438=H)

     

    If Mumbai is your native place or you intend to stay there for 15 days or more and you enter in Mumbai at 04:40 then your journey came to an end and you became muqeem. Now, you have to offer four rakats in the above mentioned situation.

     

    (2) It is not necessary to reach ones destination but if he enters in the city after sunset then he will do qasr and if he reached before sunset and the salah was missed then he will pry full salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
