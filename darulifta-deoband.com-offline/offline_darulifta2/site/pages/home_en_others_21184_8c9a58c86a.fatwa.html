    Answer ID: 21184Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 761/583/H=1431)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. It is glad tidings for you, your parents, uncles and all relatives. In-sha-Allah you will receive in hereafter the special favour of shifa'at (intercession) of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم), while in this world you will get humbleness, modesty, love of poor, simplicity and maximum taufiq from Allah in following sunnah. May you get fully the blessing of this dream!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
