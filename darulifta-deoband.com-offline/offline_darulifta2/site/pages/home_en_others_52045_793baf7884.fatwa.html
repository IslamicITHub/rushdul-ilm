    Title: Dream interpretation

    Question: I am Golam Kibria from Dhaka, Bangladesh. Last night, I saw a dream that one of uncle/relative is coming to the airport, I could not able to recognize him, maybe he is my elder uncle, I saw one small girl with him, I thought that he is one of my cousins, but not sure. After some times, after receiving him I said him that it is time to prayer, so let us pray. Then I was making ablution/wudhu, during this time another person came and said to me to give place to make him wudhu from another tap.

    Answer ID: 52045Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 633/629/N=05/1435) One should take great care about salah and should not miss it. Also one should care for others betterment and comfort.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
