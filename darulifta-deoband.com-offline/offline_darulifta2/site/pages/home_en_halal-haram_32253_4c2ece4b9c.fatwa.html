Both company are taking resistration charge for one year and after that they give online job we get income after deduction of goverment tax Our Question is that is this inincomeis legle as islamaic view

    Answer ID: 32253Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 936/L=349/TL=1432) After studying the details of the survey companies, we concluded that their system is not based on Shariah principles; hence, one should avoid being a member and getting benefit.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
