    Question: AAWW! Sometime back I saw a dream and I would be grateful if u could give me tabeer for the same. I saw that I am standing just outside my house in the lane. Some lions are approaching me from the other end. I am a little scared. But when they come close to me, the lions become meek and submissive. And I realise that though I was afraid of them, they are actually here to help and protect me. Soon, I see these lions stand next to me to counter the lions coming from the opposite direction. Pl guide with a tabeer.

    Answer ID: 24716Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1257/1006/D=1431)

    It indicates towards safety from the enemies. In-sha-Allah, you shall be saved from the wickedness of enemies with Allah’s protection.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
