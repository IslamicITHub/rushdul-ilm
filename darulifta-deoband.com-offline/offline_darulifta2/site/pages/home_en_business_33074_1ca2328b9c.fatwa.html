    Title: income tax prectice

    Question: I am an advocate doing practice of income tax in my profession. Whether it is haram or not. I can calculate tax on income of interest and other income, business income of my client. I request please advise me.

    Answer ID: 33074Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1149/965/B=1432) If you have not to be involved in telling lies and cheating in this profession, then it is allowable for you to do this job.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
