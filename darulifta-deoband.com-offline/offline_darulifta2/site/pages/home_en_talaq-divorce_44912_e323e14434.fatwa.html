    Title: working in khula period

    Question: (1) I am a doctor [second year MD student]. Can I go to my college and hospital in iddah period after taking khula? (2) What is my correct iddah period after khula?

    Answer ID: 44912Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 576/458/H=1434) (1) It is not lawful to go to college or hospital after khula during iddah period. (2) In case of pregnancy, iddah is over when the woman gives birth to the child; otherwise the iddah is to complete three haiz (menstruating cycle) after khula. If the khula was done during menses, then that particular period shall not be counted rather iddah shall start from the first period after khula takes place.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
