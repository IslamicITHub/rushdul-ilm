    Question: 1. One night my wife saw a dream that she is performing namaz. What is mean of that dream. 2. Other day she saw she is reading Yashin Surah in Dream. 3. Again few days after she saw she is going for Hajjo- Umara by aeroplain, plain stop at sky and she walk on clouds for some time, after that she again come to plain for going to makkah. Please send the meaning of these dreams.

    Answer ID: 22400Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 783/616/D=1431)

    (1 & 2) It is nice and blessed dream. In-sha-Allah, you will get tawfeeq of good deeds. And you will be honoured with knowledge and wisdom.
    (3) In-sha-Allah by practicing good deeds you will get firmness in religion and success in the world. Thank Almighty Allah and supplicate for firmness on good deeds with utmost efforts.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
