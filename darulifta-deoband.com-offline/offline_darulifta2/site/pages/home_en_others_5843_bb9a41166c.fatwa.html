    Question: Assalamu alaikum, I have a question about dreams, I almost daily see Masjid in my dream, someting i will be doing Wazu, sometime i will be giving Azan and sometime will be prearing for Namaz and sometimes wll be doing Imamath, even if the dream is good or bad i see atleast building of masjid in my dream, I am really concerned about, I want to know the meaning of it, or is Allah SWT expecting me to do something, please brief me, About me, i am around 26 years, and I am not very paband on 5 times prayer, will pary 4 times , sometimes 2 times, but i feel a lot that i am not able to pray 5 times, sometimes while sleeping i get tears thinking of me that why I am like this, why cant i pray 5 times namaz.

    Answer ID: 5843Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    Fatwa: 1294/1294=H/1429)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah, the dreams are good, their meaning is that you should control the self i.e. keep doing good deeds specially perform all times salah punctually, refrain from sins, particularly do not to miss salah. According to your status and on suitable occasions, you should serve the mosque, though wealth and hands, and clean it. Thus Allah wants to grant you countless bounties in the world and the hereafter. You just need to have guts.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
