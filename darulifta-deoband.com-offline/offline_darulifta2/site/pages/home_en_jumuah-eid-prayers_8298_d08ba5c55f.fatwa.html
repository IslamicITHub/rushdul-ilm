    Answer ID: 8298Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2085/1664=/1429)

     

    Doing musafaha (shaking hands) and hugging specially after Eid prayer is said to be the custom of the Shias. Neither after Eid is a time for musafaha nor after Fajr and Asr; this is a Shiite custom. We should not follow these customs. However, there is no wrong to say 'Eid Mubarak' to each other. It is lawful to do musafaha with one hand as well, but it is better to do it with both the hands.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
