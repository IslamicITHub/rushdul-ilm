    Title: I have a problem related to nikah me and my mother is ready to marry with my Antis girl (khala ki ladki)she is always ready to marry me but my father is not ready then what to do in this situation?

    Question: Moualana I have a problem related to nikah me and my mother is ready to marry with my Antis girl (khala ki ladki)she is always ready to marry me but my father is not ready then what to do in this situation?

    Answer ID: 26534Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1626/1164/L=1431)

    In the above-mentioned circumstance, make your father ready for nikah. Give his consent first priority and do not take any step for nikah without his permission. In it lies the welfare of the world and hereafter. In doing nikah by making your father angry, there is the loss of this world and hereafter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
