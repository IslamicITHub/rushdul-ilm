    Title: How must a muslim person treat his parents if they are murtad?

    Question: How must a muslim person treat his parents if they are murtad?

    Answer ID: 767Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 252-252=L)

     

    If parents of a Muslim turn murtad (apostate) he should neither have a relation with them nor eat or drink with them:

    ولاترکنوا الی الذین ظلموا فتمسکم النار الآیہ وفی الکشاف: والنہی متناول لہ الخطاط والانقطاع الیہم ومصاحبتہم ومجالستہم الخ (الکشاف:۲/۲۹۶)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
