    Title: Name Meaning

    Question: My name is Mohammed Ismail Shariff. My daughter born on march 15th 2013, Islamic date is 3rd of Jumada I, timing 8:15 pm. My first daughter’s name is Ameena Fariyal and my wife’s name is Arshiya Sultna. I want to keep second daughter name Afiya Kulsum. Please suggest me right name.

    Answer ID: 45068Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 693/671/N=1434) Afiya Kulsum is a good name for girl, you may keep it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
