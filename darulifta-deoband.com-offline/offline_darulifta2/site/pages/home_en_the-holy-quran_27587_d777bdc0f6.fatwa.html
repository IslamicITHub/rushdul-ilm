    Title: Is it allowed for any body to listen to quran when he/she is in a state of Zanabat? Also, is it allowed to listen to quran when any woman is undergoing monthly menstruation cycle?

    Question: Is it allowed for any body to listen to quran when he/she is in a state of Zanabat? Also, is it allowed to listen to quran when any woman is undergoing monthly menstruation cycle?

    Answer ID: 27587Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1805/1312/L=1431)

    Listening the recitation of the holy Quran in the state of Janabah or menstruation is not forbidden. A junubi (person in the state of impurity) or menstruating woman can hear the recitation of the holy Quran.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
