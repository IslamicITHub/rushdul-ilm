    Title: why we write word Hazrat or August with some names and not with others?

    Question: why we write word Hazrat or August with some names and not with others?

    Answer ID: 4206Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 282/282=M/1429)

     

    Hazrat or August is respectful titles and codes which are used customarily for elders. Since, we have been ordered to revere elders; therefore the personalities, according to their positions and ranks, are given respectful codes. And those, who are not regarded as elders, no title is written for them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
