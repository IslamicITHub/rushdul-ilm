    Answer ID: 23378Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1301/985/H=1431)

    It is feared that your wife will meet some loss as a result of the carelessness of your sister-in-law. She should try to gain the highest rank of taqwa and modesty.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
