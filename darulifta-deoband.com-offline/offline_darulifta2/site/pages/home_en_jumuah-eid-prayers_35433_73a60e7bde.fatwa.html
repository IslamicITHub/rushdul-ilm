    Question ID: 35433Country: India

    Title: We are from Air force situated at Delhi area. Our duty time ends at 1430 hrs, so for everybody’s convenience we have kept the Jumuah khutba at 1450 hrs

    Question: We are from Air force situated at Delhi area. Our duty time ends at 1430 hrs, so for everybody’s convenience we have kept the Jumuah khutba at 1450 hrs, but somebody said that khutba namaz cannot be offered so lately. Please, guide for mass benefit.

    Answer ID: 35433Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1907/1537/B=1432) The Jumuah time starts from zawal and ends when the Asr time starts. It is better to offer Jumuah just after the zawal. However, if it is offered at 2:00 or 2:30 due to some reasons, it is absolutely lawful. One who suggested that khutbah salah cannot be offered so late is wrong. You can offer Jumuah Salah at 2:30 or 2:45. Your khutbah as well as Salah shall be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
