    Title: Suppose there are two hadith that contradict each other like saying Amen loudly or silently

    Question: Suppose there are two hadith that contradict each other like saying Amen loudly or silently. In such cases what aspects does our great scholar like Imam Abu Hanifa consider?

    Answer ID: 39202Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 934/934/M=1433) It is better to say Amen silently as it is a dua and silence is best in dua as it is mentioned in the holy Quran: ادْعُواْ رَبَّكُمْ تَضَرُّعاً وَخُفْيَةً Also it is mentioned in hadith: خير الدعاء الخفي.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
