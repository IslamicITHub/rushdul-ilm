    Title: What is the opinion of the islamic jurists about the effects of masturbation on fasting?

    Question: What is the opinion of the islamic jurists about the effects of masturbation on fasting?

    Answer ID: 2357Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1297/178=B)

     

    Masturbation nullifies fast and makes it invalid. One has to make qaza (makeup) it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
