    Title: If anybody intercourse with his wife without giving denmohar,what will be the condition of their child?will it be the legal or illegal child?

    Question: if anybody intercourse with his wife without giving denmohar,what will be the condition of their child?will it be the legal or illegal child?

    Answer ID: 24629Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1245/1245/M=1431)

     

    According to Shariah, if the child is born after six months of nikah, it will be legitimate and his lineage will be proved, whether the husband paid the mahr of wife or not. The payment of mahr has nothing to do with the legitimacy of child.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
