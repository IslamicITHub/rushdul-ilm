    Question ID: 156834Country: India

    Title: Marriage hindrance

    Question: I am 28 year old girl. My father is no more since 2 years. I have dreamed about a strange event two three days ago ie: (1) There was a dinner going on I don't remember about the people with me over there but the dinner was poisoned. I was so scared as I had been given that dinner although I think I took that but couldn't eat and due to my scariness I woke up. It was Fajr namaz time after offering namaz when I again fell asleep I dreamed that I am preparing to move towards examination hall hardly and finally after doing great efforts I reached to the examination hall despite of keeping roll no into my bag. I was unable to find it n the exam seemed to be begin. After sometime I found the roll number and tried to search the classroom and finally reached there and started paper as well then my dream broke down (I always see this kind of dream).

    Answer ID: 156834Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 353/319/L=3/1439)

     

    These seem to be satanic dreams. Go to bed in the state of wudhu and read four Quls (Surah Kafirun, Surah Ikhlas, Surah Falaq and Surah Nas) before going to bed and blow on both the hands and pass them over your body. Repeat it thrice. In-shaAllah, gradually you will stop seeing such dreams.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
