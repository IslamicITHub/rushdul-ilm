    Title: We want to go to perform umrah but i doubt that my wife may have Menstrual Cycles (Monthly Period)during that time. actually we can not avoid to go during those days. What is fatwa for this case?

    Question: We want to go to perform umrah but i doubt that my wife may have Menstrual Cycles (Monthly Period)during that time. actually we can not avoid to go during those days. What is fatwa for this case?

    Answer ID: 22876Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 926/926/M=1431)

    In order to stop menstruation during this period if you want to use any medicine etc then there is no harm in it according to Sharia, but it should be done with the advice of a doctor. If it is harmful according to medical perspective and your stay at Makkah is too short and it is difficult to postpone tawaf till the menstruation stops, then in this case put off umrah plan.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
