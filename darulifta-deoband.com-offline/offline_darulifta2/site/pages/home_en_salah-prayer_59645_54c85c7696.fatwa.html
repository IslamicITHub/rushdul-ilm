    Question ID: 59645Country: India

    Title: Position of rukuh while offering nawafil after witr

    Question: As we know that offering nafl after witr was the practice of the Holy Prophet Muhammad, may peace and blessings of Allah be upon him. So how to do ruku while offering nafl while sitting at no health problem so to say if one is not ma’azur.

    Answer ID: 59645Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 726/722/B=08/1436) يطاطأ راسه مع انحناء الظهر يسيرا . One should bow one’s head in ruku and bend the back slightly. Some people go to an extent that they become closer to the position of sajdah which is not correct.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
