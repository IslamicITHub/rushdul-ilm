    Question: I want lots of children (boys) and also Allah make them Alim. That is my own demand from Allah. Is this prayer (dua) correct?

    Answer ID: 39019Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1095/918/B=1433) Yes, your dua is correct. May Almighty Allah accept your dua and fulfil your desire and ambition! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
