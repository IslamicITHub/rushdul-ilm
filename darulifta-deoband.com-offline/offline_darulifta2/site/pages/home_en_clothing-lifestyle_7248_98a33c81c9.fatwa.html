    Title: Is it jaayaz to wear saree and churi(bangals)? is embroidry or any other design is permitted on hijab?

    Question: Assalu Aalaikum, Is it jaayaz to wear saree and churi(bangals)? is embroidry or any other design is permitted on hijab?

    Answer ID: 7248Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 856/856=M/1429)

     

    If it is covering the body completely and is not characteristic to a particular religious group then it is allowed to wear sari. The women can wear bangles as well.

    (2) Embroidery or design is against the spirit of hijab, therefore it is not right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
