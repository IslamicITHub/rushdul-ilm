    Title: Dream at Tahujjud time

    Question: Few weeks back, I saw a dream before Fajr salah time, in the dream I saw that holy Prophet (PBUH) praying nafl or sunnah salah, I tried to stand behind Prophet (PBUH) and pray nafl or sunnah salah, then my brother came and tried to move me so that he can pray, but he left and I stand to pray. Can you please interpret this dream?

    Answer ID: 45338Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 716/560/H=1434) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. The meaning is that in-shaAllah both of you (brothers) shall get the taufiq of good conduct and following sunnah. May you get full benefit from the blessings of the dream! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
