    Title: Doll statues with face covered with cloth

    Question: Can we keep face covered doll/animal statues/teddy bears in house for decorating purpose? Note: Only face will be covered with clothe but body of the doll will be visible.

    Answer ID: 156286Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 323/275/H=2/1439)

     

    Merely by covering the face with cloth the sin of keeping the picture shall not be avoided. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
