    Title: Is it aloud according to the Hanafi Madhab to make duaa in English language (using translation of wordings that are mentioned in the Qur'an and Sunnah or that which is not normally possible to ask other humans for) in the last Tashahhud of salaat (fard & nafila) before the taslim? 2. Is it aloud according to the Hanafi Madhab to make duaa in English language (using translation of wordings that are mentioned in the Qur'an and Sunnah or that which is not normally possible to ask other humans for) in the Sajdah of salaat (fard & nafila) after having said three times; Subhana Rabbiyal Ala ? 3. Which dhikr or dua must one say between two sajdah's in the Hanafi Madhab?

    Question: As salamu aleykum, 1. Is it aloud according to the Hanafi Madhab to make duaa in English language (using translation of wordings that are mentioned in the Qur'an and Sunnah or that which is not normally possible to ask other humans for) in the last Tashahhud of salaat (fard & nafila) before the taslim? 2. Is it aloud according to the Hanafi Madhab to make duaa in English language (using translation of wordings that are mentioned in the Qur'an and Sunnah or that which is not normally possible to ask other humans for) in the Sajdah of salaat (fard & nafila) after having said three times; Subhana Rabbiyal Ala ? 3. Which dhikr or dua must one say between two sajdah's in the Hanafi Madhab? Was salaam

    Answer ID: 4050Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 919/182=H)

     

    (1) It is not allowed.

    (2) No.

    (3) There is no compulsory dua. However, it is mustahab (desirable) to recite: اللھم اغفرلی وعافنی و اھدنی

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
