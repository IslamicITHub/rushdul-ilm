    Title: Dream Interpretation

    Question: My Aunty (Khala) had 2 dreams. First day she saw that she is near water pond and looking for a way to get out of water. Second day she saw that she is with her daughter who is 5 years old and saw that there is a water pond an old man wearing white dress and has tasbih on her hand and he is blind coming from water with the help of another person, came to her and gave her dates and Miswak. Please interpret this dream. She has been living with her parents for 5 years because her husband is not good.

    Answer ID: 63232Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 253/204/D=04/1437) One should take care to correct one’s deeds and morals and should think good about Allah. It is not correct to stay away from the husband. You should try to make the situation better and maintain patience in trouble and do seek help through supplication.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
