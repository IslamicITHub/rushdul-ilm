    Title: SKIN DISEASE (WARTS ON PERSONAL PART)

    Question: MY AGE IS 30 YRS MALE FROM INDIA, I AM SUFFERING FROM A SKIN DISEASE FROM LAST SEVEN YEARS, I HAVE SOME WARTS (MASSAH)ON MY PERSONAL PART WHICH IS NOT GOING THROUGH MEDICINE AND OTHER TREATMENT. DOCTOR SAYS YOU CAN NOT INTERCOURSE STILL YOU CURE. I AM PLANNING TO MARRY SOON, SO I REQUEST YOU PLEASE TELL ANY WAJIFA FOR CURE THIS DISEASE.IT IS VERY NEEDFUL FOR ME TO CURE UNLESS I UNABLE TO MAINTAIN PHYSICAL RELATION WITH MY WIFE IN FUTURE.

    Answer ID: 32779Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1157/689/D=1432) (1) Supplicate Almighty Allah after offering two rakah salah al-Hajah. (2) Recite Ya Latif 1111 times after Isha salah with durood 11-11 times before and after along with supplication. Do this practice for forty days. (3) Keep on reciting Ya Salam (یا سلام) as much as you can while walking and sitting. In-sha-Allah, you shall get cured from the illness. (4) You would better consult any good hakim as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
