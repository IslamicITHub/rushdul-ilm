    Answer ID: 2141Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1934/1480=H)

     

    (1) It was not allowed. Now, you should repent to Allah and seek His forgiveness.

     

    (2) There is a Dua named Hirz-e-Abi Dujanah in Bahishti Zewar, get it written by saffron or ink, give it the patient to wear it around her neck and ask her to recite Surah Falaq and Surah Naas (قل أعوذ برب الفلق، قل أعوذ برب الناس) with durood three times before and after, blow it on her hands and pass them on her body after every obligatory salah and while going to bed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
