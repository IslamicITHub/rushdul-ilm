    Question ID: 57678Country: India

    Title: Want to be enquried about job problem

    Question: I have a brother . Who has studied to 10th only but age is 22 years. He has got a chance of job in reliance company as a safety officer but he has not done safety engineering. And the interviewer has told us that I will select him to fill the vacancy, to face the interview they will give some safety engineer course related study material a month or two months before the conduct of interview. And they will not select unless they feel that now my brother is perfect for this job. For that they are charging Rs.80000 to make the false required certificates. Now I would like to know whether this amount which will be given to them is halal or haram. Please answer me as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 57678Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 450/444/N=06/1436) It is unlawful to make or get fake certificate as it involves lie and cheating which is unlawful and haram in the Shariah. Also it is not lawful to earn money through it. However, in case someone has got a job on the basis of such certificate then if he is fit for the job and is discharging his assigned duties properly then the salary earned through such job will not be termed as haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
