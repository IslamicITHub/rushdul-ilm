    Title: I belongs to a poor family and my parents not in condition of submitting my college fee. i want to complete my graduation in this condition can i take loan from any bank?and my second question is is scholarship is jayaj in islam which is provided to poor students?

    Question: i belongs to a poor family and my parents not in condition of submitting my college fee. i want to complete my graduation in this condition can i take loan from any bank?and my second question is is scholarship is jayaj in islam which is provided to poor students?

    Answer ID: 27122Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1973/1581/B=1431)

     

    (1) In case, you take loan from the bank you will have to pay interest, which is unlawful and haram.

    (2) It is right for a poor to receive the scholarship that is meant for the poor.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
