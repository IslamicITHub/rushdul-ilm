    Title: While sitting in tashddud some scholers say we have to lift index finger continiously and some say's only when we reach ASHHADU LAILAHAILLALAH , what is correct and which is supporting hadit for these two views?

    Question: While sitting in tashddud some scholers say we have to lift index finger continiously and some say's only when we reach ASHHADU LAILAHAILLALAH , what is correct and which is supporting hadit for these two views?

    Answer ID: 13028Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 831/831=M/1430)

     

    The second way is preferable i.e. raise the fingers at la ilaha and put it down on illallah, it is supported by Hadith and traditions:

    وفي المحيط أنها سنة يرفعها عند النفي ويضعها عند الإثبات وهو قول أبي حنيفة ومحمد وكثرت به الآثار والأخبار فالعمل به أولى ا ه فهو صريح في أن المفتى به هو الإشارة بالمسبحة مع عقد الأصابع على الكيفية المذكورة لا مع بسطها (شامي 2:217)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
