    Question ID: 37427Country: Pakistan

    Title: I am an individual of 45 years of age and seeking employment in a local bank in Pakistan in its security department

    Question: I am an individual of 45 years of age and seeking employment in a local bank in Pakistan in its security department (dealing with security of its branches, CCTV and alarm system etc.) Please guide me if it is halal or haram working in such capacity in a bank?

    Answer ID: 37427Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 354/288/B=1433) There is no harm in doing the job of security in a bank. However, it is unlawful and haram to do interest based calculation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
