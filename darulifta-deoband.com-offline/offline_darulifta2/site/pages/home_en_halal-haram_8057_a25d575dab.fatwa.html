    Title: Q-Is it true that it is Makrooh Tahrimi to enter a church or a Jewish place of worship and other religions places since this is where the shayateen stay, this is according to Albahr Araiq for Ibn Nujaym, can we go in without a very strong overwhelming reason?     Question: Q-Is it true that it is Makrooh Tahrimi to enter a church or a Jewish place of worship and other religions places since this is where the shayateen stay, this is according to Albahr Araiq for Ibn Nujaym, can we go in without a very strong overwhelming reason? Is it true that it is Makrooh Tahrimi?

    Answer ID: 8057Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1076/1076=M/1429)

     

    Yes, it is right that going into church or a temple of Jews or any religion is makrooh tahrimi (undesirable to an extent of haram). One must avoid going to such places:

    و فی الھندیۃ عن التتمۃ: یکرہ للمسلم الدخول فی البیعۃ والکنیسۃ، وانما یکرہ من حیث انہ مجمع الشیاطین لا من حیث انہ لیس لہ حق الدخول۔

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
