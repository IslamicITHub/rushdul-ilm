    Title: I am married in the month of may 2007. i have problem from before marriage. that is while going to urinals after completing that i will be getting the liqued which be like sprem i have the doubt that should i have any problem for fasting and about children. what is solution to this and should i bath after that.

    Question: aslamuailkum I am married in the month of may 2007. i have problem from before marriage. that is while going to urinals after completing that i will be getting the liqued which be like sprem i have the doubt that should i have any problem for fasting and about children. what is solution to this and should i bath after that.

    Answer ID: 2498Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 699/683=L)

     

    The liquid that is discharged after urination and does not cause slackness is wadi and it neither affects the fasting nor does it require ghusl. As far as the productivity problem is concerned, you should see any expert physician.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
