    Title: Asalaamulaikum, Please give me the meaning for the following names: Aameelah,sahla,faariah abbaad,sahl,qais,marwaan JAZAKALLAH

    Question: Asalaamulaikum, Please give me the meaning for the following names: Aameelah,sahla,faariah abbaad,sahl,qais,marwaan JAZAKALLAH

    Answer ID: 13692Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1072/916=D/1430)

     

    Aamilah: a woman who works

    Sahlah: polite, easy, soft soil

    Abbad: One worshipping very much

    Sahal: easy, soft

    Qais: proper name of Majnoon, the lover of Laila

    Marwan: name of a ruler

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
