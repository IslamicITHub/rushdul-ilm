    Question: My quwstion: i have a wife & my sister in low love me & i wish her so i sleep in nighat in sister in law so my wife halal for me or no? You give me ansewr please .

    Answer ID: 1681Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 950/950=J)

     

    The wife does not become Haram by sleeping with sister in law, but it is extremely abhorred by Islam to have intimate relation with sister in law while keeping her sister in marriage. It is Haram and it amounts to invite the anger of Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
