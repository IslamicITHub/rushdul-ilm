    Title: A sick person who is fasting has a needle inserted in the vein which provides glucose to the body is fast valid?

    Question: Assalamualaikum 1-A sick person who is fasting has a needle inserted in the vein which provides glucose to the body is fast valid?

    Answer ID: 7854Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1597/1515=D/1429)

     

    The fast does not break by getting injection and glucose in veins. However, without an exigent need, one should avoid it while fasting.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
