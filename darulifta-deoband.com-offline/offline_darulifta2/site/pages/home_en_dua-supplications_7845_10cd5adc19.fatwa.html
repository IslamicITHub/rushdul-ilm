    Title: Assalam i've some prob in my life first i'm not getting a job tell me some ayats so dat i can recite it in favour of god n pray for getting it.

    Question: Assalam i've some prob in my life first i'm not getting a job tell me some ayats so dat i can recite it in favour of god n pray for getting it.

    Answer ID: 7845Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1828/1556=B/1429)

     

    Recite the below duas excessively:

    لَا اِلَہَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ سُبْحَانَکَ اِنِّیْ کُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِیْنَ

    لا حول ولا قوة إلا باللہ العلي العظیم

    Your problems shall be solved and you shall get job, in-sha-Allah!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
