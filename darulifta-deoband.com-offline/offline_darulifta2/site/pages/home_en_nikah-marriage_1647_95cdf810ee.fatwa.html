    Question: Few days back I have asked question about love marriage that it is allowed in Islam or not. You have asked to tell the condition of love marriage. I am 19 years old and I am in love with girl who is 15 years old. We both love eachother. We have decided to marry in future almost after next 5 years. I think her parents will not allow her to marry me, but my parents will not stop me to marry her. But we both have decided to marry eachother in any case. In such conditon what should I do. Need your prayers, With best regards

    Answer ID: 1647Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 653/657=N)

     

    You should perform istekharah and pray Allah to decide what is better for you. After istekharah, if you feel that marrying her is better then you should send a proposal of marriage to her family in proper way. It is to be noted that, before marriage, it is impermissible to talk to her, see her, touch or exchange gifts.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
