    Title: Bed wetting problem in children

    Question: I am asking this question on behalf of one of my friend. She is a mother of 3 children and all of them have this bed wetting problem. The elder one is now ma-shaAllah almost 9 and the youngest one is 5. 2 girls and one boy. Please tell some dua or wazifah that she can do for them?

    Answer ID: 43782Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 334/L-160/TL=1434) Ask your friend to take some dried-dates and recite surah Muzammil three times with durood 3-3 times before and after and blow on them and give them to her children at the time of going to bed. In-shaAllah, it shall benefit.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
