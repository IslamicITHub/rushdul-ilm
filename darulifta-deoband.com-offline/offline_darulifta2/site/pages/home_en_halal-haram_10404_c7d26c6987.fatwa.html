    Title: assalamualaikum i want to ask that is female circumcision allowed in islam.Is it forbidden or mandatory?

    Question: assalamualaikum i want to ask that is female circumcision allowed in islam.Is it forbidden or mandatory?

    Answer ID: 10404Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 164/119=H/1429)

     

    Neither it is wajib (necessary) nor sunnah. For details see: Fatawa Shami, Kitab Al-Karahiyah

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
