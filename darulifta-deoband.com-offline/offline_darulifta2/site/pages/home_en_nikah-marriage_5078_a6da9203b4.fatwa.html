    Title: Can a man have sex with his two wives at the same time?

    Question: Can a man have sex with his two wives at the same time?

    Answer ID: 5078Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 329/329=M/1429)

     

    Having intercourse with a wife in the presence of second wife is shamelessness and against modesty. Also, it is sin for the wives to see each other?s naked body: وطئ زوجتہ بحضرۃ ضرتھا او امتہ یکرہ الخ

    Yes, one can have sex with both of them by turn separately provided he is permitted to do so by the wife who owns the turn.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
