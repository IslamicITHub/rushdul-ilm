    Question: Dear Mufti Saheb Assalamualaikkum, I am a profeesional Alhamdulillah. During office times Im wearing Inshirt and Topi. After reaching home Ill be changing my dress to Jubba and Amama. Once in markaz one of the "Shura Zimadar" told me to be in topi itself. Dont want to wear Amama. Asking the reason he told me to wear after a long time. Requesting you to suggest me on this regard. I am much disturbed about this fact. May Allah give U Jazay-e-Qair. Wassalam

    Answer ID: 6667Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 944/875=L/1429)

     

    It is Sunnah to wear amamah (turban) and there is no limit of age for the same.

    Why that person is stopping to do so, you should ask him to write down the reason and send it to us.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
