    Title: Can I purchase vehicle or any product in EMI?
     

    Question: Can I purchase vehicle or any product in EMI? For example: If the cost of product is 5000₹ then by opting EMI will have to pay ₹5200 or more. Is EMI permissible in Islam?

    Answer ID: 164380Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1299/973/SN=01/1440)

     

    If you directly deal with the dealer and shopkeeper with no bank or finance company involved in between then it is lawful as per the Shariah to buy any vehicle or goods on installment provided the total price and duration of payment is fixed in the initial meeting of transaction.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
