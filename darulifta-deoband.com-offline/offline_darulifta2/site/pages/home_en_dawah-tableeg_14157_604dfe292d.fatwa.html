    Answer ID: 14157Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1160/963=D/1430)

     

    You are inspired by Islam, may Allah grant you the boon of Islam and make your heart open to accept the same. You should study the book of Hadhrat Maulana Manzoor Nomani named ?Islam kya Hai? It is available in Urdu, Hindi and English in book depots in Delhi, Lucknow and Deoband etc.

    (2) We have no sure knowledge of the matter. The sentence ?kaan fil hind ?? is not from the Quran. For some personality to be prophet, we cannot say with surety just on the basis of doubt and speculation, rather we will keep quite in such matters, the knowledge of the reality rests only in Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
