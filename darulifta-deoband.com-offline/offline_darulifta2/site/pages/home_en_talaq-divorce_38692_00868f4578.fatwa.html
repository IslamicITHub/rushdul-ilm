    Title: A woman is separated with her husband more than one year ago

    Question: A woman is separated with her husband more than one year ago. She never met him in privacy at any time. Now her husband sent her talaq through Shariah Panchayat. My question is that what will be her iddah period and from when it will start?

    Answer ID: 38692Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 959/792/B=1433) Talaq will take place on the same day on which the husband wrote talaq to his wife through Shariah Panchayat. As far as the matter of iddah is concerned if she had stayed after nikah with her husband then the iddah of talaq shall be counted from the day when talaq was written and it shall be over after completing three haiz (menstruation cycle). But if the woman did not meet her husband in privacy after nikah then there is no iddah for such woman.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
