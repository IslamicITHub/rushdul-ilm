    Title: Please, tell that if husband and wife drink jhoota milk of each other some says that the relation will change and it is prohibited (HARAM). i m talking about bufflow milk plz enlight this portion.

    Question: Please, tell that if husband and wife drink jhoota milk of each other some says that the relation will change and it is prohibited(HARAM)i m talking about bufflow milk plz enlight this portion.

    Answer ID: 3798Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 18/18=M)

     

    What people say is wrong, husband and wife can drink leftover (jhoota) milk of each other whether the milk is of buffalo, cow or goat, it does neither affect nikah nor it is prohibited. Yes, drinking of wife's milk is prohibited fro husband.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
