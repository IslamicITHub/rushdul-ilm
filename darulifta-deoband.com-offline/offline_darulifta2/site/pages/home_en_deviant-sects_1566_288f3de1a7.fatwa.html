    Title: Is it allowed to meet shia peple, the ahle tasheeh, go to their home or have meal with them, what islam says about this?

    Question: Is it allowed to meet shia peple, the ahle tasheeh, go to their home or have meal with them, what islam says about this?

    Answer ID: 1566Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 503/498=D)

     

    The beliefs of Shias are contrary to the Ahl-e-Sunnah wal-Jamat, they hold abhorrent and repulsive beliefs regarding the sahaba (companions of the Prophet - Sallallahu Alaihi Walsallam); therefore they are deviant sect. It is not good to befriend and have relation with them. Otherwise, it is allowable to meet them on human ground. And, eating is also allowable if one is sure that the food is free from any impure agent. But, if there is doubt that they have mixed some impure thing, as they are famous for, then one should refrain from eating in their houses.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
