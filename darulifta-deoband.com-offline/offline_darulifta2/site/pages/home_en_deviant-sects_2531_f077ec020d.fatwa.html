    Question: I know about thoughts of Mr Zakir Hussain President of Islamic Research Foundation and translation of quran his thoughts is right or wrong?

    Answer ID: 2531Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1717/1513=B)

     

    We know nothing about Dr Zakir Hussain, his knowledge, his thoughts and his authenticity in translation of the Quran. It is advisable to avoid listening his speeches and translations until his knowledge, research and thoughts are confirmed by any reliable Aalim.
