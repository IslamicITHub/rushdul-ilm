    Title: My mother expired in 2004, my father expired in 2009 both of them by hiba gave some of their properties to their 3 sons ,4 daughters many years before . now 1 son wants to redo the hiba & distribute the properties again acording to quran. is it permitted.

    Question: My mother expired in 2004, my father expired in 2009 both of them by hiba gave some of their properties to their 3 sons ,4 daughters many years before . now 1 son wants to redo the hiba & distribute the properties again acording to quran. is it permitted.

    Answer ID: 12254Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 656/464=L/1430)

     

    If your father gifted the property and distributed it among the three brothers and four sisters and gave in your possession, so the gift was valid. Now, there is no need to redistribute it. But, if he only gifted and did not give the shares to your possession, so the gift was not valid and in this case his property will be distributed among his heirs.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
