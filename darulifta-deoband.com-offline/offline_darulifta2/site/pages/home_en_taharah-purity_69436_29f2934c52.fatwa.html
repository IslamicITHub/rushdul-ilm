    Title: Making wudhu sitting on ground

    Question: If we do wudhu when sitting on the ground but not on a high place then our wudhu will be valid or not? And the water which will be splash while performing wudhu will be pure or not?

    Answer ID: 69436Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 991/815/D=12/1437)

    It is mustahab to make wudhu while sitting on a high place so that one can avoid the splashes of water and can make wudhu comfortably. If one does wudhu while sitting on plain ground then he should avoid the splashes. But these splashes are not impure hence wudhu shall be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
