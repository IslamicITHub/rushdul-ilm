    Question ID: 46172Country: India

    Title: Mahr related

    Question: I got married in May 2005 and at that time my mahr was 75000 Indian rupees. I have not yet paid my wife any amount and now I want to give her the amount kindly let me know that how much I am suppose to pay if I pay now. It was said that its sikka raijul waqt.

    Answer ID: 46172Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1019/678/L=1434) You will have to pay the same amount of mahr which was fixed at that time i.e. INR 75000.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
