    Title: I am borrowing Rs 100000 from a person to invest in a land and after selling it I will give Rs 5-6 thousand as profit to that person within a period of three months. Is it allowable

    Question: I am borrowing Rs 100000 from a person to invest in a land and after selling it I will give Rs 5-6 thousand as profit to that person within a period of three months. Is it allowable?

    Answer ID: 40639Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1173/757/H=1433) It is not allowed as it is interest which is haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
