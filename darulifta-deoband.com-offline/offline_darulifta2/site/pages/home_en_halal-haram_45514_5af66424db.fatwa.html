    Title: about sin in shariath,how clear his sin

    Question: If one man (baligh) he touches to his sister's hand in emotional what is the sin? What is the ruling in Shariah?

    Answer ID: 45514Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 868/689/B=1434) He should repent this act. He should pledge firmly that he will not repeat the sin in future and then should keep on doing taubah and istighfar.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
