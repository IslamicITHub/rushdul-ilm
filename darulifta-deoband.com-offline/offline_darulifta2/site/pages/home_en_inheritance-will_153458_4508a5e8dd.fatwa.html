    Title: Our mother died intestate and we are 3 sons and 7 daughters how to divide the assets please explain.

    Question: Our mother died intestate and we are 3 sons and 7 daughters how to divide the assets please explain.

    Answer ID: 153458Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1113/918/D=11/1438)

     

    In the question mentioned above if the deceased did not leave any other heir except those mentioned above then her property shall be divided into 13 shares, after taking out the expenses of her funeral and the debt if she has taken any. Out of the total, 2-2 shares shall go to each son while each daughter shall get 1-1 share.

     

    قال الله تعالى في كتابه : لِلذَّكَرِ مِثْلُ حَظِّ الأُنثَيَيْنِ [النساء : 11]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
