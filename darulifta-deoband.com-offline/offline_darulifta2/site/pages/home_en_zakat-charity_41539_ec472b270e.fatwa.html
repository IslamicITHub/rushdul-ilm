    Title: What are the clauses one should keep in mind for zakah to close relatives?

    Question: What are the clauses one should keep in mind for zakah to close relatives?

    Answer ID: 41539Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 904/899/N=1433) (1-4) It is lawful rather better to give one’s zakah to a near or distance relative such as brother, sister, nephew, niece, bhanja, bhanji, uncle, phuphi, khala, and cousin brother and sister etc except mother, father, grandfather, grandmother, nana, nani and their children and the children of their children i.e. usul and furu provided they are entitled to receive zakah i.e. they are non-Sayyid, poor and needy. While giving them zakah one should intend to help them along with the intention of zakah as one shall get more reward with this intention.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
