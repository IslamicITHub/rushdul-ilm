    Title: 1)please do dua for sisters marriage. kindly tell all the wazaif 2)all wazaif for seeking education

    Question: 1)please do dua for sisters marriage. kindly tell all the wazaif 2)all wazaif for seeking education

    Answer ID: 21203Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 575/380/L=1431)

     

    (1) Ask your sisters to recite ya Latif (يا لطيف) 1111 times after Isha salah with durood after and before 11-11 times,

    (2) Recite رب زدنى علما extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
