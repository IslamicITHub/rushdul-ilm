    Question ID: 35728Country: Bangladesh

    Title: Mufti sahib, my husband Mr. Zakir Hossain living with his first wife after divorce (Fatwa: 1297/1094/B=1432). Now he lives with us 4 days and go to first wife 3 days

    Question: Mufti sahib, my husband Mr. Zakir Hossain living with his first wife after divorce (Fatwa: 1297/1094/B=1432). Now he lives with us 4 days and go to first wife 3 days, I have showed your fatwa but he said he has no way, he must has to go there if hell is fixed for him too. As a pious lady I am continuing my family life only for Allah's order. But I cannot tolerate this situation. If a man does not obey Allah's law, how can I live with him? In the rest of life I have to tolerate this? I think now I am living with a ??????.. I have a son, also I am pregnant now. It is mental pressure for me. Will ALLAH not punish that guy? Once Zakir Hossain cannot tolerate his first wife, but now he is going mad for her. Now he wants to leave us for them. What can I do now? We have heard that lady goes for black magic (tawiz). Would you please pray for my husband's hidayah? Could you give us any dua so that I can get relieve from this problem?

    Answer ID: 35728Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 53/40/B=1433) Keep on reciting the following dua extensively: اَللّهُمَّ اِنَّا نَجْعَلُكَ فِى نُحُوْرِهِمْ وَنَعُوْذُبِكَ مِنْ شُرُوْرِهِمْ . Also keep on reciting لا حول ولا قوة الا بالله In-sha-Allah, you shall get rid of the problem.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
