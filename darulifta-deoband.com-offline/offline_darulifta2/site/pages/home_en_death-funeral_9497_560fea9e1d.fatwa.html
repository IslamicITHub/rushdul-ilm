    Title:

    aurton ka mahar kam se kam kitna ho sakta hai? 2}kayamat ki kitni nishaniyan bachi hai? 3}islam me kisi colour ko khasiat hai kya ya islam ka koi colour hai kya green colour ko itna jyda kyon importance hai hadith se sabit hai kya?

    Question: aurton ka mahar kam se kam kitna ho sakta hai? 2}kayamat ki kitni nishaniyan bachi hai? 3}islam me kisi colour ko khasiat hai kya ya islam ka koi colour hai kya green colour ko itna jyda kyon importance hai hadith se sabit hai kya?

    Answer ID: 9497Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2191/2026=D /1429)

     

    (1) The minimum amount of mahr is ten dirham (30.5 gm silver).

    (2) Allah knows the best.

    (3) It is mentioned in Hadith that one should wear white dress as it is among the best dresses. One should wear white clothes in his life and the dead should also be buried in white dresses. (Khasail-e-Nabvi)  So according to Hadith, wearing white dress is better.

    What do you mean by the importance of green colour? Please, explain it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
