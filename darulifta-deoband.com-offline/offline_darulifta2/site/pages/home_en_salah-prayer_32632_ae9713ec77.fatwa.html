    Question ID: 32632Country: Pakistan

    Title: Recitation of 1st KALMA after FARZ namaz

    Question: Is it allowed to utter 1st kalma lowdly immediately after FARZ NAMAZ as is done in some masajid in Pakistan. Kindly also clarify the logic they have for this act. I find it very inconvenient if I join the namaz late and have to complete my farz namaz.

    Answer ID: 32632Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 965/804/B=1432) We do not find the proofs of reciting First Kalimah just after salah loudly in the books of hadith and Fiqh; hence you would better ask them why they read it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
