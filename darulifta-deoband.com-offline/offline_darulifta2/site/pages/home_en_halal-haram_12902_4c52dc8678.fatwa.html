    Answer ID: 12902Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 750/582=L/1430)

     

    Yes, your job is permissible and the income earned through it is lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
