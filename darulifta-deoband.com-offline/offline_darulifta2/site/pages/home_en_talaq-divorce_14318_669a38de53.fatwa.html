    Answer ID: 14318Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1099/L=207/TL/0)

     

    If, with the intention of giving talaq, you send SMS writing talaq, talaq, talaq thrice, your wife will get divorced with the three mughallazah (irrevocable) talaqs and she became haram for you. Now, you can not rejoin without a valid halalah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
