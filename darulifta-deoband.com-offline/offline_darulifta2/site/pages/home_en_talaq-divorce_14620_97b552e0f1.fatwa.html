    Answer ID: 14620Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1088/888=L/1430)

     

    By the abovementioned sentence ?TUMHARE TARAH TUMHARE PARIWAR KI LUG AGOR MUJHSE TALLAQ CHAHE, TO HUM TALLAQ DENE KE LIYE RAJI HO? no talaq will take place; therefore your nikah with Rezia Begum is intact.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
