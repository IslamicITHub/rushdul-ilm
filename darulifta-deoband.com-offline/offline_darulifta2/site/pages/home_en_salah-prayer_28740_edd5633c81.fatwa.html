    Title: 1) In iqamat when the name of Muhammad comes, one should say sallallahu alaihi wasallam or not? 2) In juma khutba when the name of Muhammad comes, one should say sallallahu alaihi wasallam or not? 3) If we missed 2 or 3 rakaats with jamaat & we join in 3rd or 4th. a) In last rakaat of jamaat if we mistakenly recite durood sharif. b) After salaam of Imam, remaining rakaats we should pray & do such mistake in the remaining sajda sehv would wajib. In both conditions we should do sajda sehv or not?

    Question: Assalam u alaikum 1) In iqamat when the name of Muhammad comes, one should say sallallahu alaihi wasallam or not? 2) In juma khutba when the name of Muhammad comes, one should say sallallahu alaihi wasallam or not? 3) If we missed 2 or 3 rakaats with jamaat & we join in 3rd or 4th. a) In last rakaat of jamaat if we mistakenly recite durood sharif. b) After salaam of Imam, remaining rakaats we should pray & do such mistake in the remaining sajda sehv would wajib. In both conditions we should do sajda sehv or not? JazakAllah

    Answer ID: 28740Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 128/128/M=1432)

    (1) At this time instead of reading Sallallahu Alaihe Wasallam, the same kalimah shahadah should be repeated.
    (2) You can read in your heart.
    (3) If one recites durood in the last rakah of salah by mistake it does not make sajdah sahv wajib. Yes, if after Imam has turned salam, and one stood to complete one’s missed salah and he commits any act that makes sajdah sahv wajib, then sajdah sahv shall be necessary.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
