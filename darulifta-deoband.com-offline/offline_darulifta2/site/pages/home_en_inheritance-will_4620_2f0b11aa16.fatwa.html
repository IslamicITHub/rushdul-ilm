    Question ID: 4620Country: India

    Title: I would be much obliged if you could pleased be kind to inform me under the Islamic Law what are the conditions for ORAL HIBBA, (2) can a person give the entire property by way of Oral hibba. (3) Some one is trying to claim the entire property after the death of the parents.

    Question: Respected Moulan Saheb, Assalam O Alaikum, I would be much obliged if you could pleased be kind to inform me under the Islamic Law what are the conditions for ORAL HIBBA, (2) can a person give the entire property by way of Oral hibba. (3) Some one is trying to claim the entire property after the death of the parents. Many thanksd, May Allah reward you for this.

    Answer ID: 4620Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

     (Fatwa: 917/915=D-1429

     

    (1) Hiba is gifting someone a thing and giving it in his possession. With verbal Hiba, it is necessary to take out the gifted thing from ones possession and give it in the possession of one to whom the gift was made. If only words of Hiba are uttered, but did not give it in his possession then Hiba is invalid.

    (2) It can be given according to abovementioned conditions but if Hiba is made to anyone or any stranger intending to deprive the legal heir, then the man making Hiba will be sinner because of harming heirs.

    (3) Does the person have any legal witness to his claim and evidences of possessing the property in the lifetime of the parents?

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
