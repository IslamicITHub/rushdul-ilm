    Title: Is it permissible to a man who belongs to a caste except Sayyids (ex: Shaikh, Pathan etc) to marry a girl who belongs to a family of Sayyid or Sayyad-us-Sadat? Please reply in the light of hadith

    Question: Is it permissible to a man who belongs to a caste except Sayyids (ex: Shaikh, Pathan etc) to marry a girl who belongs to a family of Sayyid or Sayyad-us-Sadat? Please reply in the light of hadith.

    Answer ID: 36796Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 368/244/D=1433) If the girl and her wali (guardian) agree, then he can do nikah with her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
