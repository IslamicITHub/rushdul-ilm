    Title: I saw this dream on 13.08.2008 late in the morning that i was in the class when someone told me to write/prove a verse of the Quran (a verse confirming scientific findings) then i went to the board and start writing a verse from Surate Anbia 21:30 but i couldn't write it, i misquote the verse 2 or 3 times? then whenever i would try to write the verse the Chalk would break in to pieces and the chalk also resembled a carrot. i made several attempts but couldn't write the verse because the chalk would break in to samll pieces. please give me the interpretation of this dream because i have dedicated my entire life for Quran.

    Question: Asalamu alikum, I saw this dream on 13.08.2008 late in the morning that i was in the class when someone told me to write/prove a verse of the Quran (a verse confirming scientific findings) then i went to the board and start writing a verse from Surate Anbia 21:30 but i couldn't write it, i misquote the verse 2 or 3 times? then whenever i would try to write the verse the Chalk would break in to pieces and the chalk also resembled a carrot. i made several attempts but couldn't write the verse because the chalk would break in to samll pieces. please give me the interpretation of this dream because i have dedicated my entire life for Quran.

    Answer ID: 7182Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1395/1309=D/1429)

     

    The dream indicates that those who misinterpret the Quranic verses to prove the scientific discoveries, which sometimes go against the spirit of the Quran, is futile and unacceptable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
