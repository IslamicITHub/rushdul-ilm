    Title: I am working with a girl who is Esai.but she does not believe in Allah and Rasool. I want to marry her. Can I do this?

    Question: Allah bless you for providing good service. Q. I am working with a girl who is Esai.but she does not bleave in Allah and Rasool. I want to marry her. Can I do this? Please reply me.

    Answer ID: 632Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 612/J)

     

    When the Christian girl does not believe in Allah then she is Christian only by name and she is atheist in reality; therefore you cannot marry her. If you do that the Nikah will not take place and your relation will be considered as adultery and fornication.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
