    Answer ID: 24687Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1604/1266/B=1431)

     

    No talaq took place by any of the above-mentioned sentences, and your nikah is still intact. Do not grow any doubt. Both of you should try your level best to maintain good relation between yourselves. This is termed as perfection of Iman in hadith. You should keep patience.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
