    Title: Is Mussels halal to eat in Ahnaf mazhab, if not why?

    Question: Is Mussels halal to eat in Ahnaf mazhab, if not why?

    Answer ID: 160821Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 894/817/M=8/1439)

     

    By ‘mussels’ if you mean the shell insect which is known as ghongha in Hindi then it is not halal to eat it according to Hanafi Fiqh as only fish is halal among the sea animals, whereas the seashell is an insect.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
