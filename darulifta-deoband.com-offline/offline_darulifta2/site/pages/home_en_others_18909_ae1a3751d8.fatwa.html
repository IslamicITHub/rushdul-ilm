    Question ID: 18909Country: India

    Title: I performed an Istikhara on the seeking guidance to marry my cousin whom i feel is a pious person. In the first dream i saw Myself and her in a car which was being driven very fast.

    Question: Dear Ulemas, I am a 23 year old man doing his MBA from Srinagar, kashmir. I performed an Istikhara on the seeking guidance to marry my cousin whom i feel is a pious person. In the first dream i saw Myself and her in a car which was being driven very fast. Both of us were laughing very heartily but i don't remember who was the driver. We passed through various areas of the city. However, in some places i saw some black men throwing bricks at us. but these could not do any damage to us or the car. In the second dream, I saw her in a truck and she was fully clad in a white dress, which looked like a Thobe(JUBBA) or something. The truck was moving by itself. Could you please interpret?

    Answer ID: 18909Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 113/108=H=1431)

     

    If there is no hindrance in performing nikah, you should go for it in Islamic way. The dream indicates to the same result which was sought in the istikharah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
