    Title: My question is in what way a muslim female can contribute towards the responsibility of dawat n tableeg?

    Question: My question is in what way a muslim female can contribute towards the responsibility of dawat n tableeg?

    Answer ID: 2136Posted on: Dec 18, 2023

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 530/530=L)

     

    A woman can perform the duty of tableeg by assembling the women of the house and neighbours and discussing Islamic matters. She can make them aware of religious rulings and make them listen any reliable book like Bahishti Zewar. A woman should nourish her children in an Islamic way, inculcate the love of Islam in their hearts and make them listen the seerah of Sahaba and pious people. Thus a woman can contribute towards Dawah and Tableegh.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
