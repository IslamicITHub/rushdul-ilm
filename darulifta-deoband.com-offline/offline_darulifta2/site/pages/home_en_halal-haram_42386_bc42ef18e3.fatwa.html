    Title: It is lawful to use homeopathic medicine as it contains alcohol?

    Question: It is lawful to use homeopathic medicine as it contains alcohol?

    Answer ID: 42386Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1111/1113/N=1433) The quantity of alcohol is very less in homeopathic medicines and now many alcohols are made of potato, vegetables and coals etc; hence you can use homeopathic medicines for treatment. It is lawful as per the Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
