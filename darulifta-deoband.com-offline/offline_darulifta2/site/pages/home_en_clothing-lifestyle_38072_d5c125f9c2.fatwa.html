    Title: Can ammonia free dyes that are available in the market be used for the hair

    Question: Can ammonia free dyes that are available in the market be used for the hair?

    Answer ID: 38072Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 684/595/B=1433) If the hair get black with the use of that dye then it shall not be lawful to apply it, but if the hair get red like the colour of henna then it is allowable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
