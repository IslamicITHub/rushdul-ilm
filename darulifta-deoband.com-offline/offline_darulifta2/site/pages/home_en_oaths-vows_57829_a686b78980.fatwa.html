    Title: How many Kaffara have to be paid?

    Question: If a person says that he is taking an oath that he will never ever tell lies in his life and after that he tells lie 3 times, then how many times he has to pay kaffarah for his breaking oath, one time or three times ? He did not take oath again after his breaking oath for the first time.

    Answer ID: 57829Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 353/346/H=05/1436) When he took an oath that he won’t tell a lie then he must have fulfilled it. However, after telling lie first time, he will be required to pay kaffarah and the oath came to an end. و تنحل اليمين إذا وجد الشرط مره (الدر مع الرد 5/605، زكريا) However, it is necessary to avoid telling a lie in any case.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
