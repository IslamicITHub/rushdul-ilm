    Title: Is my dad's elder brother’s wife that is my aunty a mahram or non mahram for me?

    Question: Is my dad's elder brother’s wife that is my aunty a mahram or non mahram for me?

    Answer ID: 160891Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1036/819/SD=8/1439)

     

    The wife of your father’s elder brother is not a mahram for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
