    Title: I am am insurance adviser. I do get commision from customer?s preimium. Which can be renewable or not. At the time of maturity of insurance the company is giving sum assured. So, i want to know the commision is halal or haram?

    Question: I am am insurance adviser. I do get commision from customer?s preimium. Which can be renewable or not. At the time of maturity of insurance the company is giving sum assured. So, i want to know the commision is halal or haram?

    Answer ID: 4825Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 939/875=D/1429)

     

    Life insurance is based on interest and gambling, therefore it is haram. It is unlawful to take commission on it; the income shall not be lawful.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
