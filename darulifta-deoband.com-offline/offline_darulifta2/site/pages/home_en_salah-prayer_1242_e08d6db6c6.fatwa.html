    Title: I want to ask you that performing Namaz in the Mosque constructed in graveyard is permitted (Jayaz) or not?

    Question: I want to ask you that performing Namaz in the Mosque constructed in graveyard is permitted (Jayaz) or not?

    Answer ID: 1242Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 439/437=M)

     

    It is permissible to perform salah in a mosque built in a graveyard:

    لا بأس بالصلاۃ فی المقبرۃ اذا کان فیھا موضع اعد للصلاۃ ولیس فیہ قبر (مرقاۃ شرح مشکوۃ: 2/218)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
