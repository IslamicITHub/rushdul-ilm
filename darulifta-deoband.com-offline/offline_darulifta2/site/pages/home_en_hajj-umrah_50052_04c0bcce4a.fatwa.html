    Title: Sins forgiven after Hajj

    Question: The obligatory acts like unperformed salah, sawm and zakah are forgiven after performing hajj or to be completed these even after hajj because after hajj all sins are forgiven and man becomes like a newly born without any sin. Please clarify.

    Answer ID: 50052Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1134/L-376/TL=09/1435) The rights of man such as debt or rights of Allah such as salah, fast, zakah are not forgiven by Hajj. Hence it is mandatory to make their qaza after hajj as well. قال فى البحر: فليس معنى التكفير كما يتوهمه كثير من الناس أن الدين يسقط عنه وكذا قضاء الصلاة والصوم والزكاة إذ لم يقل أحد بذالك (شامى:3/49)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
