    Question: If in ruku or sujud if no of time ruku/sujud tasbhi is different for example in ruku I recited subshsnrabbil azim 3 times and in sujud by I recited subhsn rabbil aala 5 times is it ok or I have to make obligatory sujud in akhri rakat?no of time this tasbee recitation is 3 or 5or 7?

    Answer ID: 13148Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 782/611=L/1430)

     

    It is permitted to recite Subhana Rabial Azeem thrice in ruku and Subhana Rabial A?ala five times in sajdah, this will not require any sajdah sahv (remedial prostration).

    (2) The minimum number of tasbeeh is three, there is no limit for more, but one should recite it in odd numbers.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
