    Title: Can we use Patanjali products or not? Please give me answer as soon as possible.

    Question: Can we use Patanjali products or not? Please give me answer as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 146195Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 232/156/B=3/1438)

    If you know for sure and with observation that these products contain impure things then it is haram for us to use them. And if we Muslims know with observation and surety that these products contain no impure and haram things then there is no harm in using them, it is allowable. However, if there is a doubt about it then it is better to avoid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
