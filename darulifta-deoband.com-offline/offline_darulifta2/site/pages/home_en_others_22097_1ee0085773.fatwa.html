    Answer ID: 22097Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 892/D=176/k=1431)

    Dream is not an religious authority. Neither each dream has particular meaning. The worldly glitters and thoughts which lingers in the mind sometime occur in dream as an event. It may be understood by this dream that turning towards the love of worldly glitters, wealth and children wholeheartedly that causes negligence from akhirah is harmful.

    زُيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ حُبُّ الشَّهَوَاتِ مِنَ النِّسَاء وَالْبَنِينَ وَالْقَنَاطِيرِ الْمُقَنطَرَةِ مِنَ الذَّهَبِ وَالْفِضَّةِ وَالْخَيْلِ الْمُسَوَّمَةِ وَالأَنْعَامِ وَالْحَرْثِ ذَلِكَ مَتَاعُ الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا وَاللّهُ عِندَهُ حُسْنُ الْمَآبِ [آل عمران : 14]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
