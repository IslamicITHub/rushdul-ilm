    Title: While reciting Quran Azan is recited. Are we supposed to stop reciting Quran and hear Azan or we can continue reciting Quran?

    Question: While reciting Quran Azan is recited. Are we supposed to stop reciting Quran and hear Azan or we can continue reciting Quran?

    Answer ID: 156376Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 257/282/L=3/1439)

     

    If you are reciting the Holy Quran in the mosque at the time of Azan then it is allowed to continue and in case you are at your home then you should stop the recitation and reply Azan.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
