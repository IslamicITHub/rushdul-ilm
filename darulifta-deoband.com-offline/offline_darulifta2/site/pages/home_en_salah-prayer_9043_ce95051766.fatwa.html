    Question: Assalamu alaikum Brother in Islam Kya Farmate hai Ulma-e-ikram Masla-e-zail ke bare mai: 1.Agar Azan waqt shuru hone se pehle de di gai to us Azan ko kab tak lotaya ja sakta hai ? 2.jo Azan waqt shuru hone se pehle de di gai or ab unhe(Azan ko)diye huye kafi days ya month ho gaye kya unhe bhi lotaya jayega? if yes to kese ? 3. Agar Azan nahi lotai gai to us Namaz per kya fark padega? 4.Mobile ki Ringtone mai Qur-aan ki ayat ka istemal kesa hai ? 5.Hamare Office mai Non Muslim Staff bhi kaam karta hai or wo pooja karne ke baad uska parsad sabhi ko distribute karte hai aise mai hame kaya karna chahiye ? kyu ki wahi hame work karna padta hai or wo staff hamare paas hi rehta hai ki hum us parsad ko fek nahi sakte kya kare?

    Answer ID: 9043Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2244/1837=B/1429)

    (1) It is not correct to call Azan before the time of salah, if it has been called, it should be called again within the time before offering salah:

    تقديم الأذان على الوقت في غير الصبح لايجوز اتفاقا وكذا في الصبح عند أبي حنيفة ومحمد رحمهما الله وإن قدم يعاد في الوقت وعليه الفتوى كذا في التتارخانية (الفتاوى الهندية: 1/53, الباب الثاني في الأذان, زكريا ديوبند)

    (2) The Azan called before time should be called again within the time, it will not be repeated after the time passes.

    (3) There will be no effect on salah, the salah will be valid; however one will be sinner for abandoning the Sunnah.

    (4) The objective of ringtone is to inform that a person wants to talk, so for getting this information, it is wrong to use Quranic verses or Azan, rather it is a kind of desecration to these sacred words, so it will not be right to use them. One should set up simple ringtone in his mobile, having no voice of music, song etc.

    (5) Those parsads are offered to the idols, so it is unlawful consume them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
