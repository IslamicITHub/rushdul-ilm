    Title: Can I offer salah of asr in standard time.

    Question: Can I offer salah of asr in standard time. which is 3:45, and it has proved by rest of three imams and Allah says in Quraan offer prayer in first time..

    Answer ID: 32818Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1110/1110/M=1432) According to the preferable saying of Hanafi Fiqh, the time of Asr salah starts on Mislain (when the shadow of an object doubles except its original shadow). You should offer your salah according to it. What is the verse of the holy Quran that says offer salah in its first time?

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
