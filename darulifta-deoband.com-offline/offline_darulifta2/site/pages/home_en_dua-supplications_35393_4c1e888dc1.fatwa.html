    Question ID: 35393Country: India

    Title: I had lot of credit because of loss in business, so people tried to pressurise and did black magic to take their amount from me

    Question: I had lot of credit because of loss in business, so people tried to pressurise and did black magic to take their amount from me. I could not handle their pressure and tried to commit suicide but Allah saved me. Now that God has saved me I want to ask forgiveness from Allah. What should I do now? Please, reply as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 35393Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1984/452/D=1432) Perform two rak’ah of Salah al-Taubah and beseech Allah for forgiveness earnestly. Allah will forgive you in-shaAllah. Keep on reciting istighfar and this dua: یا حی یا قیوم برحمتک نستغیث Also, recite this dua for payment of loan: اللھم اکفنی بحلالک عن حرامک واغننی بفضلک عمن سواک

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
