    Title: I married last month. In my daily life I always offered Fajr salah when I was single, but problem shall arise when I will make sex in the night as a result I shall get impure (napak) after sex

    Question: I married last month. In my daily life I always offered Fajr salah when I was single, but problem shall arise when I will make sex in the night as a result I shall get impure (napak) after sex. So it shall be a great problem for me and my wife to offer Fajr salah as a result of impurity (napaki). If we take bath before Fajr we will get fever. And we already know that it is a sin to miss a salah. So can I have intercourse after Fajr salah? Or will it not be a sin if I make Fajr salah qaza?

    Answer ID: 35941Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 105/91/B=1433) The best time of intercourse is last part of the night. You should perform ghusl immediately after having intercourse and then offer salah. If you fear that your sickness shall increase due to having ghusl in the night then you should have ghusl with warm water in some closed place. You can have intercourse any time during day. You also can have intercourse after Fajr as well. However, it is severe sin to miss Fajr salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
