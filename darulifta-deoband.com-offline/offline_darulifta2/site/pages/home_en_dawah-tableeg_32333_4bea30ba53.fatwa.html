    Title: Regarding new Testament

    Question: I completed my 10th standard from christian school??On the eve of farewell Father manager of the school gave me"New testament with Psalms" It contains something related to jesus christ...I didn't read it but It is kept with me....what should I do of it? You people are WORLD'S BEST PIOUS SCHOLARS.Any other advice is heartly welcome...JAZAKALLAH!!

    Answer ID: 32333Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1110/636/D=1432) The copies of Taurah, Zabur and Injeel that exist today are distorted. You should not keep it with you. Hadhrat Umar Farooq (رضى الله عنه) is reported to have found a copy of Taurah; he visited the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) along with it and started reading it. The signs of anger appeared on the holy face of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم). Hadhrat Siddiq Akbar (رضى الله عنه) also was present in the same meeting. He drew the attention of Hadhrat Umar (رضى الله عنه) towards it, then he stopped at once. The holy Propeht Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) said, "If Musa (عليه السلام) were alive at my time of Prophethood, he, too, would have followed me." ولو كان حيا وأدرك نبوتي لاتبعني رواه الدارمي (مشكوة ص: 32 ، باب الاعتصام بالكتاب والسنة) Now there is no need of previous books when Islam appeared with Quran and hadith, because all the previous books are abrogated as well as distorted. Hence, you should gift it to any authentic institution so that the experts of Shariah may use it for debate and dawah purposes.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
