    Answer ID: 19358Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 206/180/D=1431)

     

    Perform two rak?ah with the intention of Salat al-Hajah and engage yourself in dua, the motto will be achieved with ease. It is written in Bahishti Zewar (v 9, p 86) that one should recite this dua for twelve days 12000 times followed by dua, in-shaAllah every kind of concern will vanish:

    اللھم یا بدیع العجائب بالخیر یا بدیع

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
