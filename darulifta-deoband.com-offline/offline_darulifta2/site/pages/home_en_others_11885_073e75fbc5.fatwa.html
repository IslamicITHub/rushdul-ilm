    Title: Assalamu alaiakum.. .. my question is , i always see myself in hell, what should i do ?. i do 5 times prayer.. and cud u tell me the tabir of this dream.

    Question: Assalamu alaiakum.. .. my question is , i always see myself in hell, what should i do ?. i do 5 times prayer.. and cud u tell me the tabir of this dream.

    Answer ID: 11885Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 846/630=H/1430)

     

    The dream indicates that the heart is tempted to sins which are reflected in dreams as hell. The dream serves as warning to protect oneself from such tendencies.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
