    Title: I am the student of engineering in Roorkee. Sometimes I have class at Jumuah salah time. What should I do in that situation?

    Question: I am the student of engineering in Roorkee. Sometimes I have class at Jumuah salah time. What should I do in that situation?

    Answer ID: 145524Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 82/114/L=2/1438)

    Jumuah salah is fardh-e-Ain (obligatory) when all the conditions are found. It cannot be missed without a Shariah reason. So you should try to offer Jumuah salah in the city whether in first time or last time. And if it happens occasionally then you should take leave for some time so that you could offer Jumuah salah. You should not miss Jumuah salah.

    وهي فرض عين كذا فى التهذيب ، قال أبو دقاق : ليس له أن يمنعه فى المصر .(هنديه 1/205 ط: زكريا)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
