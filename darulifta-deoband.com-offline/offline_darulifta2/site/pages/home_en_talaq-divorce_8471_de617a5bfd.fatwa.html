The judge grants divorce degree. But he has never given islamic divorce (talak). He still wants his wife . what is the islam says. Are they divorced islamicall or not.     Answer ID: 8471Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1852/121=D /1429)

     

    (a) What has been written in the document of divorce? (b) What methods of force were adopted? If the document of divorce carries clear words for divorce and the boy has signed on it after reading or hearing it, then divorce will take place. Likewise, if he has uttered words of divorce, then also divorce will take place. You should clarify what is meant by Islamic divorce. Ask the question again in with detail of (a) and (b).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
