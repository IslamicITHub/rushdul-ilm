Is ISLAM allow us terminate the pregnancy at this stage or it is haram or gunah-e-kabira to terminate the pregnancy at this stage and does Islam allow us to do plannig for concieving the pregnancy as I have described to you. Please reply in detail as soon as possible. Jazakallahu- Khaira

    Answer ID: 24367Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1251/L=309/tl=1431)

     

    The reasons that you provided to procure abortion are not enough. It is not right to get abortion because of the above-mentioned reasons. In addition, if you fear that you are jobless right now and after birth of the child how will you support the child, then it is haram to procure abortion with this intention; because Almighty Allah has taken the responsibility of livelihood upon Him.

    وَلاَ تَقْتُلُواْ أَوْلادَكُمْ خَشْيَةَ إِمْلاقٍ نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُهُمْ وَإِيَّاكُم إنَّ قَتْلَهُمْ كَانَ خِطْءاً كَبِيراً [الإسراء : 31]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
