    Answer ID: 12897Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 843/650=L/1430)

     

    Receiving 11 thousand and giving 10 thousand is interest, such a dealing is unlawful and haram by Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
