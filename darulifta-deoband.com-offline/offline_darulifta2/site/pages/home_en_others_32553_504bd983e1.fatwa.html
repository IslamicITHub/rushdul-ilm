    Title: Dream regarding preparations of marriage

    Question: I am still a single girl. Last night a saw a dream wherein i saw a few of my relatives asking me to show my mariiage clothes. This meant that i was getting married. I saw two ladies. 1 was my cousin with whom i am not very much attached and the other was her realtive. Here i want to inform you that last mont i had got engaged but that engagmnt was broken from boy's side becoz a past relation of mine. That engagmnt remained for 15 days only. Here i want to say one thing regarding my previous relation. I had to break that relation becoz he was from a loe caste and my family did not agree to that relation. I could have fought for that but i thougt this relation will not get the respect it deserves. so i though it is best to back off. even after my engagmnt broke, this biy is still ready to marry me but i am not ready to make any efforts becoz of the same reason. Please suggest what i should do. and suggest regarding the dream i saw.

    Answer ID: 32553Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1183/742/H=1432) It is nice dream. The meaning of this dream is that you should follow the advice and instruction of your parents regarding your nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
