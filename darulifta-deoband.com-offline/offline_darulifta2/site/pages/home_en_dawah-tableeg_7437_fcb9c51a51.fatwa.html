    Answer ID: 7437Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1694/1679=D/1429)

     

    ادع الى سبيل ربك بالحكمة

    Tabligh and preaching is proved by this verse and other verses of the Holy Quran as well the Hadith. All the Prophets (عليهم السلام) especially the last Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) invited people to Allah Almighty, so how can preaching be bid?ah (innovation in the religion)? This is the Sunnah of Prophets. Also, it is Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and the Sahaba (رضوان الله عليهم) to go to villages for preaching. For this very purpose, the Prophet (صلى الله عليه وسلم) used to send a group of some Sahaba. Therefore, how can it be bid'ah be in group and go for preaching and doing tabligh.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
