    Title: Talaq

    Question: Once the divorce becomes final and irrevocable between the husband and wife, can both the parties marry again? If yes, under what circumstances and with what conditions? Is the halalah not necessary in this case?

    Answer ID: 48316Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1258/980/D=11/1434) The relation of nikah comes to an end when three talaqs (mughallazah) occur. Now their nikah cannot be valid without a Shariah halalah. The woman should marry after completing her iddah with any other man who should do intercourse after the nikah, and then if the second husband gives her talaq or dies then the woman may marry the first husband after observing her iddah. This is called halalah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
