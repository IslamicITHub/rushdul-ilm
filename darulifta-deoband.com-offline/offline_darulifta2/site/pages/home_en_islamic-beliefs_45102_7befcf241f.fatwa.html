    Question ID: 45102Country: India

    Title: Shirk and Bid'ah

    Question: I want to know regarding shirk and bida’h and Allah will pardon every sin except shirk so I want to get details of shirk and bida’h. Please suggest me some books so that I can have better idea of shirk and bida’h or explain me all the things which lead a person to shirk and bida’h.

    Answer ID: 45102Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 711/711/M=1434) In order to know the detail of Shirk and Kufr study the following books: (1) Sunant wa Bidat: Mufti Abdur Raheem Lajpuri (رحمة الله). (2) Al-Shihab Al-Saqib: Hadhrat Madani (رحمة الله) (3) Rah-e-Sunnat: Maulana Sarfaraz Khan Safdar Sahib (رحمة الله) (4) Muhazrat Radd Raza Khaniyat: Published by Darul Uloom Deoband (5) Islahur Rusum: Hadhrat Thanavi (رحمة الله) (6) Shariyat ya Jihalat: Maulana Palan Haqqani (رحمة الله)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
