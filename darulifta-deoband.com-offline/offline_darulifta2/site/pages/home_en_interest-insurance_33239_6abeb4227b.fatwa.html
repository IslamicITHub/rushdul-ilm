    Question ID: 33239Country: MM

    Title: Kasim bhai have threes daughter only, no son. Last week he is death.

    Question: Kasim bhai have threes daughter only, no son. Last week he is death.(INTEKAAL).Who consume his properties? Please answer. Jazarkaalaah.

    Answer ID: 33239Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1082/913/B=1432) If the questioner is true in his question and there is no brother, bhatija (nephew), chacha (uncle) etc of Qasim nor there is his wife or son and there is no heir except those three daughters, then all the three daughters will inherit and own the property equally.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
