    Answer ID: 11012Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 420/304=H/1430)

     

    (1, 2) Such dreams are seen due to infected substance in stomach. You should go to bed with wudhu as per the sunnah and decrease the quantity of dinner a little.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
