But i'm very strict about getting only a good pious husband who is also fully into da'wat and also a family in which i can hold steadfast onto deeni principles. So all are being wiped off and my parents are getting more anxious and tensed and therefore angry with. CAn you suggest any dua for such a spouse and to get married soon.

    Answer ID: 3437Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 493/493=H)

     

    Keep on reciting the below dua 21 times after every obligatory salah and at bed time with durood seven times before and after:

    ربنا ھب لنا من أزواجنا و ذریاتنا قرۃ أعین و اجعلنا للمتقین اماماً

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
