    Title: 2nd jamah in the mosque

    Question: Is it right to perform 2nd jamat after finishing the regular fardh jamat in a Jame mosque or a mosque where 5 times salah is performed other than Jumuah? If yes, then is iqamah needed for 2nd jamat? Please give the answer in detail.

    Answer ID: 146422Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 225/211/B=3/1438)

    According to Hanafi Fiqh, second jamah in such mosque is makrooh. The second jamah may be performed away from the boundary of the mosque. It is better to pronounce iqamah in the second jamah as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
