    Question: as salam a lyakum wa rahtilahi barkat hu, janab, my neice was married in the month of may 2008 she had come to her fathers house for shaban but due to some misunderstanding she dint go back to her husbands house and after ramzan when all the elders wanted to counsel them her husband in a fit of anger pronounced talak 3 times in front of the father of the girl and her relatives also the relatives of the boy were present,the girl was not present at tht time. request you to kindly guide us on this matter as the girl is at her fathers place. wht else can be done we all want her to be happy jazkallha nizam shaikh pune india

    Answer ID: 9269Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1580/1324=L/1429)

     

    If the abovementioned case is right and the husband of your niece given three talaqs to her, then the three talaqs took place and she became haram (forbidden) for her husband. Now, they can not rejoin except after a valid halalah:

    قال تعالی: فان طلقھا فلا تحل لہ من بعد حتی تنکح زوجاً غیرہ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
