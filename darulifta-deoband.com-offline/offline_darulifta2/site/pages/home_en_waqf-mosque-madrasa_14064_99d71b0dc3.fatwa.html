    Question ID: 14064Country: Pakistan

    Title: Dear I require fatwa for following 1. in maghrab nmaz if imam do not recite the quraan in loud voice instead he says nmaz imamat as zohar and asar, do we have to repeat the nmaz or it is ok, he remind it after finishing nmaz no one else remind him during nmaz. 2. if a person is saynig his nmaz and one have to pass in front of him, how much minimum distance he should keep to pass from the front side 3. if some one say that he is not muslim (mazaq not serious), then can we say prayer in his imamat as we know him that he is muslim and he says his prayers regularly. 4. if a masjid is made on a place by occupation of land ie without approval from government for government land or without purchasing the land from the owner, then how is the nmaz in that masjid. regards

    Question: Dear I require fatwa for following 1. in maghrab nmaz if imam do not recite the quraan in loud voice instead he says nmaz imamat as zohar and asar, do we have to repeat the nmaz or it is ok, he remind it after finishing nmaz no one else remind him during nmaz. 2. if a person is saynig his nmaz and one have to pass in front of him, how much minimum distance he should keep to pass from the front side 3. if some one say that he is not muslim (mazaq not serious), then can we say prayer in his imamat as we know him that he is muslim and he says his prayers regularly. 4. if a masjid is made on a place by occupation of land ie without approval from government for government land or without purchasing the land from the owner, then how is the nmaz in that masjid. regards

    Answer ID: 14064Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1010/813=L/1430)

     

    If imam recites the Quran silently in loud salah, he must perform sajdah sahv. So, if the imam performed sajdah sahv, there is no need to repeat the salah, but if he did not perform sajdah sahv within the time of that particular salah which was wajib, the salah will not be performed again, only doing istighfar is enough.

    (2) If it is a big mosque i.e. 40 feet long, one can pass after 2-3 rows. But, if the mosque is small, it is unlawful to pass.

    (3) Where and why did he say this, ask him to write it in his own words, and submit the question again.

    (4) The government land is for public; therefore if mosque was built at government land, it is not makrooh to perform salah therein. But, as long as the government will not allow it to be a mosque, it will not be called Masjid Shara?i and by performing salah there one will not have the reward of a Sharai mosque. If a mosque is built at the land of someone without his permission, performing salah therein will be makrooh as long as the owner gives his permission. It is unlawful to build mosque without permission.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
