    Title: mother -in-law can perform hajj with a son in law

    Question: My mother in law want to perform hajj this year she is 58 years old, her husband was death before 30 years, she have only two daughter, no son. Her brother and relatives now don't want to perform hajj and don't want to perform hajj with her. So can she perform hajj with son in law to take as a mahram. I am her elder daughter’s husband, so please send reply to me whether can she perform hajj with me?

    Answer ID: 51406Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 495/495/M=04/1435) In the case mentioned above, your mother in law can go with you to perform hajj.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
