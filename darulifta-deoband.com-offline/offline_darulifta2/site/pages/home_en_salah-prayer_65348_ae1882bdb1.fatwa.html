    Title: Method of offering qaza salah

    Question: I am 18 years old. I have missed number of fardh salah till this age. Can I compensate that salah anyhow? If yes, then how, please.

    Answer ID: 65348Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 583/583/SD=07/1437)

    As much salah you have missed it is mandatory to make qaza of them. It shall not be dropped by kaffarah. Hence you should estimate it and offer the salahs which you have missed. You can make the intention saying that you are offering the first Fajr salah among all your missed salahs and then the salah after it till the last one.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
