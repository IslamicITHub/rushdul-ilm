    Question: assalamoualaikum mufti sahab plz clarify following points 1)if a person gives one talaq wht r the condition to keep her as a wife 2) after giving one talaq will it be two talaq valid for life time or after certain time interval it will be again counted as three.3)one talak was given within 40 days of haiz after child birth so will be the talaq counted as three again after certain period or only two r left till lifetime Plz answer as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 15926Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1543/1234/L=1430)

     

    If someone gives one talaq to his wife in clear words, he can revoke the same within iddah and after iddah he can take her back with new nikah.

    (2) If the woman does not marry anywhere else, he will possess only two talaq throughout his life.

    (3) This has same ruling as No. 2.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
