    Title: My question is this that many people are sleeping in the Masjid, so i want to know is it Allowed to sleep in the Masjid or Not allowed ? Please send me the reply with the Refference. Shukriya!

    Question: Assalamu Alekum , My question is this that many people are sleeping in the Masjid, so i want to know is it Allowed to sleep in the Masjid or Not allowed ? Please send me the reply with the Refference. Shukriya!

    Answer ID: 5652Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 829/730=B/1429)

     

    A musafir and mu?takif (one who retires to a mosque with the intention of reward) can stay night in mosque. The other people should not use mosque like an inn, they should sleep in their houses. It is against the etiquettes of mosque for people to sleep there.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
