    Title: Imam facing Masbooq after Asr?

    Question: After Asr salah, if there are masbooq (people who are making up their missed rakah) behind the imam, should the imam turn to the side or turn opposite, so he is facing the masbooq?

    Answer ID: 50455Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 350/350/M=04/1435) If there is masbooq (one who is completing remaining rak’at after imam) is just opposite to the imam facing him then the imam should not sit face to face to him rather he should sit turning his face right or left.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
