    Title: Mehar of All prophets?

    Question: I heard in some bayan that Adam (as) gave 11 durood Sharif for mahr of Hawa (as). Is it right? I want to know what Prophet Muhammad (saw) gave in mahr to all wives. And my marriage is on Nov 1, 2013. Can I give Same mahr as prophet (saw)?

    Answer ID: 49254Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1516/1516/M=01/1434) (1) We could not find this hadith. It is neither one from the requisites of religion nor any Shariah ruling that requires research is based on it. (2) The mahr of some ummahatul mominin (رضى الله عنهن) )mothers of the believers) are as follows: (1) Hadhrat Khadija (رضى الله عنها) 500 dirham, (2) Hadhrat Saudah (رضى الله عنها) 400 Dirham, (3) Hadhrat Ayesha (رضى الله عنها) 400 Dirham, (4) Hadhrat Umm Salmah (رضى الله عنها) 10 Dirham and some goods, (5) Hadhrat Zainab bint Khuzaima (رضى الله عنها) 500 dirham, (6) Hadhrat Zainab D/o Jahesh (رضى الله عنها) 400 dirham, (7) Hadhrat Umm Habibah (رضى الله عنها) 400 Dirham, (8) Hadhrat Juveria (رضى الله عنها) 400 dirham. (Seeratul Mustafa Vol. 3, Zikr Azwaj-e-Mutahharat)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
