Nothing is working for me. It's like my fate has it's doors closed nothing works for me. Can you please tell me what to do? Can you recommended me a Duva that can help me. Thank You

    Answer ID: 15286Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1262/1005=L/1430)

     

    One should not despair hope in Allah?s mercy. Allah tests His servants with both poverty and affluence. In poverty He tests his patience while in affluence He tests his gratefulness to Allah. A servant should submit himself to the will of Allah. Therefore, you should not be frustrated and hopeless, rather continue your efforts and recite this dua at least 100 times daily, in-shaAllah you will be favoured with ease:

    أللھم أکفني بحلالک عن حرامک وأغنني بفضلک عمن سواک

    It is proved from the Quran that ease is accompanied with hardship:

    فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

    سيجعل الله بعد عسر يسرا

    Keep on striving and praying to Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
