    Answer ID: 23454Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1384/1024/H=1431)

    It is very blessed dream. The meaning is that Allah will soon provide you pious and good match, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
