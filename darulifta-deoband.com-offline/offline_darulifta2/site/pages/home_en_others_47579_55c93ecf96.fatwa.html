    Title: Dream

    Question: In 2011 I went for coaching classes at Kota, Rajasthan and there I came in contact with people who were linked with the work of dawah and by Allah’s grace I also moved towards tabligh and rose a beard ma-shaAllah and started listening a lot to eminent Ulamas and at a night while sleeping alone in my room I heard somebody saying that now Nabi Sallallaho Alaih Wasallam will give you his zyarat and suddenly a corner of my room started to fill with Noor and an image of a person is created and suddenly I got afraid of it, and opened up my eyes for fraction of a second and saw that my face was in the direction of that same corner and again gone asleep without any fear ma-shaAllah please tell me about this dream.

    Answer ID: 47579Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1331/1005/B=10/1434) In-shaAllah, you shall be honoured with the visit of Haramain Shareefain, will be blessed to perform Hajj and also will have the blessing of spending time in tablighi jamah with sincerity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
