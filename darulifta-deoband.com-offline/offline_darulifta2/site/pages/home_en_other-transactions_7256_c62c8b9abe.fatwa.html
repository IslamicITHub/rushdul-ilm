    Question: Assalam alaikum kya islam mey BROKARING ka karobaar durust hai, kya. makanath dilana aur us par paisey lena. dallali, baraye meherbani tafshil sey boliyega. JAZAKALLAH.

    Answer ID: 7256Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1081/947=L/1429)

     

    It is alright, provided the wage is fixed.

    This is also permissible if the wage is fixed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
