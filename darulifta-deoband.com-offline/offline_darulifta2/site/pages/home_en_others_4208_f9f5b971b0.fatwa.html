    Question: In the present day there is a beloved person of Allah about whom Holy Prophet Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) ordered in dreams of various people to immediately commence allegiance. Should they?

    Answer ID: 4208Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 877/762=B/1429)

     

    In the abovementioned case, only due to dream one by Shariah is not bound to obey.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
