    Title: Assalamualikum, I am working in a company. During the past few years i was using the companys, phone, stationary, internet, printing etc for my personel use also. Do i need to pay for these usage to the company. or should i inform to one of the partner of the company. please reply.

    Question: Assalamualikum, I am working in a company. During the past few years i was using the companys, phone, stationary, internet, printing etc for my personel use also. Do i need to pay for these usage to the company. or should i inform to one of the partner of the company. please reply.

    Answer ID: 15592Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1323/1064=L=1430)

     

    If there was no permission from the company to use these things personally, but you used the same, so you should pay an appropriate amount to the company. Your responsibility will not be discharged only by informing to one of the partners.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
