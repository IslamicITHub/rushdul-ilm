    Answer ID: 9594Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2288/2083=D/1429)

     

    It is mentioned in the Hadith of Shafaat (intercession):

    فأخرج من كان في قلبه مثقال ذرة أو خردلة من إيمان (مشكاة 489)

    (So, O Prophet! Take out (from Jahannam) those who have iman in their hearts even equal to a particle or a seed of mustard)

    Iman is the name of yaqeen (belief) which is not small or big. So, the words (in Hadith) zarrah or khardala mean the quality of yaqeen and its effects. If these effects are present in his heart even equal to seed of mustard, Allah Almighty will forgive him also on the intercession of the Prophet (صلى الله عليه وسلم).

    As far as the matter of Jannah ten times bigger is concerned, every person in paradise will not have less than it, since the last person going to the Paradise has been promised to be given a Paradise ten times bigger than this world.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
