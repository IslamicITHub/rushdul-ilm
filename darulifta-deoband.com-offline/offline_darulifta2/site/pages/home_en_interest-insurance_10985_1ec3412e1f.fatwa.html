    Title: Dear Mufti sahib! my father deposited some money in the bank in 1997/98, at that time he doesn't know much about the interest, later on in 2008 he realized that extra money that bank will give to him is interest. so he decided to give the money to the deserved peoples. but my father died in august last year. i want to know.     Answer ID: 10985Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 434/303=H/1430)

     

    (1, 2) The entire money should be given to the poor and needy, whatever the value may be.

    (3) Yes, it can be given, even if they know, then also it is right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
