    Title: I have done my Umrah in the month of Ramadan 2016 in the last 15 days. I need to know whether Hajj is compulsory on me?

    Question: I have done my Umrah in the month of Ramadan 2016 in the last 15 days. I need to know whether Hajj is compulsory on me?

    Answer ID: 156733Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 369/282/B=3/1439)

     

    Hajj will be fardh on you if you afford it. Hajj becomes fardh when one has the capacity to perform it and it does not become fardh by performing umrah. If right now you are not able to perform Hajj then it would become wajib for you when you will be able to perform it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
