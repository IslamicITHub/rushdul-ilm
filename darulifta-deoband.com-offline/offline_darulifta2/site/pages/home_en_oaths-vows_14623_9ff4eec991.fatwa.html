    Title: Assalam alaikum. My question is if a husband takes a kasam of allah in anger that for a particular time duration he will not have humbistari with his wife and if he breaks it by doing so. what is the thing/amal that he can do to correct this?

    Question: Assalam alaikum. My question is if a husband takes a kasam of allah in anger that for a particular time duration he will not have humbistari with his wife and if he breaks it by doing so. what is the thing/amal that he can do to correct this?

    Answer ID: 14623Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1087/883=L/1430)

     

    If he had taken oath not to have sexual relation with wife for four months, he is liable to pay kaffarah for his oath.

    وحکمہ وقوع طلقۃ بائنۃ إن بر ولزوم الکفارۃ أو الجزاء إن حنث )ملتقی الابحر(، و في مجمع الأنھر: قولہ ولزوم الکفارۃ أي کفارۃ الیمین إذا قال: واللہ لا أقربک أربعۃ أشھر (ملتقی الأبحر مع مجمع الأنھر 2:95)

    The kaffarah is feeding ten poor persons for two times or giving them cloth to wear or keeping fast for three days for one who is not able to do the above.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
