    Title: My wife afride for delivery time pain.so 3rd child no like she want family planing operation? what is shariyath law i am what doing?

    Question: Asalamu alaikum warhammathulli wa barakathu !!! My wife afride for delivery time pain.so 3rd child no like she want family planing operation? what is shariyath law i am what doing?

    Answer ID: 6704Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 657/657=M/1429)

     

    The concept of family planning is against Islamic teaching. It is haram and unlawful to have such operation which permanently removes the reproduction capability. You should politely get your wife convinced that it is a major sin.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
