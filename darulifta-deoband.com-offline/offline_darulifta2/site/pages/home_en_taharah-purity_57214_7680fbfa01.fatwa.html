    Title: taharah

    Question: (1) If the feelings of sex makes some fluid like material coming out of penis (whether it is sperm or something else) makes the need of bath (ghusl) or not? (2) Should pubic hairs be cleaned near anus also or only near penis?

    Answer ID: 57214Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 240/237/B=03/1436) (1) Yes, if the semen discharged with a jerk then ghusl shall be wajib. If it discharged without lust and jerk then ghusl shall not be wajib. (2) Yes, the pubic hair surrounding anus also should be cleaned.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
