    Title: Please interprete my dream

    Question: Assalamualaiku, "I saw a dream in which my eldest brother is getting murdered by someone, after sometime I come to know about the murderer. I decide to take revenge for my Brother's murder and kill the person but I am not doing it". I remember upto this. I am very much worried for my brother. please say the meaning of this dream. I am sending this second time as you have not answered me the previous time. Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 31927Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 955/622/H=1432) It means that your brother shall receive much benefit from you and other people.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
