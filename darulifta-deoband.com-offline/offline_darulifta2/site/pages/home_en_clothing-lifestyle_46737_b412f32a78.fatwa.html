    Question ID: 46737Country: India

    Title: Hair

    Question: I usually and very often come across of hairs in my life in my foods, plates, tables etc. I believe it is all my body hair but I consider it as unholy. Kindly remove my wahem if it indicates any message or it is just due to my life style. I live alone.

    Answer ID: 46737Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1084/841/H=9/1434) Falling hair in plates, table etc is common and there is no evidence in Quran and Hadith that it is misfortune. Your suspicion is baseless. You should not pay any attention to it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
