    Title: Can a women pray in clothes that she wore during her haidh if there are no physical impurities on them?

    Question: Can a women pray in clothes that she wore during her haidh if there are no physical impurities on them?

    Answer ID: 237Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 412/J)

     

    Yes, she can pray in the clothes that she wore during her menses provided they are tahir (clean and pure).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
