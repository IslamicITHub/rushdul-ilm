    Title: ALLOCATION OF SHARES OF DECEASED LADY

    Question: An average old lady dies issueless, survived by one younger real sister, one nephew and two niece’s children of predeceased elder real sister. No other sharer. How the agricultural field in a village left behind by the deceased shall be shared/inherited.

    Answer ID: 49697Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 46/46/M=01/1435) In the above mentioned case if none among the husband, mother and father of the deceased woman is alive then all the property left by the deceased woman shall be divided into 2 shares. Out of the total, 1 share shall go to her real sister and 1 share to her bhatija (son of brother). The bhanjis (daughters of sister) of the deceased woman shall get no share. The detail of the Shariah division is as follows: Details: Sister: 1x1=1 Bhatija: 1x1=1 Total: 2

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
