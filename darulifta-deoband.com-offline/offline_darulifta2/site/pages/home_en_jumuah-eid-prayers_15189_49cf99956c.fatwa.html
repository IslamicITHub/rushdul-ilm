    Answer ID: 15189Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1492/1218=D/1430)

     

    The Jumua Khutbah should be recited in Arabic, it is unlawful to deliver the same in non-Arabic. There is no proof of non-Arabic khutbah from the time of the Sahabah and those who followed them. You should try to perform salah in a mosque the khutbah is recited in Arabic. Those are right who say that the khutbah should be in Arabic.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
