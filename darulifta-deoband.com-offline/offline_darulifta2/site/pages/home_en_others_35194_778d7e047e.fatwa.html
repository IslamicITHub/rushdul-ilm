    Title: Dream Interpretation

    Question: I saw a dream regarding my friend who is Ma-sha-Allah very pious. I do not remember exactly but it was like a rally was proceeding and I and my friend were a part of it. It passed by my house and I went to my home. Then somehow the rally assembled in the dining room of my house and I was standing at the gate and watching. It was a rally of the Jews and the speaker asked to the public if it was possible for women in this age to wear a burqa? The entire public was of the opinion that its not possible but from amongst them a Jewish women in burqa stood up and said that she thinks its possible. The speaker asked that why does she not accept Islam then? She said she wanted to do so and she will accept Islam. My friend (in the rally) got happy and took her and made her accept Islam.

    Answer ID: 35194Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2265/H=942/TH=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. It means that other nations shall accept the effects of sincere and pious people and their preaching, and will try to gain the blessings of this world and the hereafter, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
