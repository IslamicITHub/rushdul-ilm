    Title: dream of falling into well

    Question: I dreamt that I had fallen into a well filled with water while driving my red car. After falling I walked out of the car and the well, and the red car sank into the water. Please provide me the clarification.

    Answer ID: 43627Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 306/200/L=1434) You should see your income sources. If you are getting haram or doubtful income from anywhere then you should stop it. Illegal money goes waste as it comes, while the halal and pure income has barakah and is safe against disasters even if it is less.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
