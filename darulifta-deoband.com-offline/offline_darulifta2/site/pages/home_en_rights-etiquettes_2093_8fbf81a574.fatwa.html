    Title: I have seen two of my friends(male) are very close..at the time of sleeping some time they share one bed .. many time one boy completely covering other boy from his back side and sleeping..is it allowed ...what is the level of friendship among two youths..which is allowed between two friends and which is not allowed ? whether two youth can sleep together in one bed if both have no bad intension..is it allowed in islam

    Question: I have seen two of my friends(male) are very close..at the time of sleeping some time they share one bed .. many time one boy completely covering other boy from his back side and sleeping..is it allowed ...what is the level of friendship among two youths..which is allowed between two friends and which is not allowed ? whether two youth can sleep together in one bed if both have no bad intension..is it allowed in islam

    Answer ID: 2093Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1001/1001=J)

     

    It is not allowed for two persons of ten years or more to sleep at one bed without any partition of cloth or something else, whether one is on one side and the other is on the other side. And, if there is a partition between them then it is makrooh-e-tanzeehi (undesirable):

    قال الشامی: (مضاجعۃ الرجل) أی فی ثوب واحد لا حاجز بینھما و ھو المفھوم من الحدیث الآتی و بہ فسر الاتقانی المکامعۃ علی خلاف ما مر عن الھدایۃ، وھل المراد أن یتلفا فی ثوب واحد أو أن یکون أحدھما فی ثوب واحد دون الآخر، والظاھر الأول یؤیدہ مانقلہ عن مجمع البحار أی متجردین وان کان بینھما حائل فیکرہ تنزیھاً (الشامی: 9/548)

    By ?level? if you mean to what level friendship is allowed, then you should know that friendship is allowed until it does not cause any thing prohibited in Islam. But, if it causes any unlawful thing then it should be avoided. Likewise, if friendship proves helpful in good and Islamic matters then it is desirable and praiseworthy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
