    Question ID: 46994Country: Pakistan

    Title: Madhi coming out due to sexual arousal not at the moment when something bad comes in my mind but aftr urination.

    Question: I am very tensed due to my problem, whenever something bad comes in my mind some drops of liquid which is called madhi comes out, sometime during erection and sometime without erection, and sometime it does not comes out at the moment when I am sexually aroused but when I go for urination and after the urine some drops comes out, kindly tell me that how can I purify myself, I have to take ghusl or just wudhu is sufficient.

    Answer ID: 46994Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1199/784/L=10/1434) The drop which comes after urination is wadi. Madhi and wadi do not make ghusl wajib, it is sufficient to wash the wetness and make wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
