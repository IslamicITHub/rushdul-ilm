    Question ID: 37316Country: India

    Title: I saw my late mother in my dream. I was holding her in my arms and she was naked and I said to her, why are you so light weight ammi

    Question: I saw my late mother in my dream. I was holding her in my arms and she was naked and I said to her, why are you so light weight ammi? And another dream which my wife saw that a big snake said to her to search and she along with her cousin sister started searching. First my wife found small diamonds like things (like tasbih seeds) searching more she found eggs, searching further she found gold rings; searching further she found a big amount of money (all 1000 rupees denomination). Please interpret these, also my wife is pregnant. And I live in USA. Please help.

    Answer ID: 37316Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 561/D=94/File-D=1433) (1) You should do isal-e-sawab for your mother whether by sadaqah, charity or by feeding poor and needy ones and making clothes for them or by recitation of the holy Quran and doing Hajj and Umrah. Apply any lawful method to send reward for her. (2) The other dream which your wife saw is blessed one. In-shaAllah, you shall be blessed with pious daughter, honour and attain high status.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
