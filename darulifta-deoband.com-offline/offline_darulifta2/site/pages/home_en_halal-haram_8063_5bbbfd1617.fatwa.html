    Title: Is it permissible to have pets in your house? If so, what animals are permissible to have as pets?

    Question: Is it permissible to have pets in your house? If so, what animals are permissible to have as pets?

    Answer ID: 8063Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1043/1043=M/1429)

     

    (1) Yes, it is allowed.

    (2) One can keep cow, bull, buffalo , goat, camel, sheep etc and for riding and transportation donkey, horse etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
