    Question: Assalamualaikum, I am Mohammad Abdul Khadeer.In my dream i have seen many childrens playing out of them one child is doing stupid activites.i have observed him and i have done dua and patted on him.For that he is following me and mother for killing us.I love my mother very much.next day morning i have cried like anything i am very upset.pls give me suggestible answer for my question.     Answer ID: 8145Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1766/1663=D/1429)

     

    According to Hadith, it is a sign of Muslim to behave lovingly with children. In-sha-Allah, the good impression of it shall appear in your life. It is a good dream, you must not be worried.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
