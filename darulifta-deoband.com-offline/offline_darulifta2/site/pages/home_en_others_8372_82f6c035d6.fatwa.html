    Answer ID: 8372Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1728/270=TH/1429)

     

    It is good dream. It means that remembering Allah sincerely covers the long futile ambitions and anticipations i.e. it is the best way to get oneself reformed.

    (2) This dream is a warning that the excessive luxury in life leads a man to many wrong deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
