    Answer ID: 7797Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1838/264=B/1429)

     

    You should recite Surah Al-Nas 111 times with 11 times durood before and after, sit with a tub containing clear water. After reciting, blow on a knife and then pray to Allah to remove it whether it is an evil effect, magic or disease, then cut the water with knife making x mark, and store a half bottle of the water and drink it daily for a week, have a bath with the left water adding some more water in the same day. You should do so again after a week. Possibly, it may benefit you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
