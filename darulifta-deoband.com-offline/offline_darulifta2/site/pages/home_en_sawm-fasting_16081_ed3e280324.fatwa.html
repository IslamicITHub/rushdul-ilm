    Title: Aslamualikum: we some of my friends decided to pray TRAWEE in one of my friend's home but we did not get hafiz. can I pray with seeing the Quran?

    Question: Aslamualikum: we some of my friends decided to pray TRAWEE in one of my friend's home but we did not get hafiz. can I pray with seeing the Quran?

    Answer ID: 16081Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1745/D=252/K=1430)

     

    According to Hanafi Fiqh, the salah turns invalid if the Quran was recited by seeing; therefore you should not perform tarawih by seeing in the Quran, you should perform it with small chapters like Surah al-Fil etc which you remember by heart.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
