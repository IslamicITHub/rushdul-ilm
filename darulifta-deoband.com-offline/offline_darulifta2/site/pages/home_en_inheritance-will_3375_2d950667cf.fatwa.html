    Question: Mufti Sb. Asslamalikum. My question that there are three brothers of one mother.Their mother has not any brother & Sister. Their Grand father Nana)i.e. father of their mother registered his property in the name of two brother who served him.There is any share of third brother who not served his Nana in the light of Islam.While their mother, Nana & Nani have expired.

    Answer ID: 3375Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 342/B=43/TB)

     

    It is a gift from (maternal) grandfather to his grandsons. If the grandfather having being pleased by their services gifted the property to the two grandsons and gave it in their possession then they will be its owners, the third grandson will have no right on it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
