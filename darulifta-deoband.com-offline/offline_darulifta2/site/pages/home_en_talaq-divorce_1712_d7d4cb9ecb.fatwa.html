    Title: My wife, who is Malaysian and I am Pakistani, She wants to have divorce without a good reason. I have read in a book, that a woman seeking divorce herself shall loose rights on child. I want to know if my wife takes divorce herself, still she can have custody of the child?

    Question: Salaam, My wife, who is Malaysian and I am Pakistani, She wants to have divorce without a good reason. I have read in a book, that a woman seeking divorce herself shall loose rights on child. I want to know if my wife takes divorce herself, still she can have custody of the child?

    Answer ID: 1712Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 582/577=D)

     

    If there is separation (talaq, khula) between husband and wife owing to some reason and they have any child, then in case of son the mother has right to nourish it for seven years. Mother can keep him with her until he is able to fulfil his daily necessities. If the child is a girl the mother has right to nourish her till she is grown up. However, father will bear all the expenses incurred. In the above condition, if your wife wants to keep the children with her she can keep them till the age mentioned above.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
