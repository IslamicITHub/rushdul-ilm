    Answer ID: 1976Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1713/333=TH)

     

    It is haram to take and give interest based loan. As you know if this is the case with your business that you will be saved by taxation by taking loan, then the ruling is that you can take loan in an exigent situation. This loan can be invested for commercial purposes.

    (2) In this case, like first case, you will get the profit and as much you will spend for poor and needy you will get reward.

    (3) You will have right to receive the entire amount of profit, as mentioned in case No 2.

    (4) It will be paid back to the bank, you have to repay to the bank. If you like to ask any thing else then write it clearly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
