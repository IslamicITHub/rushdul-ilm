    Title: Am an incomplete engineer...am waiting for ma degree..and am jobless,ive been offered a job in BPO field by many of my friends through fake system i.e. am a fresher...manipulating it into experience of 1.5-2 years...so i might have to lie in an interview about my experience to the company and my new colleagues.if i tell them that am a fresher...probably i wont get a job or ill get less pay.so i might have to fake about my experience.so my question is...the salary earned by faking and telling lies to the company and staff...will be haraam or halal,and what should i do if i dont get a job..if i tell them that am a fresher.please reply me as soon as possible...

    Question: as-salaamu alaikum wa rehamathullahi wa barkathahu, am an incomplete engineer...am waiting for ma degree..and am jobless,ive been offered a job in BPO field by many of my friends through fake system i.e. am a fresher...manipulating it into experience of 1.5-2 years...so i might have to lie in an interview about my experience to the company and my new colleagues.if i tell them that am a fresher...probably i wont get a job or ill get less pay.so i might have to fake about my experience.so my question is...the salary earned by faking and telling lies to the company and staff...will be haraam or halal,and what should i do if i dont get a job..if i tell them that am a fresher.please reply me as soon as possible...jazak allah khair.

    Answer ID: 4584Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1117/961=D/1429)

     

    It is fraud and lie to show two-year experience to the company when actually you have no experience, therefore it is unlawful. Yes, if you are capable of doing the work entrusted to you then your earning will be lawful, but you will deserve the sin of lie and deception. Therefore, you should speak the truth.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
