    Title: What will be the share of a person leaving behind two daughters one wife four brothers and two sisters?

    Question: What will be the share of a person leaving behind two daughters one wife four brothers and two sisters?

    Answer ID: 36340Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 212/212/M=1433) In the question mentioned above, all the property shall be divided into 48 shares; out of which 6 shares shall go to the wife; 16-16 shares shall go to each daughter; 2-2 shares shall go to each brother and 1-1 share to each sister. If the parents of the deceased are alive then the ruling shall be different. In that case, submit the question again. The detail of division is as follows: Detail: Wife: 6x01 = 06 Daughters: 2x16 = 32 Brothers: 4x02 = 08 Sisters: 2x01 = 02 Total 48 shares

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
