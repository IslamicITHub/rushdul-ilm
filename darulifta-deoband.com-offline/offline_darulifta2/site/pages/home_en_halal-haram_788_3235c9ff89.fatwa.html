    Question ID: 788Country: Pakistan

    Title:

    Is my income halal if 10% of my job involves handling late payment surcharge cases, while 90% is lawful work?

    Question: Assalamualaikum Kindly answer the following question. i shall be thankful. I work in Sales Department of a company providing natural gas to the consumers. Some consumers do not pay their bills in time. Billing departmen of our company charges late payment surcharge. when a consumer commits default the case is referred to our department that is Sales Department. Now i check the amount which includes the surcharge and write letter to Legal Department. i want to ask whether my act is Halal. i spend 10 % of my job time on this type of correspondence and 90% on other type of work which does not include any unlawful activity. is my income totally halal or totally haram? or some portion of it is Haram? if yes what should i do? Hafiz Muhammad Azam

    Answer ID: 788Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 246=238/D)

     

    You do legal proceedings in order to receive the bills (original and surcharge), you do not have any relation with surcharge (interest transactions); then your earning must be lawful. But, you are helping others in sin; therefore, it is better for you to hand over this work to someone else.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
