    Answer ID: 11309Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 560/560=M/1430)

     

    For good memory, recite ya Qawi (یا قوی) after every salah resting the hands at head. Also, pray to Allah for the same and avoid things leading to forgetfulness.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
