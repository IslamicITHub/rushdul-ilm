    Title: Is there any sign or is there any way to know that Allah is accepting our prayers or Allah is pleased with our action? How can we increase our rank in terms of Iman to Allah?

    Question: Is there any sign or is there any way to know that Allah is accepting our prayers or Allah is pleased with our action? How can we increase our rank in terms of Iman to Allah?

    Answer ID: 8946Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1501/242=TL/1429)

     

    (1) If after the dua and good deed one is given taufeeq to do the same again, then it is a sign of acceptance.

    (2) by discussing Eiman, reciting the Quran, remembering death, keeping the company of the pious, going in the path of Allah for forty days or four months Eiman gets stronger.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
