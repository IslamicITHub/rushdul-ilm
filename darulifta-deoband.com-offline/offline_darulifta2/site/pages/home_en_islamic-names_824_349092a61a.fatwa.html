    Question: Assalam-o-Alaikum I would like to know if we can name our son Mustafa? I am confused since his complete name will be Syed Muhammad Mustafa. Is it permissible? Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 824Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 231=235/D)

     

    Yes, it is right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
