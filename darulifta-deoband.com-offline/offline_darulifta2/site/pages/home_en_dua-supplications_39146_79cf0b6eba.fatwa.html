    Title: We are trying to construct a house soon but we do not want to face any big problem during its construction

    Question: We are trying to construct a house soon but we do not want to face any big problem during its construction. Please suggest some good dua for it.

    Answer ID: 39146Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 984/984/M=1433) May Almighty Allah finalize your house construction plan easily and peacefully, and keep you safe from all mischief and evils! May Allah bring prosperity and blessing in it! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
