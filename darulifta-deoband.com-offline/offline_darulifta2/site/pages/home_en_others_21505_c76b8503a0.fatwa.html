    Answer ID: 21505Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 823/629/H=1431)

     

    If one forgives those who have hurt him with the intention of pleasing Allah and doing kindness with one's relatives, in this case one will get high ranks in jannah. You should hope for that.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
