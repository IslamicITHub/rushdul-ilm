    Question ID: 60666Country: India

    Title: zakat

    Question: We borrowed money from our relatives and bank loan (gold) for house. We do not have saving money only we some gold which we taken load from bank. So my question is we to give zakah or not?

    Answer ID: 60666Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1328/1428/L=12/1436) Zakah is not wajib on borrowed items. So it is not wajib for you to pay the zakah of those jewelry.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
