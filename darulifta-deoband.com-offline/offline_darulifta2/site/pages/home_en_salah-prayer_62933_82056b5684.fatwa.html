    Title: Reciting sunnat namaz in a little audible sound

    Question: One of my friends while offering sunnah namaz recites surah in a little loud voice that I can easily hear his recitation and get disturbed in my namaz. What should I do?

    Answer ID: 62933Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 271/000/H=03/1437) Ask him to recite in the same volume in sunnah salah as the others do. If he follows it then it is ok; otherwise you should offer sunnah at a distance from him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
