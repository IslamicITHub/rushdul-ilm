    Title: What should i do regarding asr salaat in makkah mukarrama, were in Haram al sharif (kabatullah) or in local mosque they follow the salaat timings according to hanbali maslaq, but i am hanaf. Can i pray asr with jamaat in Haram or in local mosque?

    Question: Asalaamualikum, what should i do regarding asr salaat in makkah mukarrama, were in Haram al sharif (kabatullah) or in local mosque they follow the salaat timings according to hanbali maslaq, but i am hanaf. Can i pray asr with jamaat in Haram or in local mosque?

    Answer ID: 1227Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 440=438/M)

     

    Instead of praying in local mosque, praying in Masjidul Haram is Afzal (better) since a prayer of Haram is equal to one hundred thousand prayers according to Hadith.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
