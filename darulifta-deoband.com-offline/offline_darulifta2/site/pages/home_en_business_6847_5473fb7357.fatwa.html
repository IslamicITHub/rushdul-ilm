    Title: With reverence and humble submission, In our society ( Jammu & Kashmir , India ) more and more people are joining RCM Bussiness, a commission based business; more details about this can be viewed at here( http://www.rcmbusiness.com/index.aspx ) . Now i want to know is this type of bussiness permisible in Islam. With regards!

    Question: Dear Scholar Assalamu-Alikum! With reverence and humble submission, In our society ( Jammu & Kashmir , India ) more and more people are joining RCM Bussiness, a commission based business; more details about this can be viewed at here( http://www.rcmbusiness.com/index.aspx ) . Now i want to know is this type of bussiness permisible in Islam. With regards!

    Answer ID: 6847Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1467/1276=B/1429)

     

    The business of RCM Company is not lawful as per the Islamic Shariah. There are several reasons for unlawfulness; therefore Muslims should never participate in such companies.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
