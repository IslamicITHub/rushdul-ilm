    Title: I had a dream that I was walking in the night. I looked up towards the sky and saw three moons

    Question: I had a dream that I was walking in the night. I looked up towards the sky and saw three moons. They were all different shapes (more than half moon, less than full moon) and they were positioned in a triangle shape. Could you please kindly give me the interpretation of the dream?

    Answer ID: 38462Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 797/797/M=1433) It is nice dream. In-shaAllah, you shall get various kinds of happiness in your life. You should follow the sunnah and avoid sins.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
