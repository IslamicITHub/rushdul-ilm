    Question: Assalamu Alikum, Mere 2 batche hai, do no bhi paidayeshi nabina (Blind)hai. Doctoro ka kehena hai ke ayenda hone vale batche bhi nabina paida hosakte hai, Hamare gharvale chahtehai ke Batche ki paidaish rook ne ke liye operation karvale, lakin ye haram hai, is wakth mai kya karsaktahu. Koi doosra raasta hai?

    Answer ID: 7284Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1134/981=L/1429)

     

    It is haram to have operation which permanently removes the reproduction system. However, due to the abovementioned reason, it is allowed to use temporary contraception methods.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
