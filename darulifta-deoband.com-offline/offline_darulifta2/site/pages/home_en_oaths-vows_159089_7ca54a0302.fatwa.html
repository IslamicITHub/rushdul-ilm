    Title: A person swore that he will not talk to his wife for one month. Now should he fulfill his oath or break his oath and pay kaffarah? If he should break the oath and pay kaffarah, what is the kaffarah?

    Question: A person swore that he will not talk to his wife for one month. Now should he fulfill his oath or break his oath and pay kaffarah? If he should break the oath and pay kaffarah, what is the kaffarah?

    Answer ID: 159089Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 620/575/M=6/1439)

     

    He should break his oath and pay the kaffarah. The kaffarah of oath is that ten poor persons are fed two times full stomach or they are given clothes. In case he is not able to do so then he should keep fast for three continuous days.

     

    فَكَفَّارَتُهُ إِطْعَامُ عَشَرَةِ مَسَاكِينَ مِنْ أَوْسَطِ مَا تُطْعِمُونَ أَهْلِيكُمْ أَوْ كِسْوَتُهُمْ أَوْ تَحْرِيرُ رَقَبَةٍ فَمَن لَّمْ يَجِدْ فَصِيَامُ ثَلاَثَةِ أَيَّامٍ ذَلِكَ كَفَّارَةُ أَيْمَانِكُمْ إِذَا حَلَفْتُمْ [المائدة : 89]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
