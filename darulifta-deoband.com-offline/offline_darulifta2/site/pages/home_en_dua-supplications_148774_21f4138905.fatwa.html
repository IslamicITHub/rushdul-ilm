    Title: Cancer Disease solution by Quran

    Question: My mother is suffering from cancer for last 2 years. The doctors have done operation last 6 months. The treatment is going on by Govt hospital. So please suggest me any dua to remove this disease.

    Answer ID: 148774Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 653/629/M=5/1438)

    Read Surah Fatiha seven times with durood 11-11 times before and after and blow on water and give it to her to drink. Also recite Ya Salam (یَا سَلامُ) hundred times and blow over her body in the morning and evening. May Allah cure your mother! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
