    Title: Qasr salah in airport

    Question: I am living in Lucknow. Sometimes I travel by airplane to other cities. Will I offer qasr salah in airport?

    Answer ID: 163690Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1394/1162/SD=11/1439)

     

    When a person leaves his village or its outskirt then he becomes Shariah musafir. If the airport of Lucknow is adjacent to city then it shall be counted as outskirt and it would be necessary to offer full salah there and if it is not adjacent to the city rather separate then you would offer qasr salah on the airport.

     

    Note: We have come to know that Lucknow airport is adjacent to the city. (d)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
