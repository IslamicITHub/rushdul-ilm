    Question: Assalamualiku,..i have seen lots of time in my dreams that i am in examination centre.& i do not know the answer of the question..some times i reach late to examination centre & i missed my exams...some times i am appearing in the exam & have feelings that i will be failed in this exam..presently i am employed in saudi arabia..my academic record was not good..always i managed just paasing marks..such dreams related to exams i have seen lots of time..pls tell me the tabir of my dreams...jazakallah khera for your services.

    Answer ID: 15491Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1599/1317=D=1430)

     

    It is necessary to make preparations for the test destined in the hereafter. One ought to be persistent on doing good deeds and refraining sinful acts. Try to fulfil the rights pertaining to Allah along with the rights pertaining to the creature. The dreams indicate to the same test.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
