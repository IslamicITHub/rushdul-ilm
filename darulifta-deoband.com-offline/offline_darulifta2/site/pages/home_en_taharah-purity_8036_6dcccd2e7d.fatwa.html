But when i come out and do wozo i feel tht sum drops have come out.to check it nw,it is difficult. Tel me the better way so i cn avoid the impurity.     Answer ID: 8036Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1837/1528=B/1429)

     

    When you doubtful of the drops, you should go to toilet and confirm it. If there are drops, then perform wudhu again. If there was no drop, then it was merely a movement in veins, it will not break the wudhu.

    Note: You should walk a little or lie for some time after urination, then make wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
