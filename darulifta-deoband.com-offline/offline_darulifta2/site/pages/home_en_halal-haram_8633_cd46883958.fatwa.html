    Question: assalamu alikum sual pish khidmat hai kai aik aadmi ki umar 60 sal mukaml hucuki hai aur lkhi hui ghalt hai 3 sal ka farq hai kia yh 3 sal zaid ju lkhi hui umar hai utni mudat tak kia mulazmat karin ya retairmint lailin vzahir nahi kar saktai hain usama

    Answer ID: 8633Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1410/1177=L/1429)

     

    In the case mentioned above, one will get the sin of telling lie. But, if he is able to discharge the entrusted duties perfectly then it will be lawful for him to receive the salary.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
