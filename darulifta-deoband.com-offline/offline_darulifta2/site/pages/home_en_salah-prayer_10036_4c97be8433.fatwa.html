    Question: Assalaamalaikum.My mother has passed away recently. I want to know how much amount i need to pay for each of her QAZA NAMAAZ??? And how many namaaz we have to calculate in a day, 5 or 6 (6 if include vitr of isha)???? To whom can this amount be paid??? Can i give this amount in any mosque??? Or in any madrasa??? Pls inform in light of Holy Quran and Hadees mubarak. Jazaa kumullah !!!

    Answer ID: 10036Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 147/99=H/1429)

     

    Fidyah (expiation) of one salah is 1.575 kg wheat one can pay either wheat or moderate market price of it. If you have missed so many prayers then calculate 6 salah of every day and get the total quantity of wheat as per the total number of salah and pay the fidyah accordingly. It should be given to the poor and needy who are entitled to receive Zakah. You can give it in a madrasa accommodating poor students. It cannot be spent in mosque, building madrasa or in giving salary.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
