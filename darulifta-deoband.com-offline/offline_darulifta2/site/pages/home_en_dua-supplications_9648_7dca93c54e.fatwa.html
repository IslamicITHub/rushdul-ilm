    Question: salaam mufti, i have 8 month daughter, she crys alot, and doesnt have enuff sleep, and shez teething and she got really bad skin infection on bikni area.and she doesnt eat any thing, plz give dua/wazifa to read so she doesnt cry alot and she could have good sleep well and her tooth comez really fast and safe with out getting sick and she eats food. and dua for her skin infection gets better. 2.) plzzz give dua's/wazifa's for my family so they could stop fighting each other haiting each other and love the family. plzz plzz solve da problem. 3.) i lost my gold and i dont remember where r they and i lost some off them .dua so i could find them . iread kalu ina ilai hi ra ji oon but i cant find them.

    Answer ID: 9648Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2290/2085=D/1429)

     

    (1) Recite the below duas and blow on her:

    أعوذ بکلمات اللہ التامات من شر کل شیطان و ھامۃ و عین لامۃ

    أعوذ بکلمات اللہ التامات من غضبہ و عقابہ و شر عبادہ و من ھمزات الشیاطین و أن یحضرون

    (2) Recite in the morning and evening 100 times with durood before and after:

    أللھم أصلح ذات بینناو أللھم ألف بین قلوبنا

    (3) Recite the below dua, the lost thing will be recovered in-sha-Allah or you will get its best substitute:

    أللھم یا ھادي الضال ویا راد الضال اردد علي ضالتي

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
