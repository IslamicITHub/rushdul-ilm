    Title: My husband does not allow me to shave my pubic hair. Will it affect my prayers?

    Question: My husband does not allow me to shave my pubic hair. Will it affect my prayers?

    Answer ID: 2966Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 50/50=J)

     

    The salah shall be undoubtedly valid, but it is highly undesirable (makrooh-e-tahreemi) to leave the pubic hair unshaved. A woman must shave it within forty days if she does not fear severe anger from your husband. Yes, if she fears it she will be excused and her husband will deserve the punishment:

    و کرہ ترکہ وراء الاربعین ای تحریماً لقول المجتبی: ولا عذر فیما وراء الاربعین و یستحق الوعید (الشامی: ط زکریا 9/583)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
