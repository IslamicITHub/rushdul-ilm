    Answer ID: 7347Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1706/1463=/1429)

     

    Nikah is not invalidated itself by any act. Yes, if any Muslim (may Allah forbid) becomes apostate, then the nikah is nullified, or if he utters any word of kufr, then also nikah is nullified. Adultery does not make the nikah null and void.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
