    Title: Can I dye my head hair as these are turning white while i am 30 years of age and still unmarried? Natural colour of my hair is brown black and i want to dye with brown blak colour.

    Question: Can I dye my head hair as these are turning white while i am 30 years of age and still unmarried? Natural colour of my hair is brown black and i want to dye with brown blak colour.

    Answer ID: 1162Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 786/743=B)

     

    Dyeing head with black colour is prohibited in Islam. The Hadith emphatically warns us against this act. However, dyeing with menhdi (brown colour) is allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
