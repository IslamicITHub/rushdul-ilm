    Title: dream interpretation

    Question: I saw a dream regarding my friend who is Ma-sha-Allah very pious. It was exam time and I used to study in my friend's room. I dreamt that after finishing my studies I asked my friend for her dupatta (which she uses for zikr and salat) so that I could pray. She gave it to me and asked me to return it after making dua because a Maulana sahib comes every night and takes her dupatta to Prophet Muhammad (SAW) as he likes it. There was indication in the dream that the Maulana Sahib is jinn or an angel or something of that sort. What does it imply?

    Answer ID: 35195Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2266/H=943/TH=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. In-sha-Allah, you shall receive the taufiq of simplicity and following sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
