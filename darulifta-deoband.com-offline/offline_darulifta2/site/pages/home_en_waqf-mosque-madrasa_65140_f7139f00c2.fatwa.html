    Title: Minor Children attending Fard Salah in Mosque

    Question: Whether minor children, who make hue and cry in mosque during fardh salah disturbing others, is allowed in mosque? If yes or no, please enlighten us with Hadith and Quranic verses.

    Answer ID: 65140Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 785/826/L=08/1437)

    If the children are too young to have idea of things and there is probability that they shall make the mosque dirty or they may make hues and cries in the mosque then it is not allowable to bring such children in the mosque. Hadith asks us not bring such children in the mosque.

     

    قال عليه السلام: جنبوا مساجدكم مجانينكم وصبيانكم ورفع أصواتكم الخ .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
