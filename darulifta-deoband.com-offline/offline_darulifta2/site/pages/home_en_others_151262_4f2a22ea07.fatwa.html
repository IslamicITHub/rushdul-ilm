    Title: For dream interpretation

    Question: I saw this dream on the last night of Rajab....It was dark and I was outside with my auntie when I suddenly looked at the sky I saw the sacred tree (Sidratul Muntaha) and there the moon was shining brightly emanating a green light but then I couldn't spot the moon on the sky but managed to see the reflection of the moon on the ground then I started making a wudhu.

    Answer ID: 151262Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1219/1175/L=9/1438)

     

    MashaAllah, your dream is blessed one. If Allah wills you shall get religious as well as worldly benefits. You need to punctually perform salah, recitation and other good deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
