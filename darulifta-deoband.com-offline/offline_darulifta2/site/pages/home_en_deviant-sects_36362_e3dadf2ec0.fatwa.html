    Title: List out the differences which Ahl Hadith have with majority due to which they are eviant.

    Question: List out the differences which Ahl Hadith have with majority due to which they are eviant.

    Answer ID: 36362Posted on: Dec 18, 2023

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 582/277/L=1433) In order to know the different masails of Ghair Muqallidin, study the books ’Adilla Kamila’, ‘Mutala Ghair Muqallidiyat’, ’Radd Ghair Muqallidiyat’ etc. Ahl Hadith differ from the majority in many unanimous masail. They also consider the furooi masail as definite and propagate it severely. Also they declare weak ahadith completely null and void.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
