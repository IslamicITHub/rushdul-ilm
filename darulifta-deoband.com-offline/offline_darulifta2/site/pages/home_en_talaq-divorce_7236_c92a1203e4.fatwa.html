    Title: Is it permissible to give a woman talaaq during the 40 days period after childbirth if a man give his wife talaaq during this period is it valid.

    Question: Is it permissible to give a woman talaaq during the 40 days period after childbirth if a man give his wife talaaq during this period is it valid.

    Answer ID: 7236Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 851/851=M/1429)

     

    The blood which is discharged after childbirth is called nifas (postpartum period). The husband has been prohibited to pronounce divorce in a state of haiz or nifas. However, if anyone gives divorce to his wife during this period (nifas), the divorce will take place but the man will be a sinner to for giving the divorce in such state.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
