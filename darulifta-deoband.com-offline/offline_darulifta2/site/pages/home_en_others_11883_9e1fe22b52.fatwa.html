    Answer ID: 11883Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 769/644=D/1430)

     

    The result and consequence of the sins and disobediences has been shown in the dream in which the human society is involved. It is necessary for every person to assess his actions and evaluate the engagements of this valuable life. Every one should strive to shun sins and bad deeds and get stability and firmness in iman and following the Shariah.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
