    Question ID: 39009Country: India

    Title: In my native village, there is some Government land property and private land property

    Question: In my native village, there is some Government land property and private land property. These land properties are using by side land holders. And there is side by side a graveyard of a section of people. Now graveyard is going to constructing boundary with brick wall. But some people of graveyard section is encroaching the private land and government land both with the help of government machinery and their muscles power. Please let me know, the constructing boundary of graveyard is right in line with Islamic Shariah.

    Answer ID: 39009Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1108/931/B=1433) You can wall as much area of the graveyard which is mentioned in the documents. It shall be usurpation and embezzlement to include someone's personal land in the graveyard. It is major sin and it is not lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
