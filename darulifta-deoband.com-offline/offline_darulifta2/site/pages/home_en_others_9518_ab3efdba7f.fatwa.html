    Answer ID: 9518Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2510/470=TH/1429)

     

    If there is no hindrance as per the Shariah, you can marry with the mediation of your guardians. The istekharah indicates to the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
