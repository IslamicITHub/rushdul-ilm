    Title: I am am 22 year old student and hanfi my q is that ihav come across so many sahih hadith in witch this is proven that prophet (saw)did the rafia dain as in sahih albukhari and in a book of hanfi fiqh its written that rafia dain is authentic and none of them is mansookh(faizul bari2\225)moreover imam abu hanifa(ra)himself said that if u find any hadith to b authentic then that is my madhab(rasmul muti p4) but when i do rafia dain becos my beloved prophet(saw) did the same some aalims of my city says that dont do this it has been mansookh can u plzz give me just one sahih hadith that rafia dain is mansokh.

    Question: I am am 22 year old student and hanfi my q is that ihav come across so many sahih hadith in witch this is proven that prophet (saw)did the rafia dain as in sahih albukhari and in a book of hanfi fiqh its written that rafia dain is authentic and none of them is mansookh(faizul bari2\225)moreover imam abu hanifa(ra)himself said that if u find any hadith to b authentic then that is my madhab(rasmul muti p4) but when i do rafia dain becos my beloved prophet(saw) did the same some aalims of my city says that dont do this it has been mansookh can u plzz give me just one sahih hadith that rafia dain is mansokh.

    Answer ID: 7067Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1598/1361=B/1429)

     

    It is better (afzal) not to raise hands while going to rukoo and rising from it. The strongest Hadith free from every weakness and defect in this regard is:

    عن سالم عن ابیہ قال رایت رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم اذا افتتح الصلوۃ رفع یدیہ حتی یحاذی بھما، وقال بعضھم: حذاء منکبیہ، و اذا اراد ان یرکع و بعد ما یرفع راسہ من الرکوع لایرفعھما، وقال بعضھم: ولا یرفع بین السجدتین، والمعنی واحد (مسند الحمیدی 2:277، صحیح ابی عوانۃ 90)

    Besides, the Hanafis preferred this for action since the Four Caliphs used to do so.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
