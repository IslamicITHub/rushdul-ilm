    Answer ID: 897Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 716=677/B)

     

    In the above condition, by saying this there will neither Nikah be established among the boy and girl nor will they be considered as husband and wife. The girl can marry any other boy since she is not married yet.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
