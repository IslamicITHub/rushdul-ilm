    Title: Dua For growing beard quickly

    Question: I am a University student. I am already 24 years but still not able to grow beard. I have tried some beard growing cream but its not worked at all. Is there any Wazifa or Dua that I can do to grow beard quickly. Expecting your good suggestion according to Quran & Sunnah.

    Answer ID: 168094Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 522/471/M=05/1440)

     

    If your beard does not grow naturally, then you need not worry, some people have beard late. If your beard does not grow then there is no sin on you, so do not worry. If you wish, you may contact any dermatologist. Also read the below dua:

    اللهم ارزقني على وجهي نوراً .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
