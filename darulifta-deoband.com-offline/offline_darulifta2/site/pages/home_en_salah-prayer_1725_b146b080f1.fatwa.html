    Title: I want to ask you a question regarding salah. The imam of local mosque in Esah prayer said Allah u Akbar instead of Sami'Allahu li man hamedah n later did sajda saho. Does the salaah performed was correct?

    Question: I want to ask you a question regarding salah. The imam of local mosque in Esah prayer said Allah u Akbar instead of Sami'Allahu li man hamedah n later did sajda saho. Does the salaah performed was correct?

    Answer ID: 1725Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 488/488=L)

     

    Sajda sahw was not compulsory; the salah was complete without it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
