    Question: I am a working woman.my husband did not give me any money about 2 years.     Answer ID: 31182Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 627/394/D=1432) You should recite after Maghrib salah and supplicate Almighty Allah: (1) انما اشكوا بثى و حزنى الى الله (2) رب انى مسنى الضر و انت ارحم الراحمين (3) يا حى يا قيوم برحمتك استغيث 100 times each, and صلى الله على النبى الامى 11-11 times before and after.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
