    Title:

    Is contact lens jaiz and what is the daleel? Who is Shaykh Yunis of India?

    Question: Is contact lens jaiz and what is the daleel? Who is Shaykh Yunis of India?

    Answer ID: 276Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 141=140/L)

     

    If you mean the contact lens of eyes, then there is no matter whatsoever. It is a kind of treatment and does not involve anything forbidden. Therefore, it is allowed.

     

    Shaikh Yunus is the Shaikhul Hadith of Mazahir Uloom and a prominent Khalifa of Hazrat Shaikh Zakariya. He enjoys good mastery over sciences of Hadith and has been teaching Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim for a long time in Mazahir Uloom Saharanpur.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
