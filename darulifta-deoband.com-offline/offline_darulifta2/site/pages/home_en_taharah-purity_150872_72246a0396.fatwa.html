    Title: Many times I need to go outside for field work .During job as there is no water and facing the problem of janabah. Then what to do?

    Question: Many times I need to go outside for field work .During job as there is no water and facing the problem of janabah. Then what to do?

    Answer ID: 150872Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 821/734=8/1438)

    If one faces janabat(impurity due to discharge of semen) during a journey and no water is available and there is fear that the time of fardh salah will slip till one gets the water then one should do tayammum and offer salah.

    (رد المحتار على الدر المختار 1/155 ط إحياء التراث) . بدائع الصنائع 1/46 ط المكتبة العلمية ، بيروت)

    Note: It is mandatory to explain the reason of water being unavailable according to Fiqh. (D)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
