    Title: Dream interpretation of my sister about me.

    Question: The question is regarding my sister's dream she had seen two days back as I am telling her that i have seen Huzoor Mohammed Sallahualiawassallam in my dream and she is very happy to inform me about this today. Please tell us what is the interpretation of the above dream.

    Answer ID: 32714Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1060/1060/M=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is nice and blessed dream. In-sha-Allah, you shall receive blessings in this world and the world hereafter. You should pay special attention in following sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
