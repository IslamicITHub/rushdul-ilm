    Title: Assalamu Alaikum Jummma ki namaz me sunnat namaz aur nafil namaz patneki niyath kya he? jumma ki sunnat, ya zohar ki sunnat, kya niyath karna chahiye?

    Question: Assalamu Alaikum Jummma ki namaz me sunnat namaz aur nafil namaz patneki niyath kya he? jumma ki sunnat, ya zohar ki sunnat, kya niyath karna chahiye?

    Answer ID: 10613Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 134/131=L/1429)

     

    It is sufficient to intend nafl or sunnah in the heart whether it is of Zuhr or Jumuah i.e. one thinks in heart that he is going to perform so and so nafl or sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
