    Title: Wearing An Ayatul Kursi Pendant

    Question: I had been wearing a pendant on which Ayatul Kursi was written. It is very small and cannot be read with bear eyes. While cleaning the bathroom tiles, the pendant accidentally touched the floor. From then on I stopped wearing it. Is there any kaffarah that needs to be given?

    Answer ID: 148438Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 603/582/M=5/1438)

    No monetary kaffarah is wajib. You should do taubah and istighfar. If you wear it in future then make it sure that you take off before you enter in the toilet.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
