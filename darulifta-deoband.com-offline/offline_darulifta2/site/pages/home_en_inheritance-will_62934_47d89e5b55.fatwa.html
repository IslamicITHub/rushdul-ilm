    Question ID: 62934Country: India

    Title: distribution of fathers property

    Question: We are three brothers and four sisters, my father has died recently while he was in a 40 day jammat. My father was working in a government job and after his death this job was given to the younger brother with my mother's permission, keeping this in mind how should be the remaining property be distributed among us. Only one sister is unmarried till now.

    Answer ID: 62934Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 307/179/SN=03/1437) In the question mentioned above, in case the details mentioned are true whatsoever inheritance (ie land, house, jewelleries, PF and other assets) your father has left shall be divided into 80 shares, after paying other due rights proceeding inheritance. Out of the total your mother shall get 10 shares, each brother shall get 14-14 shares while each sister shall get 7-7 shares. The detail of Shariah division is as follows: Details: Wife: 1x10 = 10 Sons: 3x14 = 42 Daughters: 4x07 = 28 Total: 80 Shares Note: If the job of your father was given to your younger brother after your father’s death, it shall not drop his shares in the property. However, if he takes less shares happily and withdraws the rest for other heirs then he may do so.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
