    Title: Before sleeping i recite Surah's Kafiroon,Iqlaas,Falaq,Naas. Aytal Kursi,(3)times all. Lillahi mafissamawate,Surah Muzammil and other dua's and sleep and Alhamdulillah Pray 5 times, but my friends are telling i am weeping in my sleep calling my family.Please tell about this.

    Question: As Salaam Alaikum, Before sleeping i recite Surah's Kafiroon,Iqlaas,Falaq,Naas. Aytal Kursi,(3)times all. Lillahi mafissamawate,Surah Muzammil and other dua's and sleep and Alhamdulillah Pray 5 times, but my friends are telling i am weeping in my sleep calling my family.Please tell about this. Jazaak Allah Khair

    Answer ID: 19407Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 208/208/M=1431)

     

    Sometimes it may happen due to some illness, and sometimes it occurs while dreaming unconsciously. You should continue to recite the above-mentioned duas and include durood with it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
