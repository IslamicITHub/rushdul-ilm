    Question ID: 44810Country: NZ

    Title: Dream

    Question: I recite about 100x the surah fatiha and sleep. Then I have a dream. I am in Kabah for Umrah, all people have done tawaf, and I am the last one. I intentionally wanted to be the last one so I could get closer to the Kabah. The Kabah does not have the ghilaf (black chuddar) on it. Also, most people have come for Umrah after paying money, whilst I did not pay a single cent, I think even my fare was paid by someone else. My wife is also there but I did not see her. During tawaf, I recite Ya Allah repeatedly. Whilst doing tawaf, I wake up. I go to the toilet then come to sleep again. My second dream is I am in a small white car with a relative as the driver and we going downhill on a neat road. Near bottom, another vehicle thinks we are racing and decides to go ahead of us, we let them go, a few seconds later they crash. We go past the crash site and stop. Other vehicles are there, even the police. I see injured lady, no sign of husband (driver) and another person head lying on ground.

    Answer ID: 44810Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 507/397/H=1434) (1) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. It is quite hopeful that you would be granted with the blessings of hajj and Umrah and connection with pious elders. Read the book ‘Fazail Hajj’ and ‘Fazail Durood’ by Hadhrat Maulana Zakaria Sahib (رحمةالله) extensively. (2) The second dream is also good. But it indicates towards some deficiency i.e. you are lagging behind in keeping yourself away from sin and evils specially keeping your heart and eyes pure. Try to redress this shortcoming. There is also a small book ‘Ummul Amraz’ by Hadhrat Shaikhul Hadith Maulana Zakaria Sahib (رحمة الله) read it. All the three books may be purchased from Maktaba Yahyavi, Near Jamia Mazahir Uloom Saharanpur, UP, India.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
