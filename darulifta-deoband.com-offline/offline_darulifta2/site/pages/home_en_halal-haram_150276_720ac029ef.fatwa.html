    Title: Is it haram or makrooh to drink water that touch moustache?

    Question: Is it haram or makrooh to drink water that touch moustache?

    Answer ID: 150276Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 733/705/SD=8/1438)

    Neither it is haram nor makrooh to drink water which is touched by the moustache. It is baseless belief. However, it is makrooh to grow the moustache beyond the edge of the upper lip.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
