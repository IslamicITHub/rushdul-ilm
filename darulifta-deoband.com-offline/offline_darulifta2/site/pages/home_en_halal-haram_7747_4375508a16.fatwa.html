    Title: What are the methods of family planning permissable in Islam? can we use condom during intercourse for family planning?

    Question: What are the methods of family planning permissable in Islam? can we use condom during intercourse for family planning?

    Answer ID: 7747Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1220206=TL/1429)

     

    It is haram and unlawful to adopt a method which permanently removes the reproductive ability. Yes, if a woman is too sick to bear the pregnancy or she fears unbearable pains in case of pregnancy or it is recommended by any expert religious Muslim doctor then in these cases it is allowable to use contraceptives temporarily. And, if there is no such case, then it is against the objectives of Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
