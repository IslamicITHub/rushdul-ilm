    Title: Is tarabi namaz compulsory? Do everyone have to perform tarabi namaz?

    Question: Is tarabi namaz compulsory? Do everyone have to perform tarabi namaz

    Answer ID: 25667Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1456/1456/M=1431)

     

    Yes, tarawih salah is sunnat-e-muakkada. Everyone should offer tarawih salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
