    Title: Assalamu alaikum respected moulana saheb i will like to know what is the shar'i ruling regarding an animal that was slaughtered by a shafi who did not read tasmiyah purposely at the time of slaughter. is it permissible for hanafis to consume this animal? what is the fatwa regarding a person who goes to a church and stands under a cross? does this person loose his imaan or not? awaiting your response jazakumullah!

    Question: Assalamu alaikum respected moulana saheb i will like to know what is the shar'i ruling regarding an animal that was slaughtered by a shafi who did not read tasmiyah purposely at the time of slaughter. is it permissible for hanafis to consume this animal? what is the fatwa regarding a person who goes to a church and stands under a cross? does this person loose his imaan or not? awaiting your response jazakumullah!

    Answer ID: 13255Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 842/649=L/1430)

     

    By not reading tasmiyah properly, if you mean that according to the Shafe?i Fiqh he left reciting bismillah intentionally, then in this case it will be unlawful for a Hanafi to consume the animal, as the Quran says:

    وَلا تَأْكُلُوا مِمَّا لَمْ يُذْكَرِ اسْمُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ وَإِنَّهُ لَفِسْقٌ

    ?Eat not of (meats) on which Allah?s name has not been pronounced: that would be impiety.? (6:121)

    (2) If he goes to church and stands under cross in reverence, it is haram, rather he fears to fall in infidelity. He should repent to Allah and seek His forgiveness. Out of caution, he should renew his iman (faith) and Nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
