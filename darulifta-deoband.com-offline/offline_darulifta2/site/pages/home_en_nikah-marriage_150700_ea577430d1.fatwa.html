    Title: Am I doing better if I am not printing the name of bride in the Walimah invitation card?

    Question: Am I doing better if I am not printing the name of bride in the Walimah invitation card?

    Answer ID: 150700Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1084/1099/L=9/1438)

     

    Yes! If you do not write the name of bride in Walimah card it shall be better and suitable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
