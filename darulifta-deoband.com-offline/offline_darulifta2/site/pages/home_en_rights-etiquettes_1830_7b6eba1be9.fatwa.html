    Title: Can a real brother could kisses and hug to his real sister or not? Can a real son could kisses and hug to his real mother or not?

    Question: Can a real brother could kisses and hug to his real sister or not? Can a real son could kisses and hug to his real mother or not?

    Answer ID: 1830Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 632/627=M)

     

    Though one does not fear shahwah (lust) while kissing or hugging his sister or mother, but it should be avoided completely since it may lead him to it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
