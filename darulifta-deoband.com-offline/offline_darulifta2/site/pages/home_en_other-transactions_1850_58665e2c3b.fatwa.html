    Answer ID: 1850Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1527/1188=H)

     

    (1) Private ownership is allowed in Islam.

    (2) We do not know this discussion; neither had we seen it anywhere nor did we hear it.

    (3) We do not remember such a verdict.

    (4, 5) We did not see this thing directly in Qudoori, Hujjatullah and Izalatul Khifa. Please, write down the texts then only we can have a say in the matter. There is a booklet Â“Islam ka nizam-e-Taqseem-e-DaulatÂ” published with the second volume of Jawahirul Fiqh by Hazrat Maulana Mufti Shafee. This book deals with the subject nicely. Study it from beginning to end repeatedly with concentration, and then ask if you have any question.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
