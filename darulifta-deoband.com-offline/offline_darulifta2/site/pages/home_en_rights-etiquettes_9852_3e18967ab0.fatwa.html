    Answer ID: 9852Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 3/6=L/1429)

     

    If someone?s parents are non-Muslims, Islam does not forbid him/her to behave well with parents and visit them. But, if it is feared that they will force her to leave Islam then you can ask her parents and sisters to visit her in your home.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
