    Title: I would like to know solution to the following mas'ala under the light of Qur'anul Kareem and Ahadithun Nabavi sallallah alaihiwasallam. A couple got married (Nikah) long ago (say 40 years ago) with a MEHR of certain amount for example Rs. 5000/-. Till date he has not paid the MEHR. Now he wants to pay the MEHR to his wife. The mas'alah is should he pay the MEHR of Rs. 5000/- or any other way.

    Question: Assalamu alaikum. I would like to know solution to the following mas'ala under the light of Qur'anul Kareem and Ahadithun Nabavi sallallah alaihiwasallam. A couple got married (Nikah) long ago (say 40 years ago) with a MEHR of certain amount for example Rs. 5000/-. Till date he has not paid the MEHR. Now he wants to pay the MEHR to his wife. The mas'alah is should he pay the MEHR of Rs. 5000/- or any other way.

    Answer ID: 30583Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 391/391/M=1432)

    In the question mentioned above, only the payment of five thousand is necessary on husband.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
