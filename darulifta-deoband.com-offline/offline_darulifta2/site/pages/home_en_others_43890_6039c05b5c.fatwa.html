    Title: Dream

    Question: I saw a dream before ishraq time I was wandering outside my work place at a library. I met a girl. I went clubbing. I drank with her for the first time. We were out having a good time. I also met my brother who seemed confused seeing me like this. I also was just hanging out with the girl acting like a westerner, even though I despise them publicly in tabligh etc. I have a beard and go on tabligh often. I attend a western university there are so many fitnah in these environments. The dream seems like I am living what I deeply desire just partying with girls. In the dream, I am worried about my parents when I am outside at night. What does this mean it has got me worried because of how vivid this dream is and it seems like I can easily accomplish this type of life. I even had my beard in the dream. I was in the streets of downtown that I am familiar with.

    Answer ID: 43890Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 360/D=73/File-D=1434) All these are nonsense thoughts; do not pay any attention towards them. However, keep in mind that you should have contact with pious and good people as well as avoid haram money completely. It is mentioned in hadith: المرء على دين خليله فلينظر احدكم من يخالل . مشكوة 427 It means that a person copies the method and habits of his friend. Thus everyone must see whom he is going to make his friend. Wine in dream is interpreted with haram money; hence you should take great care for the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
