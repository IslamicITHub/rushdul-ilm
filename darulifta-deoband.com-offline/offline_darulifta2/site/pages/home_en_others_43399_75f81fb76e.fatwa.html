    Title: Dream of menstruation

    Question: My wife had a dream of her own menstruation and saw the bedspread wet with blood. Can you please tell the interpretation of this dream?

    Answer ID: 43399Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 212/161/B=1434) Ask your wife to maintain purity and keep her clothes neat and clean as well as offer salah regularly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
