    Title: Ii am having digestive problem.so,my vuzu breaks frequently.what about offering prayers&doing tilawat?i am living in hostel.we offer prayer in room.can i do imamat in this situation?

    Question: Ii am having digestive problem.so,my vuzu breaks frequently.what about offering prayers&doing tilawat?i am living in hostel.we offer prayer in room.can i do imamat in this situation?

    Answer ID: 3861Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 502/612=B)

     

    If you do not find a time in which you can offer salah with taharah (free from breaking wuzu) then you will be considered ma'zoor (excused) as per the Shariah. You will have to make fresh wuzu for every salah when the time of the salah is due. You can pray nawafil and recite the Quran as much as you like. But, in this condition you are not allowed to lead the prayer.

     

    For details, please see the detailed ruling about 'ma'zoor' in Bahishti Zewar and act accordingly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
