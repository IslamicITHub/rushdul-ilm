    Title: To cure vitiligo Disease

    Question: My child who is two years old baby boy is suffering from vitiligo. He his having white patches on his legs and face since three months. I am giving him Unani and allopathic medicines. I request you to tell me the best dua to cure this disease, in-shaAllah. Please help with this. Please send it soon.

    Answer ID: 63388Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 405/386/H=04/1437) Add 11 kalaunji (nigella seeds) in one spoon honey and recite Surah Fatiha 1100 times with durood 11-11 times before and after and then blow it on the mixture of kalaunji and honey and give it to the child in the morning to consume. May Allah grant her perfect cure, Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
