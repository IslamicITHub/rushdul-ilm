    Title: I read from online book "masai'l of hairs" Masa'lah: An unmarried woman is not allowed to use khidhab or make-up. However, even without her husbands consent, a married woman may apply make-up or khidhab.#####I am unmarried that I cannot appply khidab of mehndi and oil,is it so??

    Question: Assalaamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullaahi wa Barakatuhu! Respected pious scholar,I read from online book "masai'l of hairs" Masa'lah: An unmarried woman is not allowed to use khidhab or make-up. However, even without her husbands consent, a married woman may apply make-up or khidhab.#####I am unmarried that I cannot appply khidab of mehndi and oil,is it so??

    Answer ID: 28779Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 59/45/L=1432)

    An unmarried woman as well may apply dye.
    روى الحسن عن أبى حنيفة رحمة الله أنه قال : لا بأس بالخضاب للجارية الصغيرة والكبيرة (تاتارخانية : 18/215)
    In the same way, she can do makeup observing the shariah limits. You can use the dye of henna or oil when you need.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
