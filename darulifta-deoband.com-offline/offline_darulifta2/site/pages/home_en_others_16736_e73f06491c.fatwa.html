    Title: CUTTING HAIR /NAILS ON SATURDAY CAUSES VITILIGO,GOING TO DOCTOR ON THURSDAY IS VERY BAD ,TRAVELLING ON WEDNESDAY IS GOOD,ARE THESE THINGS SUPERSTATIONS OR THEY HAVE ANYTHING RELATED TO ISLAM ?

    Question: CUTTING HAIR /NAILS ON SATURDAY CAUSES VITILIGO,GOING TO DOCTOR ON THURSDAY IS VERY BAD ,TRAVELLING ON WEDNESDAY IS GOOD,ARE THESE THINGS SUPERSTATIONS OR THEY HAVE ANYTHING RELATED TO ISLAM ?

    Answer ID: 16736Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1996/1587/D=1430)

     

    They are not proved from Shariyah. They belong to supersutions and mistakes of people. However, it is proved by some hadith that whoever cuts nails on Friday Almighty Allah not only keeps him safe from calamity and misfortune till next Friday but three more days i.e. upto ten days as well. Cutting nails with teeth is said to be the cause of leprosy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
