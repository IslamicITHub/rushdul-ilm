    Title: Dream Interpretation

    Question: (1) It was the time for Fajr and I, though half awake, had not got up for namaz by then. I slept again and dreamt that its time for tahajjud and the holy Prophet Muhammad (SAW) got up from a mattress laid on floor next to my bed and went for wudhu. Hearing the noise of him getting up, my roommate (Masha-Allah very pious) got up too. I was lying on my bed and seeing all this. Then I woke up (actually) and went for Fajr namaz. (2) Our new house is under construction. I dreamt that Prophet Muhammad (SAW), my father and my brother are standing in and around the foundation of the house and building the Holy Kabah. (3) I have seen the death of my father around two to three times in my dream when I was a kid.

    Answer ID: 35193Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2264/H=941/TH=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. (1) It indicates that you shall get closeness to the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and sweetness in your prayers, in-sha-Allah. (2) It indicates that your house shall have the blessings of wahi, recitation of the holy Quran and peace and satisfaction, in-sha-Allah. (3) The meaning of this dream is that the good deeds of your father are accepted before Almighty Allah and he got high status. May Almighty Allah bless you and your family members as well as all your relatives all kinds of benevolence and save from all kinds of evils. Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
