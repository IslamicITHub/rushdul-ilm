    Title: Is abortion permitted at an pregnancy of 3 months, due to financial constraints and no person to take care of child?

    Question: Is abortion permitted at an pregnancy of 3 months, due to financial constraints and no person to take care of child?

    Answer ID: 4551Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 426/426=M/1429)

     

    Abortion due to financial problem is unlawful. Yes, under medical compulsions it is allowable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
