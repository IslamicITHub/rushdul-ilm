    Title: How often should one apply itr? How often should one apply oil to one's hair? Is it permissible to comb one's hair everyday?

    Question: How often should one apply itr? How often should one apply oil to one's hair? Is it permissible to comb one's hair everyday?

    Answer ID: 8671Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1470/1225=L/1429)

     

    (1) There is no limit.

    (2) It is mentioned in Hadith, used to apply so much oil that his head cloth used to be like the cloth of oilman. (Jam?ul Wasaail 1:103)

    (3) One is allowed to comb hair daily if needed, but it is undesirable to pay so much attention to it:

    واما حدیث النھی عن الترجل الا غباً فالمراد بہ ترک المبالغۃ فی الترفہ المشعر بانھا من ھوی النفس والمشیر بانھا فی تنظیف الباطن اولی (جمع الوسائل 1:99)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
