    Title: Dream

    Question: I had a dream some 7 years back. At that time I had joined tabligh and was an active member of it. The whole plot is like this Prophet (SAW) is sitting with many people and all are taking oaths, I too took an oath and then the prophet points his finger towards me and says that this boy and suddenly my room partner awoke me from sleep. What does it signify?

    Answer ID: 45778Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 963/942/N=1434) Ma-shaAllah, your dream is blessed one. It indicates that if you bring perfection in following sunnah you shall receive true love and attachment with the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم), in-shaAllah, which shall be better for you in this world as well in the hereafter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
