    Question: assalamu alaikum. molana. i have some problem with friends.and relatives. now they disconnect to speak with me.but i want to speak and want to keep good relationship with them.but they can"t. so what is the way for this.and what and which dua i should recite?that thaey again come to me and speak well.kindly inform me as earliest,thank you

    Answer ID: 28197Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 66/67/B=1432)

    You should recite Ya Aziz (يا عزيز) 200 times after Isha salah with durood before and after while turning your face towards those relatives. Do it for 40 days. It shall soften their hearts. Further, sometime send them some gift and greet them when you meet them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
