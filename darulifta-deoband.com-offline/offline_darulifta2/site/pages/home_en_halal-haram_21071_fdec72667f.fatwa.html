    Title: I started working in a chartered accountant firm and enroled myself for CA. Please tell me the recording of facts in current senario of INDIAN economic system is lawful? beacuse an accontant has to record all the financial matters.

    Question: Salaam,Recently I started working in a chartered accountant firm and enroled myself for CA. Please tell me the recording of facts in current senario of INDIAN economic system is lawful? beacuse an accontant has to record all the financial matters.

    Answer ID: 21071Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 448/448/M=1431)

     

    If the job of recording interest based dealings is related to you it is not lawful. Almighty Allah has cursed those who do interest based calculations. It is alright if you do not commit any unlawful work while fulfilling your concerning duty.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
