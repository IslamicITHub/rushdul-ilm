    Title: DSA agent of home loan...only sourcing customer to bank and getting commission in % on loan amount?

    Question: I am not working in any bank but I have DSA (direct source agency) of some banks we are just completing home loan documentation formalities and banks giving us commission for sourcing customers so is it haram or halal? We are on commission basis not on salary basis.

    Answer ID: 156197Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 312/273/H=2/1439)

     

    It is not lawful to complete the home loan documentation formalities as well as receiving charges over it from the bank is not lawful since this charge is unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
