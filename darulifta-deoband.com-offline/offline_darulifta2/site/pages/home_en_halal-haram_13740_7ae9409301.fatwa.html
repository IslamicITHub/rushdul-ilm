    Question: Assalaamualeyikum, I am working as an IT Manager in Multinational Company which creates their own scientific products. I have got an opportunity to work with Multinational Financial Bank (Deutshe Bank). I understand this bank deals in Interest. My job profile in this company will be to maintain their worldwide offices network connectivity. Is it allowed to work in Informaton Technology department in Financial banks... Jazaakumullah...

    Answer ID: 13740Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 936/739=L/1430)

     

    Your work does not involve helping in writing interest or bearing witness to it directly, therefore it is allowable to work in information technology department of a financial bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
