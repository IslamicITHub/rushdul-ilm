    Question: It has been one week since I have made a dream, I am not remember it clearly and fully, but what I remember is that a person has given me a glass of water and told me that it is water from Jannat. I drank it and told that person that am not feeling anything. The person told me to wait. I waited and I felt the water flowing or spreading or running in all parts of my body. It is all that I remember about the dream. What does this dream means?

    Answer ID: 939Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1021=757/H)

     

    It is hoped that recitation of Holy Quran and good deeds shall benefit the humanity. This is the meaning of the dream and there is a warning that one should recite the Quran and refrain from sins.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
