    Title: If somebody could'nt join jamat in ishan namaz and performed farz namaz alone,latter he joined the jamat in tarawih.

    Question: If somebody could'nt join jamat in ishan namaz and performed farz namaz alone,latter he joined the jamat in tarawih.Can he perform namze witr along with the jamat behind the imam?please elaborate the answer. Q.2-can someone perform namaz using deo or perfume? Q.3-Is it authorised to recite bismillah in namaz after surah fateha and before any ayate Quran?please elaborate if Ayate Quran consists of number of surah,can we use bismillah before every surah? Q.4-In juma khutba when Azan is given after azan is it required to recite duae-Azan?please advice what is good to recite in between khutba when imam sits down after first khutba.

    Answer ID: 32717Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 981/841/B=1432) (1) Yes, he may offer. (2) If the dew perfume contains any alcohol prepared by wine then offering salah shall not be lawful after applying it. (3) Yes, it is mustahab to recite Bismillah in salah before each Surah. (4) After the Azan of khutba one should not read Dua-e-Azan rather he should sit quite. One should do important duas in his heart between the two khutbas for himself or for his family. It is a time in which dua is accepted.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
