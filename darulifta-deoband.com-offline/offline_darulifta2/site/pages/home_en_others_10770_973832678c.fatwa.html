    Question: khab men apne shouhar ko dekha ache kapde pahne aur bohut khush aate hain aur kahte hain ke mera intiqal nahee huwa ab maen tum se shadi karne aya mere bhai ki awaz sunai deti hae ke yeh kaese ho sakta hai jaiz nahee hai(mere shouhar ki wafat hue 2 sal 7 mah hogae)barah karam khab ki tabeer bataiye (urdu men jawab chahiye)

    Answer ID: 10770Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 159/143=L/1429)

     

    It indicates that your husband is in good condition. You should exceedingly do isal-e-thawab (delivering the reward to dead) for your husband.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
