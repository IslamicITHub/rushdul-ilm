    Title: In dream I saw That I m killing a person who had killed many other muslim i.e. I was taking revenge what does it mean. As marrying uncle's wife is jaiz after his death but what if she is my maternal aunt. Many A time I see Mosque in my dream

    Question: In dream I saw That I m killing a person who had killed many other muslim i.e. I was taking revenge what does it mean. (2) As marrying uncle's wife is jaiz after his death but what if she is my maternal aunt. (3) Many A time I see Mosque in my dream.

    Answer ID: 4253Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1133/822=H/1429)

     

    (1) It means that Allah will enable you to perform such actions which will benefit all people; Muslims and non-Muslims.

    (2) One can marry the widow of his uncle (maternal or paternal) after she passes the iddah.

    (3) This is also a good dream. Seeing mosque in dream means that you will be granted safety of your imaan and belief.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
