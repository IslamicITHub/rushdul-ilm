    Title: Dua and wazifah for issueless parents

    Question: My sister is married since 7 years and my brother is married since 1 and half years. Both my sister and my brother’s wife have not conceived yet. They don't have any problem. Please tell me any dua or wazifahs for children from Sunnah.

    Answer ID: 68720Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 942/762/D=11/1437)

    (1) Your sister and brother should remember the following dua and read out it extensively specially after salah:

    رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِن لَّدُنْكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاء [آل عمران : 38]

    In-shaAllah with the blessings of this verse Allah shall bless pious child.

     

    (2) Also recite the following dua after each salah, in-shaAllah you shall have child:

     

    رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِي فَرْداً وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْوَارِثِينَ [الأنبياء : 89]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
