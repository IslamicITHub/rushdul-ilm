    Question ID: 49755Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Namaz

    Question: If a mistake has been committed by a person who is following Imam during Jamat in salat (namaz) so what he should do, whether to do the sajda e sahav or there is no need to do that. Kindly provide your opinion on this,

    Answer ID: 49755Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 181/133/D=02/1435) It is not clear what type of mistake was committed by the muqtadi. However, if he has left any wajib or similar acts then his salah will be valid and there will be no need to do sajdah sahv (remedial prostration): ففی البدائع: فاما المقتدی اذا سھا فی صلاتہ فلا سھو علیہ (1/420، دارالکتاب) But if a muqtadi leaves any fardh (obligatory act) then his salah will not be valid. Therefore, it is better to clearly mention what mistake he committed and ask the question again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
