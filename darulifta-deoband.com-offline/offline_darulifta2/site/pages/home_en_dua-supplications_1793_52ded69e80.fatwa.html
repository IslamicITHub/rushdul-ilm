    Question ID: 1793Country: India

    Title: I am having two daughter, so far I have no son recently my wife is pregnant, have you any vazifa under the light of quran or hadith which I or My wife recite then can I get son ? I will be thankfull to you if you guide me in this matter.

    Question: Assalamu alaikum, janab Mufti saheb, I am having two daughter, so far I have no son recently my wife is pregnant, have you any vazifa under the light of quran or hadith which I or My wife recite then can I get son ? I will be thankfull to you if you guide me in this matter. thanks, Rafik Lakhani

    Answer ID: 1793Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 582/571=M)

     

    Keeping yourself contented with destiny, the husband and wife should recite the first ruku of Surah Maryam after every obligatory salah, blow on hand and spread it on the body. Or, you may recite the below verse eleven times after every obligatory salah and blow them on hand and wipe it on the body:

    فلما اثقلت دعوا اللہ ربھما لئن اٰتیتنا صالحاً لنکونن من الشاکرین

    In-sha-Allah, you will be granted a son.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
