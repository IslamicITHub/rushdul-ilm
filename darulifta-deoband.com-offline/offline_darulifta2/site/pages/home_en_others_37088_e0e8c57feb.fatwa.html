    Title: My wife dreamed that he gave birth to a child. And both of us were very happy for that

    Question: My wife dreamed that he gave birth to a child. And both of us were very happy for that. Would you please help me know that?

    Answer ID: 37088Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 453/453/M=1433) The dream is blessed. It indicates towards prosperity and blessings. Pay thanks to Allah in good and sad times and do not cross the Shariah boundary.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
