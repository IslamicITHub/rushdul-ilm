    Title: I have been seeing a lot of deep sea/ocean in my dreams since school days. Sometimes I am with my husband near the sea/ocean in my dream. Please interpret.

    Question: I have been seeing a lot of deep sea/ocean in my dreams since school days. Sometimes I am with my husband near the sea/ocean in my dream. Please interpret.

    Answer ID: 36596Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 426/426/=1433) The dream indicates that your hope shall come true, in-shaAllah and your life shall be peaceful provided you save yourself from the disobedience of Allah and His Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and do not fall prey of nafs and Satan.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
