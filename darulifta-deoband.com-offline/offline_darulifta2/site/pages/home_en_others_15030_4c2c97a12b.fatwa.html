    Title: Assalam o alaikum. I would like a dream interpreted please. Background: 22 Year old Male. Originally from Pakistan just graduated from Mechanical Engineering from the University of Manchester. In my dream i spoke to Rasulullah (saw). Didnt see him just spoke to him. I asked him a question and he replied with a confirmatory negative. I asked him about taking further action about some matter and he said: No; nothing further (implying: what has been done is sufficient) And then i walked away happy that Rasulullah (saw) agreed with me. (Because i had asked the question knowing full well that i could do nothing more; and it was a formal thing) Was this dream legitimate? If so then kindly tell me what it means. Jazakallah Khair.

    Answer ID: 15030Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1711/1377=H/1430)

     

    It exhorts you to shun anti-sunnah innovations, customs, display and vanity. Just follow the sunnah and let other things aside, it is sufficient.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
