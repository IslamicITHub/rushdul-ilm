    Question ID: 7865Country: India

    Title: I have few questions to ask 1) Is it ok to eat meat , beef or chicken from non - muslim hotels & restaurants ? 2) Is it ok if non - muslims offer namaz with us ( They have faith in islam but unable tocome forward due to some reasons.

    Question: Assalamualaikum . khariat? I have few questions to ask 1) Is it ok to eat meat , beef or chicken from non - muslim hotels & restaurants ? 2) Is it ok if non - muslims offer namaz with us ( They have faith in islam but unable tocome forward due to some reasons.

    Answer ID: 7865Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1074/1074=M/1429)

     

    (1) Avoid consuming meat at the restaurants of non-Muslims. Yes, if you are sure that the meat presented there is slaughtered as per the Islamic way, then there is no wrong in eating there.

    (2) If a person offers salah with jamat, it is acceptable and it will be considered a sign of his being Muslim:

    وفی الشامی: الکافر اذا صلی بجماعۃ یحکم باسلامہ عندنا۔

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
