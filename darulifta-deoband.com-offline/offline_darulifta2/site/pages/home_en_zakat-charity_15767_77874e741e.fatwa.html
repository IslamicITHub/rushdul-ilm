Q2)If the children (who are mustahiq of zakaat)take control of the zakaat money and give it back to the School to pay for the School expensis then can that money be used to pay of the debts of the School?

    Answer ID: 15767Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1395L/278TL=1430)

     

    If the donators give the Zakah money for the same children, the school authorities can give the money in the possession of the children and then receive the same as school fee and other expenses.

    (2) Yes, the amount can be used for payment of school debt.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
