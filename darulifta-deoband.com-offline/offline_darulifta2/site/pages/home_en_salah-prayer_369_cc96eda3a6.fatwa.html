    Title: I follow hanafi Maslak. Now my question is that if i am in Safr can i pray Zohr & Asr together or Magrib & Issa together?

    Question: I follow hanafi Maslak. Now my question is that if i am in Safr can i pray Zohr & Asr together or Magrib & Issa together? Please advice from Quran and Hadith

    Answer ID: 369Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 174=170/L)

     

    According to Hanafi Maslak it is not allowed to combine prayers (in real meaning):

    ولاجمع بین فرضین في وقت بعذر سفر أو مطر (الدرالمختار مع الشامي، 2/45، ط زکریا دیوبند)

     

    Instead, one can combine in a way that, for example, one prays Zuhr at the end of Zuhr time and prays Asr at the beginning of its time.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
