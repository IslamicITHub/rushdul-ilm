    Title: I have taken one of my friend to a hospital who is a non-muslim. At the time of giving injection to him, can I pray for him by saying ya shafi ya kafi. pl. clarify this point.

    Question: I have taken one of my friend to a hospital who is a non-muslim. At the time of giving injection to him, can I pray for him by saying ya shafi ya kafi. pl. clarify this point.

    Answer ID: 7590Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1068/1065=M/1429)

     

    You cave injection by saying ya shafi ya kafi, there is no wrong in it, it is allowable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
