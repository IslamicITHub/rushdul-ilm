    Answer ID: 8698Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1947/1811=D/1429)

     

    It is indication to carelessness in nature. However, if you have grown responsible then it is satanic effect.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
