    Title: My dad(abbu) when gets angry forgets everything and even forgets that iam his daughter and starts hitting me very badly he's not bothered while he hits me if i die.

    Question: My dad(abbu) when gets angry forgets everything and even forgets that iam his daughter and starts hitting me very badly he's not bothered while he hits me if i die.....iam very upset of his behaviour he hits my brother my ammi also...please suggest us something please give me some duwa so that he can overcome this behaviour of his.

    Answer ID: 31052Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 689/450/H=1432) Dua is accepted more in tense conditions. You should behave your father with respect and politeness instead of his attitude. Recite Surah al-Quraish after each salah 7 times with durood 7-7 times before and after, blow it on water and mix it in tea, milk, soup, flour, and give it to your father to drink or eat. There is no problem if other people consume it. Also, you should make special dua for your father besides blowing it on water.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
