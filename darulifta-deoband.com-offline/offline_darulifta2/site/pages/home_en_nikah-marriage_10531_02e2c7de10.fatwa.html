    Title: (i)I came close to my maid servant, touched her and did some kissing like performances. Now I am repented and sent her away from my house. Is the relation between me and my wife affected? (ii)Everytime I see sex scenes in the internet I cry immediately, but I repeat the same case and cry again.What type of sin is this?

    Question: (i)I came close to my maid servant, touched her and did some kissing like performances. Now I am repented and sent her away from my house. Is the relation between me and my wife affected? (ii)Everytime I see sex scenes in the internet I cry immediately, but I repeat the same case and cry again.What type of sin is this?

    Answer ID: 10531Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 198/182=B/1429)

     

    (1) Kissing maidservant will not affect the validity of Nikah with your wife.

    (2) By seeing sex scenes on internet you are feared to fall in adultery; you should refrain from it. It has no benefit; neither in the world nor in the hereafter, rather it is leading to major sin. It is major sin like watching cinema.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
