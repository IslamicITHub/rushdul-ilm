    Question ID: 37246Country: India

    Title: Rent received is haram or halal.

    Question: I have a commercial office. A sports bar company has approached me to take my office on rent (Galaxy Entertainment Corporation Ltd - Mumbai ). The nature of their business is , they have big restaurants and other large spaces, in other areas outside my office premises, where people come to play different types of games and at the same time they relax and drink alcohol. Now the point is that they are not carrying out any of their business activities in my office such as playing of games or serving alcohol. They are just going to manage their accounts and administration in my office. My question is that is this rent that I shall receive - Halal or Haram. Please reply most urgently as I have to reply to them in 3 to 4 days.

    Answer ID: 37246Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 411/162/L=1433) If they only will manage their accounts and administatration in your office, then you can give your office to them on rent. And thus you will be permitted to use the amount of rent paid by them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
