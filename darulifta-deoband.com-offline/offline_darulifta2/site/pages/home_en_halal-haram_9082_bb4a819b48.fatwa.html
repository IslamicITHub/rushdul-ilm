    Question: Is it permissible for a male to have his hair cut by a ghair-mahram female?

    Answer ID: 9082Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1549/1289=L/1429)

     

    It is not allowed for a man to have his hair cur by ghiar-mahram woman.
