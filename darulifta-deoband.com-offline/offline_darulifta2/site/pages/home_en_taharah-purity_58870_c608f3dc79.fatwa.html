    Question ID: 58870Country: India

    Title: Requesting for cure from light of Quran

    Question: I am 26 years old. From past few years I have problem maintaining my taharah ie after urinating I have some urine drops coming out. This problem is very embarrassing. I want to become a pious Muslim. But when this happens I feel very disappointed. I am praying Allah (SWT) to bless me with cure for this problem. I am very disappointed. I have full faith that cure for every problem is in Quran. I want totally get rid of this problem so please tell me a Dua or Ayat from Quran which will help me in getting rid of this problem. I mean I am still 26 years and I want to lead a pious life. I want to be faithful to Allah and his Rasul. I want to live a life of taharah. I want to be clean every time as taharah is half of iman. I really want to be a pious Muslim but this problem is letting me down. Please help.

    Answer ID: 58870Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 379/379/SD=07/1436) Nowadays it is very common that many people suffer from urination drop. You need not worry. Do istibra after urination, i.e. make sure that drops are no longer falling though it takes longer time for you to urinate. Hence Shariah asks to do it so that one’s doubt is removed regarding the drops. You may use tissue paper or clay clod for urinating.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
