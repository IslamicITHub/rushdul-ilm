    Question: In my village, there is a Eidgaah where we perform both Eid and Baqr-Eid Prayers. But the large number of people cannot perform in it because it is too small. And also there is no room at both the sides so that it can be extended. So we want to know that can this Eidgaah be sold and another land can be bought. Or can it be exchanged with another land? Please reply soon. It is an urgent Mas?alah. Thanks

    Answer ID: 5959Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 673/631=L/1429)

     

    To sell the Eidgah or replace it to any other land is not right. Yes, if the space of Eidgah is not enough for those praying Eid Salah, then all the people of village should unanimously construct a mosque on the place of Eidgah and build a new Eidgah with their donations.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
