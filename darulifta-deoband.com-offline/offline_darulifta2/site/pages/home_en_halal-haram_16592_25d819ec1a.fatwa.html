    Title: as salam ale kum,my family business is tobbaco based(bidi manufacturing)some people said that earning from this business is haram it true or not?

    Question: as salam ale kum,my family business is tobbaco based(bidi manufacturing)some people said that earning from this business is haram it true or not?

    Answer ID: 16592Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1643/1304/L=1430)

     

    The income of the business will not be ruled as unlawful, but the income will be makrooh tanzihi.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
