    Title: I am living in one place Aheri with my family. and i am working at Ballarshaha 100 km away from Aheri. i stayed there 3 to 4 day in week. what is direction about Namaj? which type of namaj aplicable? KASAR or full?

    Question: i am living in one place Aheri with my family. and i am working at Ballarshaha 100 km away from Aheri. i stayed there 3 to 4 day in week. what is direction about Namaj? which type of namaj aplicable? KASAR or full?

    Answer ID: 26290Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2371/H=854/th=1431)

    If you intend to stay in Ballarshaha for two or three days, then you will do qasr there i.e. if you pray alone or behind a musafir imam you will do qasr

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
