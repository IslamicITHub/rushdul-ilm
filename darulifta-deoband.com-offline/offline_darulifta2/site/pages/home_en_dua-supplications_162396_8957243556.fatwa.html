    Title: Feeling scared and confused

    Question: I am currently working in Bangalore for the past six days I feel very scared and confused and I am getting a feel of home please tell me some dua to cure this I just started to work so I will not be able to take leave. Please respond soon.

    Answer ID: 162396Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1202/1001/N=11/1439)

     

    Keep on reading istighfar and durood sharif extensively. In-shaAllah, your problems and confusion will go away and you will feel comfortable with your work. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
