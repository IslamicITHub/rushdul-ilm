    Question: Assalamu Alaikum? My question is i saw kaba very near and am hearing azan from inside kaabain my dream my elder sister also with me, please tell me the tabir of this dream????

    Answer ID: 14876Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1663/1333=H/1430)

     

    You and your sister shall be granted khushu, khudhu? (complete dedication and devotion) and spirituality in prayers. In-sha-Allah!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
