    Title: Assalamalaikum, Which Dua is good for relieving Bank credit?

    Question: Assalamalaikum, Which Dua is good for relieving Bank credit?

    Answer ID: 13663Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 944/944=M/1430)

     

    Recite the following dua after every salah and in the morning and evening:

    أللھم أکفني بحلالک عن حرامک وأغنني بفضلک عمن سواک

    Also, keep on looking for ways to repay the debt.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
