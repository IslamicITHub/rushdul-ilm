    Title: Assalam-o-alaykum, I saw today morning in my dream that one of my friend is weeping and telling me that he has got a very bad disease and I am thinking (in that dream) is that disease happened to me too or not. Please help me in this regard. Assalam-o-alaykum.

    Question: Assalam-o-alaykum, I saw today morning in my dream that one of my friend is weeping and telling me that he has got a very bad disease and I am thinking (in that dream) is that disease happened to me too or not. Please help me in this regard. Assalam-o-alaykum.

    Answer ID: 9144Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2333/1822=H/1429)

     

    It means that you both are negligent of following sunnah. The dream is warning to remove this negligence.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
