    Title: My wife dreamt during her afternoon sleep that a woman came to her and said that she should recite chapter 2 verse 285 of Quran and Allah will help her overcome her future problems. Could you please let me know how to interpret this dream.

    Question: My wife dreamt during her afternoon sleep that a woman came to her and said that she should recite chapter 2 verse 285 of Quran and Allah will help her overcome her future problems. Could you please let me know how to interpret this dream.

    Answer ID: 5300Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 323/1002=H-1429

     

    There are some shortcomings in the beliefs and good deeds of your wife. The dream seeks to divert your attention to rectify the same. She should recite the last ruku of Surah Al-Baqrah after very obligatory salah, also she should study the Tafseer Mariful Quran (written by Hadhrat Mufti Muhammad Shafee) especially the last commentary of last ruku of Surah Al-Baqrah. If not by herself, you should read out for her, rather you should regularly study, read and hear it, it will be useful in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
