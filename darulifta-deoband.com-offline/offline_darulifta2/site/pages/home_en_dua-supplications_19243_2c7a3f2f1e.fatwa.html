    Question: Assalaam Alaikum, I am self employed,married & having a boy kid. My date of birth is 05.12.1974. Since years, i am observing that the CAT(billi) cross my road very frequently, aprrox 2-3 times a week. Most of the times from Right to Left & some times from Left to Right. When I was a teenager, that time I use to feared about the same. Now its usual for me. But still wherever the Cat cross me, I recite Darut Shareef. Please tell me what should I have to read in such situation. And why this is happening with me??

    Answer ID: 19243Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 174/152/D=1431)

     

    This is ignorant pessimism and evil omen which has no reality. One should submit his trust in Allah alone. Such thoughts were forbidden in Hadith; rather you should recite this dua at such occasions:

    اللھم لا یاتی بالحسنات الا انت ولا یدفع السیئات الا انت ولا حول ولا قوۃ الا باللہ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
