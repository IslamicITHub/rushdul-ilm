    Answer ID: 13424Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 854/664=L/1430)

     

    In order to establish nikah, it is necessary that two witnesses are present in the meeting of Nikah. Thus, the Nikah performed by you did not take place. Neither it is lawful for you to consider her as your wife nor behave with her as wife.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
