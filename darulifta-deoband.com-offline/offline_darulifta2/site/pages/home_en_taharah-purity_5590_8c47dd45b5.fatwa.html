    Title: Is the wudu gets nullified (broken) after eating the flesh of camel?

    Question: assalamualaikum wr wb, Is the wudu gets nullified (broken) after eating the flesh of camel? jazakallahu khairan.

    Answer ID: 5590Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 281/281=M/1429)

     

    Wuzu does not get nullified by eating meat of camel. It is proved by the Prophet صلي الله عليه وسلم)) as it is mentioned in Hdiath:

    كان أخر الأ مرين من رسول لله صلي الله عليه وسلم ترك الوضوء مما مست النار

    However, the traditions mentioning wuzu imply that wuzu is just mustahab i.e. it is better to make wuzu.

    فالأحسن ان يقال: إن الأمر بالوضو محمول علي الاستحباب و تركه بيان الجواز (اعلا ء السنن1/(107

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
