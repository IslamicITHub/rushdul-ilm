    Title: I want to go to my office just after the farz Friday salah. Can I offer all sunnat and nafl namaz before the Juma 2 rakaat farz?

    Question: I want to go to my office just after the farz Friday salah. Can I offer all sunnat and nafl namaz before the Juma 2 rakaat farz?

    Answer ID: 51422Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 572/468/B=04/1435) You should perform two rakat as Tahiyat al-Masjid and then 4 rakat as Sunnat Muakkadah before Fardh Salah of Jumuah. After Jumuah, first perform 4 rakat Sunnat Muakkadah and then two rakat Sunnat. Then, you should go to office. The later Sunnah salah may not be offered before Jumuah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
