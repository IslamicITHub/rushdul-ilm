    Title: want to know that talaq confirm or not ???

    Question: Since 1 month ago my brother gives her wife talaq 3 times at in the both type once call and second massage also. Then I want to know that talaq get confirm or not?

    Answer ID: 63091Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 238/238/B=03/1437) First, ask your brother if he has really given three talaqs to his wife on phone and afterward through message or not. If he confirms giving three talaqs then all the three talaqs occurred. Your brother’s wife became haram for him. Now he cannot marry him again without a valid halalah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
