    Question: My very close friend has got BONE CANCER which has been detected in april,2010 last month after diagnose it has found he is on last stage,It has just 3 months that he have married and now he has got such a disease, please let us know the dua which he can recite to completely vanish that disese and leave a happy life with his wife and also pray for him.

    Answer ID: 22585Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 856/856/M=1431)

    You should go under the treatment of any expert doctor. Also after each salah recite surah al-Quraish 41 times with durood 11-11 times before and after, blow it on water and drink it. As well as blow it on oil and apply it. Also, recite surah Fatiha 7-7 times in the morning and evening and blow on your body.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
