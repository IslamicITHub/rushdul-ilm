    Title: I am affected one side headache since four yeras. i have taken treatment from homeopathetic and unani medicine. But the pain is again coming. Kindly tell me any duwa for healing the one side headache?

    Question: I am affected one side headache since four yeras. i have taken treatment from homeopathetic and unani medicine. But the pain is again coming. Kindly tell me any duwa for healing the one side headache?

    Answer ID: 22929Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1073/873/B=1431)

    Put your hand on the place of headache and recite the following dua seven times:
    أسأل الله العظيم ، رب العرش العظيم ان يشفيني

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
