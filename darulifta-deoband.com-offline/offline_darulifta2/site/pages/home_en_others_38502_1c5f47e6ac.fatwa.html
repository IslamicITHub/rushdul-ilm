    Title: I am feeling very much tension to join fardh prayer (by jamah) but due to some reason I am unable to perform

    Question: (1) I am feeling very much tension to join fardh prayer (by jamah) but due to some reason I am unable to perform (i) The jamah is over and I want to go to other mosque to get jamah but I lost my sleeper, shoes. (ii) The people performing salah in mosque in opposite direction of Qiblah (East direction). (iii) There are many statues (animal faces) in front of people performing salah in mosque. (2) I am traveling by train or other means, some time I miss my main train and traveling in other train and I am feeling very tension. Sometime my companion has missed the train. I am feeling very tension in traveling by any other means. (3) I am using toilet which is opened or visible to other people but I have no other option to use other and I am feeling very bad.

    Answer ID: 38502Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 865/529/H=1433) (1) Your dream is very important. It indicates that the religious conditions of Muslims are declining rapidly. May Almighty Allah give Muslims taufiq to follow the religion strictly! Amen! (2&3) You pay more attention to world than the hereafter. May Almighty Allah remove this shortcoming! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
