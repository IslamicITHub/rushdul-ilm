    Title: I told in anger to my wife that go to your father home. After 2 days we again live together with love and did intercourse. Is the intercourse zina or it is right?

    Question: I told in anger to my wife that go to your father home. After 2 days we again live together with love and did intercourse. Is the intercourse zina or it is right?

    Answer ID: 152750Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1081/1012/SD=11/1438)

    If you did not intend to divorce your wife by the sentence “go to your father home” then in such a case no talaq occurred on your wife. Thus it is not haram to have intercourse after uttering this sentence.(Fatwa: 1081/1012/SD=11/1438)

    If you did not intend to divorce your wife by the sentence “go to your father home” then in such a case no talaq occurred on your wife. Thus it is not haram to have intercourse after uttering this sentence.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
