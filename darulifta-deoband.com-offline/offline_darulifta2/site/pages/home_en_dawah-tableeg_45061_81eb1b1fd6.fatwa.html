    Question ID: 45061Country: India

    Title: Aazan

    Question: What does the holy Quran and hadith say regarding the following? I have seen a muazzin and even the imam of that Masjid once or twice get up from sleep for the Fajr salah and without performing wudhu just went to the room switched on the microphone and said the azan. Now my question is that whether wudhu is necessary for saying azan or not?

    Answer ID: 45061Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 663/663/M=1434) It is not necessary to do wudhu for azan. It is also done without wudhu, but it is better (mustahab) to call azan with wudhu. ويكره أذان جنب وإقامته وإقامة محدث لا أذانه على المذهب (درمختار)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
