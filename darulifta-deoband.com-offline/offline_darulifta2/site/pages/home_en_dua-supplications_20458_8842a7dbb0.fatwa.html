    Question: Assalam Walekum Sir, I and my wife has taken a lot loan from bank to leave and to give a luxury life to our parents and to our kids, now we have stucked in this game of rolling money, some time situation comes we don't have single penny to full fill our childrens small demands.please do lets us know the dua or any other thing by which we can get rid of with depth.cause we are feed up of this calls from bank and need peace in our house and life, cause we both are fustrated in life and cant give attention to our children?

    Answer ID: 20458Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 370/282/B=1431)

     

    You did very wrong act. Recite the following dua extensively:

    اللهم اكفنى بحلالك عن حرامك و اغننى بفضلك عمن سواك

    And do it for forty days and recite durood 100-100 times after and before, also recite لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله five hundred times in between, and then supplicate Almighty Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
