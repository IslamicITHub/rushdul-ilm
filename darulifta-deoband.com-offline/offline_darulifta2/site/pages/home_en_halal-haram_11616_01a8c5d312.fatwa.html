    Title: My mother was having jwelwery it was purchased by my let father , that time he use to take bribe and i dont know what type of money used for this purchase , amount i got after selling of this jwelery is it haram or halal?

    Question: My mother was having jwelwery it was purchased by my let father , that time he use to take bribe and i dont know what type of money used for this purchase , amount i got after selling of this jwelery is it haram or halal?

    Answer ID: 11616Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 585/585=M/1430)

     

    If it is known surely or most probably that your father purchased the jewellery purely for bribe money, so the price of it will not be halal for you. If you know the real owners (from whom he used to receive the bribe), it is necessary to give it back, otherwise give it in charity on their behalf. If you are not sure that it was purchased by bribe money, then it cannot be termed as haram, but then also it should be given in charity out of precaution.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
