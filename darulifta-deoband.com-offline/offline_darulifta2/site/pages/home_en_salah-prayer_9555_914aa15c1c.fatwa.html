    Question: Assalamualaikum Hadhrat i hv a question regurding da matter of 'Du-allin' or 'Zu-allin'! Which one is right and how? my uncle who followDeobandi Maslaik in Pakistan, he say that 'Zu-allin' is right bt our Musfti Saheb who certify from Darul Uloom Deoband says that 'Du-allin' is right! Please tell me which one is right and how(details)?

    Answer ID: 9555Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 60/61=L/1429)

     

    Zuad (ض) should be pronounced with its original pronunciation, it is not right to pronounce it like dal (د); therefore the Mufti Sahib is wrong who says it should be recited duallin instead of zuallin.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
