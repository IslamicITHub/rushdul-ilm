    Question ID: 26286Country: United Arab Emirates

    Title: (May Peace,Mercy & Blessings of Allah (swt) be on all Muslims) (Q.1) Can we do Dua through the wasila of Muhammed(SAW).? (Please reply in the light of Quran & Sahih Hadith with references)

    Question: Assalamualikum-Rehamatullahi-Wabarakatahu. (May Peace,Mercy & Blessings of Allah(swt)be on all Muslims) (Q.1) Can we do Dua through the wasila of Muhammed(SAW).? (Please reply in the light of Quran & Sahih Hadith with references)

    Answer ID: 26286Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2452/H=903/th=1431)

    Yes, you can do. Adopting the wasilah (mediation) of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) in dua is not prohibited, while its permission is proved from many verses and ahadith. For details, see: Wafaul Wafa Bi Akhbar Dar al-Mustafa, Vol. 2, p. 419; Sunan Darmi Fil Muqaddamah Vol. 1, p. 34; Tafsir Ruh al-Ma'ani Vol. 1, p 289; Tafsir al-Durr al-Mansur Vol. 1, p 88 etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
