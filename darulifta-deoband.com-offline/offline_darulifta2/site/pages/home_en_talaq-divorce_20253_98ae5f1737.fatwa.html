    Title: i wanna ask that if 3 talaq has been given , and in process of halalah what is the minimum limit of sohabat with second husband?

    Question: assalmualay kum mufti sahab, i wanna ask that if 3 talaq has been given , and in process of halalah what is the minimum limit of sohabat with second husband?

    Answer ID: 20253Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 408/314/D=1431)

     

    As per hadith of Usailah, at least one time intercourse (penetration of penis in vagina) is compulsory. There is no limit for the period of muashirah:

    الطلاق لمن أخذ الساق ۔۔۔ والذي بيده عقدة النكاح

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
