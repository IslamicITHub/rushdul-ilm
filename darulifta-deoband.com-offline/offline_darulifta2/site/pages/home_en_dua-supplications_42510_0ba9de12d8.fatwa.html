    Title: Dua

    Question: I am 22 years old I want to marry a very very religious boy, but my family is not very religious. What should I do to get very religious boy for me?

    Answer ID: 42510Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1758/L=634/TL=1434) Keep on reciting the following dua extensively: رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ. In-shaAllah, you shall be married with any pious boy though your family members are not religious minded.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
