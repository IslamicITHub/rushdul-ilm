    Title: A lady relative suddenly went like mad woman, i prayed tahajjud & made dua for her,then went to sleep. i dreamed i was in sleeping position and she came towards me and dissolved in me (like she submitted her life to me) Whats the meaning please?

    Question: RE: Dream Assalamu alaikum a lady relative suddenly went like mad woman, i prayed tahajjud & made dua for her,then went to sleep. i dreamed i was in sleeping position and she came towards me and dissolved in me (like she submitted her life to me) Whats the meaning please? Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 28577Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 124/96/H=1432)

    It means that your dua benefited her and so is hoped in future as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
