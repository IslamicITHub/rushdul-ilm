    Title: I recently got engaged, and after my engagement saw a dream in the early morning time that my engagement is broken.

    Question: I recently got engaged, and after my engagement saw a dream in the early morning time that my engagement is broken. I dont remember anything else in the dream. This has made me anxious. please respond to my querey at the soonest. Jazak Allah

    Answer ID: 31473Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 608/511/B=1432) This is a satanic whispering. Do not pay any attention to it. This relation of yours will be strong and durable. You should perform nikah trusting Allah and should not fall prey to a doubt.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
