    Title: My wife seeing black snakes in her dreames but snakes doesn't harm her what is it's TABEER. One night she saw a big black snake in her dreame. she called me, then I and my son(14 years old) killed the snack, what does it mean?

    Question: My wife seeing black snakes in her dreames but snakes doesn't harm her what is it's TABEER. One night she saw a big black snake in her dreame. she called me, then I and my son(14 years old) killed the snack, what does it mean?

    Answer ID: 11358Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 603/503=D/1430)

     

    A snake is interpreted as enemy, but when the snakes do not give you any harm, then the enemies also will not be able to inflict harm on you, in-shaAllah. Even, the last dream indicates that you shall overpower your enemies; therefore you neither need to worry nor to snoop the enemy and suspect him. The nafs (self) of a man is an enemy of him, which sometime is defeated, but once it finds some chance it tries to overcome; therefore one should not be neglectful of the deviations of nafs and Satan, rather one should always watch over lest it diverts him from good deeds and lure to sins. It is said that the most bitter enemies of you is your own nafs which is in your chest. Therefore, we need to be alert ever, the dream indicates to both these things.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
