    Title: Some months ago I saw Muhammad (PBUH), but not clearly his face. He was facing other side talking to some other person and some one said that a person there is a Muhammad (PBUH).

    Question: Some months ago I saw Muhammad (PBUH), but not clearly his face. He was facing other side talking to some other person and some one said that a person there is a Muhammad (PBUH). Do I have really seen him? Please tell. And my second dream was in Ramadan month when I was sleeping in sunnah way at 3:00 am, near sehri I saw a black image coming from my left side and touched my left cheek and I suddenly got up after that and when I touched my left cheek I found it was too cold compared to other one. Please help.

    Answer ID: 36517Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 313/243/D=1433) (1) It is glad tiding and blessing for the person who saw the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه سلم) in his dream. If the face was to opposite direction as avoidance then it is a warning towards the shortcomings of the viewer. However the dream is blessed one. Follow the sunnah and read durood extensively. (2) This dream has no meaning, hence it should be ignored. It may not be interpreted.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
