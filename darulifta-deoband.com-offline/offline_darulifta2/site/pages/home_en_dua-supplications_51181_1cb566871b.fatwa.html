    Title: Special amal or Dua for winning case and heart of the judge

    Question: My relatives, poor, widow, senior citizens are fighting with their relatives for their right. A civil case in local court is delaying for no reason. We suspect our Advocate also bribed by the opposite side. Please, inform any effective amal/zikr to attain their Halal share as per Shariah. The amal/zikr should be so effective to change the mind of the opposite side / judge of the case for quickest relief. Thanks!

    Answer ID: 51181Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 463/463/M=04/1435) They should continue to adopt all possible lawful means to get their due tights. They should also perform five times salah and specially make dua after the salah. Also, they should recite Ya Mudhillu (یا مذل) 75 times (with durood 11-11 times before and after) in the morning and evening and supplicate Allah. In-shaAllah, the enemy will face humiliation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
