    Title: My question is do islam permit to celebarate the birthday.or what do shariyat says about celebrating birthday.

    Question: Assalamualiakum, My question is do islam permit to celebarate the birthday.or what do shariyat says about celebrating birthday. Jazak Allah

    Answer ID: 20918Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 516/439/B=1431)

     

    This practice is not proved by the holy Quran, Hadith, the companions of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم), tabein, (successors to the companions of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and the four Imams. The tradition of birthday celebration has been invented by the Jews and Christians. This is a nonsense practice according to Islam.

    من حسن إسلام المرء تركه مالا يعنيه (ترمذي)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
