    Answer ID: 9368Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2384/1985=B/1429)

     

    If there is a work of writing interest-base account or checking it in the bank, then apparently the salary of such kind of employment will be haram and unlawful. As it is unlawful to eat and feed interest, in the similar way it is also unlawful to be a witness to it and write its account, and all have been cursed by the Prophet (صلى الله عليه وسلم). It is mentioned in Muslim Sharif:

    عن جابر أنه قال: لعن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم اكل الربوا وموكله وشاهديه وكاتبه وقال هم سواء (مسلم 27:2)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
