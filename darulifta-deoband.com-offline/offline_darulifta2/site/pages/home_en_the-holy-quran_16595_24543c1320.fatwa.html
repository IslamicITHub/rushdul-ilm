    Question: assalamualaykum, i can pray my surah bakarah properly but i struggle to pray after this which results in me not raying at all. what can i do. Because i cant pray papras: 4-29 properly i become lazy in praying the qura'an and do no tpray . please advice me. what shoudl i do. please make dua and give me duas to read and what shoudl i do?

    Answer ID: 16595Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2036/1642/H=1430)

     

    The cure of laziness is courage. Now make firm intention and promise Almighty Allah in loneliness that the day on which you will not read the fixed anount of the holy Quran you will not eat food that day and will perform twelve rakah nafl and give one day's income in charity. And act upon this strictly the day you feel laziness and make firm tauba by performing twelve rakah nafl and promise not to repeat the same. After each salah recite the following dua eleven times and blow on your chest:

    أللهم إني أعوذ بك من الجبن والكسل

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
