    Title: if som1 left intentionallly fast of ramazan for 20 days the kaffara shoud 1200 days continous fast or 60 days,does it apply sor 2 or 3 days also

    Question: if som1 left intentionallly fast of ramazan for 20 days the kaffara shoud 1200 days continous fast or 60 days,does it apply sor 2 or 3 days also

    Answer ID: 15890Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1537/1456=B=1430)

     

    You have neither to keep 1200 fasts nor 60 fasts. It is sufficient to keep 20 fasts with the intention of qadha. If one misses 2-3 fasts, the same rule will be applied i.e. he will have to make qadha of 2-3 days.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
