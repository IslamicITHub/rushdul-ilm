    Title: My question is about dawat-e-valima, it is that whether the menu should be only Non-veg or there is some flexiblity about the vegetarian dinner of valima?

    Question: Asslam-alaikum...... My question is about dawat-e-valima, it is that whether the menu should be only Non-veg or there is some flexiblity about the vegetarian dinner of valima?

    Answer ID: 20670Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 447/333/D=1431)

     

    Serving meat in wedding feast (walimah) is not necessary. walimah may be observed with whatever is available with ease. It may be vegetable as well. However it is mentioned in hadith:

    اولم ولو بشاة

    Hold a walimah, even if only with a sheep.

    Keeping it in view, if someone presents meat believing it mustahab, then it is ok.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
