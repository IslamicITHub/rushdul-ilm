    Title: Is there a time in the day when sajdah e tilawat is not permisaable to be performed? Also if we following rulings according to Imam Abu Hanifah rahmatullahi alai are we allowed to perform the sajdah e tilawah which is specifically for Shafi'i?

    Question: Assalamu alaikum, Is there a time in the day when sajdah e tilawat is not permisaable to be performed? Also if we following rulings according to Imam Abu Hanifah rahmatullahi alai are we allowed to perform the sajdah e tilawah which is specifically for Shafi'i? Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 2907Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 131/125=J)

     

    If sajdah-e-tilawat is already obligatory on somebody, he can perform them any time except these three times (since they are makrooh time): (1) At the time of sunrise (2) at midday (3) at sunset. But, if he has recited the verse of sajdah at these times then he is recommended to offer the sajdah right at the same time even if it is makrooh time. (Hashiah Imdadul Fatawa: 1/558)

     

    (2) There is no wrong in offering the sajdah whether he is a Hanafi or a Shafei, but during salah if a Hanafi recites a verse of sajdah according to Imam Shafei then he will not offer sajdah-e-tilawat there. (Imdadul Fatawa: 1/556)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
