    Title: While performing hajj, can a menstruating woman can perform the hajj rituals?

    Question: While performing hajj, can a menstruating woman can perform the hajj rituals?

    Answer ID: 26371Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1794/1424/B=1431)

    She can go everywhere including Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifa, but she cannot do tawaf. She will perform tawaf Ziyarah after she is menstruation period is over.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
