I interpreted this dream of my sister as losing my borther. But few months later I got separated from my wife and despite several attempts it now down to Khula.     Answer ID: 12134Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 948/725=H/1430)

     

    The meaning you understood is right, there is no need for any other interpretation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
