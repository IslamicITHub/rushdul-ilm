    Question: AAS SALLAMU AALAIKUM WA RAHMATULLAHI WA BARKATUH. Respected scholars, recently from college campus placement got a job in a company.It is a group of companies into many businesses like marine products exports,selling washing machines,microwavwes and also into selling wines.They plan to place me in the consumer home appliances division and not the wine business.Under such circumstances can i work in the Home appliances division of such a company which also has wine business.Will such salary if i earn INSHA ALLAH earn be regarded as 100%halal? Plz reply soon.

    Answer ID: 4791Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1115/959=B/1429)

     

    One can do a job which involves lawful work; the earning also will be lawful. The company?s dealing in wines will not affect his earning.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
