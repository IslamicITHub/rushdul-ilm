    Answer ID: 27444Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1920/D=405/k=1431)

    Your wife was divorced with three mughallazah talaqs (irrevocable divorces) when you acknowledge pronouncing three talaqs. The nikah of both of you ended completely. Talaq also takes place in anger, and generally, it is given in the state of anger. Likewise, talaq takes place in pregnancy as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
