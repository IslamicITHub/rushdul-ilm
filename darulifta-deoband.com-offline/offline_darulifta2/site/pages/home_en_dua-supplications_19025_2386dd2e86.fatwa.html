    Question: assalamalaykum i am affected by black magic (sifli ilm) so what is the islamic way of treating sifli ilm,and please give me the address and name of the person who can treat it especially in hyderabad (india) . one amil is saying he will treat me by things like bathing in water of three rivers and by using head and legs of a goat etc... is it permisible in islam? is going to a certain place like "rehmatabad " where there is dargah, and staying there and praying to allah to get cured (particularly in that place) allowed? please do reply soon.

    Answer ID: 19025Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 171/B-6/Tb=1431)

     

    We have nothing to do with black magic (sifli ilm) and its treatment; hence we are sorry to name any aamil or supply his address. Bathing in the water of three rivers and using the heads and legs of a goat all these are not Islamic way of treatment. In the same way, going to Rehmatabad dargah and making dua having the belief of acceptance is not allowed as well. You should ask Almighty Allah staying at your home by pleading Him and do istighfar and recite durood hundred times and لا حول ولا قوة الا بالله العلى العظيم five hundred times for getting cured from black magic. Also, keep on praying Allah for getting rid of black magic. Perform this act for forty days.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
