    Title: Question regarding Salam

    Question: Is it permitted in Islam to take and give salam during eating food? Would you kindly inform me?

    Answer ID: 48489Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1492/1152/B=11/1434) We should not greet one who is busy eating. It is not wajib on the person who is eating to answer the salam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
