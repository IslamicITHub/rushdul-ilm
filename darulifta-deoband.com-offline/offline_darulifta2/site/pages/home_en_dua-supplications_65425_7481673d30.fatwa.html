    Question ID: 65425Country: India

    Title: Dua or Wazifa for good family relations

    Question: We are a family of 12 people. Father, mother and 7 children. First 2 children (I'm the 2nd son) are married. All of us stay in the same house. I feel there is a lot of misunderstanding and miscommunication amongst all of our family members. This leads to strained relations. Please tell me a dua or wazifahs that I can perform for happiness, love, unity, good relations and co-operation in the family.

    Answer ID: 65425Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 799/787/H=08/1437)

    After every salah and before going to bed, recite the following dua three times with durood before and after 3-3 times and supplicate Allah.

    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً .

    Also start reading Fazail Amal and Bahishti Zewar at your home. May Allah bless you with every virtue and save you from every evil! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
