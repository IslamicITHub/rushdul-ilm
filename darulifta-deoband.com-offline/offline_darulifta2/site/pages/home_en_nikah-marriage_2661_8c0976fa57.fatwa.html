    Answer ID: 2661Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 16/15=H)

     

    If your friend confesses that he touched lustfully or committed adultery with the mother of the girl whom he intends to marry then it is haram for him to marry the girl.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
