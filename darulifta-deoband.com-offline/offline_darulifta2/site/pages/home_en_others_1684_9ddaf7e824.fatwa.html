    Question ID: 1684Country: Pakistan

    Title: Last year i saw a dream in the time of morning( that day i was a little late for my fajr prayer. I saw that my family members are mourning. I ask what has happened. My sister says that "be quite. Masood is dead(i am masood myself).i am very worried that how is it possible because i am alive .i see a person lying on the ground on white kafan and people are gathered around my dead body. I rise one corner of kafan to see who is inside. i see myself. my body is in a very bad condition. My skin peels( sheds) like the plastic paints on the wall when it get moist. i become very afraid and wake up. since then i am very upset about my future. I pray five times a say and alhamdulillah trying act on all quran and sunnah teachings. I asked many ulama. some said it is a good dream to see yourself dead. But i am very upset about my condition of dead body. PLEASE tell me the ta'beer of this dream I will pray for you and your whole team that may Allah bless you all and enable you to serve the whole humanity and like other deoband ulama, keep the light of Islam shining on these dark days of ignorance. thank you

    Answer ID: 1684Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1442/1117=H)

     

    The meaning of the dream is that ikhlas (sincerity/devotion) is not as much as required; there is a lack of it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
