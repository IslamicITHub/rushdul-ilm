    Title: Few days ago I had a dream and I saw that I am in some very big open place(like a huge ground) and A man(Long hair, with beard and wearing white clothes,with noor on his face) is approaching near me and there are 100's of people following him.

    Question: Few days ago I had a dream and I saw that I am in some very big open place(like a huge ground) and A man(Long hair, with beard and wearing white clothes,with noor on his face) is approaching near me and there are 100's of people following him. When he is near me, I ask him "Are you Hazrat Moosa(A.S)?" But he replies "No, I am Hazrat Essa(A.S)" And that is it. There was nothing else in the dream. When I woke up after the dream for changing the side while sleeping I remember it was the time after the Fajar, when there is some light in the sky. It was early morning. Besides that, I had no influence of anything whether food, any thought etc. I had no thoughts about Hazrat Issa(A.S) or anything like that before my sleep. Kindly Tell me the interpretation.

    Answer ID: 31986Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 905/905/M=1432) It is nice and blessed dream. In-sha-Allah, you shall earn honour and assistance. You are required to follow the sunnah firmly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
