    Title: I work in a private non-Islamic bank, my job is in the Credit department where we give loans to companies in order to help them in buying new machines & financing their production cycle. All loans are given after thorough studing & analysis to companies' risks & operations, the bank ofcourse receives interest on said loans which is pre-agreed upon in the contracts. My job also involves approaching new companies as to market our loan packages. Is my salary/job permissable or not?

    Question: I work in a private non-Islamic bank, my job is in the Credit department where we give loans to companies in order to help them in buying new machines & financing their production cycle. All loans are given after thorough studing & analysis to companies' risks & operations, the bank ofcourse receives interest on said loans which is pre-agreed upon in the contracts. My job also involves approaching new companies as to market our loan packages. Is my salary/job permissable or not?

    Answer ID: 2690Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 46/47=L)

     

    It is narrated in Hadith:

    عن جابر رضی اللہ عنہ قال: لعن رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم آکل الربوا و موکلہ وکاتبہ و شاھدیہ وقال ھو سواء (مشکوۃ 1/244)

    The Prophet (peace be upon him) accursed one who devours usury, one who feeds it, one who writes it and one who bears witness to it. He added: They all are equal. (Mishkat: 1/244)

     

    Therefore, doing a work in bank which involves giving interest-based loan to people is unlawful. But, you are not asked the leave the job immediately, it is advised to look for a lawful job and when find one leave the bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
