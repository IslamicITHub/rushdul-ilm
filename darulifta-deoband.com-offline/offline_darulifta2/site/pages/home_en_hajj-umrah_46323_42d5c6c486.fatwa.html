    Question ID: 46323Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Hajj

    Question: I heard that if someone died while applying and ready to do Hajj, Allah assigns one angel on his place who does Hajj every year on the deceased name. Is that right? What will be the reward for him in front of Allah? Can we call him Shaheed?

    Answer ID: 46323Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1078/1078/M=1434) A person shall get reward for the intention of hajj and its preparation, in-shaAllah. But we could not find the virtues which you have mentioned in the book of Hadith and Fiqh. Rather there is a hadith which says that “a person who goes for hajj and dies in the way, he shall receive the reward of hajj until the Day of Judgment”. عن أبي هريرة قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: من خرج حاجاً فمات كتب له أجر الحاج إلى يوم القيمة ، رواه أبو يعلى من رواية ابن اسحاق وبقية رواته ثقات كذا في الترغيب (فضائل حج ص 25)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
