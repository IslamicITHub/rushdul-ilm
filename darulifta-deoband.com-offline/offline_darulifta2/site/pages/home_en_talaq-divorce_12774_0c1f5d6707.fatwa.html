    Title: ASAKhazraat i would like to know that a certain momim brethen has given talaaq by saying talaaq 3 consecutive times in front of other people off course the talaaq was validated according to sharia , but now he says that he didnt say talaaq he pronounced it talaat so what is the status pls inform me at the earliest as these couple has 3 small kids ALLAH HAFIZ

    Question: ASAKhazraat i would like to know that a certain momim brethen has given talaaq by saying talaaq 3 consecutive times in front of other people off course the talaaq was validated according to sharia , but now he says that he didnt say talaaq he pronounced it talaat so what is the status pls inform me at the earliest as these couple has 3 small kids ALLAH HAFIZ

    Answer ID: 12774Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 814/814=M/1430)

     

    The Talaq takes place even it was pronounced wrongly. Therefore, if he said to his wife ?talat?, then also the divorce took place. If he confesses to give three divorces or a valid witness is there, his wife will be divorced with three irrevocable talaqs. They will no longer remain as husband and wife and he cannot take her as wife back without a valid halalah:

    ویقع بھا أي بھذہ الألفاظ وما بمعناھا من الصریح ۔۔۔ وفي الشامیۃ: قال في البحر ومنه الألفاظ المصحفة وهي خمسة فزاد على ما هنا تلاق وزاد في النهر إبدال القاف لاما (شامي 4:459)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
