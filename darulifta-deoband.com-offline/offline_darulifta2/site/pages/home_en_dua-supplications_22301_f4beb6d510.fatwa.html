    Title: I failed in my H.S.C exam in physics. I have given exam again. But my exam did not become well. Plz plz tell me a dua or zikr for passing in the Exam.

    Question: I failed in my H.S.C exam in physics. I have given exam again. But my exam did not become well. Plz plz tell me a dua or zikr for passing in the Exam.

    Answer ID: 22301Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 788/788/M=1431)

     

    Hard work is the key to success. If you do full preparation for the examination and write correct answers, then in-sha-Allah you will surely secure excellent. After each salah, recite the following surah seven times with durood 11-11 times before and after:

    إذا جاء نصر الله والفتح, ورأيت الناس يدخلون في دين الله أفواجا، فسبح بحمد ربك واستغفره إنه كان توابا

    [النصر : 1]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
