    Question ID: 35626Country: Pakistan

    Title: My question is that I am having very serious problem know about masturbation, I want to know that is masturbation totally haram in Islam and in all Fiqh

    Question: My question is that I am having very serious problem know about masturbation, I want to know that is masturbation totally haram in Islam and in all Fiqh? And can you kindly prove me with hadith. Please help me with this; I am having a huge conflict with this question!

    Answer ID: 35626Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2010/1625/B=1432) It is mentioned in hadith: ناكح اليد معلون. One who marries with hands (masturbator) is accursed. It is also mentioned in another hadith that the finger of a masturbator shall be pregnant on the day of judgment i.e. he will be humiliated in front of all the people. These hadith speak openly that it is haram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
