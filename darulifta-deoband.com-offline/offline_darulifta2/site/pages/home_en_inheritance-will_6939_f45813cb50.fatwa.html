    Title: Q-In the presence of a son, is it permissible for the daughter (who has money) to pay the debts of deseased Father?please provide reference.

    Question: Q-In the presence of a son, is it permissible for the daughter (who has money) to pay the debts of deseased Father?please provide reference.

    Answer ID: 6939Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 955/878=L/1429)

     

    If a late person is debtor to anyone, the debt will be paid off from his left property. But, instead of paying debt from the property of the late person, if any heir pays the debt from his own will, it will be alright.

    وعنه قال: أتى رجل الى النبى صلى الله عليه وسلم فقال: ان أختى نذرت أن تحج و انها ماتت فقال النبى صلى الله عليه وسلم: لو كان عليها دين اكنت قاضيه قال: نعم قال: فاقض دين الله فهو أحق بالقضاء (مشكاة)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
