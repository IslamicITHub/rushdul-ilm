    Question: As Salaam alakikum, Please answer my questions and clear my doubts regarding the below given questions 1. The flats in which we live have combine toilet & bathrooms. Also they have no sitting place to perform wudu. So can we i. perform wudu in such toilets ? ii. cane we perform wudu in basins while standing. 2. Recently i have relasied that all the money which we (me & wife & Ammi's pension) earns are spent without any savings. as such there is no barkat. please give solutions Jakaz Allah

    Answer ID: 1192Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 511/499=N)

     

    (1) It is better to avoid performing wuzu there; you should do it at a clean place with water in a vessel.

     

    (2) If there is no vessel from which you can make wuzu according to Sunnah, then you can make wuzu there in emergency conditions.

     

    (3) First, you should think whether the earning and pension is Halal or not. If it is Halal then you should refrain from squandering, give charity and keep on reciting Isteghfar. Also, pray Allah for barakah. We hope that Allah will grant barakah in your livelihood.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
