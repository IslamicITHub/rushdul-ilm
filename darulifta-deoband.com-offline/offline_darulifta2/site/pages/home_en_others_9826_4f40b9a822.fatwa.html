    Question: assalam u alaikum I would like to know the ta'bir of the dream i often use to see,that i lose my lower frontal teeth and i get very worried about it in the dream.alhumdulilla i offer five times salah with tahjud oftenly i get the chance..moreover i mostly sleep bawudu..hope your reply soon.jazakallh

    Answer ID: 9826Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2408/2132=D/1429)

     

    The dreams are not effective in themselves. Sometimes, dreams are inspired from satan. Recite the below dua thrice whenever you see such dreams:

    أعوذ باللہ من الشیطان وشر ھذہ الرؤیا

    And then spit thrice to the left side and change the side you slept on.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
