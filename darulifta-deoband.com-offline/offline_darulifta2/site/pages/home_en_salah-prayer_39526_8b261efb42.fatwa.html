    Question ID: 39526Country: Bangladesh

    Title: I work in a government office. In 1st floor we have a mosque for our office employers

    Question: I work in a government office. In 1st floor we have a mosque for our office employers. This Imam is bidati person, like he says Ya Nabi Salamualika, he does Qiyam, he do not like tabligh Jamat etc. For that reason I do not go in our office mosque and I arrange a Jamah with my colleagues and others staff in our floor. Most of the staffs are not going to the mosque but when I arrange for jamah then they join us. My question is that whether it is right to arrange these Jamah or if I am wrong then what I need to do? Please suggest me.

    Answer ID: 39526Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1017/622/L=1433) If it is not a Shariah mosque i.e. the place is not fixed from ground to sky for the interest of the mosque then you along with your colleagues and other staff members can arrange for separate Jama’ah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
