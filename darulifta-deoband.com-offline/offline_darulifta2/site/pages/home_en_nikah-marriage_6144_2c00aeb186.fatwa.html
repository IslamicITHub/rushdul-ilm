    Title: If the person who held nikah sermony , knows that , guy is homosexsual and fornikater , while bride is pure virgin muslim girl , then the nikah will be legal or not.

    Question: If the person who held nikah sermony , knows that , guy is homosexsual and fornikater , while bride is pure virgin muslim girl , then the nikah will be legal or not.

    Answer ID: 6144Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1169/142=B/1429)

     

    If the boy is Muslim then the Nikah shall be valid. But one should not give any virgin and virtuous girl in the Nikah of a fornicator and homosexual knowingly. It is injustice with the girl.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
