    Title: asalamualaykum, Is my wifes sister my mahram? Is my wifes mother my mahram? Please give detailed explanation. JazakAllah Khair

    Question: asalamualaykum, Is my wifes sister my mahram? Is my wifes mother my mahram? Please give detailed explanation. JazakAllah Khair

    Answer ID: 14835Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1131/923=L/1430)

     

    Sister of wife (sister in law) is non-mahram, one should observe hijab.

    Mother of wife (mother in law) is mahram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
