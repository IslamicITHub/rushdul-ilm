    Question ID: 33101Country: India

    Title: I have given two talaqs to my wife in writing almost 13 months back from here.

    Question: I have given two talaqs to my wife in writing almost 13 months back from here. First talaq was given and after a few months the second talaq was given in presence of two witnesses and a qazi. I have not given third talaq to her. Now with our mutual understanding, I want to remarry her. Is this permissible? If yes, what are the conditions for remarrying her?

    Answer ID: 33101Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1135/706/L=1432) In the question mentioned above if you gave your wife one talaq and again before passing the iddah period i.e. before completing three menstruating period, you gave second talaq then two talaqs occurred on your wife. If both of you want to marry again you can do so but in future you shall have the right of only one talaq. If ever you will give your wife one talaq all the three talaqs shall take place on your wife and she shall become mughallazah bainah (irrevocably divorced) and haram for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
