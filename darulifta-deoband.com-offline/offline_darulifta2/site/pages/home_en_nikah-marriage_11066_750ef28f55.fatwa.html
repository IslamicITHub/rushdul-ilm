    Question: As salamu aleikum mufti sahab. Is it permitted group sex (one husband but two or more wife)simultaneously in a single room.plz reply me as soon as possible. thanks

    Answer ID: 11066Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 251/59=TL/1430)

     

    Anyone having more than one wife is not allowed to have sex with one wife in the presence of other wife:

    کما أنہ لا یحل لہ وطء زوجتہ بحضرتھا ولا بحضرۃ الضرۃ (شامي 5:320)

    Therefore, one must not engage in sexual act with a wife in the presence of other wife.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
