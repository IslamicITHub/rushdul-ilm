    Answer ID: 6118Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1548/1180=H/1429)

     

    Keep on convincing your wife and sister-in-law?s family with wisdom, politeness and insight. If they give you a gift you should accept it and give it in charity to the poor and needy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
