    Title: Respected Ulema Assalamu Alikum (W.W) please for your answer to the question below a namizi sits in a chair in the saf for his salaat. where of should the back legs of his chair be positioned. should it be in line with the heels of the other namazis was Salaam!

    Question: Respected Ulema Assalamu Alikum (W.W) please for your answer to the question below a namizi sits in a chair in the saf for his salaat. where of should the back legs of his chair be positioned. should it be in line with the heels of the other namazis was Salaam!

    Answer ID: 10071Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 81/75=B/1429)

     

    The back legs of his chair should be in line with the heels of the people offering salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
