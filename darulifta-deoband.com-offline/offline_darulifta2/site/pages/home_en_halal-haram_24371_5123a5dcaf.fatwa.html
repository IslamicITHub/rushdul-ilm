    Title: As a Muslim woman am I able to uncover my hair in front of known homosexuals?

    Question: As a Muslim woman am I able to uncover my hair in front of known homosexuals? Your sister in Islam Mariam

    Answer ID: 24371Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1193/1193/M=1431)

    It is not lawful for a woman to uncover her hair in front of non-mahram male without a valid reason, though he is an acquaintance or a stranger. It is necessary to cover the hair as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
