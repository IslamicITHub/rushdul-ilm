    Title: I want to ask if qasr is applicable to me, then also if I offer 4 rakahs fardh for Zuhr and Asr in respective times instead of 2 rakah qasr then salah is valid or one must offer qasr only

    Question: I want to ask if qasr is applicable to me, then also if I offer 4 rakahs fardh for Zuhr and Asr in respective times instead of 2 rakah qasr then salah is valid or one must offer qasr only?

    Answer ID: 38169Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 794/666/B=1433) According to Ahnaf it is wajib for musafir (traveller) to do qasr. If he offered full salah knowingly he shall be sinful and it shall be wajib to re-offer that salah. And if he offered four rakah unknowingly then salah shall be valid after performing sajdah sahv.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
