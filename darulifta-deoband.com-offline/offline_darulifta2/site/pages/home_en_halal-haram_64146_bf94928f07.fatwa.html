    Question ID: 64146Country: india

    Title: Regarding doing business of Digital printing of Hording,banner

    Question: I am providing services of advertisement agency which includes (printing of photographs of men, animals, other religious statues (devi, devtas) by way of digital boards, hoardings, banners and printing of flex papers. I approached some Ulama but I am not satisfied by their views. Please I seek your advice about this business. Is it halal or haram? Please understand the question and give detail explanation in view of Shariah.

    Answer ID: 64146Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 651/651/SD=08/1437)

    It is not lawful as per the Shariah to print the pictures of human beings, animals, devi devtas on digital board and printing flex paper. Hadiths sternly warns on making the pictures of living beings. Hence it is not lawful to provide service of advertisement agency that contains living beings.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
