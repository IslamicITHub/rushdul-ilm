    Question: As Salamu Alaikum Wa Rahmatullahi Wa Barkatuh.Respected Muftis what does shariat say about a person who is into Sales and Marketing job of selling Micro Wave Ovens, as per some doctors what i understand is this that micro wave cooking leads to Cancer in the long run.Now is it ok to sell micro wave ovens.(2) What does shariat again says for people who work for selling cigarrettes and other health hazard products. Jazakallahu Khaira.

    Answer ID: 11909Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 706/706=M/1430)

     

    (1) If expert doctors are of the opinion that microwave ovens leads to cancer, one should avoid using it for a long time. As far as, dealing in this item is concerned, it cannot be termed as unlawful.

    (2) It is better to avoid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
