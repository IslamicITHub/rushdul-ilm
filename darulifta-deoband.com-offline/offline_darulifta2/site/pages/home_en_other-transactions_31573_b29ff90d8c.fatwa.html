    Title: interest of SBI account

    Question: I am working as a lecturer in government polytechnic krishnarajpet, mandya distric karnataka.our salary is paid through SB account.As we have to pay annual income tax to the goverment. can we used the interest amount of the bankto pay the income tax at financial year end.

    Answer ID: 31573Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 758/266/TL=1432) The interest amount can be paid in income tax.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
