    Title: After fard salaat with jamaat is done the imam in our masjid asks dua in arabic in conregation is it right or wrong.

    Question: Asalamualaikum My question is. After fard salaat with jamaat is done the imam in our masjid asks dua in arabic in conregation is it right or wrong.

    Answer ID: 19956Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 322/322/M=1431)

     

    Supplicating after fardh salah is proved from Hadith; therefore the imam is not wrong to do so. There is nothing wrong if it takes the form of collectivity, but after salam, the salah comes to an end, one can go if he has any need without participating.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
