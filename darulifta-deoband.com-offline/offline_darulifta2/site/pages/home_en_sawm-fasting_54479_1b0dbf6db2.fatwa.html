    Title: time of ramadan romantic telephonic talk

    Question: Shall I talk with my wife through mobile with sexual feelings?

    Answer ID: 54479Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1210/1210/M=09/1435)

     

    If it is feared that talking one’s wife during fast with sexual feelings will cause the semen to discharge, then it should be avoided as it is not free from being makrooh. And if there is no fear of semen discharge then there is no harm.

    وكره قبلة و مس ومعانقة ومباشرة فاحشة إن لم يأمن المفسد، وإن أمن فلا بأس (در مختار).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
