    Title: Please give me a wazifah/tasbih/dhikr for barakah in rizq and wealth. How many times it has to be recited and also the best time to recite it.

    Question: Please give me a wazifah/tasbih/dhikr for barakah in rizq and wealth. How many times it has to be recited and also the best time to recite it.

    Answer ID: 146922Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 315/286/L=3/1438)

    Recite Ya Mughni (يا مغني) 1111 times after Isha with durood 11-11 times before and after for prosperity and blessing in rizq (livelihood). And after Maghrib salah recite surah Waqiya and ask your family members as well to recite the same. In-shaAllah, it shall bring prosperity and blessings in rizq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
