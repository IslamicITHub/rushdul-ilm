    Title: I miss my Fajr prayer

    Question: I wanted to know that I sleep at late at night because I don’t get sleep early so I am not able to wake up for Fajr. So missing Fajr prayer in such condition will it be considered as mistake? And I also wanted to know what should I do to seek forgiveness as I have been missing my Fajr salah continuously from one week?

    Answer ID: 156961Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 383/309/B=3/1439)

     

    This practice of yours is not good. You should go to bed early immediately after Isha salah and sleep with the intention of offering Fajr salah with jamah, you shall get up in time in-shaAllah. You should also keep on doing true taubah and istighfar.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
