    Title: My sisters daughter name is SABAAT,maening Sachai(truthness),Majbooti . Is this write name? if no, suggest any good one.

    Question: AsSalamalaikum My sisters daughter name is SABAAT,maening Sachai(truthness),Majbooti . Is this write name? if no, suggest any good one. JazakAllah

    Answer ID: 21113Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 459/459/M=1431)

     

    Sabaat (ثبات) name does not sound much good for a girl, though it carries good meaning. However, it is better to choose a name from among the names of the wives of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and Sahabiyat i.e. Ayesha, Hafsah, Safiyyah, Maimunah etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
