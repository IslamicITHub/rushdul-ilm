    Question ID: 165221Country: Kerala

    Title: Regarding validity of salah due to mistake in recitation of surah

    Question: During salah in the fourth ayath of Surah Kafirun I used to read 'wala anaa' instead of 'walaa ana'. Recently only I came to know that the alif in 'ana' is not pronounced. Would this mistake change the meaning and is my salah valid. Do I need to repeat my salah?

    Answer ID: 165221Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 09/33/B=03/1440)

     

    It is written as ‘anaa’ but it is read as ‘ana’. There is no difference in meaning. All your salahs which you offered with ‘anaa’ are valid without any doubt; there is no need to repeat them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
