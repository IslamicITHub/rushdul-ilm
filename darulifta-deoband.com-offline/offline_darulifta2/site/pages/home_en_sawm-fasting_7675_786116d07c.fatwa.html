    Title: A woman who is Fasting the Doctor puts cream in her Vagina does Fast break?

    Question: Asalamualaikum 1-A woman who is Fasting the Doctor puts cream in her Vagina does Fast break?

    Answer ID: 7675Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 973/973=M/1429)

     

    In the case mentioned above, the fast of the woman will break.

    الاقطار فی أقبال النساء یفسد بلا خلاف و ھو الصحیح

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
