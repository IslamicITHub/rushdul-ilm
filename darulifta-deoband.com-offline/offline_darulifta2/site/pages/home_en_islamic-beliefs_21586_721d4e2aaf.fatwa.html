    Title: I have been a sunni muslim throughout my life, but now I have started doubting Sahih Muslim and Sahih Bukhari Hadeeths. Though I have strong faith in Quran. ->Is it important to believe in Sahih Hadiths to be a true Muslim. Your answer is very important for me

    Question: Assalamo Alaikum, Sir, I have been a sunni muslim throughout my life, but now I have started doubting Sahih Muslim and Sahih Bukhari Hadeeths. Though I have strong faith in Quran. ->Is it important to believe in Sahih Hadiths to be a true Muslim. Your answer is very important for me ! Allah Hafiz

    Answer ID: 21586Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 667/440/L=1431)

     

    Yes, for a true Muslim it is necessary to have faith on the sahih ahadith. To consider the collection of hadith totally unreliable or have doubt about them is a belief of kufr. You should do tauba and istighfar wholeheartedly and truly as well as recite la haul (لا حول ولا قوۃ الا باللہ) and durood extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
