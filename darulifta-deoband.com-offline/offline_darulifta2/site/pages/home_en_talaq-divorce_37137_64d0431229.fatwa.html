    Title: Is saying three times talaq talaq talaq to the wife made talaq

    Question: c? I want to understand the 228 surah of the holy Quran of chapter 2 Al-Baqra.

    Answer ID: 37137Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 485/272/H=1433) (1) Talaq mughallazah (irrevocable) takes place by saying talaq, talaq, talaq to one's wife with whom one has had intercourse and thus she becomes haram on her husband. It is proved by Bukhari Sharif vol. 2, page 791, Fatawa Hindiyah vol 1, page 501 etc. (2) What types of information do you want from this ayah? Write it clearly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
