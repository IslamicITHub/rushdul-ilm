    Question: Assalamu alaikum Iam depressed because i have been shifted to another branch of my work place (because an employee has left suddenly)which is far to travel.besides i want to stay in my work place.Please suggest a dua or a quranic aayah that i can recite so that they will find a suitable replacement soon.(I follow the Shafi madhab) JazakAllahu Khair

    Answer ID: 11216Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 413/K=40/K/1430)

     

    (1) Recite ya Latif (یا لطیف) 111 times after Isha Salah with durood 11 times before and after, then pray to Allah.

    (2) Memorize the below dua and recite it after obligatory salah and other times:

    اللھم الطف بي تیسیر کل عسیر فإن تیسیر کل عسیر علیک یسیر وأسئلک الیسر والمعافاۃ في الدنیا والآخرۃ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
