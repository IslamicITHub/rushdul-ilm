    Title: my wife's father died some 5-6 years back. He was very fond of food. He used to love to invite people to his house for dawat. Now my question is that my wife very often sees her father in her dreams. She sees that her father has called lots of people for food at their home & ordered her to cook food for them which she does but she is very scared that the food that she has prepared would be liked by them & her father or not. Please tell me the tabeer of this dream. She also sees lots of her dead relatives in her dream. What does this mean.

    Question: As salaam alaikum Mufti sb., my wife's father died some 5-6 years back. He was very fond of food. He used to love to invite people to his house for dawat. Now my question is that my wife very often sees her father in her dreams. She sees that her father has called lots of people for food at their home & ordered her to cook food for them which she does but she is very scared that the food that she has prepared would be liked by them & her father or not. Please tell me the tabeer of this dream. She also sees lots of her dead relatives in her dream. What does this mean. jazaak allah.

    Answer ID: 21413Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 822/628/H=1431)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah these are nice dreams. The concerns of wife are the proof of her love for her late father. Your wife takes good care for kindness towards relatives. This is the meaning. Yes, you need to pay more attention to do isal-e sawab.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
