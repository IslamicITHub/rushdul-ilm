    Answer ID: 1208Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1208/875=H)

     

    If you find a suitable alternative who can manage mosque-related affairs and you are satisfied with him, then you can resign. Rather, you are suggested not to solve the matter by a fatwa but deliberate whether the accusations labeled on you are right, whether you are committing laziness in salah or not, you will be reckoned before Allah on the Day of Judgment, there we can find no lame excuse; therefore you should pay special attention towards self reform. Shortly, keep your affairs with Allah clear and do not resign only at the complaints of the people.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
