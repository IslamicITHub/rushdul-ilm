    Title: Reciting the iqamah twice in slaah

    Question: Dear Mufti Shab, Assalamualaikkum. While saying iqkkamath during salahs, accordind to hanafi madhab we will say all lines twice.but some people say that the lines of iqkkamath should be said once is correct,only that method is available in hadis but whereas our procedure is not found in hadhis.please answer this with related hadhis. zisakallah.

    Answer ID: 18540Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 103/98=L=1431)

     

    Pronouncing the iqamah twice is proved from hadith:

    عن عبد اللہ بن زید قال: کان أذان رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم شفعاً شفعاً في الأذان والإقامۃ (سنن الترمذي ۱:۲۷، باب ما جاء في أن الإقامۃ مثنی مثنی)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
