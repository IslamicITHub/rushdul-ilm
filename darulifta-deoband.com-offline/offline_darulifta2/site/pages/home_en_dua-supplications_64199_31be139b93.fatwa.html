    Question ID: 64199Country: India

    Title: Urine infection problem

    Question: I am really facing some serious health issues like urine infection, stomach acidity (from past 1 year), headache, joint pains and pain in kidney (as I had a operation many years ago) all together. Every month I have to consult an individual doctor and still I am not getting better. This effect my mind and I feel frustrated. I am really worried about this as my age is 26 only and I have to depend on so much medicines. Due to urine infection I am not able to pray namaz as my clothes gets dirty from urine drops. I pay sadaqah also. I am still unemployed and I have many exams coming but such health issues break my concentration. Is there any dua or amal or anything that can be done in order to gain healthy life?

    Answer ID: 64199Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 564/564/M=06/1437)

    May Allah grant you perfect health! Consult any good and expert hakim or doctor for treatment. Be regular in your salah. Salah is not exempted in any case. Tell about the urine infection to some local mufti or alim and know the ruling to perform wudhu and offer salah in this condition. Also, do the following amal:

    Recite surah Fatiha 41 times before Fajr salah and blow it on water and drink it. Do it for forty days. Also supplicate for your health after each salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
