    Title: dua for nazar for baby not taking milk

    Question: Please, provide me a dua for nazar on bottle milk for my 3 months baby? She is not drinking milk. Please, give me a dua from the Quran.

    Answer ID: 49838Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 169/125/L=02/1435) You should keep on reciting Surah al-Falaq and Surah al-Naas thrice every morning and evening and blow on your daughter. She will start accepting the milk, in-shaAllah. You may recite the following dua thrice and blow: وَإِنْ يَكَادُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَيُزْلِقُونَكَ بِأَبْصَارِهِمْ لَمَّا سَمِعُوا الذِّكْرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُ لَمَجْنُونٌ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
