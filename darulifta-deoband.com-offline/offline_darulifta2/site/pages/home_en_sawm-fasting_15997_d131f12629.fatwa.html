    Title: assalamualaikkum mufti sb. i just wat to know whether is it permissible to give intravenous injections,insulin,ivfluids,minorsurgeries to the fasting paients?

    Question: assalamualaikkum mufti sb. i just wat to know whether is it permissible to give intravenous injections,insulin,ivfluids,minorsurgeries to the fasting paients?

    Answer ID: 15997Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1479/1481=M=1430)

     

    It is allowable to give intravenous injection or glucose to a patient who is fasting, it will not invalidate his fast. By minor surgeries, if you mean surgery of acnes etc in hands or legs it will not harm the fast.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
