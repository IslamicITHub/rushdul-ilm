    Title: I want to make all qaza namaz. I want to know can I pray all my qaza namaz instead of sunnet in all prayers.

    Question: I want to make all qaza namaz. I want to know can I pray all my qaza namaz instead of sunnet in all prayers.

    Answer ID: 2091Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1002/1002=J)

     

    It is afzal (better) to perform qaza salah (missed prayers) instead of praying nafl (extra prayers), but we should not leave sunnan-e-muakkadah (recommended prayers before and after obligatory salah).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
