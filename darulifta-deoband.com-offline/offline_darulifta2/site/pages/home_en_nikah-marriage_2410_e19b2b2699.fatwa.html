We want to marry each other. but her parents arranged her marraige somewhere else saying that love before marraige is immoral in islam. Is it correct that love for somebody before marraige is haram in islam. can we get married.

    Answer ID: 2410Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 636/621=L)

     

    In Islam, it is prohibited to have love affairs with a non-mahram girl. Yes, if the boy and girl love each other in their heart with the intention of marriage and do not commit any thing unlawful then there is no sin. However, if she is already married to anyone else then it is haram for you to marry her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
