    Question ID: 36894Country: India

    Title: Is it permissible in Shariah that a man who has a land and gives his own land or shop to bank for ATM machine on rent

    Question: Is it permissible in Shariah that a man who has a land and gives his own land or shop to bank for ATM machine on rent. Is it permissible in Islam? Tell me shortly, please.

    Answer ID: 36894Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 440/351/D=1433) It is lawful but it is better not to give it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
