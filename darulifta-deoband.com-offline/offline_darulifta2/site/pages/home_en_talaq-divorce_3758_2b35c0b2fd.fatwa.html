    Answer ID: 3758Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 473/583=B)

     

    Yes, it will cause a rajai tataq (revocable). If you like to keep her as your spouse you can revoke the talaq within iddah period, there is no need of halalah or renewal of nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
