    Question: My mother is suffering from ligament tear in her left leg and doctor has now said that the cartilage has degenerated. The problm is from last 3-4 months and it happened at all at once. There seems no improvent by the medicines. Pls give some wazifa or any thing else.

    Answer ID: 22305Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 809/638/D=1431)

    Below are three acts, practice the one which is easy for you. 
    (1) Ask your mother to recite surah fatiha 41 times early morning with durood 3-3 times before and after.
    (2) Recite surah fatiha 11 times with durood 3-3 times before and after, blow it on water and drink it. 
    (3) Recite Ya Salam (يا سلام) 134 times every day with durood 3-3 times before and after. In-sha-Allah you shall get cured.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
