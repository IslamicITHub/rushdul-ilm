    Title: Dua for Halal Job

    Question: Mein Qatar pe Rahata hun,eak company pe job karahah hun.Lekin a job mera Education se alag hai,Mein chata hun mujhe Education ke Mutabik Job mile.Bohat kosis kar rahahun.lekin avi tak kuch nahi hua,aap mujhe koi DUA batayen,JOB MILNA ASAN HOJAYE.

    Answer ID: 35013Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1673/1673/M=1432) Read يا وهاب and يا مغني in the morning and evening 100 times with durood 3-3 times before and after. Also offer five times salah regularly and recite Surah Quraish and Ayatul Kursi 3-3 times after each salah. You should also supplicate Almighty Allah for halal livelihood.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
