    Question: dear mufti sahab i want to ask" YE KA HAMARE MASJID ME AIK SATHI HAAN JINHO NE MASJID KA LIYE QALIN DIYE HAN MASLA YE HA KE WOH BANK ME AS A PEON KAM KARTE HAN(STATE BANK OF PAKISTAN).KIA IN QALIN PAR NAMAZ PHRNA ZIKAR TALAWAT KARNA JAIZ HA.AUR KIA YE MASJID KA ADAB KA KHILAF NAHE.IS KA BARE ME AP KIA KHTE HAN?

    Answer ID: 9700Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1688/1391=L/1429)

     

    In the abovementioned case, it is lawful to pray, do zikr and recitation on the carpet. It is lawful to do such a work in bank which does not require help in interest like being peon; the salary will be lawful and halal. Therefore, one should unnecessarily not fall in doubt about prayers etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
