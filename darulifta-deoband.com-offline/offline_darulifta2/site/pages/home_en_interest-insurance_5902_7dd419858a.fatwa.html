    Title: I want to take a house through ICICI or HDFC bank in Mumbai. Is it permissible. Thanks!

    Question: I want to take a house through ICICI or HDFC bank in Mumbai. Is it permissible. Thanks!

    Answer ID: 5902Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 943/884=D/1429)

     

    If both the banks mentioned above build house or purchase the house and add some amount to the spent money (though in the name of interest) and then you purchase the house on the total amount which you will pay yourself in lump sum or instalments as per the dealing, then both the ways are permissible. In this way, buying a house from the banks is right. You should write clearly if there is any other way of purchasing a house.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
