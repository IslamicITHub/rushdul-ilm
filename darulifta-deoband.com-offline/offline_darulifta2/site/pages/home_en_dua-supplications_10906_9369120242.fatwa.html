    Question: salaam mufti sahib, can u give dua for my baby, so they follow islam ,livehealthy and safty, allah give them parda and dua/wazifa my baby grows up lisen to me and give me love and respect. 2) dua/ wazifa for me i could get gud nowlaage and get samart,and get healthy and and my laziness goes, and dua/wazifa for i could handel my house and i get neet and tide the house.

    Answer ID: 10906Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 346/237=L/1430)

     

    Recite the below dua, it is tested dua for children being pious and obedient:

    ربنا آتنا في الدنيا حسنة وفي الآخرة حسنة وقنا عذاب النار

    ربنا هب لنا من أزواجنا وذرياتنا قرة أعين واجعلنا للمتقين إماما

    (2) For every thing mentioned in the question, recite the below mentioned dua 21 times after every obligatory salah with durood thrice before and after:

    اللھم إنی أسئلک الھدی والتقی والعفاف والغنی

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
