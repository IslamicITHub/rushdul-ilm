    Title: Reciting Sura Fatiha in dream

    Question: (1) In my dream, I saw that I am reciting surah Fatiha and when I wake up from sleep my mother told me that you were reciting surah Fatiha in your dream. (2) In another night's dream, I saw a very beautiful girl, may be poor and then I was thinking myself that If I can marry like such beautiful girl. So what will be the interpretations?

    Answer ID: 45712Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 865/655/D=1434) (1) The dream is blessed one. In-shaAllah, you shall attain knowledge and wisdom. Recite the holy Quran regularly and follow the commandments and avoid prohibited acts. (2) This dream is also blessed one. In-shaAllah, you shall pass your days in good way.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
