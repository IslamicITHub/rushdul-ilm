    Answer ID: 7123Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1399/1313=D/1429)

     

    (1)     It means that you shall be protected from mischief and trials in your marital life.

    (2)     Get a meditation that Allah sees you and strive hard to shun from sins.

    (3)     Recite the tasbih of لاحول و لاقوۃ الا باللہ  in the morning and in the evening.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
