    Question ID: 37650Country: Pakistan

    Title: My name is Adnan and I am living in London nowadays with a person Named Arshad Hassan. He is such a pious man I have ever seen in my life

    Question: My name is Adnan and I am living in London nowadays with a person Named Arshad Hassan. He is such a pious man I have ever seen in my life. He is blind having 3 children two of them are Hafiz-e-Quran. The elder one is 18. His wife passed away couple of years back. He had invested money with a man from Bangalore, India. That man showed himself a very religious person and in every matter was narrating Quran and Hadith. So Mr Arshad trusted on him and invested 25,000 pounds in his business which was the earning of his whole life. He invested so that he can get something against this investment. All of a sudden this person disappeared and took the whole amount and came to India. Now he is not giving his money back to Mr Arshad. He is in great trouble. On behalf of Mr Arshad Sahib I am asking about wazifah so that We do it and Allah put rahem in that person's heart and so that he become willing in giving his money back.

    Answer ID: 37650Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 519/B=73/TB=1433) A person does not return the due money who is greedy and dishonest and does not fear Allah. Arshad Hassan committed a blunder that he gave all the earning of his life to a strange, cunning and deceitful person for investment and he ran away taking all your investment. انا لله وانا اليه راجعون Have patience, Almighty Allah shall give you its good return. Recite durood 100 times and لا حول ولا قوة الا بالله العلي العظيم 500 times and then supplicate Allah. And after Isha salah recite Ya Aziz (يا عزيز) 200 times and durood 3-3 times before and after in the state of wudhu turning your face towards the home of that person. Do this practice for forty days. And keep on reciting the following dua 100-100 times in the morning and evening daily: اللهم اكفني بحلالك عن حرامك واغنني بفضلك عمن سواك .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
