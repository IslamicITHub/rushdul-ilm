    Title: When maujin give the ajaan and he say that ashdu anna mohammadar rasullha,

    Question: When maujin give the ajaan and he say that ashdu anna mohammadar rasullha,when we listen these word we say that mohammad-sallahoo-alhi-wa-salam or we send darud ,some pepoles say that it is wrong and a i am confused about this its wrong or right. Another Qusetion is I purchase stolen goods for our use its haram or halal

    Answer ID: 31099Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 479/367/L=1432) (1) One should repeat the same words of Azan while answering it. While answering أشهد ان محمدا رسول الله only the same words أشهد ان محمدا رسول الله should be repeated. However, after the Azan is complete one should recite the durood and then the dua of Azan. (2) It is not right to buy stolen goods for oneself when one is aware of it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
