    Title: Alhamdulillah we are expecting our first child. My wife has just got pregnant. please let me know any wazifa or dua for her/us for getting the things easier and make our child dean-daar, intelligent and healthy.

    Question: Asak Mufti sb, Alhamdulillah we are expecting our first child. My wife has just got pregnant. please let me know any wazifa or dua for her/us for getting the things easier and make our child dean-daar, intelligent and healthy. JazakAllah

    Answer ID: 29364Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 236/221/B=1432)

    If you wish for a boy, keep on reciting the following dua extensively:
    اللهم ارزقني ولدًا صالحًا .
    (2) When the time of birth start, write the following ayah on a piece of paper and wrap it in clean cloth and tie it on the thigh of the woman and untie it immediately followed birth:
    إِذَا السَّمَاء انشَقَّتْ ، وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ ، وَإِذَا الْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ ، وَأَلْقَتْ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتْ .
    Note: You should carefully mind untying the tawiz just after birth.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
