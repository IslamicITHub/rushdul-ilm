    Title: What if the husband says only talak talak talak and there were no witnesses there and he said it when he was mad and also he didnt say the wifes name to say the talaq?

    Question: What if the husband says only talak talak talak and there were no witnesses there and he said it when he was mad and also he didnt say the wifes name to say the talaq?

    Answer ID: 22049Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 720/720/M=1431)

     

    For the occurrence of talaq, neither the presence of witnesses nor mentioning the name of wife at the time of pronouncing talaq is necessary. It is enough if husband admits pronouncing talaq. But the talaq given by mad person does not take place. In the above-mentioned question, the whole condition and detailed situation of husband's madness is not mentioned; hence we are sorry to write any ruling regarding the talaq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
