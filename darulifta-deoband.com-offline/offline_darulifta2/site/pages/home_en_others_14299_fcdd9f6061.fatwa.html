    Question ID: 14299Country: Pakistan

    Title: - DREAM - I had this dream long ago. I have 4 brothers and 1 sister. I dreamnt that we were living in a lone house, in the middle of a desert. Our car was parked some way away from the house. One night (in the dream) I went to the car and started the ignition. But instead of going forward the car raised up into the air. It kept rising up and reached a most beatiful place with abundant greenry, trees and breathtaking scenery. The grass was of a very rich colour and I remember a stream flowing some way away. I have not seen a place so beautiful in my lifetime. I heard my brothers calling from down below .. they said that "Mum is calling you" .. but I was so immersed in the beauty of the place that I refused to go back down. The scene changed: I was down again. It was another night and I went to the car again. I started the ignition again. The car rose but but was stopped 5-6 feet into the air.. My brothers had tied a rope to the back of the car and now they started pulling me back. i remember insisting on going up but they brought me down. I was really sad. I kept thinking about the place. On the 7th day after I had gone to the beautiful place, I looked at the moon and found a face on it. I was about 8-10 years old when I saw this dream. Now I'm almost 19. I have changed recently and now pray 5 times, sometimes Tahajjud, Ishraq, Awwabein too etc.

    Answer ID: 14299Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1586/1261=H/1430)

     

    All the dreams are good. They indicate that you shall be given taufeeq for doing good deed and refraining from sins, in-shaAllah provided you maintain to follow sunnah in each matter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
