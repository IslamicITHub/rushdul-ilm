    Title: If I am performing salaah and one of my parents calls me, should I terminate my salaah and answer them or should I continue with my salaah and answer them when I am finished?

    Question: If I am performing salaah and one of my parents calls me, should I terminate my salaah and answer them or should I continue with my salaah and answer them when I am finished?

    Answer ID: 12672Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 722/538=L/1430)

     

    If you are offering fardh salah and your parents are not calling you due to some problem, you are not allowed to cancel your salah:

    وبہ صرح في الإمداد بقولہ: أي لایجوز قطعھا بنداء أحد أبویہ من غیر استغاثۃ وطلب إعانۃ لأن قطعھا لا یجوز إلا لضرورۃ، وقال الطحاوي: ھذا في الفرض (شامي 2:426)

    But, if you are offering nafl salah and the parents know it, then also one may continue his salah, but if they are unaware one should cancel the salah and respond them:

    فإن کان في نافلۃ إن علم أحد أبویہ أنہ في الصلاۃ وناداہ لا باس أن لا یجیبہ وإن لم یعلم یجیبہ (شامي 2:426)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
