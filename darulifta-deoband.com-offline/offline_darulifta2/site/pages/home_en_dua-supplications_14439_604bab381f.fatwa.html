    Answer ID: 14439Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1596/1270=H/1430)

     

    The livelihood and business get barakah (prosperity) by making a difference between halal and haram, avoiding being overactive or underactive in earning and spending wealth moderately without squandering. The wealth granted by Allah after hard work increases if spent in good conduct with relatives and religious matters and Allah grants protection against many external and internal troubles. You and your husband should keep on reciting Surah Quraish (لایلاف قریش) 11 times after each salah with durood 11 times before and after. Repeat the same action while going to sleep on clean bed. Try to follow sunnah in each action, refrain from sins as much as possible, have determination to live a simple life, pay special attention to fulfil the rights and avoid encroaching upon anyone?s right. In-shaAllah, you shall see the blessings and prosperity in abundance soon. May Allah favour you, your husband and all concerning with the blessings of this world and the hereafter, and protect you against all undesirables. May you be given khair in each matter and be safeguarded from all evils.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
