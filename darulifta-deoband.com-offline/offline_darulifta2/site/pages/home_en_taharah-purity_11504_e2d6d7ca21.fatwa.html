    Title: Assalaam alaikkum I WANT TO KNOW IS IT COMPULSARY TO HAVE A BATH FOR WOMEN AFTER having a sex USING THE CONDOME?

    Question: Assalaam alaikkum I WANT TO KNOW IS IT COMPULSARY TO HAVE A BATH FOR WOMEN AFTER having a sex USING THE CONDOME?

    Answer ID: 11504Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 493/364=L/1430)

     

    Using condom without any valid reason is makrooh. However, if anyone had sex using condom then both will have to take bath:

    أولج حشفتہ أو قدرھا ملفوفۃ بخرقۃ إن وجد لذۃ الجماع وجب الغسل وإلا لا علی الأصح، والأحوط الوجوب أي وجوب الغسل في الوجھین (الدر المختار مع الشامی 1:303)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
