    Title: While in the middle of wudhu, if any of the causes that break the wudhu takes place

    Question: While in the middle of wudhu, if any of the causes that break the wudhu takes place, then should I start wudhu from beginning again?

    Answer ID: 39691Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1007/601/L=1433) Yes, in case you encounter anything which breaks the wudhu while making wudhu you would have to repeat your wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
