    Answer ID: 9907Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 39/40=H/1429)

     

    The dream means that Allah almighty shall favour you with religious prestige, in-shaAllah. It is an indication also that sunnah must be observed in all the matters.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
