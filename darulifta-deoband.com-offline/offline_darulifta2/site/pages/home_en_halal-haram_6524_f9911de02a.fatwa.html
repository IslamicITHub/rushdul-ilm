    Title: I am living in Lucknow. I am interested in animation designing.Does animation courses in computer science are hallal according to shariyat? Please help me and clearify my query in the light of Quran and Hadith.

    Question: I am living in Lucknow. I am interested in animation designing.Does animation courses in computer science are hallal according to shariyat? Please help me and clearify my query in the light of Quran and Hadith. Shukriya Allah Hafiz.

    Answer ID: 6524Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 827/757=L/1429)

     

    It is unlawful and haram to make picture of living being. The Hadith cursed one who does so. Therefore, as per the Shariah it is wrong to do a course which involves picture making of living being. Yes, one can make pictures of non-living beings like tree, car, train etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
