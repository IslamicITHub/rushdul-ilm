But my question is that the banks gives profit to government of india(RESERVE BANK OF INDIA) and the same government gives subsidy through public distribution system. gives subsidy to hajis, and gives all infrstructure facilities to people and the money kept of madrasas and masjids which encourage the banks business.Then all above things are jayaz or not jayaz and i have seen that in many muslim countries the preimeses are given to many international banks.

But my question is that the banks gives profit to government of india(RESERVE BANK OF INDIA) and the same government gives subsidy through public distribution system. gives subsidy to hajis, and gives all infrstructure facilities to people and the money kept of madrasas and masjids which encourage the banks business.Then all above things are jayaz or not jayaz and i have seen that in many muslim countries the preimeses are given to many international banks.

    Answer ID: 15543Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1595/1315=D=1430)

     

    It is allowable according to Imam Abu Hanifah, but makrooh according to Imam Muhammad and Imam Abu Yusuf. Therefore, giving land on rent to banks is allowable in case of need, but still it is better to have some other renter except the bank.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
