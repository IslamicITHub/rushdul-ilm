    Title: Is it permissible to affix the Quranic verses at the door of the toilet or on the urinal

    Question: Is it permissible to affix the Quranic verses at the door of the toilet or on the urinal? It may fell inside the toilet causing 'be-adabi' of the verse. The verse is" Allahumma in-nee a'oothu bika minalkhubu-thee wal khabaa ith"

    Answer ID: 34908Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1664/1141/L=1432) It is not right to stick Quranic verses or duas in toilet or istinja khana (urinal); because it will cause desecration of the verse or duas.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
