    Title: At Present I am Working in Pvt Ltd Co As an Accountant. I want to Know about Bank Job. Can Muslim Do Job In Bank or Insruance Company.

    Question: At Present I am Working in Pvt Ltd Co As an Accountant. I want to Know about Bank Job. Can Muslim Do Job In Bank or Insruance Company.

    Answer ID: 21153Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 466/466/M=1431)

     

    The job of writing and calculating interest based work in conventional banks and insurance companies is not lawful for a Muslim.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
