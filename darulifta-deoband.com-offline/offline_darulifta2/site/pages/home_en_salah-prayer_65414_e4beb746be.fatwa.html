    Title: How to offer salah missed during a journey?

    Question: What is the procedure/process/hadith in respect of salah which were missed during a journey?

    Answer ID: 65414Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 818/924/L=08/1437)

    Qadais similar to ada (in performing), so as much salah you have missed during a journey offer them and offer them with qasr i.e. pray the salah of four rakah only two rakah. There is no qaza of sunnah salah. Yes, there is qadha of witr salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
