    Question ID: 46051Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Dream

    Question: Recently, we have shifted our house to a new building. After one week we went to Umrah with family and very next week, (1) My wife got a dream that someone (some ladies) is pulling my car from falling from the tip of a Pyramid like place. The place is known to be the place of Khawaja Gharib Nawaz. After two days, my new car met with an accident. (2) Next week, we have given sadqah to all neighbors. On the same day, there is one more dream that around 12 Black Crows are flown away in the dream. (3) Meanwhile, one more dream that a Black Cobra (huge one) came all along the way from Hajr-e-Aswad on my wife's face and looking at her face. Please help me interpreting these dreams.

    Answer ID: 46051Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1033/806/B=1434) All these three dreams are troublesome. Follow the Shariah rules strictly and arrange for halal income. In-shaAllah, you shall be saved from all kinds of problems.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
