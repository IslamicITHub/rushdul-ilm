    Question: Assalamualaikum Most Respected Hazrat Mufti Sahib Dam Barkatuhu "If a person Text Massagees on 3 different times Talaaq, Will Talaaq Happen?" Jazakumullah Khair!

    Answer ID: 14747Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1392/1137=D/1430)

     

    If the husband writes it with the intention of giving talaq, talaq will take place, and it will be counted as much as many times he will write.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
