    Answer ID: 9845Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 6/9=L/1429)

     

    Praying with the wasilah of the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) is lawful; even it is effective in acceptance of dua.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
