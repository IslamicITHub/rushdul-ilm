    Title: heavy memory loss

    Question: I am 20 years old n i am suffering frm heavy memory loss so please sugest me any islamic solution to increase memory power if u are sending any ayath please type it in english r give zer zabar to the arabi words

    Answer ID: 32728Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1000/600/L=1432) You should recite Ya Qawi (يا قوي) 11 times by putting your hand on your forehead after each salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
