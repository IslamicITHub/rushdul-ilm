    Title: MY YOUNGER FAMILY MEMBERS AND RELATIVES CALL ME BY THE NAME OF "LAI" MEANING - BIG BROTHER. MY WIFE ALSO CALLS ME BY THAT NAME TOO. SO IT IS PERMISSIBLE FOR HER TO DO SO??

    Question: MY YOUNGER FAMILY MEMBERS AND RELATIVES CALL ME BY THE NAME OF "LAI" MEANING - BIG BROTHER. MY WIFE ALSO CALLS ME BY THAT NAME TOO. SO IT IS PERMISSIBLE FOR HER TO DO SO??

    Answer ID: 10301Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 99/99=M/1429)

     

    The wife should avoid it in future; she should not call you with Lai.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
