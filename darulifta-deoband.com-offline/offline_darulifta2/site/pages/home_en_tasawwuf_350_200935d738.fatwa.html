    Title: Is gathering of communal loud thikr in the Masaajid permissible?

    Question: Is gathering of communal loud thikr in the Masaajid permissible?

    Answer ID: 350Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 16=162/L)

     

    If there is none offering salah, and people get involved in loud Zikr individually then it is allowed even it seemingly creates a scene of collective Zikr:

    وفي حاشیة الحموي للشعراني: قد اجمع العلماء سلفاً و خلفاً علی استحباب ذکر الجماعة في المساجد وغیرھا الا ان یشوش جھرھم علی نائم او مصل او قارئ (شامي: 2/434، ط زکریا، دیوبند)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
