    Title: Can we buy items from Chor Bazar in Delhi as people say that thief sells there items which they steal.

    Question: Can we buy items from Chor Bazar in Delhi as people say that thief sells there items which they steal.

    Answer ID: 151057Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 981/870/H=8/1438)

     

    It is not lawful for you to buy such goods about which you know that they were achieved through theft, but if you have only doubt then it shall not be taken into consideration.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
