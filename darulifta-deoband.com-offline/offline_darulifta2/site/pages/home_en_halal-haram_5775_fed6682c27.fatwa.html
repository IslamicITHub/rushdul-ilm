    Answer ID: 5775Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1242/1242=H-1429)

     

    The proposed business you are planning includes plentiful wrong even un-Islamic and unlawful things, and it is difficult for you to save yourself from those things. In this case, the present job in the company sounds better. It is a principle of Hadith and Fiqh that when there are two troubles to confront with and there is no way out except choosing one of them, then chose one which is lighter and easier. So, you should keep doing the job in the company and also keep on doing repentance and seek forgiveness from Allah exceedingly. When you find good job free form unlawfulness then leave the company job.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
