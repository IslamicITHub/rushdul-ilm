    Answer ID: 3657Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 500/394=B)

     

    As far as matter of nikah is concerned it is obvious that if a mature girl accepts Islam with her consent she can marry herself to any man without even a guardian i.e. she can marry a man of her match without the consent of her father. This nikah will be considered valid as per Shariah, but we suggest you not to fall prey of her, it is feared that you are persuaded to Qadyanizm through the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
