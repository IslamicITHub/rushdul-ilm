    Title: Dua for Shoulder pain

    Question: What is the dua for remedy of shoulder pain?

    Answer ID: 60454Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1230/1222/L=10/1436) Read out the following verse seven times and blow on some oil and apply it on the place of pain, in-shaAllah, it shall relieve you. وَبِالْحَقِّ أَنزَلْنَاهُ وَبِالْحَقِّ نَزَلَ وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلاَّ مُبَشِّراً وَنَذِيراً [الإسراء : 105]

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
