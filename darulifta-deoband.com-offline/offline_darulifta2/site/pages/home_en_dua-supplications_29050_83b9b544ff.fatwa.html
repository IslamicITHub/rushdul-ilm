    Title: Wht is the dua between two sajdaah? in which namaz we should recite it i.e. farz,sunnat,nafil,witr. reply soon

    Question: asalamualaikum.... wht is the dua between two sajdaah? in which namaz we should recite it i.e. farz,sunnat,nafil,witr. reply soon

    Answer ID: 29050Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 140/140/M=1432)

    The following dua is narrated between the two sajdahs:
    اللهم اغفرلي ورحمنى و عافنى ، و اهدني و ارزقني .
    But it is related to nafl i.e. one should recite it in tahajjud etc or even in fardh salah in case of offering it alone.
    و ما ورد محمول على النفل أى تهجداً أو غيره الخ .(درمختار مع الشامى)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
