    Answer ID: 17125Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1762/1378/L=1430)

     

    (1 & 2) You are not responsible for the maintenance of your brother and sister except your parents, except that your brother or sister is too needy and it is difficult for him or her to survive. Therefore, if they are earning enough for their living, their expense is not wajib on you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
