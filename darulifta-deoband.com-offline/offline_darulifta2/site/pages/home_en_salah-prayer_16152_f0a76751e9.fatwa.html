    Question: Maulana sahab, here when we pray witr in masjid ul haram the witr is prayed in such a way that first 2 rakats and then one, so since i am a hanafy so what should i do then, also that when in last raka'ah of witr when imam does dua can we raise our hands and thereafter wipe them on face? Jazakallah!

    Answer ID: 16152Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1873/1480/D=1430)

     

    You should not turn your head for salam at two rak?ah and offer the third rak?ah with imam. It is allowable to join the witr in the Holy Haram with imam in the abovementioned way, not at any other place.

    (2) Neither raise hands nor say aamin, just keep silent:

    قال في المراقی: وإذا اقتدی بمن یقنت في الفجر قام معہ في قنوتہ ساکتاً في الاظھر (حاشیۃ طحطاوي علی المراقی 385)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
