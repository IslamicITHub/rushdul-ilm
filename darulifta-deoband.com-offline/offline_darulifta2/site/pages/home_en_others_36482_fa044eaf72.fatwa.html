    Title: Yesterday night I saw a dream in which I was eating a meat curry (ghost salan) prepared by my mother. But the meat was human fingers

    Question: Yesterday night I saw a dream in which I was eating a meat curry (ghost salan) prepared by my mother. But the meat was human fingers. I was saying to mother that this is haram, why you bought human meat, but my mother was insisting me that it is not a problem and can be eaten. But I was not happy and I was denying it in my heart. Please guide me about my dream.

    Answer ID: 36482Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 344/195/D=1433) Assess your life regarding worship particularly namaz. Lest you are showing laziness in offering salah as per fardh, wajib, sunnah, and missing jamah or your salah is not going in vain. Or assess your salah as per the sunan and etiquettes lest your reward is being decreased due to laziness.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
