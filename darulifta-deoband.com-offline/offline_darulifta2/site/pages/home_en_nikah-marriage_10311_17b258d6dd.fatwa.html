    Answer ID: 10311Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 187/165=B/1429)

     

    Oral sex is not good, one must avoid it; it is shamelessness and obscenity. It is culture of the Jews and the Christians. We should shun from such inhuman ways, it is unnatural and animalistic behaviour.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
