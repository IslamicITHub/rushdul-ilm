    Answer ID: 22374Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 837/662/D=1431)

    Ma-sha-Allah the dream is very nice. You should pay special attention towards following sunnah and sharia, and avoid nonsense and evil deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
