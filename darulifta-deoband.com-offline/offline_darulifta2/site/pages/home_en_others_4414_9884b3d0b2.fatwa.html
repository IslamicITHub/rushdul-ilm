    Answer ID: 4414Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 646/621=L/1429)

     

    Your dream is obvious. The hot lava which exploded and encompassed the world is the illustration of the mischifs occuring before Doomsday and spreading in the entire world. You have been guided the way to deliverance as well that you should engage yourself exceedingly in salah, tilawah and zikr, these mischiefs shall never raom around you, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
