    Title: Assalamualaikum , Can a Girl Marry her Mother's Uncle's son. Means apni Ammi ke Chacha ke bete se kya nikah Jayaz Hai.Jabki wo ladka us ladki ka MAMA lagta hai.

    Question: Assalamualaikum , Can a Girl Marry her Mother's Uncle's son. Means apni Ammi ke Chacha ke bete se kya nikah Jayaz Hai.Jabki wo ladka us ladki ka MAMA lagta hai.

    Answer ID: 11440Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 505/375=L/1430)

     

    The boy is not the real mamun (maternal uncle) of the girl, therefore they can marry.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
