    Answer ID: 12515Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1054/825=H/1430)

     

    The dream indicates that you should follow the sunnah. This is the interpretation of your dream. Leave reciting the Dau-e-Ganjul Arsh. And which durood you recite? Write it down and submit the question again.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
