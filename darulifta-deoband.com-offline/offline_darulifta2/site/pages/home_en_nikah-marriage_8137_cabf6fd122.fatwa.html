    Title: Is contraceptive pills/Condom/Tablets like Mala-D are permissible in Islam to control birth temporarly?

    Question: Is contraceptive pills/Condom/Tablets like Mala-D are permissible in Islam to control birth temporarly?

    Answer ID: 8137Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1050/1050=M/1429)

     

    On the basis of valid reasons, it is allowable to use temporary contraceptives (pills or condoms), otherwise it is makrooh (undesirable). You must have written what is the problem with you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
