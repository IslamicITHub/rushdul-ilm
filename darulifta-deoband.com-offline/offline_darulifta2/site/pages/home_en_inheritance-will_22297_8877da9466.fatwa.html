    Title: My question is regarding the division of property.There are 4 sons with a daughter and mother. what should be the percentage of division of property among sons, daughter and the mother.what percentage should each son,daughter and mother get respectively?

    Question: assalamualaikum My question is regarding the division of property.There are 4 sons with a daughter and mother. what should be the percentage of division of property among sons, daughter and the mother.what percentage should each son,daughter and mother get respectively? JazakAllah!!

    Answer ID: 22297Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 833/590/L=1431)

     

    After due rights preceding inheritance, all the property of the deceased shall be distributed in 54 shares; out of the total 9 shares will go to his mother and each son will get 10 shares while the daughter will get 5 shares.

    Detail:

    Mother:             9

    Son: 10x4=        40

    Daughter:          5

    Total:               54

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
