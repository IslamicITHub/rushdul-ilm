    Title: I have onle six or seven hair in beard.i want full beard that for can i use midicin for that. is it ligal in islam

    Question: assalamuylikum I have onle six or seven hair in beard.i want full beard that for can i use midicin for that. is it ligal in islam

    Answer ID: 25049Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1637/1283/B=1431)

    Yes, you can use medicine in order to grow your full beard. Islam does not prohibit it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
