    Question ID: 45559Country: India

    Title: Hajj

    Question: My question is my friend sending his mother and father to hajj. He is in merchant navy. Last one year he has not joined his job because of his exam. At present he has no money in his hand. He is taking Rs, 5,00,000/= loan from the bank for sending them on hajj. It is right or what? Please tell me in the light of hadith.

    Answer ID: 45559Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 864/685/B=1434) When your friend or his parents do not have the capacity to go for hajj then hajj is not fardh on them. There is no need to take loan on interest. Your friend should send his parents on hajj when he has enough money.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
