    Title: kuttay ko marna(halak karna) jaiz h ya nahi? hamaray gar k agay ak kutta betha rahta hay aur ghar walo k ilawa koi naya aye to us par buhat bhonkta hay. yehi khatra laga rahta hay k kat na lay. aur phir agar koi mahman aye to acha bhi nahi lagta.

    Question: kuttay ko marna(halak karna) jaiz h ya nahi? hamaray gar k agay ak kutta betha rahta hay aur ghar walo k ilawa koi naya aye to us par buhat bhonkta hay. yehi khatra laga rahta hay k kat na lay. aur phir agar koi mahman aye to acha bhi nahi lagta.

    Answer ID: 9180Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2110/1940=D/1429)

     

    Get it away from your doors or tame it by giving food so that stops barking. If you fear any harm from it you can kill it, but you should not kill only at the basis of doubt:

    إذا کثرت الکلاب في قریۃ و أضرت بأھلھا أمر أربابھا بقتلھا (شامي 2:237)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
