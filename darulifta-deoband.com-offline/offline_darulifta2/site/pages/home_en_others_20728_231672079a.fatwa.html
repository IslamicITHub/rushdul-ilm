    Question: I have seen a dream that i m in a Masjid... i offered my maghrib prayers in the imamat of Hazrat Omar (R.A) , then stayed in the masjid and offered isha prayers in the imamat of Hazrat Bilal... then slept in that masjid and the next morning offered Fajr prayers in the imamat of Imam-e-hussain ....when i finished the prayers i went to shake hands with Imam-e-hussain but suddenly i came to know that he was Imam's son (not imam-e-hussain himself). i saw him , he was very old with a white beard... i have the book tabeer ur ru'yaa for this dream ... there is different analysis for each prayer that i offered ... but i m not content with it becuase i wanna know that i havent just seen those personalities , infact i have offered prayers in thier imamat and all the prayers were in tandom...in contact with the previous one .... sir, i i want an answer with should be given after a thourough research becuease i feel it is not an ordinary dream by any means... Wassalm

    Answer ID: 20728Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 629/492/H=1431)

     

    It is very nice dream. The meaning is that you would obtain firmness in religion and modesty as well as the  love for the family of holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) and abundant shares from the good qualities of the companions of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) which is the greatest honour in both the worlds. This is the meaning of your dream. It would be better for you if you turn your attention towards polishing and developing the above mentioned good qualities and exceptional morals. You can acquire it by studying Hikayat Sahaba and Hayatus Sahaba (رضوان الله تعالى عليهم اجمعين) (Urdu and Arabic according to your ease) and be regular in listening and reading it, in-sha-Allah very soon you would get this great blessing. May Almighty Allah give you and your relatives blessings of this blessed dream.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
