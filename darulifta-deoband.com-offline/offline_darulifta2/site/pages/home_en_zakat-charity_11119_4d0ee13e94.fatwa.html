    Question: kis insan par zakat dena kab farz hai? (2) kya har adme ko apni kamai se Rs. 100 me se Rs2.50 ke hisab se paise nikalna zaroori hai . aur ager nahi nikalega to kya wo ganahgar hoga ? (3) agar zakat farz hai to kis hisab se nikalai jayega. quran aur hadith ke hawale ke sath batane ki maharbani kare . jazakallah

    Answer ID: 11119Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 256/256=M/1430)

     

    (1) One has to pay zakah who owns 7.5 tola gold or 52.5 tola silver or cash or business commodity equal to 52.5 tola silver and one year passes over it.

    (2) No, Zakah is not obligatory on every person, it is obligatory only on those owning wealth equal to nisab which has been mentioned above.

    (3) 2.50% of total wealth will be taken out as Zakah e.g. 2.50 rupees out of 100 rupees, thus you will have to give Zakah of your wealth.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
