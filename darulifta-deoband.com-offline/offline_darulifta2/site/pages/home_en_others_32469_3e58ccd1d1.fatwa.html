    Title: Kindly give the meaning of the dream in which i saw that i was sitting on the back of a truck and the driver had crossed the river within seconds

    Question: Kindly give the meaning of the dream in which i saw that i was sitting on the back of a truck and the driver had crossed the river within seconds (the water was somewhere dirty & somewhere very neat & clean)and on reaching the other side of the river i saw few strange people who shaked hand with me and congrats me for doing so,then at once i realise that i was standing on the river and my shoes were still dry.

    Answer ID: 32469Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 877/724/B=1432) In-sha-Allah, you shall be safe from haram income and earn lawful and halal earning.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
