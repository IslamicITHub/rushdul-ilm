    Title: I am a government employee. Last week I got annual prize in terms of money and certificate. Is it halal to use? And I have donated this money to masjid. Is it ok?

    Question: I am a government employee. Last week I got annual prize in terms of money and certificate. Is it halal to use? And I have donated this money to masjid. Is it ok?

    Answer ID: 161450Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 964/773/B=8/1439)

     

    It is alright to take the prize and use it. It is also alright to donate it to the mosque and it is a cause of reward as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
