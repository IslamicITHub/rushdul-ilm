    Title: I want to know if it is lawful to become a life time member of any magazine newspaper for just Rs. 1000 or less-more. Please also tell me whether it is lawful to become a lifetime member of Hutch, Airtel, BSNL etc. for a certain amount or not?

    Question: I want to know if it is lawful to become a life time member of any magazine newspaper for just Rs. 1000 or less-more. Please also tell me whether it is lawful to become a lifetime member of Hutch, Airtel, BSNL etc. for a certain amount or not?

    Answer ID: 614Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 286/M)

     

    It is allowed to become a lifetime member of any magazine or newspaper with the intention of cooperation and contribution. (Fatwa Rahimiah) Likewise, it is permissible to become lifetime member of AirTel, Hucth and BSNL etc provided the norms are decided and it does not involve any Shart-e-Fasid (incorrect condition) and fear of Zarar (harm).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
