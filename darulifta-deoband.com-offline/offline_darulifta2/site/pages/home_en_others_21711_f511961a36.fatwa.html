    Answer ID: 21711Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 899/689/H=1431)

     

    Whatever ferocity and fear you are feeling from the external appearance of the dream is natural. The dream means that insha-Allah you will be graced with the visit of Harmain Sharifain (the two holy Mosques, may Almighty Allah increase their honour, dignity, glory and prestige). In order to remove the fear visit the grave of your late mother and regularly send her rewards extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
