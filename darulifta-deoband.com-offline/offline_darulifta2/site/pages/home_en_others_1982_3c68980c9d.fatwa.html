    Question: As salam alaikum brothers (1) Is my name MOHAMMED SABEEL ULLA is correct lots of people says that my name is wrong alhamdullilah i practice namaz 5 times a day (2) Is it ok to wear contact eye lences while performing whudhu .(3) Please clarify me about the difference between shirk and wasila i am confused about this things for years and yet i didnt get any useful opinion about this from anyone I have performed hajj in 2006 there in madina i met a scholar whao said wasila is also haram (4) and lastly for some many nights i am having dreams in which iam seeing 3-4 snakes 2 or 3 are nag snakes and one is a comman green snakes they appear after raining allover around house among those i kill one but i dont know about others usually i dont have these kind of bizzare dreams .I think my request will be fullfilled i will be thankfull for you brothers and allah for your kind services i am confident that you will be fully rewarded by allah for these kind deed "allah hafiz"as for my details i am an engineering graduate my birth day is 17th of zil hijjah 1403 hijrih i will be waiting for your reply

    Answer ID: 1982Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1304/1173=B)

     

    (1) The name is correct

    (2) What is contact eyelence? We could not understand it. Clarify what type of optic it is.

    (3) To regard an associate in Allah and His attributes i.e. believing in someone other than Allah as God, Nourisher, Giver of life and death etc is shirk (polytheism). While, supplicating Allah by the mediation of any virtuous act, any prophet, sahabi, the Throne of Allah, Key of His Treasures or His Eight Names (as mentioned in Hadith) is waseelah. Shirk and waseelah are far different from each other. Waseelah has been enjoined in the Quran as well: وابتغوا الیہ الوسیلۃ, it is surprising enough how you have been told that waseelah is haram whereas it has been mentioned in the ­duas of many sahih hadiths as well.  

    (4) There are many enemies behind you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
