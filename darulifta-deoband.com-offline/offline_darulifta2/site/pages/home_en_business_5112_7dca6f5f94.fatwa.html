    Answer ID: 5112Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 490/465=L/1429)

     

    In the abovementioned case, you can purchase house through bank in a way that the bank purchases the house and includes the interest amount in net price then sells it to you and you pay the total price on instalments.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
