    Title: As salam alaikum, respected ulama, my question is regarding rakat. i will tie my hands not below the naval or not on the chest in middle is it ok.I am the follower of hanafi madhab. please clarify in the light of quran & hadith.

    Question: As salam alaikum, respected ulama, my question is regarding rakat. i will tie my hands not below the naval or not on the chest in middle is it ok.I am the follower of hanafi madhab. please clarify in the light of quran & hadith.

    Answer ID: 12182Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 959/722=H/1430)

     

    It is proved from some companions that they regarded putting the hands below navel as sunnah. The Hanafi School of Fiqh follows the same opinion. If you are a Hanafi, you should practice the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
