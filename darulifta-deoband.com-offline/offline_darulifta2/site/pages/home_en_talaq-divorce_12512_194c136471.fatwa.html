    Answer ID: 12512Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1055/826=H/1430)

     

    Take the case to any local or nearby Sharai Panchayat or Darul Qadha. They shall note down the statement of her husband and after due investigation they will cancel the nikah and separate the husband and wife. Therefore, having passed her iddah period, she can marry anywhere she likes. In short, this case is not related to Darul Ifta, rather it can be solved in any Sharai Panchayat or Darul Qadha.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
