    Question: Assalamualaykum Hazrat, I had heard there is some kind of amal to get good and beautiful wife. I want to know any amal which is available in islam to get good and most beautiful wife?

    Answer ID: 11900Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 778/653=D/1430)

     

    One should recite this du?a after every obligatory salah:

    وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
