    Question ID: 167147Country: India

    Title: Keeping Islamic apps on phone

    Question: (1) Can we keep Islamic screensavers on our phones? (2) Can we carry such phones inside the washroom which have Qur'an apps (although the apps are not on)? (3) My phone plays the adhan at the time of salah. Is it a sin to turn it off if I am in a meeting?

    Answer ID: 167147Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 401/356/L=04/1440)

     

    (1) Yes, you may keep but if there is fear of disrespect then it is better not to keep.

    (2) If the apps are closed then there is no harm in taking it to toilet.

    (3) It is not a sin.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
