    Title: Assalamualaikum. I am a TYBSc Computer Science student. We have to do a project in this year. I have chosen LOAN MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE for a bank. Is it right or wrong to build this software?

    Question: Assalamualaikum. I am a TYBSc Computer Science student. We have to do a project in this year. I have chosen LOAN MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE for a bank. Is it right or wrong to build this software?

    Answer ID: 16029Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1787/1440/D=1430)

     

    In the abovementioned software, you will have to write accounts of interest; therefore should avoid it and try to look for other projects for work.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
