    Title: Can we use tooth past while fasting ?

    Question: can we use tooth past while fasting

    Answer ID: 25350Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1866/1348/H=1431)

    No, you cannot do so, and if the elements of toothpaste will enter into the throat the fast shall be nullified.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
