    Question ID: 21765Country: Pakistan

    Title: my question is that sometimes while in examination my friends do ask me to cheat my answers with them so i do to help them...how can i make them realize that it is a bad habit though sometimes with the fear of getting fail i do commit this sin...plz help..

    Question: assalam o alaikum my question is that sometimes while in examination my friends do ask me to cheat my answers with them so i do to help them...how can i make them realize that it is a bad habit though sometimes with the fear of getting fail i do commit this sin...plz help..jazak allaho khairan

    Answer ID: 21765Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 652/652/M=1431)

     

    Make your friend understand that while appearing at the exam helping any one i.e. copying answers and solving paper is an offence by law. On being caught action may be taken against both of them. And the violation of law is not right Islamically. One should avoid it. You can guide him before exam as much as you can and your friend should appear at the exam with full preparation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
