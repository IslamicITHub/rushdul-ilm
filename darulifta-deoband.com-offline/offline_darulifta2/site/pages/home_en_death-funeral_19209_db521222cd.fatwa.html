    Title: Performing Janazah salah on road in shoes

    Question: HAMARE TOWN ME JANAJA KI NAMAZ ROAD PE PADHI JATI HE TO AESI SAKAL ME CHAPPAL(FOOTWEAR)NIKAL KAR ROAD PE KHULE PAV SE NAMAZ PARNA KESA HE?

    Answer ID: 19209Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 134/134/M=1431)

     

    If there is no impurity on the road, it is lawful to perform janazad salah over there after taking off shoes. If the road is most probably impure, one should take off shoes and perform salah standing at the shoes provided the upper part of the shoes is clean.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
