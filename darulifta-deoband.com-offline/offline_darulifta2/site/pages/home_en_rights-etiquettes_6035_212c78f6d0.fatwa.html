    Question: My father given a divorce to my mother I am stay with my father I am 30 years old my mother she don't have any source of income I wish to help her but my father always telling me don't help her I am confussed what i have to do please suggest me shall i help her or follow the instruction of my father is this case what is the hadith.

    Answer ID: 6035Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 522/522=M/1429)

     

    According to Hadith, the status of mother is higher than father; hence, you must help your mother and it is better to help her secretly so that your father could not know it. And if by any means it comes to your father?s notice and he stops you from assisting your mother then you should listen to your father?s words silently and convince him with wisdom and politeness. Continue serving and helping your mother besides showing good conduct to your father.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
