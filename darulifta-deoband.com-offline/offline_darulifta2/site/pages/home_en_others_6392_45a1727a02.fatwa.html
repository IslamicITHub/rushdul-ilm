    Title: I once had a dream that iwas reading a book and i zoomed in to these words subhanallahi wabihamdihi subhanallahilazeem

    Question: assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuhu wamagfiratuhu...i once had a dream that iwas reading a book and i zoomed in to these words subhanallahi wabihamdihi subhanallahilazeem

    Answer ID: 6392Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1579/1196=H/1429)

     

    It is a nice dream. The meaning of the dream is that by studying, reading out and listening Fazail Aamaal in-sha-Allah you shall get the benefits of higher books.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
