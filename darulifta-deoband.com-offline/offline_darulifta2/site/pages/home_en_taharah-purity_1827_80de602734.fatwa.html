    Question: I am a boy of 24 year old i have some problem that whenever i think about sex or automaticlly some thing related to sex is came in my mind a drop or some drops of spermatic fluid (muzy)is ejected from my body.so in this case it is necessary for me to take bath or not.i m worring to much for that please help me by giving proper solution.

    Answer ID: 1827Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 680/676=N)

     

    Mazi (spermatorrhea) invalidates wuzu but it does not make ghusl compulsory:

    لا عند مذی أو ودیّ بل الوضوء منہ ومن البول جمیعا علی الظاھر۔ قولہ 'لا عند مذی' ای لا یفرض الغسل عند خروج مذی۔ (در مع رد المحتار: 1/304، ط زکریا، دیوبند)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
