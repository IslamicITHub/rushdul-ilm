    Question: SALAAM, i m married and have 3 kids.i am now going to do sencond marraige and i signed divourced paper of my first wife but my wife dont know about this.i have no physical relation with my wife sice 2 months.plz tell me is this divource exists if my wife dont know about divource papers or i cant divource my wife untill she knows about it?

    Answer ID: 21950Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 786/570/=1431)

     

    It is allowable to the extent of dua only. However, add these words in your dua: "O Almighty Allah, if doing nikah is better for me with her then ? ? ? It should be noted that before marriage she is stranger for you, it is not lawful to talk to her rather it is necessary to observe purdah with her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
