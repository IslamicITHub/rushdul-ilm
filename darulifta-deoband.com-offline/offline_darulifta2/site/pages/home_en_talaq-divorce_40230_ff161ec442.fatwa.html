    Title: I want to know the difference between one talaq bain and two talaq bain. Is there any different

    Question: I want to know the difference between one talaq bain and two talaq bain. Is there any different? Does one talaq bain completely break the marital relation in khula or still 2 more talaq bain will be required to break so.

    Answer ID: 40230Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1177/748/L=1433) In khula, nikah comes to an end completely by one talaq bayin. After one talaq bayin, there is no need of two more talaqs for termination of nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
