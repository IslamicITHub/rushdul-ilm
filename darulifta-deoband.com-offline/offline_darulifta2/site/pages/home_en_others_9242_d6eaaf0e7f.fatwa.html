    Question: As-Salaamu-Alaikum-Wa-Rahmatulla-Hi-Wa-Barakaatuhu!!! Dear Mufti Saahab, May Allah(SWT) bless you with the best for the work you are doing. Few days ago a very pious person passed away in our town. He was not a Aalim or Haafeez but a Daaiee who was involved very much in the work of Daawaah. I wasn't there in my town during that time neither was I close to this person during his hayaat but, use to see him often while going and coming from prayers. Coming to the point. I with my family and my brother-in-law were discussing about this person after which we went to sleep. I dreamt about the person who passed away that "It is a time for prayers (Don't remember which prayer but, I feel Magrib) and this person is asking me to lead the prayers and upon his saying I led the prayers and I started reciting aayaahs (Qiraat) and I remember that I was stopping after each aayaah and was very slow in recitation (Don't remember which aayaahs). This person whom I am talking about, in the state of Salaah started reading the Aayaahs louder and little faster and I taught he is prompting me and even I started reciting it fast. Please interpret this dream of mine (Jazaakumullah).

    Answer ID: 9242Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2276/1882=B/1429)

     

    The dream is an indication that you should also be religious and mindful of salah, and you should also be dayee (inviter) like him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
