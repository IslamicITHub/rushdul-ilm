    Answer ID: 8987Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 15847=265=L/1429)

     

    Wrong theories of Barelvi sect have been refuted in many books, like Radd-e-Razakhaniat, Mutala-e-Barelviat, Ibarat-e-Akabir etc. You can buy these books from any book depot of Deoband. You should strive to bring them to the right path with wisdom and insight in the light of these books.

     

    You should often recite the Holy Quran and keep remembering your death. According to Hadith, both the things are sources to purify ones heart. Also, recite the third kalimah, durood and istighfar one hundred times each in the morning and evening. If there is a pious follower of Sunnah Shaikh in nearby places, you should do bai'ah with him and follow his instructions. You should also go in tablighi jamat for one for forty days or four months.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
