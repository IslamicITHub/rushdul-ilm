    Question ID: 12970Country: India

    Title: asl, iam commission agent.my work is to sale the machines and get the commission from two side,(purchare & saler) in that soft work. no hard work only to introduce both party.i will ask money with my expectation not parties offer,whether this is halal income or haram.

    Question: asl, iam commission agent.my work is to sale the machines and get the commission from two side,(purchare & saler) in that soft work. no hard work only to introduce both party.i will ask money with my expectation not parties offer,whether this is halal income or haram.

    Answer ID: 12970Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 975/827=D/1430)

     

    It is lawful to receive the charge agreed mutually, provided you have dealing with both the parties to take the charge of introducing them with each other and no deception and cheating is involved therein. Such income will be halal and lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
