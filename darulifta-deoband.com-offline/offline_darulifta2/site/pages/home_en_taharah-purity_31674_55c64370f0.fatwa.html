    Title: I've many problem relating to urination. After urination I use toilet paper and wait for stopping the drop of urine, but it takes long time to stop, after sometimes of urination a few drops come again.

    Question: I've many problem relating to urination. After urination I use toilet paper and wait for stopping the drop of urine, but it takes long time to stop, after sometimes of urination a few drops come again. I've also a problem of frequent urination, amount of urine more or less, and most of the time I can not hold this. I consulted with a few homeopathic physician but there are no improvement. Once I went to a urology specialist, but he wanted to see my penis, then I came from him without showing my penis. Now, what can I do for these problems? Is there any A'mal ?

    Answer ID: 31674Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 961/D=222/File D=1432) (1) You should get yourself free from urination earlier than salah and use tissue paper after it so that the drops are absorbed in it, and throw away the tissue paper at the time of salah and perform wudhu. (2) Do not perform wudhu immediately after urination, rather walk a few steps or lay down. Also, consult any pious hakim or homoeopathic practitioner. (3) The ruling and definition of excused person (mazoor) is mentioned in Bahishti Zewar. Until you are not confirmed as excused as per the Shariah it is necessary for you to avoid the urine drops; because wudhu shall break by discharge of drop when it is known definitely. However, you should not pay any attention to doubt

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
