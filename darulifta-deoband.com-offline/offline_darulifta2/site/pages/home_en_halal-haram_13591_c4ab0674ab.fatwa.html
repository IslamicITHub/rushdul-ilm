    Title: upto what age can a boy and girl allowed to intermingle? after what age should a boy or girl expected to perform parda?

    Question: upto what age can a boy and girl allowed to intermingle? after what age should a boy or girl expected to perform parda?

    Answer ID: 13591Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1101/935=D/1430)

     

    When a girl reaches adolescence, which is around nine year of age when she feels tended towards the other sex, she should observe purdah and should not meet strangers (non-mahram).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
