    Title: Bank Job

    Question: I would like to know that if i work in Investment Bank as a Secretary, does my job is Halal or Haram ?

    Answer ID: 32308Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 940/L=347/TL=1432) While doing the job of secretary in a bank if you have not to support in taking interest, giving interest, writing interest or bearing witness to it, then such a job is lawful. But if you have to support in giving interest, taking interest, writing interest or bearing witness to it, then such a job is not lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
