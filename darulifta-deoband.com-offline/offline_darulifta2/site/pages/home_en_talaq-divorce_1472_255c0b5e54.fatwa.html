    Answer ID: 1472Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 610/605=N)

     

    Khula and talaq are husband?s rights. Khula can not be issued in husband?s absence. Khula will never be accepted until husband furnishes it orally or in writing. Therefore, you are suggested to send the khula nama written by city Qazi. Having gone through it, we shall have a say in the matter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
