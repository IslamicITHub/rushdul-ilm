    Title: salah

    Question: Is it halal to offer namaz behind an imam who is trying land grab somebody property by illegal ways? If it is haram please advice how to deal with such people.

    Answer ID: 45228Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 715/582/B=1434) One of the characteristics of imamah is that the imam should be pious. A pious person is one who avoids major sins. If any imam is committing any major sin then he is impious. It is forbidden to make an impious person imam. لا يؤم فاجر مومناً (ابن ماجه) Hence salah shall be makrooh tahrimi behind such Imam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
