    Title: agar se kisi 2 ya 2 se jyada log ki namaz jamaat se choot gaye, to kya wo khud se dusri jamaat kar sakte hai (masjid me).Agar yeh sahi hai har koi apne waqt ke mutabik masjid me aakar jamaat se namaz padehenga, kya ye sahi hai.

    Question: agar se kisi 2 ya 2 se jyada log ki namaz jamaat se choot gaye, to kya wo khud se dusri jamaat kar sakte hai (masjid me).Agar yeh sahi hai har koi apne waqt ke mutabik masjid me aakar jamaat se namaz padehenga, kya ye sahi hai.

    Answer ID: 10328Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 112/112=M/1429)

     

    The second jamaat is makrooh in mosque campus, as it is mentioned in Fatawa Shami; therefore the second jamaat must not be performed in mosque.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
