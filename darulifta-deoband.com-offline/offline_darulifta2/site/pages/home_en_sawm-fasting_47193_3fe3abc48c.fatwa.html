    Title: Dua

    Question: Please tell me whether the dua "Bisaume GHadin Nawaitu Min Shahre Ramadan" for the intention of sehri is lawful or not. If not, please give an alternate dua.

    Answer ID: 47193Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1213/1212/M=10/1434) Niyah means the intention of heart and one eats sehri with the intention of fasting. Then eating sehri is tantamount to intention. There is no need of intention in words separately but if one reads the said dua in sehri then it is lawful and better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
