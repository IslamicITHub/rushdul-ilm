    Question: As salam walekum. i have one problem, for which i seek your help. I always dream of snacks. Many times i see them (snacks) harming me or pursuing me. (that time i used to stay in hostel which was above hospital). please tell me - 1. what is the meaning of dream that i saw. 2. And what was that when i felt fragrance smell. 3. Now a days if i sleep late (near by 12 am or late), i could sense as if some one is sitting near by me. i always sleep on my right hand side & can feel someone towards left side (at my back side). then i pray duas till i get asleep & never open my eyes to see whats there.     Answer ID: 10032Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 67/68=L/1429)

     

    These are effects from the jins. You should recite Ayatul Kursi and four Quls (Surah Al-Kafiroon, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and Al-Naas) before going to bed and blow on your hand and pass over entire body. Get a copy of Manzil and recite it daily once.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
