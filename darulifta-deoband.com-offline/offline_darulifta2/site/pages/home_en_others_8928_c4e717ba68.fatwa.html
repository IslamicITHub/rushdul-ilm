    Question: Assalam alaikum wa Rahmathullahi wa Barakatuhu.. My Husband passed in an unfortunate accident on 24th Ramadan this year. I have seen a dream recently that My Husband is feeding me lot of food eventhough I'm full, He is asking to eat more..and then He says that let's go upstairs and eat Vegetarian food..suddenly, His uncle comes in and tells us that you both first go around the mall and then eat later..so My husband takes me to a very big shopping mall and in the mall i open a window i see a beautiful crystal blue water fall and there are beautiful white coloured water birds with long legs.. we are watching the water fall..and as I turn around to see My Husband, I find that He's already gone away..and the dream ended. Please advise the meaning of this dream. Jazakallah!

    Answer ID: 8928Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2282/1776=H/1429)

     

    It means that your husband enjoys divine favours in the life hereafter. However, you should keep on doing dua and isal-e-thawab for him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
