    Answer ID: 9877Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 66/37=H/1429)

     

    The best and safest way is that you marry that girl with the mediation of parents as soon as possible. Once Nikah is complete, talking, exchanging gifts etc will automatically be lawful. Also, you should study Bahishti Zewar and Fazail Aamal and read it out for others in house.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
