    Title: If a husband imagines that he is giving talaq 3 times to his wife, does that talaq occurs (valid)?

    Question: **URGENT please** Assalaamualikum, dear ulema ekram, if a husband imagines that he is giving talaq 3 times to his wife, does that talaq occurs (valid)?

    Answer ID: 27724Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1674/1674/M=1431)

    Talaq does not take place merely by imagination until one does not pronounce it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
