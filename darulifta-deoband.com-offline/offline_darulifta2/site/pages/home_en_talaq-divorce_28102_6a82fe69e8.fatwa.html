    Title: I am 30 years of age.i was married some 3 years ago.after few months of my marriage,for certain domestic problems,my brothers compelled me to divorce my wife.i did so{tripple talaq}.i was greatly shocked by doing this and decided not to marry for ever.one day,i utteered repeatedly,in anger,the following sentences........     Question: mufti shb!assalam o alaikum..i am 30 years of age.i was married some 3 years ago.after few months of my marriage,for certain domestic problems,my brothers compelled me to divorce my wife.i did so{tripple talaq}.i was greatly shocked by doing this and decided not to marry for ever.one day,i utteered repeatedly,in anger,the following sentences........ [kisi bhee namuhrem aurath{woman}ke saath jabhee me nikah karroon ya koi aur shakhs uskay saath meri nikah chahay jis tariqay se bhee karray,tu har baar wo aurath mujh par talaq ho.]Now would you,plz,guide me that whether islamic shariah allows me to marry,so that i may not do sins.thanks.

    Answer ID: 28102Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 177/157/B=1432)

    In the question mentioned above, the state of your nikah would be that if you marry any woman one talaq bayinah would take place. Then, you can marry the same woman again, but in future, you will have the option of only two talaqs.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
