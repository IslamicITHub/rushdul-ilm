    Question: Asalamikum, Hope u r fine by the grace of Almighty Allah mufti sahib, my question is my elder sister took khula nearly seven years back and she has a daughter everything was fine she did not marry again and from past some years she is in love with a person who is already married and has four children and some how his wife came to know about my sister and her husband relation and fought with him,and their husband and wife relation got spoiled they do not talk to each other.now the problem is that he cant marry my sister because this will not be tolerated by his wife or her family so he is telling my sister that without marriage we can be husband and wife as we cant marry openly.     Answer ID: 19301Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 341/47/TH=1431)

     

    It is unlawful and haram for both to love each other in this way, and no Nikah will take place without legal witnesses according to Shariah. If, according to Shariah, there is no hindrance, then ask the sister to live with him after valid Nikah, otherwise to keep away from him completely.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
