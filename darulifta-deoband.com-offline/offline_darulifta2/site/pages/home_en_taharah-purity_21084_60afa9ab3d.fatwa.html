    Title: I am having fibroid in my uterus and doctor asked me to do a vaginal ultrasound test. So, after the test do i need to take ghusl or just make wudhu and pray.

    Question: Assalamvalaikum, I am having fibroid in my uterus and doctor asked me to do a vaginal ultrasound test. So, after the test do i need to take ghusl or just make wudhu and pray. allah hafiz

    Answer ID: 21084Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 465/465/M=1431)

     

    If during the test the semen is discharged with lust, then ghusl shall be wajib, otherwise not.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
