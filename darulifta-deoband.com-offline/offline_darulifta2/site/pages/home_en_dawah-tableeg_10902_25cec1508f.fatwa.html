    Answer ID: 10902Posted on: Dec 18, 2023

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 349/240=L/1430)

     

    Since, in this age, when the followers of the Prophet (peace be upon him) exist in every country and Islam is known to all; therefore one will not be excused if he or she does not deliberate in it and accepts it, Allah will not forgive him/her. The Quran is very clear about it:

    ومن یتبع غیر الاسلام دینا فلن یقبل منہ

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
