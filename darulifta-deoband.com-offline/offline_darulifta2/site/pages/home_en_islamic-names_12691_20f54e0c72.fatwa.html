    Question: Assalmvaleku.. Mufti saheb I want to know Names meaning.... 1) Mohammed Muvaz 2) Mohammed Hasib M.

    Answer ID: 12691Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 752/723=B/1430)

     

    Mu?aaz means: One who is given refuge or protection, while Hasib means: sufficient or one who reckons.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
