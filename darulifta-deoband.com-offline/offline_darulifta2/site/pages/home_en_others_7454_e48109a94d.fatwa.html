    Answer ID: 7454Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1632/1401=B/1429)

     

    You suffer from stomach problems i.e. indigestion. Get a treatment. It is meaningless and nonsense dream. Such dreams come due to problems in stomach.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
