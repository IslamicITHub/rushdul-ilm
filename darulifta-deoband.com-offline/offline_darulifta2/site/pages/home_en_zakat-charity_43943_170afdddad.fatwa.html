    Title: Charity

    Question: I have 1000 Rs. per month charity which way is the best according to Quran was Sunnah spend on a poor boy going to school not madrasa. Or spend on a poor family medical treatment. Please guide me.

    Answer ID: 43943Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 358/260/D=1434) Give preference to most poor ones, or those who are relatives or neighbours and the pious ones among common Muslims.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
