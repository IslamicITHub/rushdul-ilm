    Title: Inheritance

    Question: Please guide me how to divide Rs10 lakhs among 8 brother and 3 sister?

    Answer ID: 65600Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 896/889/N=09/1437)

    If there are only 8 brothers and 3 sisters among the heir of any deceased person and he/she has left 10 lakh rupees then it shall be divided as per the below mentioned method:

    Each brother shall get 105363.15 while each sister shall get 52631.57 as a brother/son gets just double of what a sister/daughter gets.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
