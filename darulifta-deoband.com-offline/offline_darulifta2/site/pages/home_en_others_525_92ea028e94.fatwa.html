    Question ID: 525Country: Pakistan

    Title:

    Is it permissible to have a toilet positioned only 10 degrees away from the Qibla, or should it be adjusted further?

    Question: Assalaamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullaahi wa Barakaatuhu! I wanted to ask that we are making new home. And while constructing bathroom we have made an error. However that can be corrected at this point in time. Our commode and Indian WC is in only 10 degree difference with qibla. Is this degree permissible and if not how much degree is permissible to make Indian W.C or commode?  

    Answer ID: 525Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 556/J)

     

    Toilet should at least be 45 degree diverted from exact Qibla (Kaba direction); it is not allowed if it is less than it. Therefore, you should not keep your toilet at the existing position but get it diverted more than 45 degree from exact Qibla direction.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
