    Question ID: 1224Country: India

    Title: If someone has performed Umra one time. Is it compulsory for him/her to perform haj also? Waiting your reply according to Quran and Sunnah.

    Question: As sam o alaikum, If someone has performed Umra one time. Is it compulsory for him/her to perform haj also? Waiting your reply according to Quran and Sunnah. With regards!

    Answer ID: 1224Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 364=356/D)

     

    If Hujj was not obligatory on him and he went for Umrah in Hujj months (i.e. Shawwal, Zulqad and 8 days of Zulhijj) then Hujj will be obligatory on him, whether he performs it the same year or later.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
