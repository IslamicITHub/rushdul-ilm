    Question ID: 30704Country: Bangladesh

    Title: My sister on Sunday 6/2/2011 dream that she was praying salat while two other sunnoty dressed man was also praying salat.

    Question: My sister on Sunday 6/2/2011 dream that she was praying salat while two other sunnoty dressed man was also praying salat. after salat one of the two man told her that she is going to die after 18 days so she should pray more.please reply as my sister and my mother and all of the family members are in tension.

    Answer ID: 30704Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 685/446/H=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. It means that if you adopt simplicity in your dress and dedication in worship and follow the sunnah in each matter, you shall achieve a high status within a short span of time. Death means high status not real death.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
