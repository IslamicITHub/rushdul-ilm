    Answer ID: 12094Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 737/690=B/1430)

     

    Recite the du?a excessively in the morning and evening:

    بسم اللہ علی دیني ونفسي وولدي وأھلي ومالي، واللہ خیر حافظا وھو أرحم الراحمین

    In-sha-Allah, you will be protected from every kind of evil, trouble and fear of life and wealth.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
