    Title: Can a Muslim woman read Ayatul Kursi and the 4 Quls ( Kaafiroon , Ikhalas, Al -Falaq & An naas) while she is menstruating as a form of duaa for her protection and not as a form of tilaawat ? Please clarify.

    Question: Can a Muslim woman read Ayatul Kursi and the 4 Quls ( Kaafiroon , Ikhalas, Al -Falaq & An naas) while she is menstruating as a form of duaa for her protection and not as a form of tilaawat ? Please clarify.

    Answer ID: 24987Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1377/1377/M=1431)

     

    A menstruating woman can recite ayatul kursi and four Quls as dua. So is mentioned in the book of fiqh and fatawa.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
