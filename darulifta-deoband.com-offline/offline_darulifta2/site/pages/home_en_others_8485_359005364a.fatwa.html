    Answer ID: 8485Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1337/1131=L/1429)

     

    Most of dreams are satanic. Recite ayatul kursi and the four Quls (سورۃ الکافرون، الاخلاص، الفلق، الناس), blow on your body before going to bed.

    Please, give a bit detail of the dream 1 and 2.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
