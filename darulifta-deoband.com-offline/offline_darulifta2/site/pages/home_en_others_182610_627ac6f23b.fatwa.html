    Title: Is the dung of hen, duck and cow impure?

    Question: My question is that if the dung of hen, duck and cow (whether wet or dry) get on my clothes or body, are my clothes still pure or do I need to wash. Can I offer Salah with those same clothes without washing? Please explain. Please reply at the earliest. please...

    Answer ID: 182610Posted on: Sep 8, 2021

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 24/48/M=1/1443)

     

    The dung of hen, duck or cow is impure and Nijasat-e-Ghalizah (with severe impurity), whether it is dry or wet, and it is thick impurity so if it smears in clothes etc. the weight of four and half Masha or less then it may be permissible to offer salah without washing, but it is bad not to wash and continue praying in the same condition; therefore, it is better to wash. And if it is more than that, then it is not permissible to offer salah without washing it. (Akhtari Bahishti Zewar, vol 2)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
