    Title: barbar shop business

    Question: The barber shop business is halal or haram as barber has to cut the hair as per customer request may it is not as per Islamic rule (like man want the hair like woman). Also he has to cut the beard (dari) as shaving beard is against sunnah.

    Answer ID: 46464Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1163/1074/N=9/1434) It is haram and unlawful and major sin to shave the beard or keep it less than one fist. It is also not correct to cut the hair differently in length as per the English style, it is makrooh tahrimi. So the barber who avoids haram things in his profession like shaving beard etc his profession and income is lawful and one who does not avoid it and shaves beard or cuts it less than one fist then his profession is not lawful and the income which he derives by shaving the beard is not lawful and halal. It is mentioned in Tabyeen Al-Haqaiq (5:125): ولا يجوز على الغناء والنوح والملاهي لأن المعصية لا يتصور استحقاقها بالعقد فلا يجب عليه الأجر من غير أن يستحق هو على الأجير شيئا – إلى أن قال – وإن اعطاه الأجر وقبضه لا يحل له ويجب عليه رده على صاحبه اهـ.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
