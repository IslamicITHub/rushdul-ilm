    Title: If a Muqeem Muqtadi follows a Musafir Imam and after two rak'at Imam makes salam and Muqtadi also makes one salam.Thereafter realizes that error,stand up and complete the rest two rak'at.

    Question: If a Muqeem Muqtadi follows a Musafir Imam and after two rak'at Imam makes salam and Muqtadi also makes one salam.Thereafter realizes that error,stand up and complete the rest two rak'at. Does Muqtadi need to make sajda-e-sahw as he is a Laahiq .

    Answer ID: 32525Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 876/723/B=1432) Yes, in the question mentioned above the muqim muqtadi should do sajdah sahv in the last. He has the status of lahiq as well as masbuq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
