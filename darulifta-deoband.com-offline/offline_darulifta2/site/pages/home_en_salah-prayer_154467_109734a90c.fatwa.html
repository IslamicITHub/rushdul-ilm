    Title: Salat Al-Kusoof (Prayer of the Solar Eclipse)

    Question: According to the opinion of Imam Abu Hanifah Salat Al-Kusoof is read quietly in congregation. While Imam Abu Yusuf and Imam Muhammad hold the view that Salat Al-Kusoof is read out loud in congregation. As I am a follower of the Hanafi school of thought my question is which opinion should i follow? And why? Please provide dalail (proofs) and references.

    Answer ID: 154467Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1498/1496/L=1/1439)

     

    According to Fatawa Hindia, the opinion of Imam Abu Hanifah (may Allah have mercy upon him) will be preferred, thus you should follow him.

    ولا يجهر بالقراءة فى صلاة الجماعة فى صلاة كسوف الشمس فى قول أبى حنيفة رحمه الله كذا في المحيط والصحيح قوله . (الهندية: 1/152)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
