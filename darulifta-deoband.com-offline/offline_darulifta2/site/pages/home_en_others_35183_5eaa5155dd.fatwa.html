    Title: My question is regarding a dream which my wife saw sometime back

    Question: My question is regarding a dream which my wife saw sometime back. In the dream she sees that she has got a job of cleaning toilets in the vicinity of Kabah. The toilets are already clean and she is further cleaning them with pure water. In the dream she feels that although it is a bad job but since it is nearby Kabah she likes it.

    Answer ID: 35183Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2246/1293/H=1432) Ma-sha-Allah, it is very nice dream. The meaning is that your wife shall be blessed with the barakah of wahi. In-sha-Allah, she shall get the taufiq of sunnah. Ask her to study the book Bahishti Zewar, Fazail Aamal, Muntakhab Ahadith extensively. Also make the arrangement of reading and listening to it in your house among the women.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
