    Answer ID: 12573Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 699/525=L/1430)

     

    These dreams seem satanic. Recite Surah the four Quls (Surah Kafiroon, Ikhlas, Falaq and Nass) and Ayatul Kursi and blow on body before going to bed. In-sha-Allah, you will not see such dreams.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
