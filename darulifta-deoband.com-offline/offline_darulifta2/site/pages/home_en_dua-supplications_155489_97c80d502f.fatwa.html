    Title: Please let me know wazifah, dua for a suitable job for my son who is unemployed.

    Question: Please let me know wazifah, dua for a suitable job for my son who is unemployed.

    Answer ID: 155489Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 94/91/SD=2/1439)

     

    After Maghrib salah recite Surah al-Waqia regularly. Also read Ya Mughni (يا مغني) 1111 times after Isha salah with durood 11-11 times before and after. Also read the following dua after fardh salah regularly:

     

    اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
