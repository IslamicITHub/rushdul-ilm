    Answer ID: 3535Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 291/294=L)

     

    The cause of anger from parents about your wife mentioned by you is not reasonable; therefore it is not necessary to divorce wife obeying the parents. Yes, if they ask to divorce for some genuine Sharai reason then it is necessary to obey them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
