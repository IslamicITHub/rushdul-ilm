    Title: Aqiqah for a Child Born in Saudi Arabia: Can It Be Performed in India?

    Question: If the child is born in Saudi and his parents want to do his aqiqah in India, is it possible to do this or the aqiqah should be done in Saudi where the child was born?

    Answer ID: 172732Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1265/1092/D=12/1440)

     

    It is not necessary to do aqiqah in the birthplace hence you may also do the aqiqah in India as well. (Fatawa Darul Uloom Deoband, vol. 15/619)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
