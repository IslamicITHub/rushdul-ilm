    Title: THE ZAKAT

    Question: For those persons/madarsas/masjids/ any other organisation we can donate the zakah.

    Answer ID: 51838Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 688/688/M=06/1435) It is not lawful to give zakah money in a mosque. However, zakah may be given in a madrasas which provides food and lodge to needy and poor students. You may also give zakah to the poor and needy persons and to those who are eligible for zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
