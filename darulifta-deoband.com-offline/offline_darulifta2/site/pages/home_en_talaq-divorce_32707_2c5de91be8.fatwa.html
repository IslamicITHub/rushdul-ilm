    Question ID: 32707Country: India

    Title: KULA

    Question: Is it possible when the women has told kula and same time the man also agreed and told thalaq in this case wheter she can apply for maintenance according to the shariyad law? (she leaved with the husband's family for 15 days only and she also reback the jehaz articles at the period through the police)

    Answer ID: 32707Posted on: Aug 28, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1148/1148/M=1432) In the question mentioned above, the woman can demand for maintenance of her iddah period provided no defiance is found from her side.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
