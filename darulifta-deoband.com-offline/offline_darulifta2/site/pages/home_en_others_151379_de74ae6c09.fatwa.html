    Title: I want to know that the gift sent by bride father as a Eid Teohari (like dry fruits, sewayi etc) exist some sunnat with this. Is it okay to send teohari?

    Question: I want to know that the gift sent by bride father as a Eid Teohari (like dry fruits, sewayi etc) exist some sunnat with this. Is it okay to send teohari?

    Answer ID: 151379Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1061/1069/M=9/1438)

     

    It is not a sunnah to send gift (dry fruits Siwaiyan etc) in the name of Eidi or Teohari to the place of bride’s in-laws and it is wrong to think it mandatory and otherwise considering a cause of curse and complain.  It is not good to give gifts under the pressure of customs. Yes, if the gifts are given and taken happily without the pressure of customs and society then not only it is lawful rather good and desired and it is lawful without any karahat. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
