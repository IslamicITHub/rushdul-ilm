    Title: Having sex in my dream

    Question: In my dream I see myself having sex with my sister which I do not like. I feel very guilty when I see this type of dream so please help me out why I see this type of dream. And what does this dream means.

    Answer ID: 48841Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1587/1236/B=11/1434) The dream indicates that your sister shall gain any religious and financial benefit from you. You should recite اعوذ بالله بسم الله and then four Quls (سورۃ الکافرون، سورۃ الاخلاص، سورۃ الفلق، سورۃ الناس) and blow on your palms and pass them over your body as long as it reaches. Do it thrice. Then recite Ayatul Kursi and go to sleep. Also recite the following dua: رب اعوذبك من ھمزات الشياطين واعوذبك من أن يحضرون.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
