    Question ID: 148223Country: india

    Title: I am chartered accountancy student CA in practice mainly involved in tax matters which require client to pay bribes to tax officers. CA may pay it and get reimburse from client. Is it halal or haram? And to what extent? The entire fee charged or bribe alone?

    Question: I am chartered accountancy student CA in practice mainly involved in tax matters which require client to pay bribes to tax officers. CA may pay it and get reimburse from client. Is it halal or haram? And to what extent? The entire fee charged or bribe alone?

    Answer ID: 148223Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 341/255/SD=4/1438)

    It is not lawful for CA officers to take bribe from the customers. They may only take their due fee. In the question mentioned above you may practice tax related matters but after you become an officer it shall be lawful for you only to take your due fee, it shall not be lawful for you to take bribe.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
