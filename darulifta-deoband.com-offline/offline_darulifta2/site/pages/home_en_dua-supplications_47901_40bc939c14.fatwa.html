    Question ID: 47901Country: India

    Title: Dua supplication

    Question: Please suggest some dua for my three and half year old son. He cannot speak clearly, he speaks everything but not clearly (tutlata hai). And he is very short tempered, having bad manners (bado par hath uthana, zid kar na etc) 4 month before we lost our 6 years old elder son, he missed him very much please suggest some dua for him n for us also.

    Answer ID: 47901Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1365/1038/B=10/1434) Recite the following ayat and blow on water and give it to your child: قَالَ رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
