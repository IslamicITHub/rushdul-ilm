    Title: What is the Sunnah length of keeping one's hair? What is the Sunnah method of combing one's hair? What is the Sunnah method of applying oil to one's hair? What type of oil is Sunnah to be applied to one's hair?

    Question: What is the Sunnah length of keeping one's hair? What is the Sunnah method of combing one's hair? What is the Sunnah method of applying oil to one's hair? What type of oil is Sunnah to be applied to one's hair?

    Answer ID: 8362Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2103/1650=H/1429)

     

    It is sunnah to keep hair up to the earlobe.

    One should start combing from right side then from left side. One should apply oil in this way.

    Whatever oil whether olive or mustard can be applied, it will suffice for sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
