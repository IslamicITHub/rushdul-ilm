    Title: During the swan (roza) time can give 10ml blood to HIV test.

    Question: assalam o alekum.During the swan (roza) time can give 10ml blood to HIV test.

    Answer ID: 25909Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1496/1496/M=1431)

    Blood donation does not nullify the fast. One can give blood at the time of need and compulsion.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
