    Title: ABOUT NIKAH

    Question: Can a boy marry with his uncle's (real chacha and real mamu) daughter?

    Answer ID: 52623Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 727/561/D=06/1435) If there is no relation of fostering (i.e. they are not foster brother and sister) then it is lawful to marry the daughter of real chacha or mamu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
