    Question: As-Salamu Alaikum Wbr, My sister married 2 months back. His Mother in law always scolding. each time they hurt each and every moment. My Sister husband does not support. 90% he is not support 10% support. 10 minutes or 1 days he/his mother talks/behave well after a minutes or day he/she shouted her. she keep calm. 2 days before problem goes like that she trying to suicide. any solution for this quran and hadith?

    Answer ID: 2810Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 142/D=17/K)

     

    She should tolerate her scolding considering her as mother and should always treat her with love, respect and good morals, in-Sha-Allah by the virtue of good morals and politeness the mother-in-law's heart will be softened and your sister also shall get its reward. The Quran says: "the good deed and the evil deed cannot be equal. Repel (the evil) with one which is better, then Verily! He, between whom and you there was enmity, (will become) as though He was a close friend." (Al-Quran, Surah Haa Meem Sajdah 41/34)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
