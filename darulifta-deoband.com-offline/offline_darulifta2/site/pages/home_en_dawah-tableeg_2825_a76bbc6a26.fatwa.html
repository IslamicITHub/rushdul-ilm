    Title: Please guide me and the other viewers as to spending Seh Roza, or Chehla or 4 months or One Year or 7 months in the path of Allah are thought UNISLAMIC in Tableegh by some of the brethern? Are they right? Or are they wrong? Please enlighten me with a detailed answer.

    Question: Dear Ulema-e-Deoband! AoA.....Please guide me and the other viewers as to spending Seh Roza, or Chehla or 4 months or One Year or 7 months in the path of Allah are thought UNISLAMIC in Tableegh by some of the brethern? Are they right? Or are they wrong? Please enlighten me with a detailed answer.

    Answer ID: 2825Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 207/197=B)

     

    It is un-Islamic to regard going to tabligh for these durations as un-Islamic, what they say is wrong. Actually, going in tabligh is for personal and social reform. It aims at gaining strength in religion, acting upon the commands of Allah and his Messenger and making efforts for reform and success of all Muslims rather entire humanity. Are all these matters un-Islamic? May Allah grant healthy brain and right understanding, amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
