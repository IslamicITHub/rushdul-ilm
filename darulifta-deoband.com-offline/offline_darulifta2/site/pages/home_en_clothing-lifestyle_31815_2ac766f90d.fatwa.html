    Title: Application of Surma

    Question: Is wearing Surma by males sunnah, what is the hadees?

    Answer ID: 31815Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 919/919/M=1432) It was the practice of the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) that he used to apply kohl (surma) three times in each eye before going to sleep. So, we should follow this sunnah. ان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم كانت له محكلة يكتحل منها عند النوم ثلثا في كل عين (شمائل ترمذى).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
