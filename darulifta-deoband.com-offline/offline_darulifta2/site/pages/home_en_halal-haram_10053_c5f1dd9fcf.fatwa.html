    Title: Salam I was going through some magazines. I found one interesting question. A muslim boy asked that he has an audio cd of quran and its translation, and he want to install it on his computer, so his question was can he install quran cd on his laptop or not? Please answer this question.

    Question: Salam I was going through some magazines. I found one interesting question. A muslim boy asked that he has an audio cd of quran and its translation, and he want to install it on his computer, so his question was can he install quran cd on his laptop or not? Please answer this question.

    Answer ID: 10053Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 122/122=M/1429)

     

    It is allowable to install it with good intention provided there is no sound of woman and no desecration of the Quran, otherwise not.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
