    Title: Removing hair of a new born baby

    Question: (1) After removing hair from the baby boy’s head on 7th day after birth should we apply saffron on his head? And what is the reason to apply it? (2) Is it allowed to put saffron on baby girl’s head?

    Answer ID: 45088Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 645/505/H=1434) It is mustahab to apply saffron on the seventh day of the birth after shaving the head, whether it is a boy or a girl, the head should be shaved on the seventh day of birth and the saffron should be applied over it. If you apply any other aromatic oil then it is also correct.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
