    Title: Fear of being infertile

    Question: I am almost near to the age of getting married. I always get waswasah of not being able to bear children. Please guide me how to act to these Islamically. And what should I do to get a pious partner and nek offspring. Please make dua for me.

    Answer ID: 155171Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 56/49/B=1/1439)

     

    Solemnize your nikah considering it sunnah. Begetting a child is not your task, it is Allah’s task, He can give child if He wishes from the semen of anyone. Keep on reading Ya Latif (یا لطیف)and Ya Jame (یا جامع) extensively.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
