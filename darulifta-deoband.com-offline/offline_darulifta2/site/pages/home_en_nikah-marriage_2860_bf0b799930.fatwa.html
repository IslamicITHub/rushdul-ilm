    Title: My bhabi was in love with her relative, but her parents force her to marry with my brother. She signed for marraige unweelingly, now they have one child, do their marraige is valid? if no then what to do now?

    Question: My bhabi was in love with her relative, but her parents force her to marry with my brother. She signed for marraige unweelingly, now they have one child, do their marraige is valid? if no then what to do now?

    Answer ID: 2860Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 119/120=L)

     

    If your bhabhi kept silent when her father sought her permission at the time of nikah and she signed the nikahnama (marriage paper) without rejecting it verbally then the nikah was valid, now there is no need to renew the nikah:

    فسکتت عن ردہ مختارۃ، و فی الشامی: اما لو اخذھا عطاس او سعال حین اخبرت فلما ذھب قالت: لا ارضی او اخذ فمھا ثم ترک فقالت: ذلک صح ردھا لان سکوتھا کان عن اضطرار (الشامی: 4/160)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
