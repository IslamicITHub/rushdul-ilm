    Title: I had an issue can you please guide me I and my manager was on business tour for 2 days from Riyadh to Jeddah. When we are going back from Jeddah (still we are in Jeddah) we both dint pray magrib salah ,we reached airport(Jeddah) we heard Isha salah azaan so which prayer we have to pray first.

    Question: Assalamualikum, I had an issue can you please guide me I and my manager was on business tour for 2 days from Riyadh to Jeddah. When we are going back from Jeddah (still we are in Jeddah) we both dint pray magrib salah ,we reached airport(Jeddah) we heard Isha salah azaan so which prayer we have to pray first. Thanks!

    Answer ID: 19132Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 151/137/D=1431)

     

    When you were sahib tartib (i.e. you have no salah left to be performed after puberty), you should first perform Maghrib and then Isha. But, if you are not sahib tartib you can perform Isha timely during journey and can delay performing Maghrib until you return to your place.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
