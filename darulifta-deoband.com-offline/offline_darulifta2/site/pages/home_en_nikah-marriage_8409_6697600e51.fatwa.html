    Title: Is it ok for a girl whose both parents are syed to marry a guy who isnt a syed when everything else is ok or should the parents wait to see if they find a syed boy?

    Question: is it ok for a girl whose both parents are syed to marry a guy who isnt a syed when everything else is ok or should the parents wait to see if they find a syed boy?

    Answer ID: 8409Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1305/1120=L/1429)

     

    If the wali (guardian) of the girl is ready to marry her with any non-syed boy, then he can do it. However, it is better that she is married with a syed boy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
