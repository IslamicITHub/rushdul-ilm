    Answer ID: 5423Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 976/976=H)

     

    The meaning of the dream is that you and your brother will get benefit while struggling in religious deeds. And if you face some troubles, they also will soon vanish away, In-sha-Allah. In the dream, there is a forewarning that the majority of those visiting graves of pious men do not have religious objectives i.e. they do not observe the Islamic instructions and rules. May Allah Almighty keep you and us from all kinds of abominations! Amen

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
