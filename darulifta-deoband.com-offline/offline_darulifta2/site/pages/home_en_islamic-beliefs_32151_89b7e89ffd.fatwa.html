    Title: I get lot of wasawis during namaz like does allah really help us in getting a new job, or like does HUzur (SAW) had been to meraj, or Huzur (SAW) has seen allah etc during namaz and forcing me to apply my logic,

    Question: I get lot of wasawis during namaz like does allah really help us in getting a new job, or like does HUzur (SAW) had been to meraj, or Huzur (SAW) has seen allah etc during namaz and forcing me to apply my logic,,,, whereas i really believe in these things cent percent but suddenly shaitan does make me to think like these issues so i break my namaz and i'll do tauba (astaghfar) and i'll recite kalima and then again i'll start namaz from the beginning,,, pls help me out... thank you,,, may Allah (SWT) bless us all.

    Answer ID: 32151Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1007/638/H=1432) It is not bad if one had feelings during salah, rather it is bad to recall them. Hence, you need not to break your salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
