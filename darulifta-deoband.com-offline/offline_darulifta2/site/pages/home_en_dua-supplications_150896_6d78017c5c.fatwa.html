    Title: Please send me safe pregnancy duas or wazifah.

    Question: Please send me safe pregnancy duas or wazifah.

    Answer ID: 150896Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 868/827/B=9/1438)

     

    Keep on reciting the following dua extensively:

    فَاللّهُ خَيْرٌ حَافِظاً وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ .

    There is no number or time fixed for it, you may read it when you wish and you may read it as many times as you wish. In-shaAllah, you shall have a safe pregnancy.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
