    Title: Dream Meaning

    Question: I saw in dream that I am flying and behind me 4-5 white colour birds are flying and saying to me say our Salam to Allah ( Allah ko humara Salam kehyega ). Please tell me the meaning of this dream.

    Answer ID: 155392Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 131/117/B=2/1439)

     

    The dream tells that you will be blessed with a pious child.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
