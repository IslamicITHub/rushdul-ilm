    if we have any knowledge like that one women of muslim indulge in prostitution and her father compelled her . allah give me power i can prevent this what should i do left her on allah or try to stop it and compelled her parents to marry her anywhere.

    Question: if we have any knowledge like that one women of muslim indulge in prostitution and her father compelled her . allah give me power i can prevent this what should i do left her on allah or try to stop it and compelled her parents to marry her anywhere.

    Answer ID: 9215Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1213/1213=M/1429)

     

    You must stop as per your capacity. Also, convey her parents that prostitution is haram and a major sin in Islam; therefore they should get her married as soon as possible. Also, give them possible help to get her married. If, by your efforts, she forsakes this sin you shall get its full reward.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
