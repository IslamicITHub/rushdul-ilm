    Title: Bismillah

    Question: The number 786 is generally used for Bismillah. Is there any other religion which uses this number also?

    Answer ID: 43835Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 187/147/H=1434) (1) As per “ابجد” this number (786) is of بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم.These numbers indicate towards tasmiya. They are not used for tasmiya. (2) We do not know whether it is used in other religion or not.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
