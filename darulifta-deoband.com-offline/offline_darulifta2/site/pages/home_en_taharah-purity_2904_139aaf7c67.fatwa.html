    Title: I use a tissue paper for toilet.i mean for peshab and pakhana.as i wam working in a company and there they have english tiolets.so what to do in such cases. please give a brief idea about it .

    Question: I use a tissue paper for toilet.i mean for peshab and pakhana.as i wam working in a company and there they have english tiolets.so what to do in such cases. please give a brief idea about it .

    Answer ID: 2904Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 100/97=J)

    Tissue paper can be used in toilets either after urination and stool. After using tissue paper (without using water) one can offer salah. But, if the impurity spreads on the area other than makhraj (anus) then using tissue paper only will not suffice and one has to use water as well. Yes, in case one does not find water to use then he can offer salah after using tissue paper:

    ویوخذ منھا عدم الکراھۃ فیما لایصلح لھا (ای الکتابۃ) اذا کان قالعاً للنجاسۃ غیر متقوم، و قال ای یفرض غسلہ ان جاوز المخرج نجس مائع (الشامي: 1/550, 552)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
