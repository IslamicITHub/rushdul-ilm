    Question ID: 6820Country: Pakistan

    Title: Now, i need to know that is the usage of CONDOM is allowed in ISLAM at time time of intercourse with wife because me and my future wife will not let any one know of our intercourse relationship before rukhsati (not even our parents). Please send me the answer ASAP and put some detail also, if required.

    Question: Assalam o Alaikum, I have read your reply against my question: Answer: 6433 27 Jul, 2008 (Fatwa: 763/711=L/1429) Now, i need to know that is the usage of CONDOM is allowed in ISLAM at time time of intercourse with wife because me and my future wife will not let any one know of our intercourse relationship before rukhsati (not even our parents). Please send me the answer ASAP and put some detail also, if required. Jazak Allah Khair!

    Answer ID: 6820Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1001/890=L/1429)

     

    When the Nikah is known to all, then there is no wrong if your wife gets pregnant. The excuse put by you is not enough for using condom. Therefore, it will be makrooh to use condom in the abovementioned case.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
