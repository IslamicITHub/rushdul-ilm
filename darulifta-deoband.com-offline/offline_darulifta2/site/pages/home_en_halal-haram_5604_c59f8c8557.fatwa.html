    Answer ID: 5604Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 791/682=B-1429)

     

    Television is an instrument of entertainment and sport. There are presented nude and arousing pictures and indecent matters. The programmes screened over there go absolutely against the Islamic Shariah. Today, television is the sole factor behind the evils prevailing in the society. Therefore, due to all these evils, watching television is unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
