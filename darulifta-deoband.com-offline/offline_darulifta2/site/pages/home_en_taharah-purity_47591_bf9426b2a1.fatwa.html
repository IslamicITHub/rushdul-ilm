    Title: Taharah

    Question: I want to ask that I am suffering from madhi discharge all time 24 hour after washing it. Please tell me how can I offer namaz. It is a disease due to penis weakness. Please give home remedy, Islamic solution and medical solution. Am I a Sharai mazur? Please reply in detail. I have beard so I think it is not good for me to go to a doctor for this. He will think wrong about Islam.

    Answer ID: 47591Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1332/1006/B=10/1434) If you do not have time to an extent in which you could make wudhu and perform salah with wudhu then you are ma’zur (excused person) as per the Shariah. You should make wudhu after the time of salah starts and offer salah; you may offer salah though madhi keeps on coming out. Your salah shall be valid in this case as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
