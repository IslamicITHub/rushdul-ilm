    Title: My question is if your not married anymore do I need a wahlkil to get married and if I do how would I go about it?

    Question: My question is if your not married anymore do I need a wahlkil to get married and if I do how would I go about it?

    Answer ID: 3790Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 47/47=M)

     

    If the boy and girl who are to marry are present in the meeting of nikah then there is no need of appointing a wakeel. Yes, in case any of the two is not present in the meeting of nikah due to any reason then he or she has to appoint a wakeel who will either put offer of nikah or will accept it; since the offer of nikah and its acceptance is to be made in single meeting.

     

    A wakeel of nikah should be one who is trusted. While appointing him wakeel you should say: "Get me married to that person." If he accepts this offer he will be your wakeel. Generally, the qazi becomes wakeel from girl who acts with permission of the girl or her wali (guardian).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
