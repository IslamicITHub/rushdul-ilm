    Title: please suggest me any dua as my wife doesnt perform namaz

    Question: Assalamualykum, please suggest me any dua as my wife doesnt perform namaz so i can recite that dua for her to be regular in namaz. Thanks

    Answer ID: 19300Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 158/138/L=1431)

     

    Recite the below-mentioned dua excessively:

    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

    Start reading Fazail Aamal in the house, in-shaAllah, she will soon begin performing salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
