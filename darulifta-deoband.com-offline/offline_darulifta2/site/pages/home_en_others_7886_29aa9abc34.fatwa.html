    Question: Assalamualaikkum. I dreamt it on Morning hours when I was sleeping after fajar namaz in Ramadhan. Dreamt "I was engaged in sodomy. Because of that they opposite person with whom i done the sin is taking all my money. He left. Then he returned and again when he tried to take money from my home I beaten him and very crudely he left Means he left starring at me" Pls suggest me the meaning of the same. Wassalam

    Answer ID: 7886Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1603/1522=D/1429)

     

    One is not answerable for what one sees or does in dream. The dream denotes that this wicked act may destroy one?s reputation, wealth etc; therefore one should shun from such bad acts when he is awake.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
