    Title: Question for umrah

    Question: My nephew is working in Makkah; he can do Umrah for me? Alhamdulillah, I already performed Hajj and Umrah several times.

    Answer ID: 47976Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1259/1259/M=11/1434) Your bhatija can perform Umrah for you. In-shaAllah, you shall get its reward.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
