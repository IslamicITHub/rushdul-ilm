    Question: Is gutkha or masala haram in Islam?

    Answer ID: 36286Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 236/236/M=1433) If it is injurious to health then it is unlawful to be consumed.
