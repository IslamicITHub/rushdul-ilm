    Title: What are the rulings of having walima? How soon after the meeting of the bride & groom should walima be held?

    Question: What are the rulings of having walima? How soon after the meeting of the bride & groom should walima be held?

    Answer ID: 431Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 191/L)

     

    (1) When husband and wife meet first time, it is recommended for husband to invite his relatives and friends to a feast (walima) as per his capacity.

     

    (2) The walima can be held in the morning or the other day. Inviting people to a feast after that will not be called as walima:

    ولیمة العرس سنة و فیھا مثوبة عظیمة وھی اذا بنی الرجل بامرأتہ ینبغي ان یدعو الجیران والاقرباء الی قولہ ولاباس بان یدعوا یومئذ من الغد و بعد الغد ثم ینقطع العرس و الولیمة (عالمگیري: 5/343)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
