We don?t sell any liqueur or lottery. The mother company keeps pushing the owner to sell liqueur or he would loose the store.     Answer ID: 27769Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2022/D=429/k=1431)

    As it is haram to brew wine and give someone to drink in the same way, selling wine is haram as well. The holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) has cursed all these people. Hence, if your manager has firm intention not to sell it, then you can work with him and there is no need to leave the job. And if you fear that his intention might change, then you should intend to leave the job and should not think what he will think about you. It is must for you to save your iman. 

    Also when the inspection is done the manager should damage it because it is not lawful to take its price.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
