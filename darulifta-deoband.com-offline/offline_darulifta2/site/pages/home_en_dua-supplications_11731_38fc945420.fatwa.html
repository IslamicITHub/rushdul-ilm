    Question: 1) while doing parda is it necessary to cover the face or hijab is also ok? 2) tell me a tasbi or dua for easy delivery of a child. 3) while offering prayers is it necessary for a women to join feet while standing or there should be some gap in between? 4) for halala what are the conditions? is it necessary to have sex with the new husband and stay with him for a month or an year?

    Answer ID: 11731Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 539/539=M/1430)

     

    (1) While doing purdah, a woman should cover her face too.

    (2) Recite the below mentioned dua on jaggery and give her to eat, or write on paper, get it wrapped in a peace of cloth and tie it in the left thigh of woman, and untie it once it is done. In-shaAllah, it will cause easiness.

    بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم،  إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انْشَقَّتْ وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ وَإِذَا الأرْضُ مُدَّتْ وَأَلْقَتْ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتْ وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ، اھیاً اِشراھیاً، اللھم سھل علیھا الولادۃ،  فَقَدَّرَهُ، ثُمَّ السَّبِيلَ يَسَّرَهُ

    (3) There should be distance of four fingers between the feet.

    (4) In Shariah compliant halalah, it is necessary that the new husband has intercourse, but it is not necessary to live with him for a month or for year. The correct way of halalah is that the divorced woman marries a man, and her new husband has intercourse with her and gives her Talaq on his own wish or he dies. In both the cases, she can marry her former husband after sitting in iddah period.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
