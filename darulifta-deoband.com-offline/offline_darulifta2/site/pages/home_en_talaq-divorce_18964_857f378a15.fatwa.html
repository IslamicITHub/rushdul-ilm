Pls, give us fatwa is talaq taken place if 1) said in extreme anger without any control on himself 2) As per Quran on one occassion if 3talaq said and does they count one or three and her wife is no more halal for him pls., give your valuable feed back - also let us know if we can reconisle

    Answer ID: 18964Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 299/223/L=1431)

     

    (1)    Talaq given in anger takes place as well.

    (2)    Three talaqs of single sitting shall be counted three.

    قال تعالى: الطلاق مرتان [إلى قوله تعالى] فَإِن طَلَّقَهَا فَلاَ تَحِلُّ لَهُ مِن بَعْدُ حَتَّىَ تَنكِحَ زَوْجاً غَيْرَهُ  [الآية] .

    (3) Now the wife of your friend cannot be halal for him without a legal Halala.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
