    Answer ID: 23563Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1376/1016/H=1431)

    Recite the following ayah after each salah 3 times with durood 3-3 times before and after, and supplicate Almighty Allah: O Allah, arrange for me pious wife. Amen!

    رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً [الفرقان : 74]

    Also repeat the same before going to bed. Sleep in the state of wudhu and according to sunnah and follow sunnah in each and every deed. May you attain firmness in religion, may Almighty Allah save you from all harms and bless you with pious wife as soon as possible.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
