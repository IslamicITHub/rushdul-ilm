    Answer ID: 3492Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 403/358=D)

     

    Human being is not answerable for acts he commits in a state of sleeping. But, the thought that occupy ones mind in awakening state they are reflected in sleeps. Therefore, first of all you should shun from such thoughts and promise not to let them in, and in case such thoughts creep in you should repent to Allah and cast away it from your mind. Pray the below dua with Ayatul Kursi:

    أعوذ بکلمات اللہ التامات من غضبہ و عقابہ و شر عبادہ و من ھمزات الشیاطین و أن یحضرون

    And, recite isteghfar (استغفار) and change the side in case such thought comes in your mind while going to sleep. Discard such thinking when you find yourself doing the movement. Try to overcome this habit, in-sha-Allah this it will go away.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
