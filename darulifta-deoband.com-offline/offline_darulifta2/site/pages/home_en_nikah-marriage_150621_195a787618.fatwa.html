    Title: Marriage with chachi's daughter

    Question: I want to ask a question about milk feeding and nikah. My mother fed milk to my big cousin brother and my aunt also fed milk to my big brother but I am 3rd number after my brother who feed milk of aunt I want nikah with my cousin (name not mentioned)who is 4th number after my cousin brother who fed milk of my mother now I want fatwa may I nikah with my cousin(name not mentioned) or not.

    Answer ID: 150621Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 932/852/SN=8/1438)

    In the question mentioned above you may marry that daughter of your chachi, there is no issue according to the Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
