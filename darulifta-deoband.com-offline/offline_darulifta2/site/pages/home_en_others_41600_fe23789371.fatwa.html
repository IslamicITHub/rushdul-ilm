    Title: Dream

    Question: An Alim lives near me. We are quiet friendly with each other. Once I intended to go for Hajj with him, I had dreamed his beard is short. On another occasion I had a dream, he held his hand over my head but I slipped away. What does this mean?

    Answer ID: 41600Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1488/1115/D=1433) If that Alim follows sunnah and Shariah completely then you should think good about him and you too should follow sunnah completely. You can go for Hajj with him and should not pay any attention towards the dream.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
