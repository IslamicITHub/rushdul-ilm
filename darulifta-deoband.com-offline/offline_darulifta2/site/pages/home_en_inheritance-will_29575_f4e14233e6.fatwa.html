    Question ID: 29575Country: Canada

    Title: My late husband is survived by wife, two daughters and no son. also two full brothers and one step sister( same father different mother). kindly advice on shares of each.

    Question: My late husband is survived by wife, two daughters and no son. also two full brothers and one step sister( same father different mother). kindly advice on shares of each.

    Answer ID: 29575Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 210/180/L=1432)

    In the question mentioned above, all the property of your deceased husband after due rights preceding inheritance, shall be divided into 48 shares. The wife shall get 6 shares, each daughter 16-16 shares, and each real brother 5-5 shares, while the step sister shall get no share.

    Detail:
    Wife: 1x6 06
    Daughter: 2x16 = 32
    Real brother: 2x5= 10
    Total: 48 Shares

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
