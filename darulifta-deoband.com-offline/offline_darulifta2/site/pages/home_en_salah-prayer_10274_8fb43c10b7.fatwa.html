    Title: In lieu of below fatwa we have asked the same two months earlier with Moulana/Mufti Anees Sb of chouhan bangar,Jafrabad & he told that " Ise Momool na banaye lekin jamat e sani agar kabhi kabhi karni ho to kar sakte hai, aur uske liye main mussallah chor kar kahi bhi jamat e sani kar sakte hai aur yeh jamat bina Ehqamat ke karni hogi, Imam bina takbeer ke niyat bandh le.     Answer ID: 10274Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 301/204=L/1430)

     

    According to Hanafi Fiqh, any mosque where five times regular prayers are offered, it is makrooh to hold second jamaat therein. If second jamaat is held with azan and iqamah, then the three imams (Imam Azam, Abu Yusuf and Muhammad) agree that it is makrooh tahrimi (to an extent of haram). If the azan and iqamah is not repeated and the salah is offered away from mihrab, then Imam Abu Yusuf regards it lawful and Imam Abu Hanifah also regards it makrooh only (not tahrimi). (Kifayatul Mufti 3:148)

    The way prescribed by Mufti Anees Sahib is not free from being makrooh; therefore the remaining people should offer salah individually if they want to offer there. If there is any shop, house or open place outside the main mosque where jamaat is possible then it can be done there, as it is mentioned there.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
