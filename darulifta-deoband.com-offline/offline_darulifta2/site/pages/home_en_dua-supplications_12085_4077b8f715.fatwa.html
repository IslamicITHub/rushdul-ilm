    Title: as salaam muftisaheb my finance is very poor so please give me dua or zikhar for me and please do dua for me thankh you!

    Question: as salaam muftisaheb my finance is very poor so please give me dua or zikhar for me and please do dua for me thankh you!

    Answer ID: 12085Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 602/602=M/1430)

     

    Recite ya Mughni (یا مغني) after Isha Salah 1100 times with durood 11 times before and after. It is a useful dua for prosperity and expansion in livelihood resources. Your fiancé should recite it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
