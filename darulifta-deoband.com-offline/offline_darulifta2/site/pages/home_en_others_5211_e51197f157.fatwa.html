    Title: I saw a dream that i was climbing my grand mas home to up stairs with a huge mango, there i saw a monkey sitting on the upstairs and it frightened e then i gave my mango to it and then the monkey started with bismillah and started reciting the quran. whats all these mean?

    Question: assalamualikum, I saw a dream that i was climbing my grand mas home to up stairs with a huge mango, there i saw a monkey sitting on the upstairs and it frightened e then i gave my mango to it and then the monkey started with bismillah and started reciting the quran. whats all these mean?

    Answer ID: 5211Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1111/836=H/1429)

     

    You are slacking in fulfilling the rights pertaining to Allah and His creature. In the dream, your attention was diverted specially to the same.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
