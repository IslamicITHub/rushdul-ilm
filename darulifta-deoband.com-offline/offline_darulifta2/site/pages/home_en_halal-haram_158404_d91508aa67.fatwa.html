    Title: Job in IT company

    Question: I work in an IT company. The nature of work of my company is to provide and support different products to Bank globally. I am working on one of the products which is used to transfer money online. My work involves testing of the application which is used by banks (not Islamic of course). I would like to know, is my earning and work halal?

    Answer ID: 158404Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 573/540/M=6/1439)

     

    If your task in the above mentioned company does not involve writing and calculating interest and interest based dealing then your job and income shall not be termed haram and unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
