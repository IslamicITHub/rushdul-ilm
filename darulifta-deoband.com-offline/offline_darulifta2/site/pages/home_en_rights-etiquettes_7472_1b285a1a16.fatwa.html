    Title: I have two questions. 1. If any non-muslim greet me with Asslaamuleyikum, Shall i reply him/her with Waaleyikumussalaam??? What does quraan and our shariaah says about this. 2. If any non-muslim wish to go inside the mosque and see it, Shall i bring him/her in the mosque. What does quraan and our shariaah says about this.

    Question: Assalaamualeyiukum, I have two questions. 1. If any non-muslim greet me with Asslaamuleyikum, Shall i reply him/her with Waaleyikumussalaam??? What does quraan and our shariaah says about this. 2. If any non-muslim wish to go inside the mosque and see it, Shall i bring him/her in the mosque. What does quraan and our shariaah says about this. Jazaakumullah!

    Answer ID: 7472Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 882/882=M/1429)

     

    Reply greeting of a non-Muslim with: وعلیک  or ھداک اللہ.

    (2) A non-Muslim can enter mosque provided it is not intending desecration.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
