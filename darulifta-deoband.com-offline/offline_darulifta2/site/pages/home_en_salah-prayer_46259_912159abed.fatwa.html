    Title: Sunnah after witr

    Question: Can the two rakah sunnah after Isha be prayed after the witr salah?

    Answer ID: 46259Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1194/917/B=1434) The two rakah salah which is offered after witr is nafl. Yes, you may offer it after witr.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
