Wearing Burkha aur Hijab is Sunnah Wajib or Farz on Muslim Women n about covering the face? 3. Keeping beard how much is it Wajib or sunnah? 4. A person asked me that if a women will die n goes to jannah which husband she will get the husband which died or the Husband who was her last husband If a women has remarried? may be after divorce or death of her previous husband?

    Answer ID: 14349Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1089/1089=M/1430)

     

    (1) No.

    (2) A woman should observe purdah before non-mahram, she should cover her face.

    (3) It is wajib (necessary) to grow beard to a size of one fist.

    (4) Such a woman will be given option to choose to live with man she likes.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
