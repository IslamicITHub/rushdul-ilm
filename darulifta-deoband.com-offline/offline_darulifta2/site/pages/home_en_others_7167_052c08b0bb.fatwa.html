    Title: What is the meaning of "Maulana"? To whom can we call "Maulan"?

    Question: What is the meaning of "Maulana"? To whom can we call "Maulan"?

    Answer ID: 7167Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1022/903=L/1429)

     

    Maulana means: our senior, elder, master etc, but traditionally it is called to one who is graduated from any madrasa in Islamic Sciences.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
