    Question: jin aurton se boso kinar kiya gaya ho unki aulad se shadi kaisi hai chahe aurat ki kitni bhi umar ho

    Answer ID: 3465Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 262/248=J)

     

    It is haram for a person to marry the daughter of a woman whom he has kissed with lust (shahwah). If he marries any of her female offspring the nikah will never be valid however aged she might have be:

    وحرم أیضاً بالصھریۃ أصل ممسوستہ الخ و فروعھن مطلقاً ھذا اذا کانت حیۃ مشتھاۃ و لو ماضیاً کعجوز شوھاء لأنھا دخلت تحت الحرمۃ فلا تخرج (الشامی: 4/110)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
