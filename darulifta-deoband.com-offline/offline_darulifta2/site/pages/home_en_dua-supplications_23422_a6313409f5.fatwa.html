    Title: I would like to ask u if i recite any surah or any part of Quran Majeed and want to tranfer the reward/nekiyan or sawab to my dead father or any other relative so ,it is posible in the light of shariah if not then what the possible ways for doing so?

    Question: I would like to ask u if i recite any surah or any part of Quran Majeed and want to tranfer the reward/nekiyan or sawab to my dead father or any other relative so ,it is posible in the light of shariah if not then what the possible ways for doing so?

    Answer ID: 23422Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 998/998/M=1431)

    One can transfer the reward by reciting the surah or ayah of the holy Quran to one's deceased father or any other relatives. It is proved and possible according to Islamic Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
