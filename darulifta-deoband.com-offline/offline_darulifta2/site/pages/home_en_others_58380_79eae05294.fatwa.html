    Title: Dream

    Question: I saw many times in my dream that I can fly. Once my mother was with me (she was not flying) but most of the time I was alone. Is there any meaning of such dreams? I am a student (age-25y) not married.

    Answer ID: 58380Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 474/463/H=06/1436) Such dreams come due to stomach problems. Eat little in dinner and recite at least half Para (of Quran) before going to bed. Also recite Surah Quraish thrice after each salah with durood 3-3 times before and after and blow it on your hands and pass your hands over your body. Also develop the habit to sleep with wudhu according to Sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
