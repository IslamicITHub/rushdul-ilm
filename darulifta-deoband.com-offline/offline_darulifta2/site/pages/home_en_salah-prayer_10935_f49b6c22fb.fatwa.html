    Title: ASSLAMU ALYCUM 1. COLLECTIVE DUA AFTER SALLATT IS SUNNATH OR BEDUATH, EXPLINE WITH HADEES. 2.SHAKE HAND(MUSSAFATH) WITH ONE OR TWO HAND WHICH IS SUNNATH. 3.REALESTATE BUSSINESS (SELLINGLAND)IS ALLOWABLE.

    Question: ASSLAMU ALYCUM 1. COLLECTIVE DUA AFTER SALLATT IS SUNNATH OR BEDUATH, EXPLINE WITH HADEES. 2.SHAKE HAND(MUSSAFATH) WITH ONE OR TWO HAND WHICH IS SUNNATH. 3.REALESTATE BUSSINESS (SELLINGLAND)IS ALLOWABLE.

    Answer ID: 10935Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 235/235=M/1430)

     

    Praying after salah is proved from hadith, but it is wrong to consider collective dua necessary and revile those who do not do so. Everyone needs dua and has been asked to supplicate, so there is no wrong if seemingly it looks like collective.

    (2) Shaking hand is mustahab (desirable) with both hands.

    (3) It is lawful if no unlawful thing takes place.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
