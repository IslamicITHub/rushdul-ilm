    Answer ID: 4613Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 918/907=D/1429)

     

    Third party insurance is allowed in emergency cases, but you should avoid comprehensive insurance. If the banks purchases the car and sells it to you on profit then it is lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
