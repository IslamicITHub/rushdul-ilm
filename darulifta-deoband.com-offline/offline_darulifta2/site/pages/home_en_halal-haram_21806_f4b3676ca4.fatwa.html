    Question: Assalaamualaikum, I married to a Aalima, alhamdulillah we are leading a peaceful life. Obviously we need to keep visiting to in-law's house. But, my father-in-law and brother-in-law works in a bank. My question is, Is their incom is halaal? They gave me some cloths, is wearing those cloths is halaal to me. Jazaakallaahu khair,

    Answer ID: 21806Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 892/682/H=1431)

     

    If they are doing any lawful job in the bank i.e. watchman, peon etc then the income will not be termed as haram and if they are doing any haram job, then excuse them in a good manner.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
