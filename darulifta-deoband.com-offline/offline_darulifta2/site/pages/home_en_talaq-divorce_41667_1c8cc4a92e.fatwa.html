    Title: Can one communicate thru sms or mobile with her divorced wife

    Question: Can one communicate with her divorced wife during iddah period through SMS or mobile? Also is it allowed to communicate after halalah since both are genuinely interested in reconciliation? Kindly guide us.

    Answer ID: 41667Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1436/1436/M=1433) After three talaqs, a woman becomes haram for her husband. Hence it is not allowed to talk to a woman who is divorced with three talaqs without a need. Yes, after Shariah halalah when you do nikah again then she would become your wife and it will be correct for you to talk to her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
