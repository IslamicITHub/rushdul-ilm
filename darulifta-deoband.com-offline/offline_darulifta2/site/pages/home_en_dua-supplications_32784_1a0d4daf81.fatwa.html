    Title: Black MAgic

    Question: We a Family of 5 persons 3 brothers and father mother my elder brother age is 40 years second one is 35 and mines is 33 years we all are not married we have shown this to so many amils but till date nothing has change in our house we are fed up of life please advise in this matter

    Answer ID: 32784Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1021/621/L=1432) You should get Surah al-Baqarah recited for four months at your home without a gap. In-sha-Allah, the satanic effect shall be vanishing with its barakah. However, for getting married, you three brothers should perform salah regularly and arrange special dua. Also, recite Ya Latif (یا لطیف) 1111 times after Isha salah with durood 11-11 times before and after.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
