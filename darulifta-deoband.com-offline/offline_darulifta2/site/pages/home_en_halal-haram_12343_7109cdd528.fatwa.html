Your earlier response will be appreciated

    Answer ID: 12343Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 865/727=D/1430)

     

    Taking credit card and giving 10 thousand rupees is a kind of debt, so receiving 1% service charge is unlawful for you, it is interest.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
