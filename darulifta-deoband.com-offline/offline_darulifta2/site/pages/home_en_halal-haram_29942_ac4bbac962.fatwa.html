    Title: Using tobacco powder (masheri)for tooth brushing is lawful ?

    Question: using tobacco powder (masheri)for tooth brushing is lawful ?

    Answer ID: 29942Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 322/322/M=1432)

    If the tobacco powder is not intoxicating, stinking and injurious to health, then its use is lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
