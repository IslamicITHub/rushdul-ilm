    Title: What is the equivalent weight of one sa'a in kilograms? Please give details.

    Question: What is the equivalent weight of one sa'a in kilograms? Please give details.

    Answer ID: 35050Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1676/1676/M=1432) According to prevalent measurement, one sa' is 3150.280 gm. (Al-Auzan al-Mahmuda)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
