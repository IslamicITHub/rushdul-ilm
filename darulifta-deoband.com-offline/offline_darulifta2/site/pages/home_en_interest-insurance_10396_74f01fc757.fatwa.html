    Answer ID: 10396Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 126/159=D/1429)

     

    (1) It is unlawful to use interest in medical expenses and in repaying debts.

    (2) Yes, it can be spent in income tax paid to the government.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
