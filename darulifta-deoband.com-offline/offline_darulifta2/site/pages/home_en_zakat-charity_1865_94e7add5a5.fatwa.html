    Answer ID: 1865Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 695/691=N)

     

    You can help the poor Muslim boys and girls by the collected zakah money from the above-mentioned fund in a way that you hand over the zakah money to them and they use it in their medical and educational needs; since giving the money in possession of the poor is compulsory: ویشترط أن یکون الصرف تملیکاً لا اباحۃ (در مختار: 3/291)

     

    If you paid the educational fees or paid the bill of medical expenses directly without giving the money in their possession, then the zakah­ will not be valid.

     

    Note: Spending the above-mentioned money in the help of poor women is not allowed until the contributors of zakah allow it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
