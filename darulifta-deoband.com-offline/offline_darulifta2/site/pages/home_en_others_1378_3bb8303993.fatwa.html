Also, there is another contemporary seerah book written by Shaikh Mohamad Al Ghazali called "Fiqh al Seerah". Are the Ulama aware of this book and do they recommend reading of this book?

    Question: Bismillah, Respected Ulama Assalamu Alaykum Wa Rahmatullaahi Wa Barakatuhu, In answer to question to #570, a book called "Salafiat: Aik Jaizah" written by Shaikh Ramzan al-Buti was recommended for self study. Have the ulama also translated his seerah book in Urdu called Fiqh-us-Seerah? Do the Ulama recommend reading of this book? Also, there is another contemporary seerah book written by Shaikh Mohamad Al Ghazali called "Fiqh al Seerah". Are the Ulama aware of this book and do they recommend reading of this book?

    Answer ID: 1378Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 419=410/D)

     

    Sorry, we do not know any book named ?Fiqh-us-Seerah?.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
