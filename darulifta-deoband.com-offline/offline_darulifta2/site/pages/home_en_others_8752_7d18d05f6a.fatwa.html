    Answer ID: 8752Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2168/1694=H/1429)

     

    Deliver the rewards (of financial and physical good deeds) to your late grandfather as much as you can, you shall have its benefit and reward in the world hereafter, in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
