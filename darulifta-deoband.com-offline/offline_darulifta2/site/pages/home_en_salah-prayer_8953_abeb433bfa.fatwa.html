    Title: 1) Can I pray Umr Qada when travelling along with Qasr. For eg. I pray Two Raka'as Dhuhr Qasr and then Four Raka'as Dhuhr Umr Qadha.

    Question: 1) Can I pray Umr Qada when travelling along with Qasr. For eg. I pray Two Raka'as Dhuhr Qasr and then Four Raka'as Dhuhr Umr Qadha.

    Answer ID: 8953Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2024/1874=D/1429)

     

    Yes, you can pray. If you are musafir and pray the timely salah with qasr, if you like to make qaza of the salah missed during your stay at home then you can make up them by offering full (4 rakah).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
