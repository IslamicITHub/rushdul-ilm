    Question: jume ki namaaz main sunnate muakkda kitni hai or us ki padhne ki tarteeb kya hai?

    Answer ID: 12009Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 601/427=L/1430)

     

    There are four rak?ah sunnah muakkadah before Jumua?h, while after Jumu?ah also it is four, but according to Imam Abu Yusuf and Imam Muhammad it is six:

    یستن بعدھا أربعاً أو ستا علی الخلاف أي أربعاً عندہ و ستا عندھما (الشامی 3:436)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
