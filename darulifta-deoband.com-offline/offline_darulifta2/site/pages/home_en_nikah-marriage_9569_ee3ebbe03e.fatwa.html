    Title: If anybody sucks milk of his wife intentionally or unintentionally, will their marriage be nullified?

    Question: If anybody sucks milk of his wife intentionally or unintentionally, will their marriage be nullified?

    Answer ID: 9569Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2510/2064=B/1429)

     

    The Nikah is not nullified by sucking the breast of wife, but it is unlawful to drink the milk, one must refrainn from it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
