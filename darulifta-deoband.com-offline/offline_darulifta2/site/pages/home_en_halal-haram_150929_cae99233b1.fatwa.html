    Title: I want to read Homeopathic. According to Shariah whether it is lawful or wrong.

    Question: I want to read Homeopathic. According to Shariah whether it is lawful or wrong.

    Answer ID: 150929Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 876/785/B=8/1438)

    There is no harm in learning the lawful and halal sciences. Studying medicine whether it is homeopathic or allopathic is lawful if it is in line with Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
