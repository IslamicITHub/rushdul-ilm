    Title: If I marry with a widow with child what will be rule of wirasat for that child after my death?

    Question: If I marry with a widow with child what will be rule of wirasat for that child after my death?

    Answer ID: 168774Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 646/563/M=06/1440)

     

    In the above mentioned case the child will not be your heir.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
