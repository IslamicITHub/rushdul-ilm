    Title: i know a person who started masturbating at the age of 13 but did nor have a wetdream till age of 14. does he have to pray namaaz since age of 13 or 14?

    Question: i know a person who started masturbating at the age of 13 but did nor have a wetdream till age of 14. does he have to pray namaaz since age of 13 or 14?

    Answer ID: 21134Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 605/405/L=1431)

     

    He will have to offer salah from the time when he starts experiencing wet dream, provided semen is not discharged due to the masturbation, otherwise from that time onward he will have to make qaza of missed salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
