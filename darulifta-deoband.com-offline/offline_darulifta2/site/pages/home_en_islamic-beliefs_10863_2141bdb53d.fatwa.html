    Question: assalamu alaikum, can you describe the arrival about imam mahdi(as)im being told that he has already come and buzurge deen or searching for him, can you describe what should we do if we come to know about the arrival of mahdi(as)do we have to go to makkah to join with him wassalam

    Answer ID: 10863Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 309/271=D/1430)

     

    The traditions about the emergence of Hadhrat Mahdi abound in hadith books. According to these traditions, he will openly come up; there will be no need to look for him after emergence. One should act according to the situation of the time and this will also be disclosed to everyone. As far as other matters are concerned, they could be enquired about by Ulama of those days.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
