    Title: I went to bed reading Uswai-Rasul-Akram in the end the letter from Moulana Zakariya Khadlawi (PBUH) which regards to Dr.Abdul Hai (RA). Thereafter I slept and saw: I saw myself in a madarasa with many students with white clothing and I was sitting among them also. Thereafter I saw some moulana sahib with a bright beautiful smiling face and he gave me something to eat, we were sitting underneath a table. Thereafter I saw someone scolding a young kid (somewhere like 19 year old) who was sitting outside and was facing tough time. Then dream was over. Please I ask my scholars to interpret this dream and recommend me steps to take in my life, I am currently in a very bad shape in terms of spirituality, please mention me in your duass I feel very very lost.

    Question: as-salam-elay-kum respected scholars and may peace be upon you. I have a very quick question the topic is Dream Interpretation and I request you in advance please do not post this on website rather email it to me, thankyou. I went to bed reading Uswai-Rasul-Akram in the end the letter from Moulana Zakariya Khadlawi (PBUH) which regards to Dr.Abdul Hai (RA). Thereafter I slept and saw: I saw myself in a madarasa with many students with white clothing and I was sitting among them also. Thereafter I saw some moulana sahib with a bright beautiful smiling face and he gave me something to eat, we were sitting underneath a table. Thereafter I saw someone scolding a young kid (somewhere like 19 year old) who was sitting outside and was facing tough time. Then dream was over. Please I ask my scholars to interpret this dream and recommend me steps to take in my life, I am currently in a very bad shape in terms of spirituality, please mention me in your duass I feel very very lost. Jizakallah, Salam-e'lay-kum.

    Answer ID: 19911Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 376/313/H=1431)

     

    It is very blessed dream. It means that you shall get rid of the worldly love. May Allah make it mubarak for you and grant you bliss in this world and the hereafter and protect you from evils. Amen! Keep Hayat al-Muslimin, Fazayil Amal (specially its second part) in your study along with giving time for Jama?ah, it will be beneficial for you in-sha-Allah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
