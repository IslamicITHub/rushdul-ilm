    Title: Can i spend the bank interest amount to pay income tax?

    Question: As salamu alaikum. Can i spend the bank interest amount to pay income tax?

    Answer ID: 21208Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 469/469/M=1431)

     

    If interest amount is received from government bank, it is allowed to pay it in income tax.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
