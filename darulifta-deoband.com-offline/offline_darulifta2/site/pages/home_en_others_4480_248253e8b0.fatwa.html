    Question: Assalamu alaikum, I want to ask a question regarding the tabeer of dream my first question is my aunt who hails from bihar saw a dream that there were so many earthworm like creature resting on her bed after three days in the noon time she saw that that those earthworm like creature were being fried in a frypan,please tell me the tabeer of this khwab,i will be grateful to you My second question is that my sister saw a dream in which she saw that she was walking somewhere , while walking she saw many dead bodies and at the same time she heard the verses of HOLY QURAN she also heard the name of our prophet's name please tell the tabeer of this dream.

    Answer ID: 4480Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1306/990=H-1429)

     

    The evils of loving post and wealth are emerging like insects on earth; common people are oblivious of these poisonous vices.

    (2) The dream points out that there is negligence towards isal-e-sawab (conveying reward to the death). One should more often recite the Holy Quran and durud sharif and convey the rewards to the death.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
