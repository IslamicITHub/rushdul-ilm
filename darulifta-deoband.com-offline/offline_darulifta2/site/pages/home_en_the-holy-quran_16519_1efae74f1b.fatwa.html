    Question ID: 16519Country: United Kingdom

    Title: Assalamualaykum 1-When going in to Sajdah for Sajdah e tilawat is it wajib to say the takbeer? 2-When going in to Sajdah for Sajdah e tilawat is it wajib or sunnah to say the tasbeeh? 2-When getting up after performing Sajdah e tilawat is it proven from hadith to say the takbeer or one should get up without saying the takbeer because my friend said that its not sunnah to say the takbeer?please provide reference from sahih hadith.Jzk

    Question: Assalamualaykum 1-When going in to Sajdah for Sajdah e tilawat is it wajib to say the takbeer? 2-When going in to Sajdah for Sajdah e tilawat is it wajib or sunnah to say the tasbeeh? 2-When getting up after performing Sajdah e tilawat is it proven from hadith to say the takbeer or one should get up without saying the takbeer because my friend said that its not sunnah to say the takbeer?please provide reference from sahih hadith. Jzk

    Answer ID: 16519Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1877/D-299K=1430)

     

    Both the takbeers before and after Sajdah Tilawah are sunnah not wajib.

    هكذا في المراقي ص 498 وكيفيتها أن يسجد سجدة واحدة بين تكبيرتين تكبيرة للوضع، و تكبيرة للرفع وهما سنتان, كذا قال في مبسوط فخر الاسلام التكبير ليس بواجب و صححه في البدائع ...

    (2) وتسبيحها مثل الصلاتية سبحان ربي الأعلى ثلاثا و هو الأصح

    Tasbeeh i.e. saying سبحان ربى الاعلى thrice is Sunnah. If one does sajdah tilawah in farz prayer, he should say the tasbeeh thrice. And if one is doing sajdah tilawah in nafil salah or doing sajdah tilawah individually then besides سبحان ربى الاعلى it is better to say other dua which is mentioned in hadith, i.e.

    أللهم اكتب لي عندك بها أجراً و ضع عني بها وزراً واجعلها لي عندك ذخراً و تقبلها مني كما تقبلتها من داؤد. (ترمذي شريف)

    (3) Yes, it is sunnah to say takbeer while standing as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
