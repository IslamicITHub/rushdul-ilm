    Title: Dream

    Question: I dreamt that I returned from hajj, and people were sitting on the floor in black burqas. Only 1 person I knew. I went and looked in the mirror and I had sparkling black diamond like gems on my eyeballs. I thought to myself should I remove it or leave it as it will leave dents if removed. And I blinked a few times and thought to myself, I will leave these and not remove it as it looks very pretty. This dream was just before awaking for the Fajr salah. Can someone please tell me the meaning?

    Answer ID: 44839Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 552/433/H=1434) Ma-shaAllah, it is nice dream. The meaning is that you shall receive the blessings of Hajj and Umrah and the taufiq of doing service of your parents and other relatives, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
