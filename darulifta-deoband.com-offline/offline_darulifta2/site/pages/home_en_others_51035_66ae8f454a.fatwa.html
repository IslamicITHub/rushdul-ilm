    Title: dream interpretation

    Question: I am a Muslim girl, age 20 years, I live in a hostel. I request you to please interpret these two dreams. (1) I see myself naked going to school or college, what does this mean? (2) I often dream that my hands are filled with my shining white teeth, what does this mean? I am worried.

    Answer ID: 51035Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 337/337/M=04/1435) (1) If you observe Shariah purdah then the dream indicates towards virtues and piety, thus it is a nice dream. But if you do not observe purdah then it is cause of evil and you should observe full purdah. (2) To see the palm of hand filled in the dream is the indication that marriage shall take place and you shall have prosperity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
