    Answer ID: 6411Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1305/1115=B/1429)

     

    Recite آیۃ الکرسی after every obligatory salah and blow on the body.

    Reciting سورۃ الفلق and سورۃ الناس is also very useful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
