    Title: Dua

    Question: Highlight of my life is that I have suffered brutally from black magic done by mamani (mamu’s wife) for almost 8 years and still I do not know if it ends yet. I am 23 years old and currently in Italy. Basically I am from Lahore, Pakistan. Dreams that I suffer from are that I see black snakes but they tend to run away from me. Once I have killed snake too in my dream and it made me happy but now even after killing I saw it again. It never harms me. It is just that I just see it. What does it signify? Second dream is that we had very good life when I was a kid. My father was a very high rank officer and we used to live in a huge bungalow. We don’t live there anymore because my father had to switch job from there. But what happens is no matter what happens I keep seeing that place and seeing myself happy with my mother and father. I have not been to that place in last ten years. Please help me with answers dear sir?

    Answer ID: 44174Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 401/326/B=1434) Recite surah al-Nas and surah al-Falaq after each fardh salah once and blow on your body. While going to bed in the night, recite surah al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq and al-Nas and blow on both of your hands and pass your hands over your whole body. Do the same thrice. In-shaAllah, soon these effects shall come to an end.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
