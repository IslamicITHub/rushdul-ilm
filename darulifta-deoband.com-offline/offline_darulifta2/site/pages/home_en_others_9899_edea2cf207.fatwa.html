    Question: Assalaamu 'Alaikum, A long time ago, a woman had a dream that her dead father is telling her to tell her husband to cut a tree (he indicated which tree it is) that is at their backyard. She told her husband this but her husband said that he cannot cut it and so it wasn't cut. What is the meaning of that dream? Should her husband have cut it? Please answer in English as soon as possible. Jazakallah khairan wassalaamu 'alaikum.

    Answer ID: 9899Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 41/27=H/1429)

     

    The woman and his husband have less interest towards good deeds. The dream indicates to rectify this state of affairs.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
