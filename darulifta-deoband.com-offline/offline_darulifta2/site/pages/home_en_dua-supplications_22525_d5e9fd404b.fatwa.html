    Title: I have a question my sister got married a year ago ,his husband is a engineer have a child of 5 years. now only we came to know that my sisters husband has some previous affairs with an other girl.now he wants to live with her. and left my sister . in this case what can my sister do?

    Question: I have a question my sister got married a year ago ,his husband is a engineer have a child of 5 years. now only we came to know that my sisters husband has some previous affairs with an other girl.now he wants to live with her. and left my sister . in this case what can my sister do?

    Answer ID: 22525Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 914/661/L=1431)

    Ask your sister to have patience and keep on behaving with her husband in good manner. If he does second marriage, then too she should live with him and ask him not to give her talaq. To get her husband become kind, ask your sister to take 11 pieces of black peppers after Isha salah, and recite Ya Latif Ya Wadood (يا لطيف يا ودود) 1100 times with durood 11-11 times before and after, and while reciting she should think of her husband to be kind. After completing this act, she should blow it on the black peppers and put it into flaming fire and supplicate Almighty Allah. In-sha-Allah, her husband would turn kind. This practice should be done for forty days. (Bahishti Zewar) In such conditions it is better for a wife to turn towards Almighty Allah and behave with husband in good manner. Leaving all these things and resorting to jurisdiction and behaving husband tightly bring nothing but loss. They do nothing but lengthen the dispute and increase its gravity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
