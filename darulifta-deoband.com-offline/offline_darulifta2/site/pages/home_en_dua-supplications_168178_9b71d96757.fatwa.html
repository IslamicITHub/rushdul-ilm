    Title: Please tell me to get success in examination, which is not qualifying the exams?

    Question: Please tell me to get success in examination, which is not qualifying the exams?

    Answer ID: 168178Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 444/372/D=05/1440)

     

    Read يا أرحم الراحمين extensively. In-shaAllah, you shall succeed in your exam. Similarly, read the below dua extensively:

    رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْماً

    رَبِّ انْفَعْنِي بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنِي وَعَلِّمْنِي مَا يَنْفَعُنِي

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
