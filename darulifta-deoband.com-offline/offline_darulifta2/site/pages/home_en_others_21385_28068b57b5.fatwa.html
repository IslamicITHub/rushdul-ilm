And my wife also see sometime in her dreams that i am in a toilet and urine around me on the floor. I am very disturbed with such kind of dreams so please help.

    Answer ID: 21385Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 822/628/H=1431)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah these are nice dreams. The concerns of wife are the proof of her love for her late father. Your wife takes good care for kindness towards relatives. This is the meaning. Yes, you need to pay more attention to do isal-e sawab.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
