    Title: Dua or quranic ayat for protection from eneymies

    Question: Please give me dua or ayat from Quran to blow on water spray all corner of my house to protect me and my family and house from enemies and poisonous animals.

    Answer ID: 46163Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1211/1004/H=10/1434) Recite the following dua 120 times with durood 3-3 times before and after and blow on water and sprinkle it in the corners and walls of the house. إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْداً وَأَكِيدُ كَيْداً فَمَهِّلِ الْكَافِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْداً

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
