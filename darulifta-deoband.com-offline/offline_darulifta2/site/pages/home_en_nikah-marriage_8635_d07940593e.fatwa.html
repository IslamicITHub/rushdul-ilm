    Answer ID: 8635Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1431/1195=L/1429)

     

    It is not right to talk to one?s fianc?e before Nikah, it is sin and it must be avoided.

    Except the mother in law, one can talk to brother and father in law.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
