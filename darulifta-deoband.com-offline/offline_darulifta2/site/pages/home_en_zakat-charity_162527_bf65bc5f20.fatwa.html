    Title: My name is Mustafa. Every Ramadan I calculate my zakah. But this time I invested some money in Mutual Fund. Should I calculate invested money, so how to calculate my zakah.

    Question: My name is Mustafa. Every Ramadan I calculate my zakah. But this time I invested some money in Mutual Fund. Should I calculate invested money, so how to calculate my zakah.

    Answer ID: 162527Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1332/1169/L=11/1439)

     

    Yes, you will have to include the amount of Mutual Fund while calculating your zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
