Now my question is if we are already divorced and we can not marry or live together again?? Or it was just a one dirorce and we can live together without or with nikah?? I would really appreiciate your answer as we are very confuse at this stage and really need help from our brothers who really know the matter and can guid us properly.

    Answer ID: 1768Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1343/1165=B)

     

    When you have uttered the above mentioned sentence thrice with the intention of talaq then the three talaqs have occurred. Now, you can no longer live with her without halalah sharaiyah.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
