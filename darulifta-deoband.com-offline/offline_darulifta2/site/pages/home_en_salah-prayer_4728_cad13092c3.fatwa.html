    Question ID: 4728Country: India

    Title: I am an indian , and at the moment i am in the island of fiji. we left india on 18th of march , reached fiji at nadi on 18th morning and reached sova the capital which was 180 km drive from nadi on 19th . now we will stay in sova till 2.04.08 and go to another place called lautoka which is about 150km away from sova .and go back to india on 16.04.08 now please tell that do we have to read qasar every where or not?

    Question: assalaam alikum bhai i am an indian , and at the moment i am in the island of fiji. we left india on 18th of march , reached fiji at nadi on 18th morning and reached sova the capital which was 180 km drive from nadi on 19th . now we will stay in sova till 2.04.08 and go to another place called lautoka which is about 150km away from sova .and go back to india on 16.04.08 now please tell that do we have to read qasar every where or not?

    Answer ID: 4728Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1204/1013=B-1429)

     

    In the journey mentioned above, since you did not stay in any city 15 days or more; therefore you will be msuafir as per the Shariah and you will have to perform qasr salah. However, if one intends to stay for 15 days or more in a city, he, as per the Shariah, will be regarded muqeem and will have to perform full salah:

    صلي الفرض الرباعي ركعتين حتي يدخل موضع مقامه أو ينوي إقامه نصف شهر (الشامي)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
