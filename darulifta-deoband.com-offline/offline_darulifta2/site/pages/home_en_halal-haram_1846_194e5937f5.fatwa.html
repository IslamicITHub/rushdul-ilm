I went to wedding on the weekend and there was video cameras and people taking pictures and filming everyong. I was approached to join in a photo but I refused.     Answer ID: 1846Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1525/1187=H)

     

    It is unlawful to store pictures of living beings and films of weddings etc in cameras or mobile and so on.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
