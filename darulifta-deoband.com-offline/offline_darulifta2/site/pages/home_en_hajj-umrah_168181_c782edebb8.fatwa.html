    Title: Is it valid to stitch below ihram cloth from one side just like closed lungi?

    Question: Is it valid to stitch below ihram cloth from one side just like closed lungi?

    Answer ID: 168181Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 861/521/H=05/1440)

     

    It is lawful to stitch the cloth of ihram from one side i.e. its one corner. There is no harm in it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
