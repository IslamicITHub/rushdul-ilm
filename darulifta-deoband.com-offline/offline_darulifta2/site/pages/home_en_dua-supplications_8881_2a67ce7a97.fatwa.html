    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2281/1779=H/1429)

     

    Make a fresh ghusl, put on new or washed clothes and offer two rakah Salatut Taubah. Having completed the salah, recite durood 500 times and istighfar (استغفر اللہ ربی من کل ذنب واتوب الیہ) 500 times and then durood 500 times, then supplicate to Allah in earnest and beg for his forgiveness. Make firm intention not to go near adultery and things leading to it. Avoid privacy and brazen relation with the aunty and or with any strange woman. Repeat the abovementioned action twice in a week and continue it to six months at least. Pray five time salah and recite the Quran regularly. In-shaAllah you shall get pious. If there is any pious follower of sunnah sheikh around you, you should pay him visits and try to apply in your life the instructions given by him.

    (2) By following the answer 1, in-shaAllah you shall find all ways open for you.

    (3) There is no need to seek pardon or to mention the sin.

    (4) May Allah protect you from bad dreams and their bad effects. Also, you should go to jamah as per the local schedule.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
