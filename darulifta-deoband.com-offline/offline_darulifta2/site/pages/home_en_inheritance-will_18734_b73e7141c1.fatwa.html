    Title: My question is about distribution of assets (movable and immovable) inherited from parents. I have 04 sisters and One of them is not normal mentally. I am the only son of my parents. Kindly advice what percentage should go to whom.

    Question: AsSalaamA'laikom, My question is about distribution of assets (movable and immovable) inherited from parents. I have 04 sisters and One of them is not normal mentally. I am the only son of my parents. Kindly advice what percentage should goto whom.

    Answer ID: 18734Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 86/86=B=1431)

     

    According to Shariah, all the property and wealth of your parents will be distributed in 6 shares; out of which you will get two shares and each sister will get 1-1 share.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
