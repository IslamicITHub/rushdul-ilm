    Title: How we can distribute Zakat? Whether deposit it Pakistan Government account or inditually. Secondly pls inform we use in our buisness Runnning Finanace and Trust Receipt from a banking company.

    Question: How we can distribute Zakat? Whether deposit it Pakistan Government account or inditually. Secondly pls inform we use in our buisness Runnning Finanace and Trust Receipt from a banking company.

    Answer ID: 2151Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1926/1475=H)

     

    It is advisable for you to deliver your zakah yourself to the poor and give it in their possession. And, if you are satisfied that the government spends the zakah on poor by giving it in their possession then it is allowable to deposit it in the account.

     

    (2) We could not understand this matter well; please ask this question again clearly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
