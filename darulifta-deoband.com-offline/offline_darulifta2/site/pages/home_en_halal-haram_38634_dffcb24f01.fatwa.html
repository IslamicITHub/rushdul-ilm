    Title: I wanted to know whether teeth implant is allowed as per Shariah

    Question: I wanted to know whether teeth implant is allowed as per Shariah. I had an accident in which I lost my front six teeth. Doctor has recommended tooth implant to replace the lost teeth, so please guide me whether it is allowed as per Shariah.

    Answer ID: 38634Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 926/760/B=1433) Yes, you can implant the teeth, it is allowed as per the Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
