    Question ID: 50445Country: India

    Title: Wudhu

    Question: We pray namaz at the roof top of the office building. My question is regarding performing wudhu. There are only western style toilets. The main concern is regarding washing of feet, what we do is we perform wudhu in the wash basin dry the hand and face with handkerchief and then take a bottle of water and wash the feet on the roof. Is it allowed? Washing the feet is not possible in the bathroom as the floor is very dirty also the socks and shoes get wet. We can't use much water there to perform full wudhu as the admin may object as there would be water logging. Please give a detailed answer to it.

    Answer ID: 50445Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 421/346/B=03/1435) You should make wudhu in wash basin and take water into bottle and wash your feet on the floor. It is allowed. Wudhu shall be valid in this way as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
