    Title: I saw that i am readin surat akhlas and i dumm it on one child who become blind.

    Question: I saw that i am readin surat akhlas and i dumm it on one child who become blind. I also saw that i was at quila or khander i afraid and read kalma tayyab up to only laila il lalla than one man on the gate talqeen me to read whole kalma than his face was full with big massa.

    Answer ID: 31029Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 667/667/M=1432) It indicates that you shall be useful for others, in-sha-Allah. There is warning in the other dream that you are indifferent towards following sunnah, so you need to adopt complete Islamic way of life.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
