    Question ID: 19584Country: India

    Title: i am married 4 5 years still dont have child. i had 2 misscarriage. i visited doctor but doctor recommed surgery due 2 endrometrosis.can i go 4 surgery? please tell me dua and bagifa 2 get pregnant as soon as possible.

    Question: Assalam o alikum ,mufti shabeh i am married 4 5 years still dont have child. i had 2 misscarriage. i visited doctor but doctor recommed surgery due 2 endrometrosis.can i go 4 surgery? please tell me dua and bagifa 2 get pregnant as soon as possible.allah hafiz

    Answer ID: 19584Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 394/314/B=1431)

     

    You would better not to go for surgery. Trust Almighty Allah and beseech Him, and use any medicine to energize your uterus. Recite the following dua extensively:

    رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِي فَرْداً وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْوَارِثِينَ [الأنبياء : 89]

    Our Shaikh Hadhrat Zakariya Muhajir Madani RA had suggested the following amal:

    Boil two hen eggs early morning and after peeling them write the following ayah on one egg which the husband would eat:

    وَالسَّمَاء بَنَيْنَاهَا بِأَيْدٍ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ [الذاريات : 47]

    On the other egg write the following ayah which the wife would eat:

    وَالْأَرْضَ فَرَشْنَاهَا فَنِعْمَ الْمَاهِدُونَ [الذاريات : 48]

    This should be done for forty days without any gap, and during this period they should have intercourse as well. It is hoped that Almighty Allah would bless you a child.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
