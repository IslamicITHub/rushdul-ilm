    Title: In past I made lot of sin in my life just like zina, homosex, masterbation and exhibition of my organs to many women etc. 2) once I worked in a book shop there are many books and quran also,my fellow who is a non muslim were seated at holy quran.     Question: 1) assalamoalaikum,In past I made lot of sin in my life just like zina, homosex, masterbation and exhibition of my organs to many women etc. 2) once I worked in a book shop there are many books and quran also,my fellow who is a non muslim were seated at holy quran. I can't stoped him .just still saw him. now I am feeling guilty I am a greater sinner how can I confess and how will I pure. Please help me. pls.send answer of my both problems. thank you

    Answer ID: 5036Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 903/784=B-1429)

     

    You have committed very iniquitous things and according to the shariah, you deserve severe punishments. You must earnestly repent to Allah and seek His forgiveness and resolve firmly to shun from these heinous acts and keep yourself away form such things.

    (2) Sitting on a religious book is an utter desecration. It is a sorrowful matter that you did not stop that wicked person. You should strictly warn him to avoid this act in future and give respect to all religious books; especially it is very essential to respect the Holy Quran.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
