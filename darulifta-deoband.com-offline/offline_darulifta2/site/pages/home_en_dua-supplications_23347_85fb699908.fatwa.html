    Title: Did nabi peace be on him recite surah muzamil evry nite? What is the virtue of this surah? What is the fazilat of reciting surah fajr in the morning?

    Question: Assalamoalaikum. Did nabi peace be on him recite surah muzamil evry nite? What is the virtue of this surah? What is the fazilat of reciting surah fajr in the morning? JazakAllah hu khaira. Wassalamu alaik

    Answer ID: 23347Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1301/1301/M=1431)

    We could not find any source that the Holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) recited Surah Muzzammil each night. We do not know any special virtue of this surah as well as the virtue of reciting surah Fajr in the morning.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
