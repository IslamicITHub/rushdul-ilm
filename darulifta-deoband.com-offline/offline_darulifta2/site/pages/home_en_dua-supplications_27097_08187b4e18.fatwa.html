    Question: Dear Mufti Sb, Assalamu-alaikum, I got married last month and wife is very decent and religious too. I work different place from my native land and i will bring her here after some time. The problem is that my mother is very sort tempered and create problems for small issue though she is very religious. We all are not happy for this kid of behavior. Please advice me and let me know any dua or wazaif for me and also for mother to bring peace within her. Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 27097Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1731/1257/L=1431)

    Recite بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم seven times, blow it on water and give it to your mother to drink. Also ask her to recite Ya Latif Ya Aziz Ya Ghaffar (يا لطيف يا عزيز يا غفار). In-sha-Allah, it shall soften her temper.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
