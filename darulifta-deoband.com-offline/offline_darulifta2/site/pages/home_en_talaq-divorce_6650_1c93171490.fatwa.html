    Answer ID: 6650Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 891/838=L/1429)

     

    There is no need of intention in direct Talaq, it takes place without an intention too. So, if your husband sent you SMS writing: Â‘I divorce youÂ’, then the Talaq will occur. Yes, if he wrote Â‘I divorce youÂ’ only once and did not give any other Talaq, then you were divorced by Talaq RajÂ’i (revocable divorce) i.e. your husband can revoke the Talaq within iddah, and after iddah he can do Nikah with you without halalah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
