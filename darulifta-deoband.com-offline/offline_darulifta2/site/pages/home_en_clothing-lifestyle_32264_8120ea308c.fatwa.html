    Title: pardah

    Question: In addition to Answer: 30381 i would like to say that MY MOTHER was the one who drank her fathers, relatives (a far related phoppi) milk. so the brother of my mothers wet nurse/foster mother become My Mothers mahrah. so do they also become my mahram.? since my mother is the one who drank her far related phoppis milk.

    Answer ID: 32264Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 972/L=375/TL=1432) Yes, he will also be your mahram. لقوله عليه السلام: يحرم من الرضاع ما يحرم من النسب.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
