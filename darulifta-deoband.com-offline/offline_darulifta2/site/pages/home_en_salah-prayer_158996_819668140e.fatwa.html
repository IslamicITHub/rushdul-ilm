    Title: I am following Hanafi Madhhab. My question is, can we raise our hands in witr salah (while reciting dua al qunoot) as the imams of Haramain do?

    Question: I am following Hanafi Madhhab. My question is, can we raise our hands in witr salah (while reciting dua al qunoot) as the imams of Haramain do?

    Answer ID: 158996Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 730/853/L=8/1439)

     

    In such situation the Hanafi should read Dua-e-Qunoot, but he should not supplicate raising his hands.

     

    وَيَتْبَعُ الْمُؤْتَمُّ قَانِتَ الْوِتْرِ أَيْ وَلَوْ كَانَ الْإِمَامُ شَافِعِيًّا يَقْنُتُ بَعْدَ الرُّكُوعِ لِأَنَّ اخْتِلَافَهُمْ فِي الْفَجْرِ مَعَ كَوْنِهِ مَنْسُوخًا دَلِيلٌ عَلَى أَنَّهُ يُتَابِعُهُ فِي قُنُوتِ الْوِتْرِ لِكَوْنِهِ ثَابِتًا بِيَقِينٍ (منحة الخالق / 2/ 79/ ط زكريا ديوبند)

     

    Note:It should be noted that if an imam leads witr salah with two salam other than Haramain then a Hanafi muqtadi should not pray behind him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
