    Title: Dream Interpretation

    Question: I am getting frequent a dream that in my old home which is demolished 6 yrs back. The dream is as I enter my old home the lock is broken and that there is thief in the house before seeing the thief I get up with shivering.

    Answer ID: 44445Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 498/385/D=1434) These are confused thoughts. When you have such dreams and you awake then recite لا حول ولا قوة الا بالله thrice and spit lightly to your left side thrice and turn your side and sleep. In-shaAllah, it shall not give you any harm. If such thoughts come while you are awake then recite لا حول ولا قوة الا باللهl and turn your mind towards good deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
