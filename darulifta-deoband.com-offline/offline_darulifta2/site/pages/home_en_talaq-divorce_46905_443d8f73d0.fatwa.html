    Question ID: 46905Country: India

    Title: Related to divorce?

    Question: If a husband saying that he said three words of divorce to his wife in front of his wife's father and his relative but his wife and father-in-law not accepting this fact that he said this three words of divorce in front of him so this divorce will be valid or not.

    Answer ID: 46905Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1182/764/L=9/1434) In the question mentioned above, when the husband confirms to give three talaqs then all the three talaqs took place on his wife, no matter whether the wife and father in law accept it or not.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
