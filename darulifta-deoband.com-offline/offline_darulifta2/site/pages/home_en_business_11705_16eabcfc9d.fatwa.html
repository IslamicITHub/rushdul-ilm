    Answer ID: 11705Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 597/421=L/1430)

     

    As per the Shariah, there is no limit of profit set in business.

    من اشتری شیئاً و أغلی في ثمنہ جاز

    ?It is lawful if one purchased a thing and sold it on higher price.?

    But, receiving more profit is against morality; therefore the articles should be sold with proper rate of profit, it is against morality to take extraordinary profit.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
