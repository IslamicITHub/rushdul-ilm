    Answer ID: 9685Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 40/41=B/1429)

     

    (1) Mazi (pre-seminal fluid) is discharged due to foreplay or sexual thoughts and it intensifies lust. It breaks the wudhu; one has to wash it from his body and cloth. While, Mani (semen) is discharged with force and causes exhaustion and relaxation, it requires ghusl.

    (2) The most important thing after eiman is salah. Let alone missing a salah, a Muslim is not even supposed to think of missing it. Though it is not shirk, but it is a major sin. Missing salah amounts to demolishing religion.

    (3) We do not know any such Hadith, but loving a girl is unlawful as per the Shariah. However, if one falls in love he should marry her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
