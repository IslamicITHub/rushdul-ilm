    Title: Is it permissible to use internet forums where males and females engage in online Islamic discussions?

    Question: My question is, is it permissible to use internet forums which are set up in the name of Islam but where males and females engage in online discussions? Shukran

    Answer ID: 122Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 245/N=241/N) It is allowed to use the internet forum if it is according to Shariah. If there is online discussion (by writing) then there is no problem, even if there is a woman at another end. But, if there are pictures or voice of women, then it will not be allowed even if it has only Islamic contents. So, it is allowed to use internet in itself provided that there is no picture of living being and voice of women.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
