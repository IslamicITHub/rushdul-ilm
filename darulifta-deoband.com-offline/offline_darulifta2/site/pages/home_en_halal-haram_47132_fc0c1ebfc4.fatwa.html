    Title: american cow milk

    Question: Does the milk of American cow is permuted or prohibited in Islam? I heard it from someone that the American cow is the breed of Pig.

    Answer ID: 47132Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1415/1058/H=10/1434) Its milk is not unlawful. First, it is not established whether it is a pig breed. However, if it is born out of pig sperm and delivered by cow then it will carry the rulings of cow. In animals the lineage belongs to mother.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
