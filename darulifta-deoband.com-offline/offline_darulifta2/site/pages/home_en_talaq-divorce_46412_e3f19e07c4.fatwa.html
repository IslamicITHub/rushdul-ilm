    Question ID: 46412Country: India

    Title: Divorce has not happened but for official reasons husband did not mention the name of spouce on some papers. Will this will be considered talaq?

    Question: Divorce has not happened but husband did not mention the name of spouse on official papers and leave space blank or nil. But in real he has no purpose to separate from his wife. Wife or her guardians did not know about such papers. Will this be considered as talaq?

    Answer ID: 46412Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1084/702/L=1434) For talaq to take place, it is necessary to write or utter the words of talaq. Talaq did not take place by leaving the space for wife name blank in the official papers.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
