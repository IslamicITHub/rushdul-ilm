    Title: Will zakah be wajib on flat bought with the intention of selling to start some business and make home?

    Question: I have one flat which I have taken from bank on loan. At the time of purchasing I thought that after selling it I will start my own business and make home. My father also gave me some area for resident. Am I eligible for this property?

    Answer ID: 67320Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1084/1112/N=10/1437)

    The land which your father gave you for residence no zakah shall be wajib on it as it is not business item. However the plot which you bought with the firm intention of selling and still you have the same intention then it is business item as per the Shari’ah and thus zakah shall be wajib on it yearly. And while paying the zakah of plots the price of the day of payment shall be taken into consideration. Thus the day on which you want to pay the zakah whatsoever price in the market is of the plot, you should pay its fortieth part as zakah.

    لا زكوة فى اللآلئ والجواهر وإن ساوت ألفاً اتفاقاً إلا أن تكون للتجارة ، والأصل أن ما عدا الحجرين والسوائم إنما يزكي بنية التجارة الخ (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار ، كتاب الزكاة ، 3: 194، ط: مكتبة زكريا ديوبند)، وقال في غنية ذوى الأحكام : والخلاف في زكاة المال فتعتبر القيمة وقت الأداء في زكاة المال على قولهما وهو الأظهر ، وقال أبو حنيفة رحمة الله : يوم الوجوب كما فى البرهان (غنية ذوى الأحكام 1: 181، ط: كراتشي).

    Also see Fatawa Darul Uloom Deoband (6: 131, 145, Question: 196, 221, publ. Maktaba Darul Uloom Deoband), Fatawa Mahmoodia (Jadid Dhabel, Kitab al-Zakah) and Fatawa Usmani (2: 50, 54, pub. Maktaba Darul Uloom Karachi) etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
