    Title: Dua for controlling a software

    Question: Professionally I am a transcriber and using some software for transcribing, but not getting sufficient worth from them, so I would like to know is there any particular dua so that I can recite to get support from Allah in my work.

    Answer ID: 62782Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 182/183/L=02/1437) Recite the following du’a 111 times daily with concentration on its meaning: حَسْبُنَا اللّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ Allah is fully sufficient for us, and the best One in whom to trust.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
