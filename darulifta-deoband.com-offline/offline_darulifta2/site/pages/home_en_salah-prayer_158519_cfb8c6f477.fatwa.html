    Title: Can male member of the family perform jamat at home with his family both male and female i.e. a male member becomes imam and his father, mother, wife and daughters/sons becomes muqtadi.

    Question: Can male member of the family perform jamat at home with his family both male and female i.e. a male member becomes imam and his father, mother, wife and daughters/sons becomes muqtadi.

    Answer ID: 158519Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 662/591/H=5/1439)

     

    The male would lead the prayer and the adult members will stand in row behind him and then children and in case there is only one child he will stand in the row of adults and then the row of women should be arranged; the salah of all shall be valid. 

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
