    Title: If gum bleed at time of fasting whether fast can carry on or rejected

    Question: (1) I have pyria for that I often get bleed from my gum. For that my fast can rejected or I can carry on the fast. (2) Also a question about wudhu (purity for praying salah) that for same reason could my wudhu validate or have to do wudhu again, because the blood cannot stop at that moment. (3) I am a patient of Asthma for that I have to take inhaler but at the time of fast what could I do?

    Answer ID: 151098Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 826/782/SD=9/1438)

     

    (1) Fast does not break if blood comes from the gum, you may keep the fast. It should be noted that if the blood of the gum is too much that it dominates the saliva or it is equal to saliva then if it enters the stomach then the fast shall break and qaza shall be incumbent.

    قال الحصكفى: (أو خرج الدم من بين أسنانه ودخل حلقه) يعني ولم يصل إلى جوفه أما إذا وصل فإن غلب الدم أو تساويا فسدو إلا لا . (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: 396/ 2، باب ما يفسد الصوم).

    (2) If the blood that comes from the gum dominates the saliva i.e. the saliva turns red then it shall break the wudhu.

    (3) It is not lawful to use inhaler in the state of fasting, it shall invalidate the fasting. In case there is fear of illness if inhaler is not used then it is allowed not to keep the fast and make its qaza later.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
