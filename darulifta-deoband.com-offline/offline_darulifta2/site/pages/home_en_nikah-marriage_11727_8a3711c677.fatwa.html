    Answer ID: 11727Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 742/409=H/1430)

     

    (1) No, you should marry as soon as possible when you find a suitable match for yourself.

    (2) Hold fast taqwa (piety), Taharah, strength in iman, firmness on good deeds and avoidance from sins.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
