    Title: My issue is that i have some intestinal problem (constipation). Thus whenever i offer prayer i feel i have done "hahath" but i never use to be sure. It happens to me in every prayer. Sometimes i control hadath. Please tell me what should i do,as i cannot offer any prayer with 100% concentration.

    Question: My issue is that i have some intestinal problem (constipation). Thus whenever i offer prayer i feel i have done "hahath" but i never use to be sure. It happens to me in every prayer. Sometimes i control hadath. Please tell me what should i do,as i cannot offer any prayer with 100% concentration.

    Answer ID: 2677Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 61/61=L)

     

    Keep on praying salah until you are sure of passing wind, only doubt does not invalidate the wuzu:

    عن ابی ھریرۃ رضی اللہ عنہ قال: قال رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم: اذا وجد احدکم فی بطنہ شیئاً فاشکل علیہ ا خرج منہ شئ ام لا فلایخرجن من المسجد حتی یسمع صوتا او یجد ریحاً (مشکاۃ: 1/40) و فی حاشیتہ او یجد ریحاً ای یجد رائحۃ ریح خرجت منہ و ھذا مجاز عن تیقن الحدث لانھما سبب العلم بذلک، کذا قال علماؤنا۔ (ibid)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
