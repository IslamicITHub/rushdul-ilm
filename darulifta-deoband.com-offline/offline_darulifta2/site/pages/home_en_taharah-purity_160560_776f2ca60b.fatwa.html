    Question ID: 160560Country: UAE

    Title: Urine drops issue

    Question: Alhamdulillah, I pray five times a day, but after finishing toilet the drops come for long time sometimes even more than an hour, I put tissue paper for this I try to somehow manage it, but in the morning timings it is very difficult to handle. Can I pray without relieving myself, in this there are chance of gas will come out. What should I do for this? Thanks

    Answer ID: 160560Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 800/672/N=7/1439)

     

    It appears from the question that the drops come after toilet for 30 or 45 minutes and thereafter no drop comes till you go to toilet for the second time i.e. the drops don’t come regularly. Thus if the case is so then you are not a Shariah mazur and when you are not excused person then your wudhu shall break if drop comes after you make wudhu and you will have to make fresh wudhu. You should urinate 45 minutes prior to Fajr salah as you do in other salah so that the drop stops by then and you may wash the private parts or the place where the urination is smeared and make wudhu and then offer salah with jamat.

     

    However, consult any expert Hakim for the treatment of your problem so that it is solved.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
