    Question ID: 44255Country: Pakistan

    Title: Salah

    Question: Can you please tell about performing salah behind Ahl Hadith and Barailavi? Whether we can offer salah behind them? If it is allowed then in which conditions I need brief answer.

    Answer ID: 44255Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 384/304/B=1434) Both are deviant sects. One should not offer salah behind them. If someone offers by chance due to compulsion then he shall get the reward of Jamah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
