    Question: Assalamualaikum, m student of A'Level (intermediate level) and beside m in touch with Tabligh. In most of the time i have to face questions that are 'Where it is written to sleep in Mashjid?' and 'Where it is written going for Chilla (40 days)?' Please clearify the facts! Jazakallahu khayra.

    Answer ID: 10044Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 54/54=M/1429)

     

    The scholars of Fiqh say that traveller and one who has had an intention of itikaf can sleep in mosque. (Fatawa Hindiyah)

    The elders of Jamat have designed the schedule of 40 days; since 40 days have a special importance in changing conditions. It is proved by indications from the Quran and Hadith and experiments.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
