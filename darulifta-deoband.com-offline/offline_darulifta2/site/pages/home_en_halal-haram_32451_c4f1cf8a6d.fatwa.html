    Title: Doing business with cats.

    Question: Can I tame cats ? make it big enough and sell it ? and continue doing business like this ? will it be halal for me to like this, since petting cat is jayez and selling it would be jayez too ?? Please answer as soon as possible. Thanks.

    Answer ID: 32451Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 891/741/B=1432) Yes, you can tame the cats and sell them for benefit. It is allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
