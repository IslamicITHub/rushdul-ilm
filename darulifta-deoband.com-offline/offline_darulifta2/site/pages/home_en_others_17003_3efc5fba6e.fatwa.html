    Answer ID: 17003Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2040/1621/D=1430)

     

    Marriage should be free from things which are prohibited in Shariya and it should be done according to the Sunnah. And, one should try to keep himself away from westernism in living. The suit and tie indicate the evils present at home.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
