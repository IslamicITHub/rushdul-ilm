    Question ID: 148603Country: India

    Title: About qasr salah

    Question: I am staying in hostel for three year which is 200 km from my native place. If I go home and return in 6 days for only 13 days not for 15 days. Will I be muqim at my hostel? During 6 days at my home I pray full namaz, am I right. Please guide me.

    Answer ID: 148603Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 617/586/M=5/1438)

    It is correct to offer full salah while staying at home for six days in the question mentioned above. If you stay in the hostel for 13 days then you shall be musafir and would offer salah with qasr.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
