    Title: Consuming things which contain tobacco is haram or not?

    Question: Consuming things which contain tobacco is haram or not?

    Answer ID: 1531Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 501=498=M)

     

    It is allowed but undesirable to use tobacco without a reason. Therefore, it is better to avoid consuming any thing that contains tobacco.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
