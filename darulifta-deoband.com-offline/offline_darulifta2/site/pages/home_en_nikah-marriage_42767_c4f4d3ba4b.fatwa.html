    Title: Istikhara

    Question: In case of marriage, should istikharah be performed even though you have no confusion about the girl/boy and your intellect and heart are both inclined towards him or her?

    Answer ID: 42767Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1717/1717/M=1433) Istikharah is not necessary but it is better to do it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
