    Answer ID: 5283Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 710/659=D/1429)

     

    Though seeing the entire body of wife is permissible but it is against modesty. While doing intercourse one can reveal the parts of the body only as much as necessary, but it has been prohibited in Hadith to get naked while having sex.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
