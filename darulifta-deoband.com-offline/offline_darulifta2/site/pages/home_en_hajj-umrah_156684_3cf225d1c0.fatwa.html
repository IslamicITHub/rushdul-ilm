    Question ID: 156684Country: India

    Title: Are there any basic conditions for Hajj/Umrah basal?

    Question: (1) Are there any basic conditions for Hajj/Umrah basal? Zaid's father is very old. He wants to send his grandson for Umrah. Somebody said that he has to recite Holy Quran twice in Makkah, so that his Umrah is accepted. Kindly do suggest if there are any conditions like this. (2) While on tour for 8 days, is it obligatory to perform qasr or can we pray fully during that periods.

    Answer ID: 156684Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 325/281/L=3/1439)

     

    (1) It is not mandatory to read complete Quran twice in Makkah in order to validate the Umrah, whosever said it is wrong.

    (2) If the journey is of 77.25 km or more then it is mandatory to offer qasr salah in case one intends to stay less than fifteen days.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
