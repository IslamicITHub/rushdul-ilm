    Title: I want to ask that can we wear taweezat. i have listen from somewhere that it is shirk please explain.

    Question: Asslamualyikum, I want to ask that can we wear taweezat. i have listen from somewhere that it is shirk please explain.

    Answer ID: 21642Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 652/461/D=1431)

     

    A taweez (amulet) which contains the verse of the holy Quran or the duas which are proved is allowed. And the taweez in which help is sought from devil and jinn or the words of kufr are written therein or it contains a mantar having no meaning, then carrying such types of taweez is certainly shirk, it was forbidden in hadith.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
