    Title: My parents are searching since a long time to buy a good house

    Question: My parents are searching since a long time to buy a good house. Please suggest a dua which will help us find a good and affordable house.

    Answer ID: 35214Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1727/1727/M=1432) Keep on looking for good house. And keep on supplicating Almighty Allah after the five salah for this purpose. Also keep on reciting Ayatul Kursi 3-3 times with durood 11-11 times before and after in the morning and evening. If Allah wills, you shall achieve your goal.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
