    Question ID: 55385Country: India

    Title: Halal or Haram

    Question: Can I work for an insurance company where my work is to feed data of only two wheelers (bikes, scooters) insurance in computer software and send data to insurance company? Is this type of job lawful in Islam or not? Please answer me as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 55385Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1477/1516/N=01/1436) Since the insurance of vehicles is a legal compulsion hence it is allowed. If your task involves only entering the insurance data of two wheelers in the insurance company then your job is lawful. However, if you adopt any other job then it is better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
