    Title: What is the ruling concerning both still photography and video?

    Question: What is the ruling concerning both still photography and video? wassalam

    Answer ID: 221Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 582=288/H)

     

    The pictures of living beings, whether still or moving, are unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
