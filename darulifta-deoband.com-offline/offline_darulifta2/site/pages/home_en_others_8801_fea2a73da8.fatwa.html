    Title: Please tell me what does this dream mean? My beard is full gray/white (without a single black hair) and full black hair (without a single gray/white hair) on my head. I remember only this much about my dream.

    Question: As-Salaamu-Alaikum-Wa-Rahmatullahi-Wa-Barakatuhu! Please tell me what does this dream mean? My beard is full gray/white (without a single black hair) and full black hair (without a single gray/white hair) on my head. I remember only this much about my dream. Jazaaku-Mullah

    Answer ID: 8801Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2183/1691=G/1429)

     

    It is good dream; you shall get insight and strength in religious matters in-sha-Allah, provided you mind following sunnah in every matter.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
