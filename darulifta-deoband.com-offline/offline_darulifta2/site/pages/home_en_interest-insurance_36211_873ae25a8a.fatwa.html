    Question: I want to take a mediclaim policy from any health insurance company. Is it right or wrong according to Islamic Shariah? Please give me reply.

    Answer ID: 36211Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 204/204/M=1433) If medical policy bears interest or gambling then it is not allowed as per the Shariah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
