    Title: Bank job is haram or halal?

    Question: Assalamalaikum. Bank job is haram or halal.

    Answer ID: 9749Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 5/7=H/1429)

     

    One can adopt a lawful employment in bank which does not involve interest in any way, while doing haram work is unlawful like writing interest, bearing witness to it etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
