    Question ID: 31945Country: India

    Title: 1. Is it allowed for children to play with toys which have pictures or are in the shape of fairy tale characters(like dolls)?

    Question: 1. Is it allowed for children to play with toys which have pictures or are in the shape of fairy tale characters(like dolls)? 2. Is it allowed to watch cricket on TV? 3. Is reading the newspaper allowed? there are mostly photographs of politicians and other famous personalities in it. 4. Is it allowed to watch religious discourses on TV by male clerics?

    Answer ID: 31945Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 934/610/H=1432) (1) It is not lawful (2) It is unlawful. (3) It is allowable if one reads it while ignoring the unlawful pictures as much as possible. (4) What do you mean by “to watch religious discourses on TV by male clerics”? Write it clearly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
