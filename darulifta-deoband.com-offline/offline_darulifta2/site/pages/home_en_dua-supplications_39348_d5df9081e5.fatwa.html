    Question ID: 39348Country: India

    Title: The maid working at my home has a 15 or 16 years old son. These days he is addicted to drinking whitener

    Question: The maid working at my home has a 15 or 16 years old son. These days he is addicted to drinking whitener (it has a pungent smell which he loves so much that he drinks it). He pulls rickshaw, whatever he earns by it he spends that in that addiction. His mother is very upset, he is her only son. kindly tell me a wazifah so that he gets rid of his habit.

    Answer ID: 39348Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1263/1035/B=1433) Recite the following dua 40 times and blow it on water and give it to him for drinking, and also keep on supplicating for him after each salah. وَوَجَدَكَ ضَالّاً فَهَدَى

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
