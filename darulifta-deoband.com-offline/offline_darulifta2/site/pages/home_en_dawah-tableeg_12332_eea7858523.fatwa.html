    Answer ID: 12332Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 703/703=M/1430)

     

    The hadith says: ?One who repents of his sins is just like one who did not commit any sin.? So, when you are repenting to Allah continuously from the sins, it is hoped that Allah will certainly forgive you, in-shaAllah. In future also, you should keep on repenting to Allah and asking for His forgiveness along with doing charity and other good deeds.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
