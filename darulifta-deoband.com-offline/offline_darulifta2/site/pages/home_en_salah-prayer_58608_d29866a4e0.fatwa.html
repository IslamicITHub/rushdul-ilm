    Title: Qasr Salah

    Question: When one should offer qasr salah? Will it be started immediately after starting journey from home or after some kilometers? After returning from the journey when one should stop offering qasr salah?

    Answer ID: 58608Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 782/786/L=06/1436) If someone intends to go on a journey of 77.25 kms then it is wajib on him to offer qasr salah. The starting of qasr shall be counted immediately after leaving the outskirt of the village or town and it shall remain intact until the man enters the boundary of his village again. So it is mentioned in the books of Fiqh and Fatawa.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
