    Title: regarding to pursue mba with regard to beard

    Question: I am a student and I want to do MBA from marketing and I have beard. My question is can I do MBA from finance stream. Is doing MBA from finance haram or halal? Please guide me.

    Answer ID: 46622Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1259/933/B=10/1434) Yes, you may do MBA in finance. It is allowed as per Shariah. You may do it while keeping beard as well. It shall be good. Do not worry. Do MBA in marketing and regularly offer salah and observe fast.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
