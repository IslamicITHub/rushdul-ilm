    Answer ID: 7304Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1178/140=L/1429)

     

    When for the first time, both of you started living together after negotiation and reconciliation, and you uttered these words in any discussion: "There is no relation between us, there is nothing remaining between us." If you had meant by these words giving divorce, then your wife was divorced with talaq baain. And when you said: "You are free from my life" then second divorce took place by this. Thus, they became tow irrevocable divorces (baain). Thereafter, if you had done Eila (swearing to abstain form wife) it will not be valid; since she was no longer your wife, it is necessary for Eila to take effect that the woman is in ones nikah.

     

    So, in the question mentioned above, if you had given no other divorce before or after or within the iddah period except the two mentioned above, then you can marry her again, but if you had given her any other divorce also, then you should write the question again.

     

    It is to be noted that it is unlawful for you to live with her after the divorce takes place, she will be stranger and unlawful for you and it will be adultery if you copulate with her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
