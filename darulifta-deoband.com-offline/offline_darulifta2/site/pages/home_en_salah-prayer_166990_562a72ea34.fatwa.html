    Question ID: 166990Country: India

    Title: UAE Namaaz timing will End @3:10 Hanfi timing will remain till 3:57, Can I perform Zohar namaaz after 3: 10?

    Question: I am in UAE hear I have checked which timing I should follow for Namaz Hanafi timing is not matching @ Aser namaz? UAE Zohor Namaz timing will End @3:10 and Asr time will start and Hanfi timing will remain till 3:57. Can I perform Zohar namaz after 3: 10?

    Answer ID: 166990Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 250/228/D=04/1440)

     

    In the question mentioned above, according to the preferred saying you may offer Zuhr salah till 3:57 pm, but it is better for you to offer Zuhr salah before 3:10 pm.

     

    ووقت الظهر من زواله أي ميل ذكاء عن كبد الساء إلى بلوغ الظل مثليه قال الشامي: هذا ظاهر الرواية عن الإمام (شامي: 2/14، ط: زكريا ديوبند)  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
