    Title: Banking Job

    Question: Is it OK for an IT(Informational Technology) professional to work in a bank as a system or database administrator.This profile involves the management of server machines and all the data kept in those machines.

    Answer ID: 31884Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 912/912/M=1432) The ruling regarding bank job is that if one has to write, read and calculate interest based dealings then this job is not lawful; because it is an act of sin or helping in sin. If the job does not involve interest directly or indirectly, then it is allowable, but it is better to avoid it. You should apply your job on the said principles and see how much the said job is right for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
