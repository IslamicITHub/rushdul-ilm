    Answer ID: 14480Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1118/1118=M/1430)

     

    You should consult your parents about marrying the girl. It is advisable to you to perform istikharah too. If the family members from both sides approve the marriage, try to solemnize the Nikah as soon as possible. For performing walimah and Nikah as per the sunnah, there is no need for sufficient money. If the istikharah and consultation is in negative, you should get the thought of the girl out of your mind. If Allah wills, the next marriage proposal will be better.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
