    Title: Going in Jama for forty days or four months

    Question: I am a government teacher. I have 3 members in my family, me, my mother & wife. Can I go 4 months or 40 days in dawat tabligh with permission of my mother and wife?

    Answer ID: 145657Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 98/71/H=2/1438)

    As you leave your home for school to perform your duty in the same way you may also go in jama’ah for four months or forty days. However, if you fear that your mother or wife might face some problem when you are in jama’ah, then you should make necessary arrangements in advance.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
