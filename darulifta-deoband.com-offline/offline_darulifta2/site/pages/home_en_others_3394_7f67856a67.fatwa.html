    Answer ID: 3394Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 420/B=45/TB)

     

    Ma-sha-Allah, your dream is blessed one. It is hoped that your shall very much adhere sunnah in-sha-Allah. The same meaning is also applied to your dream of beard. As far as stopping prime minister is concerned it indicates that you shall succeed to protect yourself from influences of kuffar. May Allah enable you to perform good deeds!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
