    Answer ID: 15537Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1629/1345=D=1430)

     

    You should not be worried; the dispute of the company shall be over in-sha-Allah. Recite the following dua daily three hundred times:

    یا حي یا قیوم برحمتک نستغیث

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
