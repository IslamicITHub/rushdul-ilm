    Question ID: 30705Country: India

    Title: I want to know that, if a muslim boy married to a non muslim or a muslim girl married to a non muslim boy is non islamik as per islamic belief.

    Question: SIR , I WANT TO KNOW THAT, IF A MUSLIM BOY MARRIED TO A NON MUSLIM OR A MUSLIM GIRL MARRIED TO A NON MUSLIM BOY IS NON ISLAMIK AS PER ISLAMIC BELIEF.

    Answer ID: 30705Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 411/411/M=1432)

    The nikah of Muslim boy with non-Muslim girl and vice versa is unlawful and haram.
    وَلاَ تَنكِحُواْ الْمُشْرِكَاتِ حَتَّى يُؤْمِنَّ الآية . (القرآن)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
