    Question ID: 31486Country: India

    Title: Getting job by telling a lie, cheating and showing false experience

    Question: Assalamualaikum. I'm a M.Sc(Computers) holder completed 2008 batch. At the time of completion the software market was down. So i did not got job in IT field. Since few months IT Companies are recruiting highly and some of my friends are getting jobs by putting Fake Experience and also they are giving advise for me. I'm thinking doing like this is Fraud and also i've to tell lie. Please give me right clarification regarding Islam. Is it allowed or not.

    Answer ID: 31486Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 800/670/B=1432) It is sin to get employment by telling a lie, cheating and showing false experience. It is not lawful as per the Shariah. But if someone has got employment in this way, it shall be halal and lawful for him to receive salary after doing right work. However, he shall be sinful due to getting employment by showing false experience and cheating.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
