Again forced with all bad words and gundas again forced to say 3 time talaq again I told them NO ??I am not giving talaq?? after all pressure to save my life I had told ok ??I give her talaq?? ??MAY TUMHA TALAQ DETA HUN?? 3 times.     Question: They had forced me to say takaq 3 times. Then I told ??I am not giving talaq?? Again forced with all bad words and gundas again forced to say 3 time talaq again I told them NO ??I am not giving talaq?? after all pressure to save my life I had told ok ??I give her talaq?? ??MAY TUMHA TALAQ DETA HUN?? 3 times. So, Is talaq valid?

    Answer ID: 4905Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 600/560=L/1429)

     

    Yes, the talaq took place and since you uttered the sentence thrice therefore the three talaqs tool place and your wife got separated by Talaq-e-Mughalazah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
