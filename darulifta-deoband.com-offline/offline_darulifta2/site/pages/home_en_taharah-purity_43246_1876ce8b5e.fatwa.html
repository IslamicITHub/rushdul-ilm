    Question ID: 43246Country: India

    Title: Taharah

    Question: Can a woman do talim from Muntakhab Ahadith and Fazail-e-Amal during menstruation period as Muntakhab Ahadith contain more Quranic verses so can a woman touch that in haiz and also can a haiza woman touch a Quran which contain only Urdu translation or only English translation without having Arabic verses? Please reply in the light of Quran and hadith.

    Answer ID: 43246Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 148/148/M=1434) In the state of haiz (menses) a woman can read Muntakhab Ahadith and Fazail Amal, but it is not better to touch the book. But it is not lawful to touch the Quranic verses and she cannot recite them. The English or Urdu translation of the holy Quran should also not be touched.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
