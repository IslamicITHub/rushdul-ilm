    Answer ID: 8498Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1352/198=TL/1429)

     

    This is a satanic dream. Recite ayatul kursi and the four Quls (سورۃ الکافرون، الاخلاص، الفلق، الناس), blow on your hand and pass over your body before going to bed. In-sha-Allah, you shall be protected against such dreams.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
