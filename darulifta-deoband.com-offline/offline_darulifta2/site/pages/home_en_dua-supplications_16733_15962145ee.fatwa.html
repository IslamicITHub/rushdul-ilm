    Title: WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF PAYING ZAKAT ?PLEASE GUIDE ME ALL POSSIBLE DUAS TO INCREASE RIZQ AND HEALTH OF MY FAMILY.

    Question: WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF PAYING ZAKAT ?PLEASE GUIDE ME ALL POSSIBLE DUAS TO INCREASE RIZQ AND HEALTH OF MY FAMILY.

    Answer ID: 16733Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2003/1592/D=1430)

     

    Zakah is one of the basic tenets of Islam. Those who own wealth equal to Nisab after passing a year, it is obligatory to give Zakah of that money. The greatest benefit is that one fulfils the commandments of Almighty Allah, by which one wins the pleasure and reward from Almighty Allah. As well, zakah purifies the wealth from impurities.

    1. Recite the following dua for increasement in livelihood and for the recompense of debt:

    قل اللهم مالك الملك توتي الملك من تشاء و تنزع الملك من تشاء وتعز من تشاء و تذل من تشاء بيدك الخير انك على كل شئ قدير، الهم اكفني بحلالك عن حرامك و اغنني بفضلك عمن سواك

    For health, each and every member of house should recite the following dua:

    أللهم إني أسئلك علما نافعا رزقاً طيباً و شفاءاً من كل داء

    Moreover, there are lots of Duas in Hizbul Azam and Munajat-e-Maqbool, recite one manzil from these books daily, insha-Allah you shall receive benefit from it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
