    Question: Assalaamualeikum Brothers, Brother i am going to marry in next six month and i am afraid of one thing.I am having very Bad odor in my mouth and I am using miswak regularly for namaaz but the bad odor is not getting reduced.Will this affect my marriage life. Please give me suggestion on this or some dua and what should i do.Sorry for asking medical related question.

    Answer ID: 5306Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 332/332=M/1429)

     

    Continue using miswak, recite the Holy Quran as much as you can and chew aniseed occasionally. Better, consult a physician and get treatment. However, bad odour may not affect the validity of your nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
