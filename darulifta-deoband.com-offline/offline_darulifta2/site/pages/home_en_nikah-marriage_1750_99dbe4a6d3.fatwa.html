    Title: What is the importance of Walimah (occasion just after the day of marriage)? Is it very necessary to celeberate it? Is mandha (the occasion just before the day of marriage) allowed in islam?

    Question: What is the importance of Walimah (occasion just after the day of marriage)? Is it very necessary to celeberate it? Is mandha (the occasion just before the day of marriage) allowed in islam?

    Answer ID: 1750Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1466/1147=H)

     

    (1) Walima is a feast that is presented to relatives, the poor and needy people as a token of thanks after nikah and meeting of the bride and groom.

     

    (2) Walima is not wajib (compulsory) but only sunnah.

     

    (3) This is an un-Islamic custom; therefore it should be abandoned.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
