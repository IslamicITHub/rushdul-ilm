    Answer ID: 9651Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2039/336=H/1429)

     

    Seeing a snake in dream is means: enemy. When it did no harm, it means that you will be safe from his harms. A snake in dream sometimes means: one's own nafs (self); since the nafs tries to harm human being by making him involved in sins. So, one should be careful lest it should lead to things against belief and religion. However, keep on doing good deeds and avoiding sins.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
