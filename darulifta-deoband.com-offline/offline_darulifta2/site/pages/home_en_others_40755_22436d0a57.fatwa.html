    Title: Dream

    Question: I have seen a dream consecutively for three days. It is that a snake is going inside a shoe in a Masjid where I make my salah. And the snake is going out after sometime and I am wearing it without checking the shoes. And it is a black hard snake. And the thing is that it is not my shoe it is my colleague's shoe. Is there any reason for this to occur consecutively or it is just a dream.

    Answer ID: 40755Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1425/907/L=1433) Ask your friend to safeguard his nafs and offer salah and recite the holy Quran regularly otherwise he may fall prey of nafs and satan which might throw him away from religion.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
