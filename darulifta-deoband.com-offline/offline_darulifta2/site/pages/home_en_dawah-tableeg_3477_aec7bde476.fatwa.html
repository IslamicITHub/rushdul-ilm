    Title: What is the difference between Tabligh Jamaat and Deobandi as many people suggest that Tabligh Jamaat and Deobandi are two different groups.

    Question: SALAAM, What is the difference between Tabligh Jamaat and Deobandi as many people suggest that Tabligh Jamaat and Deobandi are two different groups. Jazakallah

    Answer ID: 3477Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 274/260=J)

     

    Deobandiyat is a school of thought. Following the creed and beliefs of Ahl-e-Sunnah Wal-Jamat is Deobandiyat. Any group having the same beliefs is Deobandi. Tablighi Jamat is also Deoabndi whether it does not call itself Deobandi or no one calls it so.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
