    Title: I want to know about the dua after farz prayers. Some people says it is bidath. Kindly inform us about this.

    Question: I want to know about the dua after farz prayers. Some people says it is bidath.Kindly inform us about this.

    Answer ID: 26245Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2368/H=829/th=1431)

    The proof of dua after fardh salah is from the holy Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) verbally and practically which are established by ahadith. Thus, it is not bidah (innovation). Those who say it bidah are ignorant.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
