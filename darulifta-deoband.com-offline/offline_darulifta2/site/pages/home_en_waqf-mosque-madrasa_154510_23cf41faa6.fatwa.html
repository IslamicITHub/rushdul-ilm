    Question ID: 154510Country: India

    Title: Using the materials of Eidgah in Masjid

    Question: I want to know that if the floor of a Edgah( which Edgah is used by total four mohallas) becomes broken which was made of bricks. Now can we use these broken pieces of bricks to such a path for repairing, which path (rasta) is used by both peoples of this area. And a masjid is also situated near the Edghah which is made by a Mohalla out of four Mohallas. And the musalli also use this path for going to the masjid. Now is it halal for the people of this mohalla to use the broken pieces of floor of Edgah for repairing of that particular path which is used by both the people of this area and the musalli of that masjid. Please reply elaborately as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 154510Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1529/1495/L=1/1439)

     

    In the above mentioned case, if those bricks can be used in Eidgah or their price after selling can be used in Eidgah then this way can be opted. If both the ways are practical, then it is not lawful to use those bricks in the road.

    لما تقرر أن مراعاة غرض الواقفين واجبة.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
