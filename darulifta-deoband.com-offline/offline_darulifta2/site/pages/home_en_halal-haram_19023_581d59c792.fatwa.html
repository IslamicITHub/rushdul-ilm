    Title: Doctor taking commissions from Diagnostic Centres OR Medical Halls

    Question: Assalaamalaikum, Im a Doctor by profession.I get alot of commission offers from Diagnostics Centres & Medical Hall. Is it permitted to take commissions from Diagnostic Centres OR Medical Halls (for the investigations I'm advicing or medications I'm prescribing).

    Answer ID: 19023Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 152/151/B=1431)

     

    It is not lawful for you to receive the commission of medical hall and diagnostic centre only in return of advice and prescription. Yes, if you undertake some efforts or struggle in the same, you can receive it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
