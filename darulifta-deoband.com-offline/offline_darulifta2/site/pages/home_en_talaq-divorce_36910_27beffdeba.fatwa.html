    Title: I would like to know about talaq given by the husband to his wife only once and the iddah period is going to end within few days and if they do not reconcile before the iddah period can the husband remarry his wife under the Islamic law

    Question: I would like to know about talaq given by the husband to his wife only once and the iddah period is going to end within few days and if they do not reconcile before the iddah period can the husband remarry his wife under the Islamic law?

    Answer ID: 36910Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 345-118/L=1433) In case of one talaq, the husband will have the right to take his wife back until iddah. He can take her back within iddah. In case the iddah passed, then he can remarry her with her consent provided he did not give two talaqs before or after this one talaq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
