    Answer ID: 8280Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1750/1641=D/1429)

     

    You should determine for marriage and look for a match, and when you find a suitable match, you should solemnize nikah and rukhsati according to the Sunnah. For finding a suitable match, you should recite the below duas 100 times each, then recite durood and do dua:

    يا لطيف يا ودود يا جامع

    You should keep reciting this: فسهل يا الهي كل صعب بحرمة سيد الأبرار سهل  

    Also, recite the below dua hundred times with durood before and after in the morning and evening and supplicate to Allah:

    يا حي يا قيوم برحمتك استغيث

    In-sha-Allah, your troubles will go away and you will get appropriate match. We also pray for you. You should not worry and think so much. Allah Almighty will help you. You should keep away from sins and keep performing salah, doing good deeds etc as well as observing hijab.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
