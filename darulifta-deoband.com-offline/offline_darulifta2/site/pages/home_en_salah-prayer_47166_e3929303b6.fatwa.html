    Title: Sajdah Sahv

    Question: Yesterday I was offering Asr salah by Jamah, the Imam did qirah in second rakah by jehri qirah “Alhamdulillahi Rabbil Aalamin" and stopped jehri qirah after this two word. He completed salah without sajdah sahv. I want to ask that salah is completed or not or imam needs to repeat salah?

    Answer ID: 47166Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1227/952/D=10/1434) The salah was valid. Sajdah sahv does not become wajib only by reading one ayah loudly.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
