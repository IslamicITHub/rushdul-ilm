    Title: My wife visits her father's home once in about two months. She stays there for 7 to 10 days. The distance is more than 100 Kilometers. Will she pray qasr at her father's home?

    Question: My wife visits her father's home once in about two months. She stays there for 7 to 10 days. The distance is more than 100 Kilometers. Will she pray qasr at her father's home?

    Answer ID: 158287Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 532/459/D=5/1439)

    If your wife permanently stays with you except seven or ten days in two months, while this is the schedule of the whole year and she visits her parents’ house only for seven or ten days as guest, then in such a case the parental house ceased to be a native place for your wife. If she stays there for less than fifteen days then she shall offer qasr salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
