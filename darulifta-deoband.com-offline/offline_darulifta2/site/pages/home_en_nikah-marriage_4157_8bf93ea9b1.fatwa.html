    Title: I want to ask u one thing regarding i am from india,hyderabad & now i am in dubai from 4 months i am always miss my wife i remind her even in namaz also so can u sujjest me wht i should do to remmember her less(yaad kamm aye) take care.

    Question: Assalam-o-alikum sir i want to ask u one thing regarding i am from india,hyderabad & now i am in dubai from 4 months i am always miss my wife i remind her even in namaz also so can u sujjest me wht i should do to remmember her less(yaad kamm aye) take care. Allah Hafiz!

    Answer ID: 4157Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 258/258=M/1429)

     

    When you are a married man then it is the right of your wife upon you that you should visit her within four months and discharge marital duties, or you should take your wife with you where you live. When she will be with you, you will not miss her, you will feel relaxed. If keeping your wife with you is difficult or you can not visit her easily within four months, then you should return India and work here.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
