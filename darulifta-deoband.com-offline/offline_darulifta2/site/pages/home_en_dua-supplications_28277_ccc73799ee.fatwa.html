    Title: I have 5 years old daughter whenever there is change in weather she suffers from acute allergy and only after strong medication and use of nabulizer she feels better, can you kindly give me some dua which i can recite.

    Question: Assalum Alikum Wa rehmatullah I have 5 years old daughter whenever there is change in weather she suffers from acute allergy and only after strong medication and use of nabulizer she feels better, can you kindly give me some dua which i can recite.

    Answer ID: 28277Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 33/33/M=1432)

    Recite Surah al-Fatiha seven times with durood 11-11 times before and after in the morning and evening and blow on your daughter. Write the following dua and hang it in the neck of your daughter:
    بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم ، أُعيذك بكلماتِ الله التامّات من شَرِّ كُل شيطان وَهَامَّة و عَيْنٍ لَامّة ، بسم الله الذي لَا يَضُر مع اسمه شئٌ في الأرضِ ولَا فى السّمآءِ وهُو السميعُ العَليم .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
