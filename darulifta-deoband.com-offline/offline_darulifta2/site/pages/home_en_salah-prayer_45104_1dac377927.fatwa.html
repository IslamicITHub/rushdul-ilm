    Question ID: 45104Country: India

    Title: Other person joining for jamat.

    Question: I am in UAE and facing following situations. Kindly tell me what should I do in the following situations: 1. Today, after normal Maghrib prayer, I was offering my yesterday's maghrib prayer's qaza. A person came and joined me. Then I started behaving as Imam (saying takbeer loudly etc. so that he can follow). I didn't ask him, but it felt obvious that he was offering his today's Maghrib prayer, not qaza. What should I have done and what should I do in situations when I am offering my qaza namaz/sunnat rakaat and people join me behind? In above case it becomes "another" jamat in the Maszid. Should I continue as single person or I become Imam for them? 2.Here people do multiple times jamats in same masjid for same namaz (not parallel). Something like we are in last rakat's salam phase and then behind in some part of masjid people who got late, form another jamat. What should be my view towards them? Consider them wrong or right? Or should I have "I am not the judge" approach?

    Answer ID: 45104Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 738/1087/N=9/1434) In fardh salah, it is necessary that both the imam and muqtadi are performing same salah: قال فی الدر مع الرد 2/324،325 ط مکتبہ زکریا دیوبند: ولا مفترض بمفترض فرضا آخر لان اتحاد الصلاتین شرط عندنا اھ۔ وفی الرد: قولہ "وبمفترض فرضا آخر": سواء تغایر الفرضان اسماً وصفۃً کمصلی ظھر امس بمصلی ظھر الیوم اھ۔ Likewise, one who performs fardh cannot follow one who is offering sunnah, the salah of muqtadi will not be valid: الدر المختار مع الرد 2/324: ولا مفترض بمنفل اھ۔ So if you were offering any missed Maghrib salah at the time of Maghrib and someone followed you mistaking that you were offering today’s Maghrib, or you were offering sunnah salah and someone followed you thinking that you were offering fardh, their salah will not be valid, they will have to reoffer their salah. And you should also not say the takbeers loudly like an imam at such occasions and tell that person after salah that you were offering missed or sunnah salah so that they can reoffer their salah. (2) It is makrooh tahrimi to perform second or third jamat in the mosque of the locality; those who offer such salah do not get reward of attending jamat rather the rewards decreases as it is makrooh tahrimi. At such occasions, one should do the second jamat outside the premises of the mosque or perform salah individually.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
