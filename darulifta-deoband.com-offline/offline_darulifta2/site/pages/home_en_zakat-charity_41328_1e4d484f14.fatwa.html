    Title: Can I pay my zakah money to the poor student who wants to join a madrasa and become an Islamic scholar (Alim)

    Question: Can I pay my zakah money to the poor student who wants to join a madrasa and become an Islamic scholar (Alim)? If it is not possible to pay zakah, then can I give my interest money to him without the intention of sawab? Same way, can I help my blood sister with any of the money mention above, so that she can provide good education to her kids, because right now her financial condition is not so good.

    Answer ID: 41328Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1395/1394/M=1433) You can give your zakah to a student who is poor and entitled to receive zakah. Likewise you can also give interest money without an intention of reward but it is better not to give interest money to him as it is impure money rather give it to any miserable poor. If your sister deserves the right to receive zakah then you can give both types of the money to her.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
