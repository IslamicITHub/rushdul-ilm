    Title: Regarding Iddah

    Question: If a woman doesn’t follow her iddah even after a year after receiving 3 Talaq and decides to marry another guy then does she have to finish her iddah or she can marry straight because her iddah is already expired?

    Answer ID: 49278Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1940/1414/H=12/1434) The iddah is over if she passes three menstruation periods or delivers a baby. It was sin not to follow all the rulings of iddah and it is necessary to do true taubah and istighfar. However, if she passed her iddah, her nikah with any other man will be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
