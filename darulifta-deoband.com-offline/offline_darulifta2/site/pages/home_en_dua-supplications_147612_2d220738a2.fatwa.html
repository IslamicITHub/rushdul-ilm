    Title: Dua and wazifah to get rid of Vitamin B12

    Question: I want to know how can I get rid of Vitamin B12 deficiency I already consulted a doctor he told to take b12 shots every week till 6 months. I am taking shots since 2 years. When I stop taking shots my feet are burning and I am suffering from constipation. I asked doctor he said your body not accepting b12 through eating meet. Please advice something, so that I can recite to get this cure?

    Answer ID: 147612Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 420/383/SD=4/1438)

    Consult any good Unani physician and undergo treatment, in-shaAllah it shall benefit you. Also do the following:

    After each salah and before going to bed in the state of wudhu, recite surah Alam Nashrah and Surah Quraish 3-3 times with durood 3-3 times before and after. May Allah grant you complete and speedy recovery. Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
