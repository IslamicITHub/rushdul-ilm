    Title: My question is that am i allowed to do sex with my wife only after NIKAH not RUKHSATI. Rukhsati is planned after 3-6 months. If me and my wife are agreed to do so without telling anyone except ALLAH then is it permissible in ISLAM.Reply ASAP Anxiously waiting for the answer.

    Question: Assalam o Alaikum Mufti Sahab, My question is that am i allowed to do sex with my wife only after NIKAH not RUKHSATI. Rukhsati is planned after 3-6 months. If me and my wife are agreed to do so without telling anyone except ALLAH then is it permissible in ISLAM.Reply ASAP Anxiously waiting for the answer. Jazak Allah Khair.

    Answer ID: 6433Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 763/711=L/1429)

     

    It is lawful to have intercourse with wife after nikah before rukhsati. As per the Shariah, there is no wrong in it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
