    Answer ID: 23687Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1288/B=264/tb=1431)

    In the first incident of talaq, there is difference between the spouses regarding the number of talaq. The husband is confessing one talaq while wife refuses it and claims two talaqs. This matter turned controversial. So you should file this case in your nearest Darul Qaza or Shariah Punchayit, whatever decision they come with, follow the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
