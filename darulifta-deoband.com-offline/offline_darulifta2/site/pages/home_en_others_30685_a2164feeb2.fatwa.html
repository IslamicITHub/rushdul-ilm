    Question: Asalamu 'alaikum wa rahmatullah, Dear respected ones, I have had a dream of going to Makkah (with a male- not sure if he was mahram)and all I got to see well is the desert, thereafter we returned without doing Hajj- although I wanted to stay for it. Upon return it was night and it seemed that we had a different house and I think there was some snow. Could you please kindly interpret this dream for me? JazakumAllahu khairan katheeran.

    Answer ID: 30685Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 604/397/H=1432)

    You lack good deeds and sincerity. You should pay special attention towards it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
