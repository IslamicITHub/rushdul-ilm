    Title: Can a husband help to bury his wife by being present in side the grave, can he kiss her forehead from top of the kafn. ZajakAllah Al Khair

    Question: Can a husband help to bury his wife by being present in side the grave, can he kiss her forehead from top of the kafn. ZajakAllah Al Khair

    Answer ID: 17030Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1814/1440/L=1430)

     

    Yes, a husband can help burying his wife being present inside the grave along with the mahram of wife.

    وقال الامام فخرالدين: يكره للناس ان يمنعوا حمل جنازة المرأة لزوجها مع أبيها و اختها ويدخل الزوج فى القبر مع محرمها استحساناً وهو الصحيح و عليه الفتوي (خلاصة الفتاوي: 1/225)

    After the death of wife, husband is not allowed to touch her, so kissing wife's forehead from above the shroud accordingly is not appropriate.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
