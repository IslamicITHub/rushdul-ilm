    Question ID: 3886Country: India

    Title: I want to ask that is purchasing anything in installments under a finance scheme with zero percent intrest allowed in Islam? In these schemes I am not supposed to pay any intrest but the bank or the funding body does take some processing charges.

    Question: I want to ask that is purchasing anything in installments under a finance scheme with zero percent intrest allowed in Islam? In these schemes I am not supposed to pay any intrest but the bank or the funding body does take some processing charges.

    Answer ID: 3886Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 416/382=L)

     

    You did not mention the detail how they take the charges and for what services. However, if the bank or funding body does not take interest in instalment, rather the bank charges some amount in return of their work and services then it will be allowed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
