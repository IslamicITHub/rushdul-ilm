    Title: If husband says to his wife " the TALAQ UPTO YOU , WHENEVER YOU WANT. i want to give talak , but you start it and you wish " now it is whose hand.

    Question: If husband says to his wife " the TALAQ UPTO YOU , WHENEVER YOU WANT. i want to give talak , but you start it and you wish " now it is whose hand.

    Answer ID: 5438Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 677/630=D)

     

    By the abovementioned sentence, the right was transferred to the wife i.e. now she will have the right of divorce and she can use it anytime she likes:

    قال لھا أنت طالق متی شئت و لایتقید بالمجلس ولا تطلق نفسھا الا واحدۃ لأنھا تعم الزمان لا الأفعال فتملک التطلیق فی کل زمان لا تطلیق بعد تطلیق و لھا تفریق الثلاث فی کلما شئت و لاتجمع (شامی: 2/531)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
