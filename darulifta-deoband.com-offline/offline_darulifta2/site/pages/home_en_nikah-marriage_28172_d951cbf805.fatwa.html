    Question: If a woman breastfeed a girl in the odd situation, so in the future could she marry the girl with her son?

    Answer ID: 28172Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 87/85/B=1432)

    They became foster-brother and sister. They cannot marry each other.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
