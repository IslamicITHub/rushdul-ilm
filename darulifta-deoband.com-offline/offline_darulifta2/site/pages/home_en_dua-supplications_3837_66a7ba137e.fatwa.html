    Question: Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem. Assalaamu 'Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu Respected Ulama of Deoband, I need some khusisi amal for getting the money which was taken by me cheatingly by a non-muslim by fake business through internet. I admit and confess to allah s.w.t about my misktakes for which i am repenting till date and have done tauba. If there is any dua by which the money can retrieved. Please inform me the at the earliest. Zakumullah khairan kasiran

    Answer ID: 3837Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 390/366=L)

     

    Recite the below verse of the Quran:

    ومن الناس من يتخذ من دون الله أندادا يحبونهم كحب الله والذين آمنوا أشد حبا لله ولو يرى الذين ظلموا إذ يرون العذاب أن القوة لله جميعا وأن الله شديد العذاب

    Recite: إنا لله وإنا إليه راجعون

    Also, recite سورۃ الم نشرح after every obligatory salah seven times. In-sha-Allah, you will get the money back, if not then you will get something better than that.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
