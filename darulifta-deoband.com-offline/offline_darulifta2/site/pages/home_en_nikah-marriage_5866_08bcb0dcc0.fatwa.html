    Question: Meri Bibi Ek Din kikehe kar Apne Bap ke ghar gayi thi lekin meri bibi loot kar nahi aai dusre din mai apni bibi ko bulane gaya to bibi ke bap n kehdiyabhi nai behejege to maine apne mamu ko bulana bheja tobibi ke bap ne kehe diya ke 15,20,din bad bhejege tomasla ye hai ke mai apni bibiko apne ghar apne sath rakhoo ya nahi Isme koyi Islami Masla to nahi hai 15,20,din bad shohar ki bina marzee bina Ijazat 15,20 ghar se shohar se door rahee

    Answer ID: 5866Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1039/924=B/1429)

     

    If a woman is away from her husband?s house without his permission, then she is nashizah (disobedient). In this case, the husband will not be liable to pay her maintenance. This act of a woman is utterly disgusting in the eye of Shariah:

    وان نشزت فلا نفقۃ لھا حتی تعود الی منزلہ (عالمگیری: 1/545) عن طلق بن علی قال: قال رسول اللہ صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم: اذا الرجل دعا زوجتہ لحاجتہ فلتأتہ و ان کانت علی التنور (الترمذی: 1/219)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
