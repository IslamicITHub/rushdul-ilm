    Title: I have no job or any rozgar from last 2 years

    Question: I have no job or any rozgar from last 2 years. I have no source of income and I feel very bad sometime. I am in a very big worry after 1 month my sister is going to get married also. Alhamdulillah, I offer salah regularly. I tried for job or to start small business but there is no success. Please tell me any dua that help me for being wealthy.

    Answer ID: 34906Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1662/1139/L=1432) You along with all your family members should recite Sura Waqiyah after Maghrib salah. Also recite يا مغنى 1111 times after Isha salah with durood 11-11 times before and after.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
