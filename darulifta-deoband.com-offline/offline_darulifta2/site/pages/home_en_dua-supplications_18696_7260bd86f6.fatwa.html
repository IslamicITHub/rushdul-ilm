    Question ID: 18696Country: India

    Title: Can you please suggest a dua for my sister who is trapped in a non-muslim's hand. my family is completely worried abt this.

    Question: Dear Mufti Saab, As-salam alaikum, Can you please suggest a dua for my sister who is trapped in a non-muslim's hand. my family is completely worried abt this. please help us and give some dua's with which she would become proper and start walkign in the right path.

    Answer ID: 18696Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 97/97=B=1431)

     

    Recite durood 100 times, لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله العلي العظيم 500 times daily for fourteen days and supplicate to Allah. In-shaAllah, you will get out of this trouble.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
