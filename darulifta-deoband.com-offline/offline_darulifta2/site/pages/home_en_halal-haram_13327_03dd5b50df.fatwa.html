It means that i arrange financing for people who want to buy a house but do not have money and i arrange loan for them from bank and i get commission from the bank.     Answer ID: 13327Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 947/889=B/1430)

     

    It is sinful and unlawful, you should look for any other job.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
