    Title: Is wearing of stones like neelam pukhraj etc for men is allow in islam or not ?

    Question: is wearing of stones like neelam pukhraj etc for men is allow in islam or not ???

    Answer ID: 26077Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1770/1401/B=1431)

    The ring of stones other than agate and jasper (aqiq and yashb) is not lawful for males. However, one can set any kind of gem inside silver ring.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
