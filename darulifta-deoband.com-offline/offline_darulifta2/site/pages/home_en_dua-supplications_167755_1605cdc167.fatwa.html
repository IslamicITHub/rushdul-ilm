    Title: If someone is lost which Dua should be recited for his welfare and comeback?

    Question: If someone is lost which Dua should be recited for his welfare and comeback?

    Answer ID: 167755Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 482/428/H=05/1440)

     

    After each salah and at the time of going to bed, read انا لله وانا اليه راجعون eleven times with durood 11-11 times before and after, and keep supplicating for wellbeing and recovery.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
