    Title: Islamic Wazifah for Healing from Psoriasis

     

     

    Question: Recently I had been recently diagnosed with Psorasis and I am 26 years old. I need wazifah to cure the disease from the blessing of Allah. Please my question confidential.

    Answer ID: 165899Posted on: Feb 26, 2025

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 147/121/M=03/1440)

     

    Undergo treatment of any expert doctor or hakim and read the below verse and blow on you:

     

    وَأَيُّوبَ إِذْ نَادَى رَبَّهُ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ الضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ فَاسْتَجَبْنَا لَهُ فَكَشَفْنَا مَا بِهِ مِن ضُرٍّ وَآتَيْنَاهُ أَهْلَهُ وَمِثْلَهُم مَّعَهُمْ رَحْمَةً مِّنْ عِندِنَا وَذِكْرَى لِلْعَابِدِينَ [الأنبياء : 84]

     

    May Allah cure you, Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
