I would like to know are these habits halaal in Islam? Because in reality you are poisoning your body just like you would by drinking alcohol. Also, many cigarettes contain alcohol so is it ok to tell brothers to not smoke for this reason? (2) Finally, what is the ruling on opiates in Islam? Jazakallah Khair

    Answer ID: 12726Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1114/863=H/1430)

     

    There is no wrong to convince Muslim brothers wisely of the harms of the tobacco and other things.

    As far as opiates is concerned, it is intoxicant; therefore it is unlawful to consume.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
