    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 911/911=M/1430)

     

    If you want Allah to help you, you must help the religion of Allah, do not commit any negligence towards Shariah matters. The best amal is to perform five-time salah regularly.     Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
