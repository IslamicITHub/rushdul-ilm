    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 266/266=M/1430)

     

    The Molvi sahib is right. In response to a non-Muslim?s greeting one should only say: wa?alaik or hadakAllah (ھداک اللہ).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
