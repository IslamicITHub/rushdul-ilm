    Title: Yesterday I saw a dream about one lion and one bear, both came to attack me but I closed the door quickly

    Question: Yesterday I saw a dream about one lion and one bear, both came to attack me but I closed the door quickly, after sometime I opened the door and checked if they are still there or not. Then suddenly bear forward his hand in the door, again I closed the door and his hand was inside the door, I forced the door and the bear take his hand outside. Can you say what the meaning of it is? Please pray for me. My parents are going for hajj this year pray for them too.

    Answer ID: 39801Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1010/613/L=1433) Your dream is nice. In-shaAllah, you shall be saved from enemies and jealous people. Be punctual in salah, recitation and zikr etc. May Almighty Allah bless your parents with accepted and blessed Hajj!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
