    Title: Breaking of jadu...

    Question: My name is Mohammad Naseeruddin (from Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh) I got married on 25 Shawwal 1435, after my marriage within 2 months my wife elder brother is gone from home. It has been almost one year crossed but he did not came back, my wife is saying somebody has done jadu on him (within her relatives only, regarding property issues). Nobody knows where he is. My wife is too sad regarding this matter, she is not at all happy, always thinking about her brother. I want to meet someone who can bring her elder brother back. But I don't know how to approach, please refer me somebody’s name who can break the jadu on him so that he will come back home. Please tell me someone’s name who can solve this issue, and if possible gives his contact number so that I can interact with him. Please help me to get out of this problem.

    Answer ID: 63073Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 361/349/L=04/1437) Darul Ifta only guides in Islamic rulings. However, ask your wife to recite Ya Mueed (يا معيد) 111 times daily focusing that Allah will return your lost brother, in-shaAllah he will come back soon.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
