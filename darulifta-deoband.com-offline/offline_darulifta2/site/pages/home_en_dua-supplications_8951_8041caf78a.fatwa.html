    Question ID: 8951Country: India

    Title: I am looking for job for many years and i have debt, i regular practicing salatul haja but could not find any job or employment please provide any wazifa for me i am very needy, and i i also want to perfome haj i can not afford it.

    Question: asalamualykum sir I am looking for job for many years and i have debt, i regular practicing salatul haja but could not find any job or employment please provide any wazifa for me i am very needy, and i i also want to perfome haj i can not afford it.

    Answer ID: 8951Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2014/1864=D/1429)

     

    You should recite the below dua in the morning and evening (after Fajr and Asr):

    اللھم اکفنی بحلالک عن حرامک و اغننی بفضلک عمن سواک

    Also, recite ya Lateef (یا لطیف) 1100 times with durood 11 times before and after. You needs shall be fulfilled and the debt shall be paid easily, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
