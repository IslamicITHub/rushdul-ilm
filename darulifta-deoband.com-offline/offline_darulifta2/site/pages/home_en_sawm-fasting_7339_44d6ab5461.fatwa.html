    Title: My name is Mohammad Irshad I need to know whether it's allowed to go for Tableeg (10 Days) in Ramadaan Month. If we go then tarveeh would be distrubed.

    Question: Assalaamuleyikum, My name is Mohammad Irshad I need to know whether it's allowed to go for Tableeg (10 Days) in Ramadaan Month. If we go then tarveeh would be distrubed. Jazaakumulllah!

    Answer ID: 7339Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 862/862=M/1429)

     

    There is no wrong in going out in Tabligh if there is any hafiz with you. Perform the tarawih behind the hafiz, or the Jamat stays in a mosque where you perform the tarawih in a mosque and stay there until the Quran completes. If there is no such arrangement, then you should complete the Quran anywhere in tarawih and then go out for Tabligh, but keep on performing the tarawih whenever you go as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
