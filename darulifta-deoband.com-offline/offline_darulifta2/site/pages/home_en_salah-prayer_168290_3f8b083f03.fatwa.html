    Title: How to perform salah in a vehicle (Qiblah, sajdah, ruku) and should I perform qaza of that salah afterwards?

    Question: How to perform salah in a vehicle (Qiblah, sajdah, ruku) and should I perform qaza of that salah afterwards?

    Answer ID: 168290Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 548/492/M=05/1440)

     

    Think in your heart about Qiblah i.e. consider the signs and other indications to determine the direction of the Qiblah; if there is someone who knows the direction then ask him, then after thinking in heart whichever direction the heart tells to face turn to that direction. If you are boarding any vehicle then get down and offer salah on some suitable place. If you are in train, bus etc and it is not in your hand to stop it then you may offer salah on that vehicle. If you are able to offer salah with qiyam, ruku, sajdah then offer salah with qiyam, ruku, sajdah and there is no need to repeat it later. Otherwise, offer salah at that time as you can and later on re-perform it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
