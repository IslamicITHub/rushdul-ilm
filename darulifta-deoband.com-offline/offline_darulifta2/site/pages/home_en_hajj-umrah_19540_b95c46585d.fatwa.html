    Title: I am having Home loan and monthly bank gets its EMI through my salary. my question is if i have money to perform Hajj can i perform hajj ? is there any any requirement that until you complete your loan you can't do Hajj ?

    Question: I am having Home loan and monthly bank gets its EMI through my salary. my question is if i have money to perform Hajj can i perform hajj ? is there any any requirement that until you complete your loan you can't do Hajj ?

    Answer ID: 19540Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 188/33/TL=1431)

     

    You can perform hajj if you can afford it, your hajj shall be valid.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
