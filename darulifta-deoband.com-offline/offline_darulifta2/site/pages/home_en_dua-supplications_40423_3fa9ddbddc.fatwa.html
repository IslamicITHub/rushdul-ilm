    Question ID: 40423Country: India

    Title: My name is Mumtaz. I am 24 years old girl. I am facing problem regarding my nikah

    Question: My name is Mumtaz. I am 24 years old girl. I am facing problem regarding my nikah. I am not getting a suitable match. I get lots of proposal but always have some problem. Please tell me some dua to recite for it.

    Answer ID: 40423Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1150/740/H=1433) Recite the following dua 11 times after each salah with durood 11-11 times before and after and supplicate Almighty Allah: رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَاماً Also do the same practice before going to bed in the state of wudhu.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
