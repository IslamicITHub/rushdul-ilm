    Title: Nikah

    Question: My grandmother has given me milk so she is my suckling mother, that makes my aunty my suckling sister, is it permissible for me to marry my aunty's daughter who is my cousin? She has not had any milk at all.

    Answer ID: 47985Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1262/1262/M=11/1434) If your grandmother suckled you while you were a baby then she became your foster mother and your phuphi became your foster sister and her daughter will be your foster Bhanji. Hence your nikah is not lawful with your phupi’s daughter as it is unlawful the nikah of real Mamu and Bhanji in the same way it is also unlawful for foster as the holy Prophet (صلى الله عليه وسلم) said: يحرم من الرضاع ما يحرم من النسب.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
