    Title: My question is that is it allowed to wear a taveez if yes kindly tell me we are entering in bathroom and wash room along with that taveez is not it a sin. Please clearify.

    Question: My question is that is it allowed to wear a taveez if yes kindly tell me we are entering in bathroom and wash room along with that taveez is not it a sin. Please clearify.

    Answer ID: 5237Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 783/708=D/1429)

     

    It is allowed to wear ­taweez of Quranic verses and Duas mentioned in Hadith. If it is sewn in cloth and packed with wax, then one can go to bathroom or washroom while wearing it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
