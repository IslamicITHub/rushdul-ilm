    Title: I have no issue and and through medical process, i have transmitted my semen to the sister of my wife with his consent who is also married now please inform me that it is right or not as per shariah.

    Question: assalamualykum, I have no issue and and through medical process, i have transmitted my semen to the sister of my wife with his consent who is also married now please inform me that it is right or not as per shariah.

    Answer ID: 7621Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1880/1570=B/1429)

     

    There is no wrong if the semen of husband was inserted into his wife directly or through medical process, but transmitting it to any woman other than wife is obscenity and shamelessness, thus it is unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
