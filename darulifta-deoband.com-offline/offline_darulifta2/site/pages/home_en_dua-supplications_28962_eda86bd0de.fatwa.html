    Answer ID: 28962Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 186/176/B=1432)

    Consult any religious and reliable aamil. Recite Surah al-Falaq and Surah al-Naas after each salah and blow on you.
