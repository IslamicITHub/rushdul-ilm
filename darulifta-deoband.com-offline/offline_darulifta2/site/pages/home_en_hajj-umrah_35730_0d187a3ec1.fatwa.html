    Question ID: 35730Country: India

    Title: I and my wife have done Hajj this year by the grace of Allah. I have some doubts

    Question: Alhamdulillah, I and my wife have done Hajj this year by the grace of Allah. May Almighty Allah accept the Hajj of Hajis! Amen! I have some doubts: (1) While I was in hajj Ihram I covered my head with Ihram chuddar and one time with cap by mistake it was for one minute or less then one minute, any how I gave 10 Riyals in sadaqah. Is it ok or need something else. (2) I touched my wife after tawaf ziyarah and head shaved assuming that we are out of Ihram or we have to wait still two days Ramee and two Mina nights to come out from hajj Ihram? (3) After alwida-tawaf we started to Madina in bus, near to Madina myself and my wife involved in touching, and during these my mani came out and once I reached Madina hotel I performed ghusl and went Masjid Nabawi. What I have done is it haram or sinful act, please clear I do not want to spoil my hajj. (4) During the periods of woman if husband plays with wife and sperm (mani) comes out, shall it be haram to do so? (5) What are the things that need to be done after hajj?

    Answer ID: 35730Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 38/17/D=1433) (1) Yes in this case, only sadqah was wajib on you i.e. 1.633 Kg wheat or its price. It was already paid by ten Riyals. Now you have to do nothing. (2) After head shaving and tawaf ziyarah, wife is halal though yet the Rami of two days was pending. Hence you did not do any thing contradictory to hajj. (3) After tawaf wida while going to Madina you were not in the state of Ihram hence the forbiddances of Ihram were not applicable to you and it was lawful for you to kiss your wife as well as have intercourse. Hence what you did was not a sin. Do not have any doubt. In-sha-Allah, your hajj shall be accepted. (4) It is not haram, rather it is allowed in excessive lust. (5) Be regular in all worships, offer salah with jamah, keep away yourself from sins in particular avoid watching obscene things on TV etc, earn halal livelihood and avoid haram earning. Be regular in reciting Durood and doing zikr as well as recitation of the holy Quran.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
