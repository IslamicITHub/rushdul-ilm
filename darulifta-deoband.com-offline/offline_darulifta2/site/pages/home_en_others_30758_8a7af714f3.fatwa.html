    Title: I was dreaming i was in a big field with Hazarat Isa (AS) PBUH on one side and myself and Hazarat Muhammad (S.A.W) PBUH on the other,

    Question: I was dreaming i was in a big field with Hazarat Isa (AS) PBUH on one side and myself and Hazarat Muhammad (S.A.W) PBUH on the other, i was saying to Hazarat Isa (AS) good luck with the battle in the future and then Hazarat Muhammad (S.A.W) was waiting behind me on an angel and i got on and we started to fly up to space and i was reading the 1st kalimah all the way and i felt that i was about to pass away peace fully but i couldn't and i opened my eyes and saw the earth but then with Hazarat Muhammad (S.A.W) on the angel, i started going down back to earth but we went in the sea but i could still breathe under water and i went to a place with a big bed somebody made out of earth and straw and there was some writing explaining something to me which i can't remember. I felt that i could not go to Jannah without doing something first. What does this mean?

    Answer ID: 30758Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 607/D/70/k=1432) The last part of the dream is its meaning i.e. a man will not enter into paradise merely by longing and wishing, rather he is required to be stable in good deeds with sincerity and to avoid sins and forbidden things completely. The dream indicates and warns against the same.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
