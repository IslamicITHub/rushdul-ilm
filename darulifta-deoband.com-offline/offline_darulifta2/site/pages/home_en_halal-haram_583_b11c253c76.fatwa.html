    Title: Is it permissable in islam to use tabboco in pan?

    Question: Assalamu alaikum, is it permissable in islam to use tabboco in pan?

    Answer ID: 583Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 549/J)

     

    Using tobacco is permissible in pan (betel leaf). Some ulama regard it as Makrooh-e-Tanzihi (undesirable) but most ulama do not regard it as Makrooh also.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
