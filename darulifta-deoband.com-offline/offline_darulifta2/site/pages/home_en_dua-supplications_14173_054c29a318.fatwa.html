    Title: ASAKM,my sister in america is very serious but her medical reports say's she has no problem.but her health is falling day by day. can u pl suggest urgently any Quranic amal that we should try or True AALIM (we r doubtful that some sahr (sifli ilm) should not have been done on her.

    Question: ASAKM,my sister in america is very serious but her medical reports say's she has no problem.but her health is falling day by day. can u pl suggest urgently any Quranic amal that we should try or True AALIM (we r doubtful that some sahr (sifli ilm) should not have been done on her.

    Answer ID: 14173Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1109/1046=B/1430)

     

    Here is one amal, acts upon it; in-sha-Allah you will get shifa.

    Having written down Surah Fatihah with Bismillah on enamelware (chini plate) with saffron water, wash the plate with honey mixed water and give it to your sister for drinking early morning. Do the same for 41 days.

    May Allah grant complete shifa to your sister! Amen

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
