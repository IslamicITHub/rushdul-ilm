    Title: I am a male can I watch video bayanat of male orator?

    Question: I am a male can I watch video bayanat of male orator?

    Answer ID: 152189Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1119/1027/H=9/1438)

     

    It is not free from sin for a male to see the picture of a male unnecessarily. If there is nothing objectionable in the speech and the speaker is reliable and authentic then there is no harm in listening the audio speech and gain benefit.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
