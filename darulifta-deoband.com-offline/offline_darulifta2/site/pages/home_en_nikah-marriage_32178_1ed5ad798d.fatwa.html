    Title: I heard from someone that nikah can not happen(not valid) if a woman is in state of menses...is it true?

    Question: I heard from someone that nikah can not happen(not valid) if a woman is in state of menses...is it true?

    Answer ID: 32178Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 900/900/M=1432) It is not right. It is not a condition for nikah to be valid that the woman is clean from menstruating period.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
