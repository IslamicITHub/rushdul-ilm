    Title: question regarding TV

    Question: I am planning to open a lodge/guest house. Is it permissible to keep TV in the rooms?

    Answer ID: 61267Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1083/1083/M=11/1436) At present TV is an equipment of amusement and play. Most of its channel and programmes include immodesty, nudity and illegal pictures which is not lawful to watch as per the Shariah. Hence it is not lawful to install TV in rooms.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
