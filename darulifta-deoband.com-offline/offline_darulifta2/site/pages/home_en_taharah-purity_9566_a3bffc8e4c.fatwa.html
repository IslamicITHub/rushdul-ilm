    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 343/D=48/K/1430)

     

    You should have a separate cloth and use it while doing istinja. Urinate at least half an hour before salah; engage yourself in your business. Then, make wudhu and spray water on the cloth. Do have fall prey of doubt and pray with contentment of heart. We should know that doubt is a kind of disease.

    (2) Urinate at least half an hour before wudhu, and this is because you fall very much in doubt.

    (3) Do not pay any attention to doubt.

    (4) Keep a separate cloth for urinating or keep cotton inside penis. The wudhu will remain intact until the outer side of cotton gets wet. If you have doubt, take out the cotton, make wudhu and perform salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
