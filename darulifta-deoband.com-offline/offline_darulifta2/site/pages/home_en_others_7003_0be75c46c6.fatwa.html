    Title: I would like to ask that do all dreams have meanings? i often see things in my dream that i saw the day before. could this have a meaning? do ALL dreams have meanings?

    Question: I would like to ask that do all dreams have meanings? i often see things in my dream that i saw the day before. could this have a meaning? do ALL dreams have meanings? jazakallah

    Answer ID: 7003Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1786/1385=H/1429)

     

    The human mind is the store of different thoughts. Sometimes, the forms seen in the day are manifested with these thoughts which are transferred by the Imagination Power the soul. This causes those scenes to appear in the dreams. Such dreams need no interpretation.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
