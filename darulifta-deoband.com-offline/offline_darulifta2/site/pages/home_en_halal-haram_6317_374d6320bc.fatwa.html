    Title: If i want to purchase television & i just want to watch sports channels for games & news channels, is it right or not, please tell me, i am awaiting your answer.

    Question: If i want to purchase television & i just want to watch sports channels for games & news channels, is it right or not, please tell me, i am awaiting your answer.

    Answer ID: 6317Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 553/553=M/1429)

     

    Purchasing television for the above reasons is also unlawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
