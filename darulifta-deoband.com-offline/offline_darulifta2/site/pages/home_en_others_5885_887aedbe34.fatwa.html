    Title: I saw a dream on 28/05/08 at 4.30 am i saw that three tigers have lept in my room they are harmless but then too i am scared of them iam trying to ward them off by flickering a torch in their eyes please interpret it.

    Question: asak. I saw a dream on 28/05/08 at 4.30 am i saw that three tigers have lept in my room they are harmless but then too i am scared of them iam trying to ward them off by flickering a torch in their eyes please interpret it.

    Answer ID: 5885Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1520/1154=H/1429)

     

    The nafs and Satan have been illustrated in the dream like tigers. There is an indication that it is easier to chase them away by following sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
