    Question ID: 37087Country: Bangladesh

    Title: My elder brother asked my mother about his wife, "I divorced her; I have no relation with her

    Question: My elder brother asked my mother about his wife, "I divorced her; I have no relation with her. He again said over cell phone to my mother, "Swear on Allah I divorced her. Again he asked my cousin over phone, "I divorced her. It is noted that he was drunken on that time. Would you please let me know divorced took place or not?

    Answer ID: 37087Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 422/422/M=1433) When your elder brother confirms that he has given talaq to his wife then the talaq took place. The talaq will take place as per the number and type of talaqs which he gave.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
