    Title: A person had said to me reciting surah Rahman 11 times and make dua and dua will comes true or accepted. Is it true?

    Question: A person had said to me reciting surah Rahman 11 times and make dua and dua will comes true or accepted. Is it true?

    Answer ID: 152397Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1098/1033/B=11/1438)

    We could not find such things in Hadith. However if you supplicate after reciting any part of the Holy Quran then  your prayer shall be answered, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
