    Title: If someone wrote 3 talaqs for her wife

    Question: If someone wrote 3 talaqs for her wife and signed it but after this did not hand over to his wife, in this case Talaq happened or not? Please help me.

    Answer ID: 34876Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1641/1122/L=1432) Yes, the talaq took place. ثم المرسومة لا تخلوا ما ان أرسل الطلاق بأن كتب أما بعد فأنت طالق فكما كتب هذا يقع الطلاق وتلزمها العدة من وقت الكتابة. (عالمگیریگ 1/378)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
