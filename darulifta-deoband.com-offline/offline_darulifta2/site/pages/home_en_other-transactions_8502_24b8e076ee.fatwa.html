    Question ID: 8502Country: Pakistan

    Title: Is use of Credit Card is permissible in Islam? What if I don't pay interest on it and pay the dues within given time period?

    Question: Assalam O Alaikum, Is use of Credit Card is permissible in Islam? What if I don't pay interest on it and pay the dues within given time period? Thanks.

    Answer ID: 8502Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1879/1755=D/1429)

     

    It is allowable to use credit card, if the debt is paid before the interest is levied then it is lawful.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
