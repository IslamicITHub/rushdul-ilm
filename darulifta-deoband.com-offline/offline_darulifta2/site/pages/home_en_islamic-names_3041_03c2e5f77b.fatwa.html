    Title: What does the name Abbas mean? I know it was the name of our beloved Nabi (Sallallahu 'Alaihi wa Sallam), but what does that name mean?

    Question: Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem. Assalaamu 'Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu, What does the name Abbas mean? I know it was the name of our beloved Nabi (Sallallahu 'Alaihi wa Sallam), but what does that name mean? Please answer as soon as possible. Jazakallah khairan wassalaamu 'alaikum.

    Answer ID: 3041Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1029/1029=J)

     

    Abbas was the name of the uncle of the Prophet (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam), this means ?lion?. It has other meanings as well.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
