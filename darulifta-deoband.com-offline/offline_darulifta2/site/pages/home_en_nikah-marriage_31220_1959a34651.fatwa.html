    Title: A person has married and after 10 days of his marry his mother tells the family members at home that she has feed the girl in child hood, what are the important way and rules to solve the issue.

    Question: A person has married and after 10 days of his marry his mother tells the family members at home that she has feed the girl in child hood, what are the important way and rules to solve the issue, please deeply describe the issue, and send the stamped fatwa in URDU Language.

    Answer ID: 31220Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 571/571/M=1432) Why the matter of breastfeeding was raised after ten days of marriage? Why this matter was not discussed before the marriage or at the time of marriage? The certification of a single woman is not enough for the confirmation of affinity. Hence, no definite ruling of hurmat shall be applicable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
