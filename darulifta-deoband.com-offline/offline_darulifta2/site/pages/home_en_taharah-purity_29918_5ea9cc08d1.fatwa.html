    Title: I have an embarassing question to ask however it is important for me to know.My wife is about to give birth in the next few weeks.We have not had intercourse for a while and I know we will not have intercourse till a few weeks after the baby is born.What can I or my wife do to satisfy me so that I don't stray from my wife?

    Question: Asalaamuailaikum I have an embarassing question to ask however it is important for me to know.My wife is about to give birth in the next few weeks.We have not had intercourse for a while and I know we will not have intercourse till a few weeks after the baby is born.What can I or my wife do to satisfy me so that I don't stray from my wife? Please help. jzk

    Answer ID: 29918Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 431/236/D=1432)

    It is forbidden to have intercourse after birth until the blood discharge stops. However, it is not necessary to stay back for forty days. If the blood stops before this period though within one week, and intercourse is not injurious for the woman, then it shall be lawful to have intercourse after ghusl.
    أقل النفاس ما يوجد ولو ساعة و عليه الفتوى وأكثره أربعون (هنديه) يمنع صلاة و صوماً وجماعاً أي يحرمه ، و حكمه أي النفاس كالحيض في كل شئ الخ . (درمختار مع الشامى: 1/492-496 كتاب الطهارة ط: زكريا ديوبند

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
