    Title: I want to know whether cow dung and human excreta are allowed to be used as mannure ( khad ) in crops and can we use food from such crops?

    Question: assalamu alaikum, i want to know whether cow dung and human excreta are allowed to be used as mannure ( khad ) in crops and can we use food from such crops.

    Answer ID: 24452Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1492/1164/B=1431)

     

    When the dung of animals and human excrement mix with soil, they turn into manure. It is lawful to use such manure in fields. However, it is lawful to consume the grains that are produced through this manure.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
