    Question: Followers of All the Semitic religions believe on the Day of Judgment, what does Islamic consent about the Day of Judgment?

    Answer ID: 1116Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1063=804/H)

     

    According to scores of Quranic verses and Hadiths, the belief of Qiyamah (Doomsday/Day of Judgement) is one of the most important beliefs of Islam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
