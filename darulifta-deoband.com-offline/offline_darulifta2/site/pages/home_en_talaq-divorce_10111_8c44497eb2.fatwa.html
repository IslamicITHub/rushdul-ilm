    Title: if any muslim person divorce his wife thrice times after that she will remains her wife his wife or not if he wnts keep her as wife

    Question: if any muslim person divorce his wife thrice times after that she will remains her wife his wife or not if he wnts keep her as wife

    Answer ID: 10111Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 30/30=D/1429)

     

    In case of giving three clear divorces, the wife becomes divorced with mughallaza bayinah, the marital relation between the spouse shall come to a complete end. Having passed her iddah, she can marry anywhere she likes. She can not marry the first husband without Halalah Shariyah and will no longer remain his wife, so it is haram to keep her as wife, both should live separately now with purdah (like strangers).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
