    Title: One of my friends, Ahmed committed a mistake two weeks ago

    Question: One of my friends, Ahmed committed a mistake two weeks ago. He committed adultery with one of his mahram women. Now he felt very upset, but both are married person. Due to bad time he did so. Could you please advise what can they do in future? But I frankly said to them, please do istighfar. Hope in future they are not doing it next time in-sha-Allah.

    Answer ID: 36641Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 287/L=186/TL=1433) Now they should do taubah and istighfar wholeheartedly. Also they should make firm intention not to repeat this act in future. Also they should completely avoid meeting and talking etc. This is the kaffarah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
