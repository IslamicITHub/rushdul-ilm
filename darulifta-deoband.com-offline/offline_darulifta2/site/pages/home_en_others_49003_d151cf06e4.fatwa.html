    Title: Regarding Performance of Salat behind imam teaching girls without veil

    Question: If a person teaches in a school/college with co-education system in front of matured students, then whether he can be made 'Imam' to perform Fard Salath (Namaz)?

    Answer ID: 49003Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1678/1387/B=12/1434) If he teaches grownup girls without a veil, then it is a sin and such a person should not be appointed as imam of salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
