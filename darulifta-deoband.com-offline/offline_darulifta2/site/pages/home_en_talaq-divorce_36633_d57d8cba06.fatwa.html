    Question ID: 36633Country: India

    Title: My husband yesterday told me that he told me talaq thrice in the month of June 2011. He is lying. He has not told anything like that

    Question: My husband yesterday told me that he told me talaq thrice in the month of June 2011. He is lying. He has not told anything like that. I have an audio tape in which he is telling me to agree for a mutual separation in the month of September. There is also no witness present. Can this be considered as a valid talaq?

    Answer ID: 36633Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 273/156/H=1433) As talaq takes place by giving, in the same way it also takes place if husband admits it, though the sentence is false. Thus in the question mentioned above, though there is no witness then also talaq mughallazah took place.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
