    Title: namaz

    Question: What are the timings of chast salah?

    Answer ID: 55269Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1633/1364/B=10/1435) The time of chast salah starts 20 minutes after sunrise and remains until Zawal. However, it is better and mustahab to offer it when one fourth day is passed.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
