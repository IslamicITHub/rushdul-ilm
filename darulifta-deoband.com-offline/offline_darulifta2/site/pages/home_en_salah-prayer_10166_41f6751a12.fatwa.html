    Title: I am following hanafi madhab. Please inform me whether I have to recite sura fatiha and zam sura during zohar and asar jamath prayers or I have to keep silent after reciting sana.

    Question: I am following hanafi madhab. Please inform me whether I have to recite sura fatiha and zam sura during zohar and asar jamath prayers or I have to keep silent after reciting sana.

    Answer ID: 10166Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 64/64=M/1429)

     

    Stand silently after reciting sana, do not recite Surah Fatihah and other surah while following an imam.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
