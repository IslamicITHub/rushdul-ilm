    Title: Is it allowed to Pray Sunnath after 30 min from the Completion of Farz Namaz.

    Question: Is it allowed to Pray Sunnath after 30 min from the Completion of Farz Namaz.

    Answer ID: 31019Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 483/483/M=1432) The unnecessary delay in offering sunnah salah after fardh is makrooh. It is mentioned in hadith that the holy Prophet Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) used to make a delay only equal to this dua اللهم انت السلام الخ after salam. The gap of 30 minutes is too long. One should offer sunnah immediately after the fardh.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
