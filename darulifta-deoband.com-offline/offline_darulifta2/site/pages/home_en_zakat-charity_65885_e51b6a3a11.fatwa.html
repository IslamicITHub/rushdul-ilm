    Title: Can a person give zakah to his teacher (Ustad)?

    Question: Can a person give zakah to his teacher (Ustad)? An earlier reply would be much appreciated.

    Answer ID: 65885Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 967/994/L=09/1437)

    If the teacher is poor and eligible for receiving zakah then a student may give zakah to his teacher. However, it is better that instead of giving zakah amount to a teacher he supports him by other ways.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
