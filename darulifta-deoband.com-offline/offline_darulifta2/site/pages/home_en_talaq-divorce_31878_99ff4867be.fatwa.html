    Answer ID: 31878Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 936/H=318/th=1432) In view of the said condition and its detail, it is better to settle the matter. But, if there is no way out at all then you should hush up the matter after giving only one talaq, not more than one talaq.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
