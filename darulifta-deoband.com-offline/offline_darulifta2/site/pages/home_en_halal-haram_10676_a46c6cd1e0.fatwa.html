    Answer ID: 10676Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 229/166=H/1429)

     

    You must obey your parents and stop speaking altogether with the conductor. It is deception from Satan and nafs; they instigate you that you cannot forget him.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
