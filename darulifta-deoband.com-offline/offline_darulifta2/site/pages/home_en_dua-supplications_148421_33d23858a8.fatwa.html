    Title: I will recently give my final exam so please tell me any dua or wazifah so I perform better in papers and got good marks.

    Question: I will recently give my final exam so please tell me any dua or wazifah so I perform better in papers and got good marks.

    Answer ID: 148421Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 320/300/N=5/1438)

    Recite Ya Nasir (يا ناصر) 21 times after each salah, you shall have a convenient exam and will secure good marks, in-shaAllah. May you secure high marks! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
