    Title: Dream regarding making Wudhu and car brake

    Question: 1. I saw dream that I am making wudhu and after completing my wudhu, I come out of wash-room. My parents are in my house (actually I am in Jeddah and they are in Hyderabad). They ask me to meet my cousin brother but when I see him, he is not my cousin and he is some stranger. My wife also comes and sits along-with my parents. I am upset why she has come in front of non-mahram. 2. Many times I see dream that I am driving car but when I try to put brake, brake is loose and car does not stop completely. And car keeps moving/running.

    Answer ID: 50282Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 259/222/L=04/1435) These are satanic dreams. You should recite Ayatal Kursi and four Quls before going to bed and blow on your hands and pass your hands over your whole body. Do this thrice. Also try to go to sleep with wudhu and lay on your right side facing Qiblah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
