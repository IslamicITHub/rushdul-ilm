    Title: How to get divorce when the woman is pregnan

    Question: How to get divorce when the woman is pregnant? Please give me right decision, as soon as possible.

    Answer ID: 34951Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1664/1341/B=1432) If there is no way of settlement between husband and wife then due to severe compulsion you can give her talaq in the state of pregnancy as well. You should give only one talaq. When the child is born her iddah shall be over. Husband shall be responsible for her maintenance up to the end of iddah. And after the child is born the expenses of the child shall be on father up to the age of 7 years.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
