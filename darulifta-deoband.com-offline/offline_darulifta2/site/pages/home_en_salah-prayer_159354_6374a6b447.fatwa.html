    Title: My question is every day I will miss the Fajr prayer, salah. Now, this time what can I do?

    Question: My question is every day I will miss the Fajr prayer, salah. Now, this time what can I do?

    Answer ID: 159354Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 664/607/M=6/1439)

     

    Go to bed early after Isha. Go to bed with the intention that you will have to get up in each condition whether your heart is not ready and think that whoever misses his one salah is as his family and wealth is looted. If possible, ask your namazi neighbour that he awakens you in the morning for salah or you sleep by setting alarm so that you get up on time.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
