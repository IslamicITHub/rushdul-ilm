    Answer ID: 3051Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 476/350=H)

     

    (1) Did the sister (among the five sisters) of your grandfather died before late grandfather or after? (2) Whom did she left behind among his relatives? (3) Are your parents alive? Please, write these things clearly, then in-sha-Allah we shall reply you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
