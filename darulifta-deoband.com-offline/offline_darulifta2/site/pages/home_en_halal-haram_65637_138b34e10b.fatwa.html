    Title: Pulling (hair on cheeks)

    Question: I am a 20 year old boy. Alhamdulillah I have beard on my face, my question is that on my cheeks can I remove hair. And can I remove it by pulling with tweezers.

    Answer ID: 65637Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 862/880/N=08/1437)

    The hair which grow on cheeks along with beard are not the hair of beard as per the Shariah. Hence it is correct to remove them with tweezers. However if the hair of cheek are not too much then it is better not to remove them. (Fatawa Mahmoodia: 19: 441,442, Question: 9414, 9438, pub.: Idara Siddiq, Dhabel).

     

    أما الأشعار التى على الخدين فليست من اللحية لغة وإن كره الفقهاء أخذها؛ لأنه إن كان بالحديد فذلك يوجب الخشونة فى الخدين ، وإن كان بالنتف فإنه يضعف البصر (فيض الباري، كتاب اللباس، باب قص الشارب 4: 380)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
