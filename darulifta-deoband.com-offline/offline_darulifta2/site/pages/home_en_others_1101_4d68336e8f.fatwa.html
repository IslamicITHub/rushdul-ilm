    Title: I want to know in detail about the practice of saying 'inshaallah'. Is it sunnah or compulsory, is there any punishment reported for not saying it, when have we been instructed to say it?

    Question: I want to know in detail about the practice of saying 'inshaallah'. Is it sunnah or compulsory, is there any punishment reported for not saying it, when have we been instructed to say it?

    Answer ID: 1101Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 419/412=M)

     

    It is Mustahab (desirable) to say in-sha-Allah (If Allah wills) when someone intends or plans to do something in future, but it is not Wajib; therefore if anyone does not do so he will not be labelled as sinner.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
