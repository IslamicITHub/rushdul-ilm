    Question: I am mother of two small daughters. i often dream that i am in a crowded place and my kids get lost in the crowd.i am very worried. Please, can you help me?

    Answer ID: 2817Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 328/239=H)

     

    This is not a dream but satanic insinuation. Keep the habit of reciting Ayatul Kursi and Dua-e-Masoor at bedtime with wuzu. Also, recite Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory salah and blow on your chest.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
