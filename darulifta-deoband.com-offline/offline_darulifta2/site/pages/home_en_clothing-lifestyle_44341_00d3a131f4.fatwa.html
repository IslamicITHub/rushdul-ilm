    Title: Leather Socks with cotton socks

    Question: Is it necessary to wear the leather socks over the cotton socks or it can be used bare footed?

    Answer ID: 44341Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 424/397/N=1434) You can wear the leather socks only, it is not necessary to wear cotton socks inside them.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
