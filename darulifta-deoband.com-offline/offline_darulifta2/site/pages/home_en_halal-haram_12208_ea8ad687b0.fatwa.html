    Question: Respected Usthad, I have two questions: 1. My marriage is fixed with a woman who was working in a bank without Islamic hijaab. Now she realized the mistake and decided to quit the job.I would like to know that if she offer her money which she earned through the banking job to me, can I use it?. If it is not halal, could you please suggest what are the Islamic ways of utilizing that money?. After the marriage is it halal for her to use that money for her personal needs?. Please give a detailed reply. 2. Please give the Islamic ruling on dyeing the hair of head and beard. What are the materials permitted for coloring the hair? Jazakallahu Khaira.... Abdullah

    Answer ID: 12208Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 717/671=B/1430)

     

    If the bank job involved writing interest or helping in interest transaction, so using such an income is not lawful, rather it should be given to a poor without an intention of reward.

    (2) Except the black dye, one can use the other colours provided it did not contain chemicals preventing water to reach to the skin.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
