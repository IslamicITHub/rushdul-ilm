    Title: Whatis the ruling about person/sect, who believe that their Imams are higher/superios in status than some/many of the Prophets? Is this belief kufar? Please answer with refernces from early scholars.

    Question: Assalam O Alaikum, Whatis the ruling about person/sect, who believe that their Imams are higher/superios in status than some/many of the Prophets? Is this belief kufar? Please answer with refernces from early scholars. JazakAllah

    Answer ID: 3171Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 235/225=J)

     

    Any person believing imams as superior than prophets is having a belief of kufr (infidelity). For details see: Shia Sunni Ikhtelaaf by Mufti Yusuf Ludhianwi.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
