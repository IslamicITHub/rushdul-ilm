    Title: Can eating food at any hidu's ivitation is halal or haram?

    Question: assalamva alaikum, can eating food at any hidu's ivitation is halal or haram

    Answer ID: 4227Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 288/288=M/1429)

     

    One can eat at a Hindu?s invitation provided one is sure that neither a haram (impure) agent is used in the food nor it is offering of Hindu gods and goddesses:

    لا باس بالذھاب الی ضیافۃ اھل الذمۃ (الھندیۃ)۔

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
