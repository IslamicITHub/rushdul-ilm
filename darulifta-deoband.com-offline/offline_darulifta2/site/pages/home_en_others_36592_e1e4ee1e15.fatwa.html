    Question ID: 36592Country: Saudi Arabia

    Title: Dream regarding Imam Ghazali Rahmahullah.

    Question: I once dreamt of Imam Ghazali (RA). He was fair and strong. He had long white shoulder-length hair and long beard. His clothes were dark orange in colour, orange turban and orange kurta, vest and lungi. He was giving khutba. I was looking at him in the face. He looked very serious. Please interpret.

    Answer ID: 36592Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 314/174/H=1433) In-shaAllah you shall gain from the knowledge of Imam Ghazali (رحمة الله عليه). Study the Fazail Sadaqat of Hadhrat Shaikhul Hadith (رحمة الله عليه).

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
