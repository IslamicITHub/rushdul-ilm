    Title: as salaam maulana please give me dua or surah against poisones snake which are roaming near our house immaidety?

    Question: as salaam maulana please give me dua or surah against poisones snake which are roaming near our house immaidety?

    Answer ID: 13483Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 919/919=M/1430)

     

    Recite the below mentioned dua in the morning and evening 11 times with durood 11 times before and after:

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ سلام علی نوح فی العالمین

    Also, recite the following dua thrice every morning and evening:

    بسم اللہ لایضر مع اسمہ شئ في الأرض ولا في السماء وھو السمیع العلیم

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
