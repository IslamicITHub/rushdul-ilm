    Title: Please let me know that is taking salary of government or personal holidays halal or haram?

    Question: Please let me know that is taking salary of government or personal holidays halal or haram?

    Answer ID: 35897Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 190/137/B=1433) If the government gives the salary of office days and the personal holidays then it is lawful to receive the salary of holidays.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
