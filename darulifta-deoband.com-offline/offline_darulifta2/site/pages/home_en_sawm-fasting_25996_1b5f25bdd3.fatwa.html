    Answer ID: 25996Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2104/H=620/th=1431)

    If there was no problem, the mas'ala said by the Islamic teacher was not right.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
