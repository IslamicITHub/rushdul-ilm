    Answer ID: 13743Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 910/720=L/1430)

     

    You can purchase the house through bank in a way that the bank purchases the house and includes the additional amount of interest as price and sells the same to you on the total price which you will pay in instalments. Taking loan (with interest) for house when you earn handsome salary is not lawful. The Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) has cursed those who take interest, give it, write it and bear witness to it. (Mishkat 244) Therefore, taking interest based debt (loan) is haram without an exigent condition and dire need. If you manage your expenses, you may save a huge amount which may help you to purchase land and build house.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
