    Title: Attending my sick mother

    Question: My mother is sick from years and now also sick with many organ diseases . I am very disappointed and I am making lots of dua and nafil namaz. Please tell me something.

    Answer ID: 164101Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1431/1237/H=11/1439)

     

    One gets reward of one accepted Hajj if one sees his old parents with love. Allah has given you the chance to serve your old sick mother. Be happy with this and continue the treatment as usual. Read Surah Fatiha (whole surah) 11 times with durood 11-11 times before and after and blow on water and give it to your mother to drink. May she live long! Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
