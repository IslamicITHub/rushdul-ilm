    Question ID: 57788Country: India

    Title: Vitamin D in Low Fat Milk

    Question: In Europe there are different types of milk based on fat quantity. The standard milk has 3% fat. But there are other milk widely used here which contains 1.5 or even 0.5 % fat. To reduce the fat content vitamin D is added on all such milks and the source of vitamin D is from sheep’s wool. They don't kill the sheep rather they cut and they extract vitamin D from sheep’s wool directly, so they just cut the wool and extract vitamin D from it. My question is that is there anything we need to worry about its halal or haram status? As the sheep is not killed at all and they just cut the wool on the skin to extract it. Please guide.

    Answer ID: 57788Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 355/355/SD=07/1436) Yes, it is lawful to cut the wool of sheep and get benefit by extracting vitamin D from it without slaughtering it. قال المرغيناني: ولا بأس ببيع عظام الميتة وعصبها وصوفها وفرنها و شعرها وبرها والانتفاع بذلك كله؛ لأنها طاهرة . (الهداية: 3/39)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
