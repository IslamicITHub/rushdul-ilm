These things ultimately have a great loss in my life and this lose makes me feel to suicide.

    Answer ID: 44194Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 393/312/B=1434) Attend the majlis of any pious scholar and express your doubts before him. He will tell the answer of your questions and will remove all your doubts and objections.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
