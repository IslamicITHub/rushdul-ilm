    Title: My friend's niece in writing divorced to her husband. Is this divorce valid or void? Please reply.

    Question: My friend's niece in writing divorced to her husband. Is this divorce valid or void? Please reply.

    Answer ID: 33533Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1181/763/L=1432) If wife writes talaq, talaq will not take place. Shariah has given the right of talaq to the husband as it is mentioned in hadith: الطلاق لمن أخذ الساق .

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
