    Title: A couple of days ago, I dreamed that I am on the edge of a small river. There were 3 to 4 temples (Hindu) like small open one room building

    Question: A couple of days ago, I dreamed that I am on the edge of a small river. There were 3 to 4 temples (Hindu) like small open one room building. I was looking for a direction and carrier how to go to my destination; meanwhile, I saw that a lady was bathing with a few dresses. The lady guessed well my thought. After a while, the lady who became now a male called on a boatman. The boatman then took my shoes just like a servant with honour. I too was astonished at why the male who seemed a great Hindu religious scholar helped and honored me a lot. When I was going to thank him for his benevolence, he lied on the floor, and was in a bit asleep with bearing a deep thought. When the boat started taking me, I woke up from my sleep.

    Answer ID: 37425Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 45/46/N=1433) In order to tell the meaning of a dream it is necessary that some main circumstances of the person who is seeing the dream are before us. It makes easy to reach the correct interpretation. However after consideration in your dream I reached to the conclusion that you are caught in some religious difficulties. Some Hindu religious priest is targeting you. He is trying to attack your faith by his satanic power and action and you are much worried about your faith and Islam. Thus if it is correct then the dream indicates that you should follow Shariah and sunnah and save your faith and Islam. May Almighty Allah bring a solution from unseen sources to get rid of it! And the Hindu priest who is seeking an opportunity shall fail with the power of your iman, and shall be impressed with you too much, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
