    Question: Which is the authentic book to strenthen the emaan (preferably in english otherwise in hindi). 2)Name the author of kuran(translated/tafseer) which is authentic in english language. 3)Why one should not offer salam(on nabi) after every namaz.pls reply me with hawala as my friend questioned me.

    Answer ID: 6726Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1450/1267=B/1429)

     

    (1) Also study Fazai-e-Amaal and Hayatus Sahaba.

    (2) You should study Maariful Quran of Maulana Mufti Muhammad Shafi which was translated into English by his son Maulana Mufti Taqi Usmani.

    (3) The practice of offering Salam after offering salah invented by the ignorant and the innovators is not proved from the Holy Quran and Hadith. It is a riya (showy thing) to recite durood after salah loudly; one will not get reward for reciting the durood this way. Durood should be recited softly with sincerity, earnestness and concentration sitting alone, then it will be effective and reward-able. You should recite durood as much as you can; rather it is better to have a practice of reciting it in the morning and evening.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
