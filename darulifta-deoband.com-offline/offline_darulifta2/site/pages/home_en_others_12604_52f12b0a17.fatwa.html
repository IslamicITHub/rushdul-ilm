    Question ID: 12604Country: India

    Title: Asalamwalakum! i saw a dream this morning that i have many relatives at my house and they are just preparing for some function.i saw i am cooking chappatis in the kitchen on a big tawa. and my small cousin is helping me.he is trying to lit fire to the wood to cook food and tell him not to do so.then i saw i go to bathroom n take bath.then i saw i am going with a very fast speed and beside me one lady with 2 small kids is on one white horse and i felt that she was running from someone and trying to save her kids.on my way ahead i kept seeing many same kind women on white horses running.i later feel may be they are just idols or some picture but as i move on i saw few sikh men trying to learn how to ride a horse and then there is a big gate where that lane ends where they are practising.now i also feel that i was also riding a horse as i was moving with very fast speed!"Please help in understanding this Allah hafiz!

    Question: Asalamwalakum! i saw a dream this morning that i have many relatives at my house and they are just preparing for some function.i saw i am cooking chappatis in the kitchen on a big tawa. and my small cousin is helping me.he is trying to lit fire to the wood to cook food and tell him not to do so.then i saw i go to bathroom n take bath.then i saw i am going with a very fast speed and beside me one lady with 2 small kids is on one white horse and i felt that she was running from someone and trying to save her kids.on my way ahead i kept seeing many same kind women on white horses running.i later feel may be they are just idols or some picture but as i move on i saw few sikh men trying to learn how to ride a horse and then there is a big gate where that lane ends where they are practising.now i also feel that i was also riding a horse as i was moving with very fast speed!"Please help in understanding this Allah hafiz!

    Answer ID: 12604Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1149/887=H/1430)

     

    The dream indicates that the Muslims in general are indifferent towards the actions guaranteeing salvation in the hereafter, while the other people are active in their worldly matters.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
