    Title: After hajj, we went to masjid ayesha for jiyaarah only and did not intended to perform Umra. We performed Two rakat namaj and came back . Is there any problem being coming out of Haram(masjid-e-ayesha )before performing Tawaful Wida? After one week we performed Tawaful Wida just before leaving Makka Mukarma.

    Question: Asslamu Alikum After hajj, we went to masjid ayesha for jiyaarah only and did not intended to perform Umra. We performed Two rakat namaj and came back . Is there any problem being coming out of Haram(masjid-e-ayesha )before performing Tawaful Wida? After one week we performed Tawaful Wida just before leaving Makka Mukarma.

    Answer ID: 29204Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 167/99/D=1432)

    The tawaf-e-wida (farewell tawaf) which you performed earlier is enough. No extra tawaf or umrah was wajib due to going to Masjid-e-Ayesha. You did well that you returned after offering two rakah salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
