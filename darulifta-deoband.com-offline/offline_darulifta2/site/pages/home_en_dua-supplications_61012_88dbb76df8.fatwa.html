    Title: Dua for conversation problem

    Question: I am an engineer and have a job. There is some conversation problem with me. I am unable to explain whatever I know .Due to this, failed most of my job interviews. On the other hand, my brain is sharp. I can grasp things quickly but not able to keep in memory for some days. I forget most of my past events quickly. I forget people names. I am reciting dua "Rabbish Rahli Sadri....." for ten years after fardh namaz. Please advise, what should I do. I am very upset.

    Answer ID: 61012Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1344/1436/L=01/1437) Consult any Hakim for your treatment. Also recite Ya Qawi (يا قوي) 11 times after each salah while putting your hands on your forehead. Use miswak regularly and recite the Holy Quran extensively. These practices shall sharpen your memory power.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
