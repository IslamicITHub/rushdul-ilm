    Answer ID: 24516Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1475/1154/B=1431)

    According to Shariah, the right of nourishment of daughter after the wife is divorced is on mother up to the age of nine and all the expenses will incur on father. Then the daughter can be handed over to the father.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best
