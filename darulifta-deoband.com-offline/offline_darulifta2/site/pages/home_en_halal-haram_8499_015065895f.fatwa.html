Clearing, collecting the customer's orders carefully: the right amount of product in real time, work was also a small quantity of alcohol sometimes customer's orders for alcohol and but it is not every day.warehouse work, whether it is haram for any work in this stock?the amount of alcohol of 1-2 percent, can I burn my 2 precent cash salary?

    Question: in November to begin the work of food in stock, I have done for four months employment contract, the job is a collector. Clearing, collecting the customer's orders carefully: the right amount of product in real time, work was also a small quantity of alcohol sometimes customer's orders for alcohol and but it is not every day.warehouse work, whether it is haram for any work in this stock?the amount of alcohol of 1-2 percent, can I burn my 2 precent cash salary?

    Answer ID: 8499Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2383/1984=B/1429)

     

    It is forbidden (haram) to do any cooperation in regard to wine. The Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) has cursed those who give help in regard to wine. However, if one had some haram money in his possession, he should give it as charity to the poor without an intention of reward. It is not lawful to burn it.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
