    Title: Is it allowed to have intercourse with your on 1st or 15th or the last night of an islamic moth. because i was told no to do it in these three days of an islamic month?

    Question: Is it allowed to have intercourse with your on 1st or 15th or the last night of an islamic moth. because i was told no to do it in these three days of an islamic month?

    Answer ID: 8311Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1955/1558=H/1429)

     

    It is lawful (without any desirability) to have intercourse on 1st and 15th nights of a month. What you have heard is false and unreliable.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
