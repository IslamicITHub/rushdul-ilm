    Title: Whether a girl is permitted to be married without her father's permission?

    Question: Whether a girl is permitted to be married without her father's permission?

    Answer ID: 7993Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1242/1063=L/1429)

     

    A mature girl can marry in her kufu (match) without the permission of parents too, but it is not better. One should marry with the permission and advise of the parents, it fetched blessing and good fortune.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
