    Title: talaq e muallaq

    Question: mufthi sahib. i was not married.but engagment was done with a girl.i-e over parents were agreed but nikah was not done.i was addicted of alcohal.i wanted to get rid of this habit.about one month ago before my marriage(nikkah) once i said in my heart(my lips moved but voice was not sufficeint that someone else can hear this or i can hear that voice) that if "i drink from today before and after marriage my wife will be Talak on me three times after 7 days of my nikah. i used alcohal after two months of my marriage.now what about my statement.whether talaq have occured. i want my answer according to hanfi maslak.shafi maslak.and imam malik

    Answer ID: 31646Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 628/527/B=1432) Pronouncing is necessary for talaq to take place i.e. talaq will take place only when the words of talaq are pronounced with tongue in a way that it is heard by him or someone else, talaq will not take place by thinking in heart. Similarly, talaq will not take place by moving lips without pronouncing.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
