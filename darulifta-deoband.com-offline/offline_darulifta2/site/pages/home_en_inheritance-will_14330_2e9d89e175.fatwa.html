    Answer ID: 14330Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1363/274=TL=1430)

     

    While living, a person is the owner of his all properties, he can dispose it where he wants, but depriving the heirs of the properties is sternly warned in Islam. Therefore, he should not give all his properties in charity, rather only one third of it.

    (2) If she lives mostly in Pakistan, she should help the people in Pakistan, it is not good to give it to some charitable organization in Canada.

    (3) Instead of a Muslim organization, it is not good to contribute to any Christian or any other organization. Giving to Christian organizations is unlawful if they are involved in anti-Islamic activities.

    (4) In case of writing a will, it will be implemented in one third of the wealth, the remaining two third will be divided among the heirs. In this case, mother will not be sinful, but she will be sinful if deprives the heirs and gives all the wealth to Cancer Charity.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
