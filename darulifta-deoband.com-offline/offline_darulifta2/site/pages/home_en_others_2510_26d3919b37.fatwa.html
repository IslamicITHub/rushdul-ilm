    Answer ID: 2510Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1768/1564=B)

     

    It is not a sign of piousness to see the Prophet (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) in dream and neither is it a source of salvation. Do not worry about this. Get your heart pure from all low morals like love of wealth, love of prestige, ego, greed and display, and get it filled with good morals like sincerity, humility, fear of Allah, kindness and good conduct with near ones. In order to achieve these things, get bai?ah (solemn promise) on the hands of any aalim who follows Shariah and Sunnah of the Prophet (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) and who has experience of guiding people. And, engage yourself in the Zikr and acts prescribed by him for you. Moreover, recite durood as much as you can, in-sha-Allah you will visit the Prophet (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) in your dream.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
