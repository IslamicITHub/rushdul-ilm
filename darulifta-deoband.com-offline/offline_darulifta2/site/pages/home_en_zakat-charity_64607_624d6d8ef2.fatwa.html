    Title: Zakah on Bank FD, Company Provident Fund & Public Provident Fund

    Question: Brothers I have 2 questions. Questions 1: Is zakah obligatory on the amount invested in Bank Fixed deposit, Company Provident Fund and Public Provident Fund? These are very popular means to save income tax, can you please tell me under what scenarios zakah is compulsory on these investment avenues. Questions 2: I want to help one Muslim brother who is undergoing cancer treatment in a hospital, he is having yearly income of around 25000 Rs. Can I pay help him with my zakah amount? What is the criteria to identify persons eligibility to receive zakah amount. I've been looking for the answers of these 2 questions for quite some time now but never got any satisfying reply. Will await your reply.

    Answer ID: 64607Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 743/741/L=07/1437)

    (1) Zakah shall be wajib on the capital amount of fixed deposit as per the condition of zakah not on its interest amount. It shall be necessary to give the interest money in charity. If the PF amount is deducted from the salary of employee without his consent then no zakah is wajib on it right now. Once the amount is received then zakah shall be wajib after passing one complete year on it provided the conditions of zakah are found. However if someone pays an amount to the fund after receiving the salary in his hand then zakah shall be wajib on the deposited amount as per the condition of zakah.

    (2) A person who does not own gold, silver, rupees, business goods, or goods that are extra from daily use in such a quantity that it does not reach to the value of 52.5 total silver (612 gram 360 milligram), he is poor and is entitled to receive zakah and it is right to give him zakah money. And a person who owns that much wealth is not entitled to receive zakah and it is not right to give him zakah money.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
