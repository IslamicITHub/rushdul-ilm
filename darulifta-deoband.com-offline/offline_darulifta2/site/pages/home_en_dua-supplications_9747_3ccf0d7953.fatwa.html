    Answer ID: 9747Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 6/9=H/1429)

    أللھم أکفني بحلالک عن حرامک و أغنني بفضلک عمن سواک

    You and your husband should recite the above mentioned dua 10 times after every obligatory salah with durood 10 times before and after, and pray to Allah to open the door of halal livelihood for you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
