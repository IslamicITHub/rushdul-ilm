    Title: Hajj-e-Badal

    Question: May I perform Hajj-e-Badal of my deceased son-in-law? I wish to go for Hajj-e-Badal with my daughter. I have done my Hajj before four years. I also want to know that what is the difference between normal Hajj and Hajj-e-Badal? What procedure one has to do extra in Hajj-e-Badal.

    Answer ID: 46224Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1087/1087/M=9/1434) There is no addition or changes in the arkan (acts) of Hajj-e-Badal and its method. What you have to do is just say while making the intention that you are doing Hajj-e-Badal from so and so person. After that you should perform all other rituals as you do in normal Hajj.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
