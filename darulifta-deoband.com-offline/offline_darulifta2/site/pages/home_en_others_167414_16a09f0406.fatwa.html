    Title: Seeing mother in dream

    Question: Last month my mother passed away (May Allah grant her Jannat ul Firdous) And she got ill suddenly and was prolonged in hospital unconscious for 42 days . All this to see death was first time to me. My mom she always comes in my dream with different story of past life (some time sad or normal or happy). And in the dream I always say my mom she got well after she is dead or after her unconscious she got conscious. Please advise me for my concern. One more doubt is planting tree on my mom’s qabar sunnah act, just to confirm.

    Answer ID: 167414Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 445/380/L=04/1440)

     

    (1) Seeing your mother in good state after her death is a sign that she is in good state in the grave. However, you should continue doing charity and other good deeds and send its reward to your deceased mother.

    (2) It is not sunnah to plant a tree on mother’s grave.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
