    Title: Mahr

    Question: What is the meaning of mahr? Why do we pay it? What is its minimum & maximum amount? Can it be refused by wife? If it has a large amount then what is the best way to pay it easily?

    Answer ID: 47596Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1312/986/B=10/1434) Mahr is in fact the compensation of living with wife. The minimum amount of mahr is 30.618 gram silver, as the rate of silver fluctuates therefore the amount in rupees may not be fixed. The maximum amount of mahr was not prescribed in Shariah. One can agree upon any amount which he can pay as per his ease. If the mahr is much which one cannot pay once then one can pay it in instalments. It is lawful and easy if the mahr was decided to be with late payment.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
