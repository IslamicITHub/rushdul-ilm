    Question ID: 4070Country: India

    Title: My cousin has no child I am writing his problem in his words as under :- I get married two time, my marriage life with first wife was about seven years but I can not get child with her, thereafter I have given divorce to her due to some other reason, Now I get married second time and period of second marriage has been passed about two year, I and my wife have also recite many wazifa/verse during my both the marriage life. But till date I have not been granted any child by god neither with my first wife nor with second one. Have you any experienced wazifa / verse under the light of Quran or hadith which I or My wife recite and then can I get child? I will be thankful to you if you guide me in this matter.

    Question: Assalamu alaikum, janab Mufti sahib, My cousin has no child I am writing his problem in his words as under :- I get married two time, my marriage life with first wife was about seven years but I can not get child with her, thereafter I have given divorce to her due to some other reason, Now I get married second time and period of second marriage has been passed about two year, I and my wife have also recite many wazifa/verse during my both the marriage life. But till date I have not been granted any child by god neither with my first wife nor with second one. Have you any experienced wazifa / verse under the light of Quran or hadith which I or My wife recite and then can I get child? I will be thankful to you if you guide me in this matter.

    Answer ID: 4070Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 758/649=B)

     

    You should keep reciting often: رب لا تذرنى فردا و انت خير الوارثين

    Better, in the breakfast boil two eggs and peel them off, then write on an egg this verse:السماء بنينها بايد وانا لموسعون  and eat it. While write on the other egg this verse: والارض فرشنها فنعم المهدون and give it your wife to eat. Do this act continuously for forty days and meanwhile keep on doing intercourse with your wife. May Allah bestow you child, illuminate your house and fulfil your desire. Amen!

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
