    Title: I have a 9 months old daughter. Can give her toys like dolls and soft toys of animals etc to play with?

    Question: I have a 9 months old daughter. Can give her toys like dolls and soft toys of animals etc to play with?

    Answer ID: 155531Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 107/9/L=2/1439)

     

    It is not lawful to buy and sell dolls of living animals, as well as to keep them in house and give it to the children to play.

    وكذا بطل مع مال متقوم كالخمر والخنزير ....ويدخل فيه فرس أوثور من خزف لاستيناس الصبي ؛ لأنه لا قيمة له ولا يضمن متلفه . (الدر المنتقي: 2/54) وقال عليه السلام لا تدخل الملائكة بيتا فيه كلب ولا صورة (الصحيح لمسلم: ج 2/200)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
