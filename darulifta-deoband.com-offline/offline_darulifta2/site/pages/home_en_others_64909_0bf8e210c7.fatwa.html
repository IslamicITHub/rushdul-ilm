    Title: Recently saw few dreams want to know tabeer of those dreams.

    Question: I have seen couple of dreams and I want to know the tabeer of that dreams. (1) I have seen in dream I am doing wrong things with my mother. (Nauzubillah) and (2) Recently I started growing beard and in dream I saw my beard hair going in nose and other places.

    Answer ID: 64909Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 771/715/SN=07/1437)

    (1) This dream is a hope for good. If your mother is alive then you keep on supplicating for her longevity and if she is dead then pray for her forgiveness. Read mansoon duas before going to bed and develop the habit of sleeping with wudhu.

    (2) It is nice dream, ma-shaAllah. You shall be blessed with prosperity, in-shaAllah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
