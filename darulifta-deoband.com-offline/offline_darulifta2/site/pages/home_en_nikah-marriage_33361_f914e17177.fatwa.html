    Title: marriage with adulteress

    Question: What are the conditions to be fulfilled to marry the same woman with whom the man is committing zina or had committed zina?

    Answer ID: 33361Posted on: Aug 29, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1167/734/L=1432) There is no prohibition in marrying an adulteress if she is not married. Howover, an adulteress is better for an adulterer. Pious person should not marry an adulteress.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
