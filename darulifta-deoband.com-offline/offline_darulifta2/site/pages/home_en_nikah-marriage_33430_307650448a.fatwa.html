    Title: Done nikah but rukhsati is pending. Want to know exact limits of rights.

    Question: Done nikah but rukhsati is pending. Want to know exact limits of rights. Question I have done only nikah and rukhsati is pending from my family side due to marriage of my elder brother. My question is as follows: (1) Can I meet my wife at her home during coming Eid time, in presence of her parents? (2) Can I give gift to her on Eid and other occasion? (3) What are other limits or boundations between us before rukhsati?

    Answer ID: 33430Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1245/1245/M=1432) You can meet your wife even before rukhsati and can give her gift. According to Shariah, there is no ban on you.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
