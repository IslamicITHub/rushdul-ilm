    Title: I am going to my father-in-law's house. His house is far away (200 km) from my house

    Question: I am going to my father-in-law's house. His house is far away (200 km) from my house. Shall I have to do qasr? Now which rule should I follow. Please suggest me.

    Answer ID: 36370Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 170/L=95/TL=1433) If you do not intend to live in your father-in-laws' house, then it shall not be considered your native place. Hence if you go to your father-in-laws without an intention to stay for fifteen days then you shall do qasr at your father-in-laws' house and in the way.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
