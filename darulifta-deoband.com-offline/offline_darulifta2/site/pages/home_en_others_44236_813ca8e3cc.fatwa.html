    Title: Dream

    Question: My daughter aged 24 years has been seeing lizards in her dream repeatedly for past many nights in the later part of the night. I shall be thankful, if this dream can be interpreted.

    Answer ID: 44236Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 392/307/B=1434) Ask your daughter to recite 4 Quls before going to bed. In-shaAllah, she shall be saved from such dreams.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
