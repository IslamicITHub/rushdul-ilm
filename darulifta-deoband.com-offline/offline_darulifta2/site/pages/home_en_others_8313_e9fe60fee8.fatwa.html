    Title: My Name is Mohammad Rasool. I saw two dreams: (1): I saw a dream that performing Tawaf and saying these words till end (لبیک لبیک لاشریکه لبیک) around the Kaba Sharif near to Fajr time. 2: In Second dream saw people saying, that person is Hazrat Mohammad (PBUH) then I went to Hazrat Mohammad (PBUH) and kiss his blessed hand, but I couldn't see his blessed face clearly.

    Question: Asalumu Alikum: My Name is Mohammad Rasool. I saw two dreams: (1): I saw a dream that performing Tawaf and saying these words till end (لبیک لبیک لاشریکه لبیک) around the Kaba Sharif near to Fajr time. 2: In Second dream saw people saying, that person is Hazrat Mohammad (PBUH) then I went to Hazrat Mohammad (PBUH) and kiss his blessed hand, but I couldn't see his blessed face clearly. Jazaakallahu khaira

    Answer ID: 8313Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1668/2084=B/1429)

     

    (1) In-sha-Allah you will be blessed with hajj.

    (2) You have high respect towards the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم), but you should pay attention to follow the sunnah and rectify the acts which are diverted from the sunnah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
