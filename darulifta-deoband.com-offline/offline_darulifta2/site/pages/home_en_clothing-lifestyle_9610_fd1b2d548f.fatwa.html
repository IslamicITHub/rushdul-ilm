    Question ID: 9610Country: India

    Title: I want to ask that my brother he is a office going person and is 26 yrs in age. His hairs are turning white due to natural factor, please tell whther applying of black colour in hairs in order to make them black is permissible or not. Reply soon.

    Question: Assalamualikum, I want to ask that my brother he is a office going person and is 26 yrs in age. His hairs are turning white due to natural factor, please tell whther applying of black colour in hairs in order to make them black is permissible or not. Reply soon. Jazakumullahu Khaieran kaseeran Kaseera

    Answer ID: 9610Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 2523/1952=H/1429)

     

    It is unlawful; we have been sternly prohibited in Hadith from doing so. In the abovementioned case also, it is unlawful.  

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
