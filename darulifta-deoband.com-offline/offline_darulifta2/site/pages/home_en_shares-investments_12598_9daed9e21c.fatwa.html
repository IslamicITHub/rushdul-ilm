    Answer ID: 12598Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 943/797=D/1430)

     

    As per the Shariah, just by getting confirmation you will not be allowed to sell the shares until it is not transferred and you possess the same. Also, even after transfer and possession as well, we should avoid successive purchase and sale which resembles satta (gambling) and partnership of the company is not meant.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
