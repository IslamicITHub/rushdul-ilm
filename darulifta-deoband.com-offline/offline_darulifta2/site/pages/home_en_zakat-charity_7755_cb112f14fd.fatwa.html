    Title: If I give (pay ) zakat (2.5 % of my earnings)every month, should I pay another 2.5% of my saving after one year also.

    Question: If I give (pay ) zakat (2.5 % of my earnings) every month, should I pay another 2.5% of my saving after one year also.

    Answer ID: 7755Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1772/1482=B/1429)

     

    After passing a year, if you have already paid the 2.5% of your total wealth during the 12 months then there is no need to give more in the name of Zakah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
