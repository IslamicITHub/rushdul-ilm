    Question: I want to ask 6 question regarding quran,hadeeth,islamic history,nabi. 1, how many ulooms are their in quran majeed & what are they? 2, how many ulooms are their in hadeeth shareef & what are they? 3, how many ulooms are their in fiqh & what are they? 4, what is ism e azam? In quran majeed allah tala said in surah al namal that i have given the ilm of kitaab (book)to that person & he brought the taqt of bilquis in a second. 5, what is qurani amaliyat? & its types? 6, in qurani amaliyat can we able to tab e jinnat as well as maalika,farishte ect.

    Answer ID: 6607Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 931/918=L/1429)

     

    It is the effect of Ism-e-Azam that Allah Ta?ala grants every dua made with it. Hadhrat Thanwi wrote in Aamal-e-Qurani that the below three verses contain Ism-e-Azam:

    الم، اللہ لا الہ الا ھو الحی القیوم

    لا الہ الا انت سبحانک انی کنت من الظالمین

    ھو اللہ الذی لا الہ الا ھو عالم الغیب والشھادۃ ھو الرحمن الرحیم

    To subordinate jinn and get his services forcefully without his consent is unlawful. (Ashraful Amaliyat: 177,178)

     

    The other questions are not related to Darul Ifta.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
