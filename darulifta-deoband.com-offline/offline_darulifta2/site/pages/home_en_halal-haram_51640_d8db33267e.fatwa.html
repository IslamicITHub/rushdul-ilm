    Title: Silver ring for gents

    Question: Is it permissible for a Muslim male to wear a silver ring with a stone studded on his hand?

    Answer ID: 51640Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 674/558/B=05/1435) If the weight of the silver is less than one mithqal (4.5 masha) then one may wear the ring studded with stone.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
