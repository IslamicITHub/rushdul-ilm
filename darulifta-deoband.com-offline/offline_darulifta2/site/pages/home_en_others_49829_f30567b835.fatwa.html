    Question ID: 49829Country: Bangladesh

    Title: Regarding dreaming hujur sallallahu alaihi wa sallam

    Question: A few months back, I dreamt Nabi sallallahu alahi wa sallam. I saw he was coming with another person walking through a road. I was seeing him from long distance. Later I saw I am going to him with one leg lame of me and my dress are new but torn. He said you are looking nice. Then I saw him delivering speech in a gathering where He took my feet in his mubarak hand and I saw some injury or disease kind of something on my feet, he just gave air blow on my feet and it went out. I am very excited and tensed to see this dream. Please help me to give the probable explanation of this.

    Answer ID: 49829Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 158/126/D=02/1435) Seeing the Prophet (صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم) in dream is a blessing. The effect seen in the leg indicates that the impoverishment (scarcity of livelihood) will be removed with its blessing.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
