    Title: Ask I work with JK tyre & industries Ltd as Marketing Professional, my Co. gives interest to the dealers who do business with JK Im not paid any interest for appointing dealer or commision, Is my job OK?

    Question: Ask I work with JK tyre & industries Ltd as Marketing Professional, my Co. gives interest to the dealers who do business with JK Im not paid any interest for appointing dealer or commision, Is my job OK?

    Answer ID: 1589Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 517/505=D)

     

    It is allowed for you to work in the company if the company produces tyres etc and you work as marketing professional. If the company is not involving in interest based transactions but it gives interest to its dealers for some reason then this is the matter of dealer and the company. If you do not help in sin your job is OK.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
