    Title: Need Name of boy match with Afraz

    Question: I plan to keep a boy nickname Afraz. Would you please suggest me some name so that I can keep as full name of my boy e.g. Mohammed Afraz or Mohammed Afraz or please suggest with my name also Shajed Ul Kabir.

    Answer ID: 162903Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1108/940/N=11/1439)

     

    For boy choose any name among the below mentioned names.

    Abdullah, Muhammad Affan, Abdur Rahim, Ahmad Abdullah, Muhammad Abdullah, Muhammad Ahmad, Muhammad Ibrahim, Muhammad Yahya, Muhammad Abu Bakr, Muhammad Umar, Muhammad Usman, Muhammad Anas, Muhammad Furqan, Muhammad Affan, Muhammad Fauzan, Muhammad Saleh, Muhammad Muzammil, Muhammad Sulaiman, Muhammad Salman, Muhammad Sauban, Muhammad Talha, Abu Ubaida, Muhammad Tahir, Muhammad Huzaifah, Muhammad Khalid, Muhammad Muavia, Muhammad Shuaib, Muhammad Sadiq, Muhammad Afraz, Muhammad Afroz, Muhammad Arshad, Muhammad Asjad etc.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
