    Title: I would also like to know that are zikr and darood ect. accepted by Allah by women when we don't have scarf on head at home? 2. and also please tell me the names of the 7 heavens and which type of people will be in each heaven and specialy which type of people will be in jannathul-firdos?

    Question: I would also like to know that are zikr and darood ect. accepted by Allah by women when we don't have scarf on head at home? 2. and also please tell me the names of the 7 heavens and which type of people will be in each heaven and specialy which type of people will be in jannathul-firdos? jazakAllah

    Answer ID: 24719Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1259/1037/D=1431)

     

    Yes, it is hoped that Allah Almighty will accept it. However, owing to the etiquette of dhikr you should do it by covering your head.

    There are eight ranks and doors in Jannah that are as follows:

    (1) Jannat-e-Adn (2) Jannatul Firdaus (3) Jannatul Khuld (4) Jannat-e-Naeem (5) Jannat-e-Mawa (6) Darus Salam (7) Darul Muqamah (8) Darul Qarar.

    أن أبواب الجنة و طبقاتها ثمانية ، فقد قال في المناجاة : هي ثمان : جنة عدن ، جنة الفردوس ، جنة الخلد ، جنة النعيم ، جنة المأوي ، دارالسلام ، دارالقرار دارالمقامة (مرقاة : 10/321 كتاب الفتن باب صفة اهل الجنة ط: امداديه ملتان)

    Those who enjoin the fair and forbid the unfair shall live in Jannatul Firdaus.

    وعن كعب : ليس في الجنان أعالى من جنة الفردوس ، فيها الآمرون بالمعروف والناهون عن المنكر (التفسير الكبير للرازي : 21/ 149 سوره كهف ط : بيروت)

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
