    Question: ASLAMUALAKUM. i married before 2 year. im working in dubai now i want to do second nikkah here in dubai with the muslim widow girl. if i need permission from my first wife and my parrent or i can do it without permission. I M TRYING TO KEEP IT SECRET FROM MY FAMILY.WAITING FOR ANSWER.

    Answer ID: 14583Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 1143/1143=M/1430)

     

    If you can afford the second marriage, you can do so, it is not unlawful, but maintaining justice with both the wives is necessary as the Quran mentions it in Surah Nisa. You should seek the consent of parents for second marriage as well, it is better. But, if you can not do justice with both the wives, you should not proceed to do nikah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,
