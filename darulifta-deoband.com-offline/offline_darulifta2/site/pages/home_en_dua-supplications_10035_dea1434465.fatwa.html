    Title: asalamualykum sir i have many skin disease for many years like acne, allergy etc. please sir provide any dua to get ride from it. thanking you

    Question: asalamualykum sir i have many skin disease for many years like acne, allergy etc. please sir provide any dua to get ride from it. thanking you

    Answer ID: 10035Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 61/39=H/1429)

     

    Recite Surah Fatiha 11 times before or after Fajr (before noon) with durood 11 times before and after. Repeat the same before going to bed and blow on water and drink it. Also, blow on your hand and pass on entire body. Moreover, recite ya Lateef 11 times after every obligatory salah.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
