    Title: As salam alia kum,respected Mufti saheb,iam going on umrah inshallah in a week, i have purchased my airline tickets online using my credit card,I will repay the due once i get back from umrah ie; before the due date. please advice me wether my umrah will be acceptable or not?

    Question: As salam alia kum,respected Mufti saheb,iam going on umrah inshallah in a week, i have purchased my airline tickets online using my credit card,I will repay the due once i get back from umrah ie; before the due date. please advice me wether my umrah will be acceptable or not?

    Answer ID: 13116Posted on: Aug 30, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 841/841=M/1430)

     

    It is better to pay the amount before performing umarh so that you have no right pending on you and you perform the umrah being free from all liabilities. However, if you cannot do so and pay the amount after returning from umrah without giving interest, it is also alright. And, the umrah will be valid and acceptable if you will perform it observing all rules and obligations.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
