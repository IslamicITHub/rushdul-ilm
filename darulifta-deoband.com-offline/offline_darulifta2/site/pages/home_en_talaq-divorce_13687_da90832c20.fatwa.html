    Answer ID: 13687Posted on: Sep 1, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 946/946=M/1430)

     

    If the husband confesses that he during quarrel said ?talaq, talaq, talaq? thrice to his wife, or there are Sharai witnesses to it, the three talaq took place and wife became divorced with mughallazah bainah. After three talaqs, it is unlawful and haram to live with her without halalah, as the Quran says:

    فان طلقھا فلا تحل لہ من بعد حتی تنکح زوجا غیرہ

    Neither there is need to announce the same nor they are allowed live together for the sake of children until she marries him after halalah shariah. And unless the nikah is made, they should live separately observing hijab. The maintenance of divorce woman in iddah and the expenses of nourishing children will be born by the man, he will give to her with purdah and through any mahram.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
