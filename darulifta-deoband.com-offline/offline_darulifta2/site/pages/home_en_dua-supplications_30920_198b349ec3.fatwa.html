    Question ID: 30920Country: United Kingdom

    Title: I have applied with my two sons for indefinite visa, being living in UK for the last ten and half years. it has been refused.

    Question: I have applied with my two sons for indefinite visa, being living in UK for the last ten and half years. it has been refused, my husband is in Pakistan as we have no visa now to travel.my kids are in the middle of their universaty and schooling and i am very depressed because we r in this situation for the last 14 months. I pray five times with tahajjud and lots of wazeefas. Kindly give me any length any type of wazifa or dua so that we can get visa and can live with husband and kids together. i cant do my job and cant leave to go because of children education. please make a special dua so i can find a way out by the grace of GOD.

    Answer ID: 30920Posted on: Aug 31, 2020

    Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Rahim !

    (Fatwa: 368/327/B=1432) You should recite ya Lateef (يا لطيف) extensively; no number is fixed. You can recite it as per your capacity as many times as you wish.

    Allah (Subhana Wa Ta'ala) knows Best

    Darul Ifta,

    Darul Uloom Deoband, India
