Reading Qur’aan over a ring then wearing it

26 August 2000

Question 11982

What is the ruling on reciting Qur’aan over a piece of cloth or a ring, then wearing it for protection from the evil eye and witchcraft?

Answer

We put this question to Shaykh Muhammad ibn Saalih al-‘Uthaymeen, may Allaah preserve him, who answered as follows:

That is not permitted.

Source: Shaykh Muhammad ibn ‘Uthaymeen
